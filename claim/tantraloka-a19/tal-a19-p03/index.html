tal-a19-v21 — In the venerable Dīkṣottara this rite, fire-put-in: haṃsa, man below his, with Rudra-bindu.

tal-a19-v22 — In the disciple's body joined, this unagitated: a hundred should he mutter; having departed by upward blink, the disciple thus may go supreme.

tal-a19-v23 — This indeed is the rite in the venerable Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine: this departure-initiation to be performed by the yogin's guru.

tal-a19-v24 — Unpracticed in prāṇa-conduct, how will he perform this? The to-be-spoken Brahma-vidyā, with parts, partless-included.

tal-a19-v25 — In his ear, or should read again, again, or then should cause to read; himself and rite should perform, tattva-purity-and-the-rest, the guru.

tal-a19-v26 — By mantra-rite-force, full-oblation-thus, he should join to the supreme; without yoga-practice even, immediate-departure-giving, the guru.

tal-a19-v27 — By knowledge-mantra-rite-meditation-force to perform: should be able; by this is departed the disciple, by force alone, one moment.

tal-a19-v28 — Having transgressed time, enjoyment is momentary; what then? Immediate-departure-giving and another, in which full-oblation then.

tal-a19-v29 — Should give when his life-breaths would certainly be desiring to depart: even without rite, by forthcoming Brahma-vidyā force, the guru.

tal-a19-v30 — By ear-muttering use, from tattva-armor-net, liberating as desired: in with-parts, in partless, in both.
