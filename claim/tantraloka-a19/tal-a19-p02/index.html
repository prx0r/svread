tal-a19-v11 — Having performed all the aforesaid nyāsa, equal-to-fire-of-time, by withdrawal-sequence together with the two scissors.

tal-a19-v12 — Having performed fire-natured dhāraṇā, all-marma-scorching: he should fill the body with breath from thumb to head-end.

tal-a19-v13 — Having drawn up from thumb to upper-end, by the to-be-spoken he should cut the marmas from the hole-end, by Kālarātrī dismiss.

tal-a19-v14 — By this sequential yoga, yoked, libation-abandoned: even the samayin goes to that initiation — thus in the Mālinī doctrine.

tal-a19-v15 — From sixteen-supports-six-wheels-three-targets-five-ether, or somewhere else, as before the beast-rite.

tal-a19-v16 — Having entered the root from the kanda-region, cutting from non-difference-discernment, by full-oblation use he should join in his own-desired abode.

tal-a19-v17 — Knowledge-trident, blazing, radiant-three-wheels-blazing: having meditated by that, his feeling, awakening, whirling.

tal-a19-v18 — Inflaming, striking, pricking, moving, again and again from-kanda-wheel-going: he should perform specially in the heart-lotus.

tal-a19-v19 — At the twelve-end, then, having performed, in the bindu-pair he should cast: in the unmarked or supreme abode, joined to the Supreme Lord.

tal-a19-v20 — Not should he perform any saṃskāra for him: thus says the god in the Gahvara — what of his full one, by śrāddha-and-the-rest considered?
