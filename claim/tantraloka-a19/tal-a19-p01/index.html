tal-a19-v01 — Now the initiation giving immediate departure is set forth: from that moment, or from enjoyment, at body's fall one may go to Śiva — so hinted in the Mālinīśāstra by the Great Lord.

tal-a19-v10 — Having performed all the aforesaid rite, from samaya-purity, he should place his blazing marma-scissors knife.

tal-a19-v02 — At body's fall, when death is near, from śaktipāta's clearness, having obtained Śāṃkara initiation: hence from the initiation-moment, the supreme.

tal-a19-v03 — The meaning 'may go to Śiva' was explained here, from prior-posterior discernment, by our venerable guru Śambhunātha.

tal-a19-v04 — When death is near and śaktipāta arises, there, in mild cases, by guru-service life may go to diminution.

tal-a19-v05 — Or else, through kin-friends-and-the-rest, the Omnipresent's [grace] falls; or before, the samayin has not at all obtained supreme initiation.

tal-a19-v06 — Even the initiated, desiring to abandon life-breaths, freed from affliction: the final guru should then perform immediate-departure initiation.

tal-a19-v07 — But not in immature impurity, nor even in remaining-karmic-body, should departure be performed: so set forth in the venerable Gahvara.

tal-a19-v08 — Having seen the disciple age-seized, oppressed by disease, having made depart, then he should join this one in the supreme principle.

tal-a19-v09 — In the qualifier-qualified relation, from desire-conduct-arrangement, the aforesaid things were here set forth by venerable Śambhunātha.
