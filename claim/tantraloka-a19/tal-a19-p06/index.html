tal-a19-v51 — Even fly-heard mantra: expiation-fitness should perform. Thus immediate-departure is spoken, by that command of the guru.

tal-a19-v52 — Samayin and the rest even, at the spoken time, for the spoken meaning's accomplishment: himself should perform practiced prāṇa-conduct going-coming.

tal-a19-v53 — Unmade-authorized or even the guru, for samaya-purity, in lower-state: but not knowledge-kindled-conscious.

tal-a19-v54 — Thus this immediate-departure hinted in Mālinī-doctrine: himself or by the guru or then as to be performed by the Great Lord.

tal-a19-v55 — All enjoyment ugly, but having deemed, body should abandon if: then by that, quickly by sequence, joined, samayin, Śiva.

tal-a19-v56 — Spoken this immediate-departure, which to-be-guarded, by the wise like life.
