tal-a19-v41 — By sense-independence inner consciousness-power, by self-luminous: prior body indeed desiring to abandon not supreme, and established.

tal-a19-v42 — In the middle, from awakening-force, would awaken the person: mantras, sound-made, by pure-reflection-nature itself.

tal-a19-v43 — By meaning-self, and shining that meaning-awakening: by that his, of dissolved-eyed, awakening arises itself.

tal-a19-v44 — From self-consciousness-same-kind-mantra-reflection-proximity: as indeed-small-speed wind, same-kind-mixed.

tal-a19-v45 — Speedy, so-self: sleeping-reflection even thus awakens; awakened he and born, not and-uninitiated, is called.

tal-a19-v46 — Initiation, for, name, saṃskāra: not but-other-is, and-his, for, hence indeed, own scripture reads somewhere in samaya.

tal-a19-v47 — Having heard that, someone if fortunate is liberated, not his that loss; scripture-censure let not this one do — of two fall-giving.

tal-a19-v48 — Thus supreme this, not uninitiated before, should read thus: as and samayin in wood, in clod, or mantra-joining.

tal-a19-v49 — Doing in that flutters thus: not loss, likewise here, for since his proof-arrival-desiring, of samayin thus.

tal-a19-v50 — Of one engaged, by nature, in that released: not indeed loss. But the sādhaka always in the to-be-accomplished fruit, from rule-constraint.
