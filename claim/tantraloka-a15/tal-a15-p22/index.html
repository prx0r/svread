tal-a15-v211 — Their division of directions comes from four faces; if a fifth were joined to them, those two would abandon their own nature.

tal-a15-v212 — Therefore, even within the cosmic egg, the Lord's power of knowledge, appearing as the sun dense with light, makes the division of the directions.

tal-a15-v213 — For example, at the equinox, where the sun is first seen is called east, and where its shadow falls is called west.

tal-a15-v214 — As the sun moves on its course, what lies to its left is the south, where it shines with fierce rays as if blazing intensely.

tal-a15-v215 — What lies in front of that is the left, slightly touched by its light; therefore it is gentle, yet not without light.

tal-a15-v216 — Where the sun sets is established as the west; and even there, for some, the earlier light can still be seen.

tal-a15-v217 — That same order was determined before for these as in sound; that direction is so, and its fruit-giving remains even in reversal.

tal-a15-v218 — Varied fruit-attainment depends on light; thus direction may be sun-dependent, various, and such.

tal-a15-v219 — Presided over by the great lord bearing varied form; what more need be said of the worshipper facing that?

tal-a15-v220 — Directions are divided east, south, north, west; that direction-division made according to own course is always Shiva.
