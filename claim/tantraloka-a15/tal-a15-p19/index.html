tal-a15-v181 — In a circle of light, in space, or on a well-smeared ground, one should worship the mothers, who stand on the trident, the sun, the bull, and Nandin, and also worship the lord of the field.

tal-a15-v182 — One should also worship the yoginīs, either with separate mantras joined with oṃ, namas, and their names, or with a single utterance, as they are called the outer retinue.

tal-a15-v183 — In worship the mantra is oṃ, the name in the fourth-case form, and namas. Having thus worshipped outside, one should sprinkle the doorway and then worship it.

tal-a15-v184 — This procedure is seen in the teaching of the Three-headed One and others. Above the doorway one should worship Gaṇeśa and Lakṣmī, on the right and left, and then continue with those associated with them.

tal-a15-v185 — In the middle one should place Vāgīśvarī, and likewise the pair Diṇḍi and Mahodara, in order on their own right and left, following that same sequence.

tal-a15-v186 — One should properly worship each one: Nandi and Kāla, the river who flows by three paths, Kālindī, and the goat-faced and ram-faced pair, on the door branches beginning from one's own right.

tal-a15-v187 — Below the threshold one should worship Dehalī, Ananta, Īśa, and the supporting power; in the middle of the doorway one should worship the great weapon together with Sarasvatī.

tal-a15-v188 — All these, situated on the lotus support, are declared to be destroyers of obstacles. In their worship the mantra is used as before, with an arrangement of two lamps.

tal-a15-v189 — One should perform the worship here with offering water, flowers, touch, incense, food offerings, and homage; and the offering water should be prepared with excellent substances.

tal-a15-v190 — Or one may perform the worship of the doorway deities with a single utterance. If one performs secret worship, then that outer retinue is included in that condensed form.
