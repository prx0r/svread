tal-a15-v591 — Because initiation gives mantra scripture and truth knowledge, he is indeed the guru; the unmanifest linga stands, and no linga is held.

tal-a15-v592 — One should not equally mix conduct with linga-bearers; only the linga-bearer is to be protected, not treated with disgust, even if deformed.

tal-a15-v593 — In the Kula it is said that liberation comes from removing doubt; this impure dwelling and even news of liberation are rare.

tal-a15-v594 — Do not write the mantra heart; extract the mantra from its limb, but do not scratch it in writing.

tal-a15-v595 — Do not fixate on non-truth, and do not act from partiality toward knowers of birth, knowledge, family, conduct, body, place, quality, or meaning.

tal-a15-v596 — Quickly abandon the eight planets and non-planets shown in the cave, and likewise those shown as horse in the Nishichara beginning.

tal-a15-v597 — If I am a Brahmin, how can this birth-planet, which obstructs others, be performed beyond what Veda scripture says?

tal-a15-v598 — Thus others are also examples; by the Kula cave path, even the unwilling shows that form in non-that nature.

tal-a15-v599 — He here covers true form, like a planet as said; in the nature of consciousness there is no conception of birth and the like.

tal-a15-v600 — Form covers that form by true form; any conception sufficiently covers the indivisible self of the consciousness principle.
