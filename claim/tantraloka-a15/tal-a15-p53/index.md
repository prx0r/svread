## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v521** — One authorized as a samaya-initiate should worship the Lord under the guru, and the guru should make that person, now given a Rudra-share, hear the samaya-pledges.
**tal-a15-v522** — In the Yāmala and related texts of the Goddess, the pledges are set forth as eight groups of eight, including restraint in speech and action, concealment, worship, satiation, and contemplation.
**tal-a15-v523** — Striking and deluding, and so on, belong to an eightfold set of pledges expressed in eight ways, forming the unified samaya conduct that reflects the nature of mantra and tantra.
**tal-a15-v524** — One should not speak false, harsh, or untrue words, nor engage in fruitless action, harm, or touching another's wife.
**tal-a15-v525** — One should not practice pride, hypocrisy, or rites for spirits, poison, and disease, and one should guard one's own mantra, rosary, and knowledge that has the nature of true knowledge.
**tal-a15-v526** — One should conceal one's conduct, qualities, hardships, and signs of success, and one should honor the guru, the scripture, the god, the fire, elders in knowledge, women, and the vow.
**tal-a15-v527** — According to one's ability, one should worship the guru-lineage and this eightfold group: the poor, the afflicted, ancestors, field-guardians, living beings, and birds.
**tal-a15-v528** — One should satiate the cremation-ground host of beings, the goddess of the body, Śiva, Śakti, and likewise the self, as well as the seal that has the form of mantra.
**tal-a15-v529** — One should contemplate worldly enjoyment and liberation as taught by the guru, and restrain attachment, hatred, envy, contraction, jealousy, and pride.
**tal-a15-v530** — One should strike down those who break the pledges and their wrong practices, as well as the cruel and hostile followers of the bound path, slanderers, and dullards.
