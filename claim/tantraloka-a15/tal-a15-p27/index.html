tal-a15-v261 — Having attained identity in the body, one should reveal a pure emanation. Beginning from the installation of the form, the emanation that has spread forth here is that pure emanation.

tal-a15-v262 — By the power and order of the installation, the emanation is brought to non-difference and made pure. Therefore those deluded people who object regarding the shaking off of the burning of bonds are mistaken.

tal-a15-v263 — When one is established in peaceful Shiva, why would he descend again? Those who ask this are refuted from the outset, for they do not understand the ultimate reality of Śambhu.

tal-a15-v264 — They do not know, because they are devoid of the essence of the flashing of their own awareness. For this peaceful Shiva is not some differentiated entity.

tal-a15-v265 — Anything excluded from all other paths is limited somewhere, like a pot. But this consciousness, whose nature is great light, truly expands.

tal-a15-v266 — That consciousness is Shiva, and Shiva's nature is to appear as all forms. Therefore, through that very manifestation, he expands by his own essence.

tal-a15-v267 — In this manifestation, both non-difference and difference of one's own self appear. When difference expands, maya unfolds, and with it the maya-bound subject comes into play.

tal-a15-v268 — When non-difference expands, that very maya-bound subject unfolds into Shiva-nature. The maya-bound subject attains this through skill gained by practicing identification with that form.

tal-a15-v269 — Shiva alone is declared to be the fruit of that practice, including nyasa and the rest. For just as one becomes shaped by contemplating, 'I am a doer of bad deeds,' so too contemplation determines one's state.

tal-a15-v270 — So too, for one who contemplates, 'I am Shiva, I am no other,' the result follows accordingly. This alone is called firmness: when it has gone to the very heart of reflective awareness.
