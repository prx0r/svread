**tal-a15-v431** — Just as a swan rises into the sky while its reflection sinks in the water, so too this method works here.

**tal-a15-v436** — This is said in the Trishiras tantra in the description of supreme purpose; then by fire purified with previously established other mantras.
**tal-a15-v437** — The offering, or the accomplished one, is simultaneous with disciples' oblation; heroic or worldly substances may be added by wish.
**tal-a15-v438** — On success of the offering, all acts come from heart-mantra union; then taking the offering, the guru fills it by royal authority.
**tal-a15-v439** — Having made a ladle or spoon according to enjoyment or liberation, there are triads of gods, powers, and yantras.
**tal-a15-v440** — Reciting the seventh mother-presence in order, one by one, with svaha and nectar syllable, offer the ghee remainder into the fire.
