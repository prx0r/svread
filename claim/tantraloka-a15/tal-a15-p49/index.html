tal-a15-v481 — Entering the heart, meditate; the sleeping child, with the guru worshipping Shankara, attains freedom in temple sleep.

tal-a15-v482 — Eat the offering with a friend, then clean the teeth, and sleep with mind fixed in the heart wheel by mantra rays.

tal-a15-v483 — In the morning the guru, having completed daily worship of Shankara, knows the dream-seen meanings of disciple and self from strength and weakness.

tal-a15-v484 — Self-seen strength is not other through awakening excess; in knowledge equality, dream equality would again arise between guru and disciple.

tal-a15-v485 — God, fire, guru, worship cause and utensils are pleasing; woman, wine drinking, and eating of uncooked meat also appear.

tal-a15-v486 — Blood drinking, head cutting, smearing with blood, feces, and urine, and riding pleasing vehicles such as mountain, horse, and elephant.

tal-a15-v487 — For pleasure it would even be; mostly that is declared auspicious; announce it for growth of satisfaction, for joy indeed is the supreme fruit.

tal-a15-v488 — Therefore other inauspicious signs there; from homa of eight hundred, inauspicious is not inauspicious; thus the guru tells the disciples.

tal-a15-v489 — Indeed great effort arises to cut rooted doubt; but for those whose doubt dissolves, dream-power arises.

tal-a15-v490 — Nothing auspicious or inauspicious would be; if thus, from variegation power the sattva self clearly sees from sign only.
