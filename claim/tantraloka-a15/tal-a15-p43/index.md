## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v421** — By promoting outer display and making consciousness secondary, such a person does not receive the descent of power. It is said in the Svacchanda Tantra that even one initiated in that state does not attain liberation.
**tal-a15-v422** — But then, what fruit does the rite performed in his initiation have? To this the knowers of Agama scripture reply: at that time, the mantras, made of Vama power—
**tal-a15-v423** — The mantras bind him firmly in the great prison-house of worldly existence. But their grace-bestowing power, when kindled by the guru, is different.
**tal-a15-v424** — One should purify the disinterested transgressive act prescribed in one's own scripture. Then, by placing things above and below, one makes an arrangement beautiful in its mutual facing.
**tal-a15-v425** — Taking the spoon and ladle as Shiva and Shakti, filled with the nectar of ghee, one does so through the method of uniting mind, breath, and body into one.
**tal-a15-v426** — With care, one should place the left hand holding the spoon handle and the supported right hand below the throat, pressing them firmly.
**tal-a15-v427** — One should place the spoon below, directing the breath upward and upward until the end of the twelve, into the fire of awareness, from the moon circle.
**tal-a15-v428** — As the supreme, delightful nectar falls from the tip of the spoon, one should cast into the fire, at the mouth of mantra, an oblation ending with Vaushat.
**tal-a15-v429** — The awareness above is reflected in the fire pit. The fire is breath, the spoon and ladle are its instruments, and the ghee is the essence of consciousness in the form of intention.
**tal-a15-v430** — Knowing thus from the beginning the fire pit, spoon, ladle, ghee, and mantras, one should contain them firmly in the fire of awareness at the twelvefold end and cast the full oblation.
