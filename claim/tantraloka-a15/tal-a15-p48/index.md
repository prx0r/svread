## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v471** — In this tantra, the ācārya is one who has obtained the higher rites through the sequence beginning with samaya; never a Buddhist, Vaiṣṇava, or the like.
**tal-a15-v472** — Thus what arose by context has been settled, and now the main topic is taken up again: having performed the Śiva-hand rite, by that the disciple's bonds are burned away.
**tal-a15-v473** — Having brought the disciple, through meditative absorption, to the point of rest, the guru then places that child-disciple in the vessel, in the weapon-vessel, in the fire, and in his own self.
**tal-a15-v474** — He should have the disciple make prostration, then cast an offering to the beings and Mothers; then, having worshipped Śaṃkara, he should prepare a bed consecrated with the weapon-mantra.
**tal-a15-v475** — Having placed the disciple on that bed and installed the mantras, the guru establishes rest in the disciple's heart-wheel and then goes to his twelve-end.
**tal-a15-v476** — Through the arising of the twelve vowels the net of kalās becomes diminished; then, on entry, he becomes bright with the sixteen kalās gathered together.
**tal-a15-v477** — As the full moon of his own consciousness, he should rest in the heart of the child-disciple until the rising, and then go to the twelve-end.
**tal-a15-v478** — He should enter the heart-wheel again in this way; this is the sequence of the sleep rite. When sleep has come, this disciple perceives the stainless moon and sun.
**tal-a15-v479** — In the heart-wheel he forcibly joins together the full and the thin moon and sun, and he truly sees the light of the forcibly stainless moon and sun.
**tal-a15-v480** — He sees a dream that clearly reveals future good and other outcomes; and it is taught that, having meditated on the full and the thin in the domain of the twelve, this occurs.
