## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v501** — Chaitra and ashvayuja; after them seven oblation sacrifices; placed fire offering, full moon, and seeing.
**tal-a15-v502** — Four-month rite, animal binding, with sautramani; these, agnishtoma first, then soma-related and sixteen vajapeya.
**tal-a15-v503** — Aptoryama and atiratra; these seven are soma-established; golden-foot sacrifice surrounded by a thousand.
**tal-a15-v504** — The thirty-eighth is horse sacrifice; householder rite would be by measure; forest and renunciation rites are considered the fortieth.
**tal-a15-v505** — Compassion, forbearance, non-envy, purity, good conduct, auspiciousness, non-miserliness, and non-desire are remembered as this eightfold set of qualities of the self.
**tal-a15-v506** — The girdle, staff, skin, lifelong observances, fire worship, twilight rite, and alms are the seven consecrations, and there are also seven vows.
**tal-a15-v507** — The Bhauta and Pashupata are two, then the Ganesha and Ganapatya, and with the Unmattaka, sword-edge, and Ghrita-lord observances they make fourteen in all.
**tal-a15-v508** — These are remembered as the principal consecrations of vow-binding; in the case of renunciation, the final rite is as if in the womb, and by that one is consecrated.
**tal-a15-v509** — Then the person becomes twice-born and fit to attain a share in Rudra; the guru should apply these consecrations in the very sequence of the breath.
**tal-a15-v510** — Alternatively, using oblations of sesame and the like preceded by mantra, one uses the formula: Om, the heart, the name, 'I purify, beloved of fire'.
