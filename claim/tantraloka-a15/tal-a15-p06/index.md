## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v51** — With water and the Malini mantra, one should satisfy the sun, the satisfier of the whole world, and also satisfy the gods, ancestors, sages, yakshas, demons, and whatever else is elemental.
**tal-a15-v52** — One should satisfy all beings through the vital breath, whose nature is power, and that power is the sun. Then one should recite the single supreme mantra according to the method of utterance taught earlier.
**tal-a15-v53** — This is regarded as the water bath, culminating in the awareness of becoming one with that. For the fire bath, ash arisen from fire is recited over with the weapon mantra so that it removes impurity.
**tal-a15-v54** — Ash consecrated with the five-limb mantras is applied to the head, face, heart, and secret place. Then a handful of ash, recited over with the root mantra and its limbs, should be cast on the head.
**tal-a15-v55** — After washing the hands and feet with water alone, one performs sipping and the other acts, then satisfaction and recitation. In this way, the ash bath is indeed the fiery bath.
**tal-a15-v56** — When a refreshing wind blows gently, like the dust raised by cows, one should perform the wind bath by uttering the mantra, or by moving back and forth in that wind.
**tal-a15-v57** — When one sits in the stainless sky with a gaze made one-pointed on the pervasive expanse, remembering the mantra, what other purity could there be beyond that?
**tal-a15-v58** — Or, if one lets water falling from the stainless sky touch the body while joined with mantra recitation, that is such a sky bath.
**tal-a15-v59** — Thus, by contemplating with the state of Shiva in the lights of moon, sun, and fire, and by immersing purified through the Malini, where would one not become fit?
**tal-a15-v60** — The self alone is the supreme lord, the great pool beyond ritual observance. Having immersed the universe there, one should abide in that very place as pure and as the purifier.
