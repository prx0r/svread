## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v71** — In the middle of the mantra circle one performs worship and satisfaction with drops. With that substance one sprinkles oneself and performs consecratory sprinkling using the jar seal.
**tal-a15-v72** — Through satisfaction of the deity, resting on the path of both body and vital breath, one obtains the fruit of all pilgrimages, austerities, sacrifices, gifts, and the like.
**tal-a15-v73** — In the wine bath, the foremost practitioner who seeks liberation may become liberated, because wine is of the nature of Śiva and all mantras arise from Śiva.
**tal-a15-v74** — There is no difference between Śiva and Śakti; the rays arise from Śakti, and wine produces their bliss. Therefore wine is of the nature of Śiva.
**tal-a15-v75** — When the full form of consciousness is awakened, one is fit for ritual action. Bathing distinguished by mantra, meditation, and absorption is real bathing; what lacks that is not.
**tal-a15-v76** — Bathing is fitting because it brings fitness for nyāsa and other rites. This eightfold bathing is twofold, as outer and inner.
**tal-a15-v77** — The inner bath is the flooding of nectar from the upper moon, established above the cranial aperture and pervading a span of one and a half finger-widths.
**tal-a15-v78** — Eight forms are taught, each arising from the dvādaśānta. One may perform any one of these baths, or do them in two, three, or more ways.
**tal-a15-v79** — Thus the rule of bathing has been declared by Bhairava for purification. Now what must be done after bathing is taught.
**tal-a15-v80** — Seeing that one's inner state is clear, one should then go to the place of worship, such as a mountain top, a riverbank, a solitary liṅga, and so on.
