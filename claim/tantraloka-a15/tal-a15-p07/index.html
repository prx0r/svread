tal-a15-v61 — Thus there is the group of eight baths, each later one better than the previous in purification. In every case, afterward one should apply that mantra in a unified form.

tal-a15-v62 — The sage devoted to the eight baths attains in order firmness, nourishment, the burning away of impurity, power, pervasion, purification, abiding, and non-difference.

tal-a15-v63 — These eight forms are indeed gracious in nature and are essentially Shiva. Through meditation on them as one's own form and as Shiva's form, they grant their respective fruits.

tal-a15-v64 — By this method in worship, performing the application beginning with the bulb and the support, whether in expanded or condensed form, one obtains the corresponding fruit beginning with earth.

tal-a15-v65 — For in the movement of yoga, the mantras are earthly on earth, watery in water, and so on, until in Shiva they become wholly of Shiva's nature.

tal-a15-v66 — In the revered Nirmaryada scripture this is well explained: earth and the other elements also have a special form approved by heroic practitioners.

tal-a15-v67 — For heroic practitioners, battle dust is the heroic water, there is heroic ash and the great wind, and also the cremation ground, forest, and sky, along with the moon and sun as brought into that setting.

tal-a15-v68 — The self, well established with all the affliction of conceptual thought shaken off, completes the heroic group of eight forms to be honored in bathing, worship, and so on.

tal-a15-v69 — In the revered Mantrishiras it is taught that one may use wine, liquor, or other such spirits, provided they are very tasty, clear, subtle, and very fragrant.

tal-a15-v70 — Using the substance as present in the bulb and so on, within and without, one performs the five consecrations: inspection, sprinkling, striking, nourishing, and veiling.
