## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v331** — The emanated limbs and so on are worshipful and spark-natured, separate; the goddess going in the middle is indeed she, formed of true being.
**tal-a15-v332** — The time-drawing goddess is fierce, peaceful, and mixed everywhere; in the siddha tantra the supreme goddess with one syllable is thus praised.
**tal-a15-v333** — The supreme mother goddess Mālinī, middle-risen, is in the middle; one should place there the all-letters-composed supreme sun-radiance.
**tal-a15-v334** — At her crest-tip is indeed the one-syllabled; her limbs and so on are thus; then the universe emerged and is worshiped in south and north.
**tal-a15-v335** — It may indeed be worshiped by that; once in the middle one should worship, as said in the Shridevi tantra sacrifice named Damara.
**tal-a15-v336** — At the nose-tip is threefold time; the time-drawing goddess, always situated in the mouth and calculating breath and inbreath, draws in the heart.
**tal-a15-v337** — Through inhalations and retentions it holds and seizes time, but through exhalation it completely seizes all time; rising with the exhalation, it does so in an instant.
**tal-a15-v338** — The supreme will-power, by name the awakener of the three powers, is to be worshipped; it draws in all and shatters the very support of time.
**tal-a15-v339** — O Shiva, the powers of seeing, action, and will, when engaged in the gross and subtle cutting of the knowable, become in order the sakala and the pralayākala.
**tal-a15-v340** — When pure but asleep, the powers become the knowledge-limited state; when awakened, knowing some divided knowables, they are called mantras.
