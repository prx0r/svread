tal-a15-v221 — He presides like the sun from varied body; even self-risen directions have own lords, and Indra and others indeed preside.

tal-a15-v222 — They are indeed portions of light-power, following light; whatever lordship of light is Indra, but that greatness is something else.

tal-a15-v223 — He is fire in the controller-desired state; Yama, Raksha, then those; Varuna and that; from unsteadiness air is called.

tal-a15-v224 — By accumulation of being, the wealth-lord at its decay is all-pervading; unseen form, endless; above Brahma, expander, all-pervading.

tal-a15-v225 — Power-portions of light only; world-guardians are declared by that. Thus even self-dependent form; direction-sun is taught.

tal-a15-v226 — There all indeed attains unwavering light-nature, as all-pervading wind is fanned by a fan.

tal-a15-v227 — The awakened would do his own action, duty-removal and so on; like that, all-pervading, all Indra-powers clearly.

tal-a15-v228 — Adepts awakened by breath give various desired fruit; thus sun-power with direction-lords, Brahma, Vishnu, Isha, Sau, Shiva.

tal-a15-v229 — Presided over by firm reassurance, giving various fruit; the adept may worship field, mandala, or house.

tal-a15-v230 — Standing according to that, Shankara becomes middle; he indeed is all-presider by middleness, its who.
