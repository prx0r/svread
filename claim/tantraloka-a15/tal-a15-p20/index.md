## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v191** — One may worship the doorway deities inwardly before the deity according to the visualized sequence. Then, throwing a flower consecrated by the weapon mantra into the blazing shrine, one destroys obstacles.
**tal-a15-v192** — Having entered, one should look upon the shrine with a gaze empowered by Śiva's rays; then one should bind the directions with the weapon mantra and cover them all with the armor mantra.
**tal-a15-v193** — There the seeker for liberation should enter facing the northern direction, for the fire of Aghora burns away the bonds that bind.
**tal-a15-v194** — Even though we speak of directions like east and west, there is no such thing as direction by itself, for no single direction can be cognized without a limiting condition.
**tal-a15-v195** — If someone says that direction is just a limiting condition called east and so on, then how could such a mere condition account for real variety?
**tal-a15-v196** — Therefore it is this light of consciousness, appearing in the form of objects, that boldly displays the variety known as the division into east and other directions.
**tal-a15-v197** — Whatever is always fit to be taken up by light is above, being of the nature of light, while what is unfit for contact is below.
**tal-a15-v198** — What is only slightly luminous is the middle, and from that the directions arise; when light spreads toward what can be slightly illuminated, that is the front.
**tal-a15-v199** — What is turned away is behind, so front and back arise as a pair; when light faces an object and grasps it, its rays become more abundant.
**tal-a15-v200** — Where light stands in conformity, that is called the right; and what stands in front of that right side is taught to be the left.
