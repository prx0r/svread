## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v461** — It is visualized with an outer lower part and a supreme rim, and with a middle trident having supreme and lower aspects, all flame-filled, reddish, and fearsome with whirling Mothers and the praṇava.
**tal-a15-v462** — When this is contemplated and seen in the outer hand, one becomes a samaya-initiate; but if agitation of the bonds and the like appears at once, that shows the person is about to depart.
**tal-a15-v463** — If he is about to depart, the guru should worship outside that and cast upon the head what separates him from the life-breaths; by this Śiva-hand he clearly becomes a samaya-initiate.
**tal-a15-v464** — For that same person, in the future proper separation from the bonds of the levels, he has the status of a son, and the initiating teachers are to join him to the supreme level.
**tal-a15-v465** — That same person, knowing the kinds of mantras and the truth of recitation, fire-offering, and the rest, is consecrated first with the liberation vessel and then with the one called Īśvara.
**tal-a15-v466** — When consecrated he becomes a sādhaka, and at the end of enjoyment he dissolves into the supreme; endowed with these qualities, one is initiated in the teaching of Śiva.
**tal-a15-v467** — One who knows the four-part scriptures, is devoted to the eighteen tantras, and knows the ten tantras and the higher path is appointed as an ācārya.
**tal-a15-v468** — In the consecration rite, the best of gurus should perform for the ācārya the joining that begins from earth and ends in liberation.
**tal-a15-v469** — By these statements it is said that the samaya-initiate, like a prince, would be authorized everywhere in the three stages beginning with the son stage.
**tal-a15-v470** — A son-initiate may be authorized in teacherhood if his joining is equal, but he is not authorized in sādhana when the joining is different.
