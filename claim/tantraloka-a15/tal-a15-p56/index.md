## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v551** — If he acts independently, everything becomes fruitless and the pledge is transgressed; one should also avoid substances offered in the Saiddhantika way or fit for Chandi.
**tal-a15-v552** — One should never utter a word that names a shakini; women are to be honored, even if they are unattractive, old, or earn their living by a craft.
**tal-a15-v553** — Women of the lowest caste, women with deformed limbs, courtesans, and women who act freely are to be honored with effort, as declared in the revered scripture.
**tal-a15-v554** — They are without conventional conduct, eat all things, are beyond right and wrong, move by their own will, eat meat, and are unrestrained; they are like goddesses.
**tal-a15-v555** — Courtesans and their houses are to be honored, and there one should worship the ritual sequence as at a sacred confluence; one must not do anything toward women that would make them feel disgust.
**tal-a15-v556** — Therefore, one should not look at them naked or with breasts exposed; and even if an old woman is present, one should not feel disgust at her mudrikā.
**tal-a15-v557** — At the gathering one should not display deformity or beauty; one should worship an empty-bodied deity image at crossroads and similar places.
**tal-a15-v558** — On all festivals, ordinary and special, worship should be performed; there is no break in observance toward the guru, and greed must be avoided in the gathering.
**tal-a15-v559** — One should never feel disgust toward wine and other vīra substances, nor criticize them; rather, one should always honor those who partake of them.
**tal-a15-v560** — For the sake of instruction, faults may be pointed out, but one must not hate in one's heart; instead, one should strive to uproot alien conceptions.
