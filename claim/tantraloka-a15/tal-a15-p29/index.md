## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v281** — Whatever merit there is in all sacred places and whatever fruit there is in all sacrifices, that fruit, O beloved, is multiplied ten million times by offering to the unimpaired one.
**tal-a15-v282** — In the revered Nandi-shikha and in the revered Ananda teaching it is said that the ladle should be full, and the spoon should be used in the offering of ghee.
**tal-a15-v283** — The rest, including worship, fire offering, recitation, and so on, should be done with the left hand alone. In this way the body becomes filled with bliss and free from turning outward toward everything.
**tal-a15-v284** — Having accomplished the body through worship, one should contemplate it as of Shiva-nature. When outward turning toward objects has dissolved and limitation has melted away, this contemplation becomes clear.
**tal-a15-v285** — When limitation has dissolved, what would remain in the body apart from the essence of Shiva-bliss? The body is filled with the essence of Shiva-bliss and pervaded by the thirty-six principles.
**tal-a15-v286** — Seeing and worshipping the body day and night, one becomes of Shiva-nature. Such a person is satisfied, resting in the body as the universal self and established in its sign.
**tal-a15-v287** — Such a person does not desire external observances like linga vows, sacred places, and similar practices. The rites were taught so that consciousness might come to rest, and for one in whom it already rests, that is enough.
**tal-a15-v288** — The higher rites, extending up to external worship, are to be accomplished here for the sake of realizing Shiva-nature. Therefore, the offering vessel should be made as the supreme reality consisting of non-difference from Shiva.
**tal-a15-v289** — Just as in the body there is a progression from burning to worship, so too the act is full of bliss-essence and satisfies all world-deities.
**tal-a15-v290** — Even in the argha vessel, by summary and expansion, which substances are for sacrifice and what argha is, is not declared.
