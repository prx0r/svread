## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v351** — That which draws in all attributes is established there as supreme, while all others stand lower; such is the principle of supreme Bhairava worship in the great rite of the revered Ḍāmara.
**tal-a15-v352** — The glorious Śambhu himself, well pleased, taught this to me: in the external rite one should worship also in the triad of lotuses.
**tal-a15-v353** — The teaching of the instructors is that the retinue multitude ends with the weapon mantra, and the fivefold group of vidyā-limbs is placed in the directions of Agni, Īśa, the demons, Vāyu, and the intermediate quarters.
**tal-a15-v354** — Here one should likewise place the limbs of Śakti and the limbs of Śiva; again in the pair, place the weapon mantra in the four directions, and in the middle place the mantra called Locana.
**tal-a15-v355** — On the eight petals place a pair of octads beginning with Aghorā, according to its own tradition; likewise arrange groups of twelve, six, and four, mixed and distributed two by two.
**tal-a15-v356** — One should worship in every way as a doubled enclosure; then, in order, worship the world guardians with their weapons in their own ten directions.
**tal-a15-v357** — Thus, abiding in identity with the goddesses up to the trident, standing here and offering the universe, one should satisfy the host of deities.
**tal-a15-v358** — Then one should perform recitation, ten times for each mantra; because each is threefold in nature and yet non-different, this holds in all respects.
**tal-a15-v359** — Remembering the mantra circle like fire in the navel, heart, throat, palate, and upper center, one should there burn the universe and accomplish the oblation.
**tal-a15-v360** — When the act of initiation is to be performed, by whichever path the teacher desires to carry it out, he should first and primarily apply that path in the body.
