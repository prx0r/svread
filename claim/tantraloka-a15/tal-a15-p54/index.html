tal-a15-v531 — One should delude the king's evil attendants and those who create obstacles, and one should worship the shakinis, who are described in this way in the revered scripture.

tal-a15-v532 — The shakinis have twofold boldness, fourfold desire, and eightfold greed; therefore they are to be feared. That is enough to say.

tal-a15-v533 — Those who claim to stand in the kula tradition but remain outside the heroic substances should not be lived with; the revered Madhava Kula says one must not dwell together with such bound ones.

tal-a15-v534 — One should always worship the circle of deities, the guru, the fire, and the scripture as equal, and should not eat anything at all that has not first been offered to them.

tal-a15-v535 — One should not take the guru's property and should honor the guru's circle; that circle is to be regarded, through knowledge, as like his brother, wife, and children.

tal-a15-v536 — That relation is not based on birth or worldly ties, for such a person remains bound; instead, one's attachment should serve the accomplishment of worship of the guru.

tal-a15-v537 — The guru's group should not be worshipped for its own greatness but honored like the guru; one must not criticize the guru or give any reason for such criticism.

tal-a15-v538 — One should not listen to criticism of the guru, should not anger him, and should not do anything in his presence without permission, except serving him.

tal-a15-v539 — Worldly and non-worldly duties, anger, play, austerity, recitation, and anything used by the guru—such as his bed, clothes, or seat—are all subject to strict restraint.

tal-a15-v540 — One should not use what the guru has used and should not touch it with the feet; instead one should honor it. The revered Traishirasa says that violations call for expiations such as krcchra and candrayana.
