## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v411** — Once the fire has arisen and been consecrated in the Shaiva way, one should worship the mass of sound and Malini as the two parents, and then dismiss them to their own pure abode.
**tal-a15-v412** — One should take a portion of the pure fire and set it apart for the oblation. Or, alternatively, one may draw the flame from the fire with the left breath and the heart mantra.
**tal-a15-v413** — Bringing it to unity with the fire of consciousness, one should cast the consecrated element with the right side. Indeed, the true seeing is a firm identification: 'I am Shiva.'
**tal-a15-v414** — Consecration is the true principle for everything. Therefore, for whatever impurity belongs to anything, one should offer nine oblations, or nine together with the self.
**tal-a15-v415** — The consecration should be made to Shiva-fire with a mantra beginning with Om and ending with Svaha, in the fire understood as the shared form of Shiva-consciousness. Then:
**tal-a15-v416** — As before, one should install the support and what rests on it, and also the circle of goddesses. Then the guru should, in order, see the ladle and the spoon as facing downward.
**tal-a15-v417** — They are to be worshipped as Shiva and Shakti. Thus is their consecration performed. Apart from seeing that principle, consecration has no other life.
**tal-a15-v418** — Thus, as the Shri Purva states, the lord of the ladle and the rest did not consecrate in that way. Then the guru, using sesame, forms the doe mudra by controlling the middle, ring, and thumb fingers.
**tal-a15-v419** — Having formed the mudra, one should satisfy the root mantra with a hundred ladles of ghee, the limbs and faces with a sixth part, and the rest with a tenth part.
**tal-a15-v420** — Even a thousandfold offering may be made for satisfaction, according to one's means. But if, even when means are available, one is seized by greed and gives priority to outer things—
