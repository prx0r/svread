**tal-a15-v146** — Having thus performed nyasa in the argha vessel, one should perform the rite according to the stated rule; thereafter worship with the installed mantras.
**tal-a15-v147** — Because all beings attain perfection from Shiva, and from the full one comes non-difference of agents in this worship.
**tal-a15-v148** — The whole host of agents is shown as non-different from Shiva; in the example of worship one attains all, even going and the rest.
**tal-a15-v149** — As a horse that has come under the rider's control through the bridle's freedom, though mounted in battle, does not transgress that training.
**tal-a15-v150** — So the agent habituated by worship-practice and made Shiva-like, though going or standing, abandons duality of agents.
