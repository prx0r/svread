**tal-a15-v325** — The higher-lower form is red and slightly pointed, not terrifying; the lower form on the left horn is terrifying and black-tawny.
**tal-a15-v326** — After placement is done in the body as before, twofold and sixfold, one should then imagine the fitting imagined form of these.
**tal-a15-v327** — According to action difference, forms with two, four, or six arms and so on bear skull, trident, club, boon, fear, pot, and so on.
**tal-a15-v328** — One imagines because of variegated left-right placement, since in reality these goddesses are universal forms of consciousness-nature.
**tal-a15-v329** — Their essence is unlimited consciousness alone, for liberation; then all limbs, faces, and so on, world-guardians, weapons, last.
**tal-a15-v330** — In the middle the goddess-named worshipful triad becomes and is worshiped, then from the middle-seated mass of consciousness and eternal unity.
