## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v401** — The purposes are to enable the ground to support Śiva's fire and to remove obstacles; then, when the fire-pit has been worshiped, it should be clearly contemplated as the power of action.
**tal-a15-v402** — One should install either Mātṛkā or Mālinī in the form of resolve; the triangle, which is the creative abode of the goddess of resolve, is of the nature of action.
**tal-a15-v403** — There one should agitate the seed-drop of knowledge three times and place it; knowledge, stirred by will, becomes active in the level of reflective action.
**tal-a15-v404** — When it is firmly established and blazes forth, clear with the five limbs beginning with knowing, one should offer an oblation once with each of those five limbs.
**tal-a15-v405** — By that much, the fire becomes purified of all consecrations beginning with birth; that five-limbed form indeed has the nature of earth and so on, bearing qualities such as hardness.
**tal-a15-v406** — Bearing powers within the fire, it should perform the rites beginning with conception; then it becomes the womb containing the circle of the good goddesses of the whole path and the supreme-and-non-supreme goddess.
**tal-a15-v407** — Remembering, and by the power of the full oblation, he should complete the consecrations of the fire; thus the fire blazes with the splendor of true resolve united with the lords of mantra.
**tal-a15-v408** — Through identity with that Śiva-resolve, the fire becomes of Śiva-nature; this is the truth of consecration, as the revered Śambhu revealed it to me.
**tal-a15-v409** — I have already shown this, so let the pure-minded examine it. Here are those who object, saying it is like the consecration of a child.
**tal-a15-v410** — They argue that consecrating fire would require another fire, and so on, leading to infinite regress. In this guru's teaching, that objection is baseless and refuted.
