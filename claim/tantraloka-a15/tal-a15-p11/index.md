## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v101** — This whole universe is already here, so what is the use of other outward displays of wandering? Thus, for those who seek the fruit of each respective circle, the matter applies both inwardly and outwardly.
**tal-a15-v102** — The difference of sacred places is varied and, in scripture, beyond counting. In the Vīrāvalihṛdaya, seven places are mentioned, along with a pair of śakti-lotuses.
**tal-a15-v103** — Places such as the path of the gods, a crossroads, a cremation ground, a solitary spot, and an empty tree are taught as sites to be understood through etymology, quality, condition, and figurative meaning.
**tal-a15-v104** — Worship for liberation may take place in a circle established there, in the body, or externally; therefore no essential difference in the worship is to be made on the basis of place.
**tal-a15-v105** — This attainment does not operate by means of place, because it comes from the clarity of one's inner state; this is also said in the revered Nishacara for those who seek accomplishment.
**tal-a15-v106** — One who seeks liberation should cast off attachment to place like a snake sheds its skin, because liberation is not produced by place; it comes when the truth reaches one's hearing.
**tal-a15-v107** — When the truth of the guru is heard, liberation comes; worship then serves to strengthen it. Wherever the heart-lotus opens, that is where this awakening takes place.
**tal-a15-v108** — In that very inner abode, the glory of both outer and inner worship is established. Liberation does not come from going somewhere else; it comes from cutting the knot of ignorance.
**tal-a15-v109** — Through the expansion of awareness in the glorious line of heroes, a pure mind directed toward the guru becomes the cause for either liberation or attainment.
**tal-a15-v110** — In both liberation and attainment, whatever is pleasing to the mind and fit for worship can be the cause, because its effectiveness comes from the completeness of conditions operating through necessity.
