## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v121** — Place na, ṛ, ṝ, ḷ, and ḹ on the crest and the garland of the head, and tha on the crown. Assign the eyes above and below, place ī on the nose, ṇu and ṇū on the two seals, and place the corresponding letters on the two ears.
**tal-a15-v122** — Place the ba-ka group with i and ā in order on the mouth, teeth, tongue, and palate. Place va, bha, and ya on the throat and the right and left shoulders, and place ḍa and ḍha on the two arms.
**tal-a15-v123** — Place ṭha on the two hands, jha and ña on the branches, and jra and ṭa on the trident and skull. Place pa on the heart and cha and la on the two breasts. Mā, associated with milk, and sa joined with visarga are said to be the life-breath.
**tal-a15-v124** — Ha is declared to be the vital breath. Ṣa and kṣa go to the belly and the navel. The group ending with ma goes to the hips, the private parts, and the pair of thighs, and likewise to the two knees.
**tal-a15-v125** — Place e and ai, and the pair that follows them, on the two shins, and place da and pha on the two feet. This is the goddess Mālinī, because she is stirred into activity by the possessor of power.
**tal-a15-v126** — Because it enters into ritual efficacy, that body is, in ultimate truth, a body of Śakti. It arises from the mutual stirring of the seeds and wombs, through an emissive arising.
**tal-a15-v127** — What accomplishment would she not grant, or what deficiency would she not fill? Although the intermixture of wombs, seeds, and phonemes exists in many forms, her power is complete.
**tal-a15-v128** — Even so, the sequence beginning with na and ending with pha is proclaimed as the primary one. By uttering from pha to na, the whole circle of the path is formed.
**tal-a15-v129** — When withdrawn into consciousness, she is full and complete, so how could she truly be described by words? For that reason the scriptures speak of her in many ways, through forms such as kula, puttalika, and others.
**tal-a15-v130** — Although she is sung in many divisions, this Malini, beginning with na and ending with pha, is the primary one. She is the inner, uncontracted form of Bhairava, who is the mass of sound.
