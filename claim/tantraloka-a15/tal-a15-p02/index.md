## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v11** — But for those who are incapable, initiation, conduct, and absorption are prescribed, and these should be preceded by knowledge. Therefore the knower is the most supreme.
**tal-a15-v12** — Knowledge comes from scripture, and scripture is not to be heard by one who is uninitiated. Therefore initiation serves for him only as the consecration that makes this possible.
**tal-a15-v13** — Wherever the teacher appoints him, he attains a fruit suited to his own understanding. This, it is said, was declared earlier regarding the knower.
**tal-a15-v14** — But if, by the Lord's grace, some divine fitness arises in both teacher and disciple, then initiation is of no use to them.
**tal-a15-v15** — Then knowledge itself is initiation, as explained in the revered Traisika. And for him there suddenly arises knowledge of the meaning of all the scriptures.
**tal-a15-v16** — Thus, according to the teaching of the revered Malini, one who possesses innate awareness is the highest-qualified. Indeed, such a knower is regarded as a teacher.
**tal-a15-v17** — Seeking benefit for himself or for others, he should contemplate this. By that statement in the Malini scripture, all of this has been made clear.
**tal-a15-v18** — Thus, in the injunction of the revered Kairana scripture, some are said to be fit for knowledge, others for conduct, others for initiation, and others for yoga.
**tal-a15-v19** — There, the one who has the stated marks, is skilled in action, yoga, and knowledge, and is elevated through successively higher levels, is declared to be the teacher.
**tal-a15-v20** — And that teacher, having examined or questioned the disciple who has been purified by the descent of any one of the powers mentioned before, should perform the rite of initiation.
