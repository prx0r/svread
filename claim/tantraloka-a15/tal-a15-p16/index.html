tal-a15-v151 — Thus for one established in unity-practice, this universe suddenly, by force, like a dancer, may flash as full Shiva-nature.

tal-a15-v152 — Our supreme guru said in the praise of worship: 'Ah, what sweet taste, what festival of Shiva-worship!'

tal-a15-v153 — The agitation of thirty-six principles, where it shines, is for full Shiva-entry into the universe; those who worship.

tal-a15-v154 — They are Shiva indeed; what fruit for the full ones? Even without knowledge and yoga, action like nyasa and worship.

tal-a15-v155 — Thus by granting unity-samapatti, bestowing other fruit, the practitioner too has attained mantra-unity with that good vipra.

tal-a15-v156 — The universe would quickly attain unobstructedness and its own perfection; it is said by the Supreme Lord: neither rule nor worship procedure.

tal-a15-v157 — Only through remembrance is success desired; thus ritual action gives absorption in that, because of this.

tal-a15-v158 — Through unity of all agents, her supreme body becomes identity with the support of the sacrificer by the order of place-purification rite.

tal-a15-v159 — By nyāsa order, the sacrificer, offering, support, instruments, gifts, and transmissions attain identity with Śiva-ness.

tal-a15-v160 — The argha vessel is the recipient, therefore it is taken; whatever water and instruments are situated in it serve purification or worship.
