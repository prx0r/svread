tal-a15-v161 — All touched by drops from the argha vessel becomes pure; other purity will arise from contact with Śiva-sun rays.

tal-a15-v162 — The Lord said in the holy former scripture: in that argha-vessel rite, one should not provide any unpurified thing at all.

tal-a15-v163 — Therefore all is pure, even what is impure; that is pure, and impurity is to be known from the intention of binding the paśu.

tal-a15-v164 — From one's own state or from what was previously provided, therefore whatever is present here is by the grace of consciousness.

tal-a15-v165 — However much that is extremely suitable in this sacrifice, like a living being, by this method of reasoning when existence is far from knowledge.

tal-a15-v166 — When consciousness arises, then there one should declare suitability or unsuitability; for heroes indeed here, mutually, the nectar of one's own image.

tal-a15-v167 — Entered into each sacrifice rite, self-realized gurus raise up consciousness that is submerged; but that is well.

tal-a15-v168 — For worship, suitable bliss arises because consciousness rises up; therefore it submerges the predominance of non-conscious body and the like.

tal-a15-v169 — Whatever produces bliss, is fit for worship, and captivates the heart is therefore praised in the teachings of Kula, Krama, the transcendent Trika essence, and others.

tal-a15-v170 — Wine, kādambarī, śīdhu, and such substances have supreme greatness, yet in the teaching meant for bound souls they are restricted in order to maintain worldly order.
