## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v371** — One should consecrate cow urine, cow dung, curd, milk, and ghee with the faces ending in the upper one and with the six limbs, and sprinkle with this kuśa-water.
**tal-a15-v372** — One should sprinkle the ground and set aside the remainder of the pañcagavya for the disciple; where the five cow products are in this kuśa-water, that is called pañcagavya.
**tal-a15-v373** — In the scripture, pañcagavya-water is said to remove external impurity; for when ordinary impurity has been removed, one may then deal with the inner impurity.
**tal-a15-v374** — To burn away impurity, one must resort to non-ordinary means such as mantra; one should remember the goddess, ending with phāṭ, who extends from earth up to Śiva.
**tal-a15-v375** — One should throw a handful of flowers into the middle and likewise offer incense, fragrance, liquor, and so on in the center of the worship-ground; by that the support is quickly empowered.
**tal-a15-v376** — The entire circle of deities is conceived as presiding there, on the stalk of Ananta, on the petals beginning with Dharma, and on the pericarp of true knowledge.
**tal-a15-v377** — In the sixfold arising, one should worship Gaṇeśa of Īśāna, placed there, with fragrance, flowers, and the like for the calming of obstacles; having worshipped him, one should dismiss him.
**tal-a15-v378** — After the missing portion of the text: then one should take the jar, filled with supremely fragrant liquid substances.
**tal-a15-v379** — The jar, worshipped and anointed with the root mantra, should be consecrated one hundred times; if the weapon-worship was not done earlier, the water-pot should be consecrated with the sword-mantra.
**tal-a15-v380** — One should worship that jar in the Īśāna direction together with the vessel placed on its left; then, taking position in the solar direction, one should worship the world-lords together with their weapons.
