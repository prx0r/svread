tal-a15-v301 — In that maya-made knot are eight beginning with dharma and adharma; fire and such are maya; its offspring is vibhava, and intellect.

tal-a15-v302 — Above the maya knot, meditate the four below the trident, of pure-vidya nature, joined with two coverings.

tal-a15-v303 — That tattva is situated and to be meditated between uvula and brahmarandhra; light-yoga occurs there, with sight, hearing, taste, and such.

tal-a15-v304 — Place eighteen shaktis clockwise from the right in the vidya lotus here; that was also said before and is shown again.

tal-a15-v305 — Vama, Jyeshtha, Raudri, Kali, Kalabalavikarika, Balamathani, Bhutadamani, and Manonmanika, Shanta; Shakracaparucira may be here.

tal-a15-v306 — Vibhvi, Jnaptikriti, Iccha, Vagishi, Jvalini, and Vama, Jyeshtha, Raudri—these from the eastern petal, all like time-burning.

tal-a15-v307 — In the petal-filament centers, remember the sun-moon-fire triad, presided by its own lords Brahma, Vishnu, and Hara.

tal-a15-v308 — The form beyond maya of Brahma and others was previously stated; this seat is indeed that, not smeared with maya collyrium.

tal-a15-v309 — Above Rudra place Ishvara god, and above that Sadasiva; he is called Mahapreta in the scriptures.

tal-a15-v310 — Mahapreta, by pervading all tattvas, is absorbed from awakening and excellent going, and would not go downward.
