## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v341** — When the whole knowable is divided, the lords of mantras and the great lords arise; when it is both divided and undivided, the path up to Sadāśiva arises, culminating in the supreme Lord.
**tal-a15-v342** — As the expansion of difference dissolves, those powers gradually expand and, mutually mixed, become the triad of tvaras; then, with difference dissolved, they go beyond it.
**tal-a15-v343** — The triad of lotuses together with unmanā becomes the seat, as the powers take it up out of a desire to cut away their mutual difference.
**tal-a15-v344** — Indeed, the divisions of power, its possessor, and so forth become worthy of worship, because from the very first point of the dissolution of difference each bears its own proper form.
**tal-a15-v345** — They bear a threefoldness, and that threefoldness of theirs is clearly perceived because of the possible impurity of the knowable and the mutual mixture they acquire.
**tal-a15-v346** — Not arriving at a clearly threefold state as before, but ready to devour the very act of cutting mutual difference, that consciousness, whose essence is freedom alone, is declared to be Kālakarṣiṇī.
**tal-a15-v347** — She again draws time back from herself and also projects it outward; therefore she is declared Saṃkarṣiṇī, and this is her true being among these Mothers.
**tal-a15-v348** — True nature, being, and attainment among the Mothers are to be understood in this way; in those world-generating powers, the supreme reality is indeed freedom alone.
**tal-a15-v349** — This worshippable reality, consisting of willing, knowing, and acting, is worthy of worship because it is unlimited; even all directions of limitation are gathered within it.
**tal-a15-v350** — Here the non-conceptual does not attain worshippability, nor does a mere single concept where many attributes are involved; therefore the attribute that gathers in that many attributes is required.
