## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v571** — It is said in the revered Madhava lineage that one should always avoid Vedic and Vaishnava teachings, and whatever is spoken by those established in other systems.
**tal-a15-v572** — Association with those of unworthy lineage brings the fear of falling from one's own lineage. Therefore, in the Kula tradition, especially where one vessel is shared, one should avoid them.
**tal-a15-v573** — If, through carelessness, one forms a friendship in a gathering with a follower of another Agama, the revered Urma teaches that one should worship the circle.
**tal-a15-v574** — One should not associate at all with someone who delights in another guru, is deluded, lives off the property of the gods, harms the shakti, or is corrupt.
**tal-a15-v575** — One should not go to a shrine or similar place in initiation and related matters out of mere doubt. If the prescribed faith has become weak, one should not act on that basis.
**tal-a15-v576** — One should not judge or speak of those in the teaching in terms of their former birth. One should deal with all simply as non-different from Shiva.
**tal-a15-v577** — Sit with true knowledge, strive for illumination, and do not fall into uninitiated fruitlessness.
**tal-a15-v578** — Night practice in the assembly center excludes ordinary people, and the devotee of the goddess does not smell burning meat.
**tal-a15-v579** — Protecting the guru's command, one abandons mantra-identification and becomes equipped with scripture, worship, japa, meditation, and discernment.
**tal-a15-v580** — Do not perform fruitless action, do not strive in threefold action, do not dispute through mantra and tantra, and do not eat poison.
