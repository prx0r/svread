tal-a15-v491 — Nothing tamasic is his pleasure, pain, auspiciousness, or inauspiciousness; indeed here, how would the one named tamasic be fit in the rite?

tal-a15-v492 — Let no one dispute anywhere indeed; quality is present; even all sattvic action in eating, if tamasic.

tal-a15-v493 — Then what is low, or high is the opposite; even arrived power descent and initiation depend on quality state.

tal-a15-v494 — The variegated self would indeed be composed in chief purpose; then the guru teaches the child mantra-preceded deity worship.

tal-a15-v495 — He would teach and do that, purify him; then the composed guru addresses six places from the heart, brahma and others.

tal-a15-v496 — He would touch the child's breath function; each of eight purifications, purified by knowledge-touch, extends to the heart-first twelve end.

tal-a15-v497 — By destruction of faults from food, seed, state, and so on, one becomes twice-born; full of vasu-veda-named purification, thus the twice-born stands.

tal-a15-v498 — Conception, male-producing, parting, birth, naming, exit, food tasting, tonsure, and the eighth.

tal-a15-v499 — In vow binding and sacrificial, sacred thread and worldly rites, in order: soma-related, cow gift, veda-moon purification, from celibacy.

tal-a15-v500 — Marriage is fifteenth; from there seven cooked sacrifices, eighth-day rites, fortnightly, ancestral offering, and the shravana-agrayana pair.
