## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a15-v511** — Thus, in sequence, with the head and other limbs, again and again: because the twice-born state is truly of the nature of consciousness, characterized by peace and the rest.
**tal-a15-v512** — Therefore awareness arises in the state of Rudra through that very sequence, just as gold and other metals, in refining, are transformed by the force of sequence.
**tal-a15-v513** — Likewise, as with silver and the rest, in the consecration of awareness within the twice-born state the womb is not the cause; there, one who is of peaceful nature is called twice-born.
**tal-a15-v514** — This has been examined by the sage in the Moksha-dharma and elsewhere, and it has also been ascertained by the Lord in scriptures such as the Mukuta.
**tal-a15-v515** — Because awareness can arise similarly through similar bodily connection, from the standpoint of common convention, in the Smarta sense the son of two twice-born parents is called twice-born.
**tal-a15-v516** — Even seers born of mothers associated with low-caste conditions of mind, speech, or birth had elevated minds; what need, then, of brahminhood alone to be a worthy receptacle?
**tal-a15-v517** — For this reason, in this secret scripture that teaches the truth of real meaning, conduct based on birth and similar factors is indeed taught for the Śāmbhava path.
**tal-a15-v518** — But the pāśava scriptures are thoroughly of the nature of vāma-śakti, serving accomplishment in creation and related aims, and also serving fear and the arrangement of results under Śambhu.
**tal-a15-v519** — For one who has been made twice-born, descent from the twelve-end comes not merely by touch, but by resting in union with one's own self and suddenly descending.
**tal-a15-v520** — By the conferring of a Rudra-share, the samaya-initiate becomes consecrated and is thereby fit for study, hearing, daily worship, and service to the guru.
