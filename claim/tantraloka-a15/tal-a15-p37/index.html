tal-a15-v361 — Remembering the trident extending from the root up to the twelve-end-point, with its tip forward, and reaching the tip of the circle of goddesses while abandoning sequence, one attains the state of moving in space.

tal-a15-v362 — This khecarī fills everything from the root-support up to the top of the space at the end of the twelve; she is called khecarī because she moves and abides in space and feeds on the nectar of space.

tal-a15-v363 — From that Śāmbhava trident one should reduce by four finger-widths for the Śākta, and from that again for the Āṇava; thus that triad of tridents is established.

tal-a15-v364 — For one whose self is offered to the higher and higher circles of goddesses on that triad of tridents, what is not born, what is not known, or what is not done?

tal-a15-v365 — Alternatively, one should worship each single goddess or each mantra in a lotus, varying the worship according to paired union, limbs, faces, and other options of being or non-being.

tal-a15-v366 — In this way, having made one's own consciousness Śiva from the level of breath up to the level of space through the yoga of worship, one should then perform the external rite.

tal-a15-v367 — When external worship is made primary and is aimed at powers and the like, internal worship is for purification; otherwise the worshipper remains a bound soul.

tal-a15-v368 — But whoever is turned away from powers and the like and worships the Lord outwardly for the sake of rising into the inner great worship, that person is fulfilled by that very means.

tal-a15-v369 — After performing the inner worship, one should take grain and the like consecrated with the weapon-mantra, throw it in the directions to remove obstacles, and then gather it back toward the Īśāna direction.

tal-a15-v370 — Inspection, sprinkling, striking and nourishing, and covering are purifications that are common in the doctrine of Triśiras.
