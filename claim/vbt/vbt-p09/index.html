vbt-p09-v83 — On a moving seat, standing now — slowly, or by the body's moving: in the calmed mind, in that state, O Goddess, the divine flood may be won.

vbt-p09-v84 — Sky, stainless, seeing — gaze made unbroken: stunned self at that moment, O Goddess, the Bhairava form may be won.

vbt-p09-v85 — Dissolved on the crown, sky all as Bhairava should be contemplated: that all — the Bhairava-form-blaze-reality may enter.

vbt-p09-v86 — Something known — duality-giving, outer light, darkness again: the universe-form of Bhairava — having known, endless-light-bearing.

vbt-p09-v87 — Thus indeed in bad night, on the dark fortnight's arrival, long: contemplating timira — Bhairava's form will come to.

vbt-p09-v88 — Thus indeed, having closed first the eyes, black lustre before: spread Bhairava's form, contemplating — made of that he may be.

vbt-p09-v89 — Of whichever organ even — from its striking and its blocking: of one entered in the nondual, in void — there itself the self shines forth.

vbt-p09-v90 — No drop, no emission, and the a-syllable of muttering great: rises, O Goddess, suddenly — knowledge-flood, the highest Lord.
