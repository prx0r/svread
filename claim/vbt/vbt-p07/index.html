vbt-p07-v61 — Of the two, of the two states, in knowing having meditated — the middle should be resorted to: at once, with both left, in the middle reality shines forth.

vbt-p07-v62 — When the state is abandoned, the restrained consciousness does not go to any other state: then, in that middle-being, contemplation expands exceedingly.

vbt-p07-v63 — All body consciousness-made — or the world indeed, fancy fully: at once, by undivided mind, the supreme rise.

vbt-p07-v64 — Of the two winds, from their clashing — inside or outside, at the limit: the yogin, of the rising of evenness-knowing, becomes the vessel of offering.

vbt-p07-v65 — All world, or one's own body, filled with one's own bliss should be remembered: at once with one's own nectar indeed — full of supreme bliss he may be.

vbt-p07-v66 — By trick, by application — at once indeed, O deer-eyed one: great bliss manifests, by which reality shines forth.

vbt-p07-v67 — By binding all the channels, by the up-going breath-power, slowly: at the crawling-ant's moment, supreme happiness spreads forth.

vbt-p07-v68 — Between fire and poison, in the middle — the mind, happiness-made, should be cast: merely wind-filled, or yoked by remembrance-bliss.

vbt-p07-v69 — Agitated by union, shaking, at the end of power-penetration: whatever happiness of brahman-reality — that happiness, self-told, is told.

vbt-p07-v70 — By licking, churning, the pounding apexes — of woman-bliss from fullness, from memory: even in power's absence, O Goddess of gods, the bliss-flood may be.
