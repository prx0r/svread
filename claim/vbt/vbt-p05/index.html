vbt-p05-v41 — In stringed instruments and the like, in the long tones of ordered resonance: with undivided mind at the limit, the body may become the supreme sky.

vbt-p05-v42 — Of the body-mantra of all, by the gross-letter course — ending in moon-drop-sound: from void uttering, one may become Śiva.

vbt-p05-v43 — In one's own body, all directions at once should be contemplated as sky; with undirected mind — of him, sky proceeds everywhere.

vbt-p05-v44 — The back-void and the root-void at once should be contemplated, and whoever: by the body-disregarding power, void-minded he may be.

vbt-p05-v45 — Back-void, root-void, heart-void should be contemplated steady: at once, from thoughtlessness — thoughtless-rising follows.

vbt-p05-v46 — In a place of the body, voidness indeed — for a mere moment should be envisioned: thoughtless, thoughtless, sharing thoughtless nature.

vbt-p05-v47 — All gone in the body-stuff, pervaded by sky, O deer-eyed one: should be envisioned then — of him, the contemplation may grow steady.

vbt-p05-v48 — Inside the body, the skin-division should be thought through as a wall: nothing inside it — for the meditating one, the one beyond the meditated's share may be.

vbt-p05-v49 — In the heart-sky, eyes hidden, gone to the middle of the lotus-casket: with undivided mind, O fortunate one — supreme fortune may be won.

vbt-p05-v50 — From all around one's own body, at the twelve's end, from mind-dissolving: of firm-minded made firm, the reality-mark proceeds.
