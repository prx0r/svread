vbt-p11-v102 — The world, Indra-net-made, cast — or picture-work-like; whirling, or the meditating one's all: and seeing — joy upsurges.

vbt-p11-v103 — Not into pain should the mind be cast, not into joy, nor scattered about: O Bhairavī — in the middle, what reality remains may be known.

vbt-p11-v104 — Having left the own-body-standing, contemplating everywhere-I-am-thus: with firm mind, by a gaze not seeing elsewhere — happy may he be.

vbt-p11-v105 — In a pot and such — whatever knowing; desire and such, or inside me: never all-pervading born — contemplating thus, all-going.

vbt-p11-v106 — Grasper-grasped awareness: common to all embodied; of yogins the distinction but — He is, in relation, carefulness.

vbt-p11-v107 — Like self in another body — He even should perceive awareness: regard of one's own body having left, pervasive in days may he be.

vbt-p11-v108 — Supportless mind having been made — thoughts should not be thought: then, in self-supreme-self — Bhairava, O deer-eyed one.

vbt-p11-v109 — All-knowing, all-doing, and pervasive, the highest Lord: he indeed I — Śiva-natured; hence from firmness, Śiva may one be.

vbt-p11-v110 — Like the waves of water, the flame-plays of fire, the sun's light: of me indeed, of Bhairava — these world-plays, manifested.
