vbt-p06-v52 — By the time-fire, arisen from the time-place — one's own city should be thought burnt; at the end, calm-shining, then it may be.

vbt-p06-v53 — Thus, indeed, the whole world burnt — having meditated, away from fancy: in the non-other mind of the man, manliness supreme may be.

vbt-p06-v54 — In one's own body, the world's or even the minute-minuter things and realities — their abiding contemplated at the end, the supreme is manifested.

vbt-p06-v55 — The thin and weak power, meditated at the twelve's range: having entered, meditating in the heart — freed, freedom may be won.

vbt-p06-v56 — The world-course and the rest formed, in order, all: through gross, subtle, supreme standing — as long as, at the end, mind-dissolves.

vbt-p06-v57 — Of this, of all, of the world — at the ends everywhere; by path-procedure, Śiva-reality meditated: the great rise.

vbt-p06-v58 — The universe, this, O great Goddess, void-become should be thought; right there and mind merged — then, of that, the vessel of merging.

vbt-p06-v59 — In a pot and such a vessel, the gaze — the wall left, cast well: at that dissolving, in that moment gone — made of that he may be.

vbt-p06-v60 — In a treeless mountain, a wall, such a place — the gaze should be cast well: when dissolved in mind, in that state, the wasting of turning is born.
