vbt-p08-v72 — From eating and drinking, the swelling of joy, bliss-blooming: should be fancied the filled state — great bliss then may be.

vbt-p08-v73 — From tasting objects such as song — happiness not shared, one-selfness: of the yogin, by absorbedness, mounted in mind — in that selfness.

vbt-p08-v74 — Wherever the mind is content, right there the mind should be held: there, there — the own-form of supreme bliss proceeds forth.

vbt-p08-v75 — In the not-yet-come, in sleep, when the outer range has perished: that state, to be gone to by mind — the supreme Goddess shines forth.

vbt-p08-v76 — By the brilliance of sun, lamp and such, in the sky made dappled: gaze fixed right there — one's own self-form shines forth.

vbt-p08-v77 — By the skeleton, by the angry, by the licking Bhairavī, by the sky-going: at the gaze's time — the supreme attainment shines forth.

vbt-p08-v78 — On a soft seat, with one buttock, hands and feet unsupported placed: by that context — the supreme, full mind may be.

vbt-p08-v79 — Having sat on the seat rightly, arms, two, made half-bent: in the armpit-sky, mind making — calm comes by that dissolving.

vbt-p08-v80 — Of gross-formed state, fixed gaze cast down, and quickly: unsupported mind made — Śiva one may go to.
