vbt-p14-v132 — Eternal, all-pervading, supportless, pervasive, and lord of all: words moment by moment meditated — aim done according to meaning.

vbt-p14-v133 — Non-reality, Indra-net-shine — this all standing: what reality of the Indra-net? Thus from firmness, calm may go.

vbt-p14-v134 — Of self, unwavering — where knowing, where action? Knowing-dependent outer states: hence void, this world.

vbt-p14-v135 — Not of me bondage, not freedom — of the frightened, these terrors: this reflection of the intellect in waters, like the sun's.

vbt-p14-v136 — Sense-doored, all — meeting pleasure, pain and such: senses thus having left, self-abiding — in one's own self one abides.

vbt-p14-v137 — Knowing-lighting all — by all self lighting: one, from one-natureness — knowing, knowable, is envisioned.

vbt-p14-v138 — Mental, consciousness, power, self, and mind — the fourfold: when, O dear, fully wasted — then that Bhairava form.

vbt-p14-v139 — Of waveless teachings, a hundred told in brief; twelve-plus — O Goddess, which having known, the knowing-knower person.

vbt-p14-v140 — Here and in one, yoked — he becomes Bhairava himself: by speech does deeds — curse-and-favor making.
