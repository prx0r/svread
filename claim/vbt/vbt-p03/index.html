vbt-p03-v21 — Just as by the world-light of a lamp, by rays, and of the sun, direction and division and the rest are known — so by power, Śiva is known, O dear.

vbt-p03-v22 — God of gods, marked with the trident, adorned with the skull: void of direction, place and time — and free of designation.

vbt-p03-v23 — What is that full-formed state of Bhairava, and by what means does its door open, O supreme Goddess? Tell me as you rightly know it, Bhairava.

vbt-p03-v24 — Breath above, life below, and the emission-natured one should utter forth: at the two arising places, filled from filling, standing.

vbt-p03-v25 — Of wind within or without, or even from the sky-pair not turning; by Bhairavī — of Bhairava thus, O Bhairavī, the manifested form.

vbt-p03-v26 — Neither should it go nor enter, the wind-formed power when it blossoms; by thoughtlessness in the middle — by her, Bhairava-formness.

vbt-p03-v27 — Whether kumbhaka-held, or exhaled, or inhaled — when it may be so: at its end this is called calm; by power, calm shines forth.

vbt-p03-v28 — From the root, ray-shining; from the subtle, the subtler-natured — contemplate her at the twelve's end, calming down: Bhairava's rise.

vbt-p03-v29 — Rising up, lightning-formed, wheel by wheel, step by step; upward to three fists — until, then at the end, the great rise.

vbt-p03-v30 — Twelvefold course rightly, divided by twelve letters; through gross, subtle, supreme standing — freed, freed at last: Śiva.
