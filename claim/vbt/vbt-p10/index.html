vbt-p10-v100 — Consciousness-natured in all bodies — distinction there is nowhere: hence, made of that, contemplating all — the world-conquering person.

vbt-p10-v91 — Of letter with emission — emission-ending awareness make: by supportless mind, eternal brahman may be touched.

vbt-p10-v92 — Sky-formed one's own self should be meditated, by directions uncovered: supportless awareness-power — own form will show it then.

vbt-p10-v93 — Something, a limb, pierced first — with sharp needle and such, then: right there, awareness yoked — in Bhairava, spotless going.

vbt-p10-v94 — Mind-and-the-rest, inner-making, is not inside me: think thus — by absence of fancies, fancies abandoned may be.

vbt-p10-v95 — Māyā, the deluding one, named: the counting of the parts stands; counting the such-and-such natures of realities — not separately may be.

vbt-p10-v96 — Sudden desire arisen — having seen it, to calm should lead it: where it indeed arose, there right there it dissolves.

vbt-p10-v97 — When my desire is unarisen — knowing, or what — then I am indeed: truly He-I, thus become, in that dissolved, that-minded may be.

vbt-p10-v98 — In desire or in knowing, when arisen — mind should be set: self-mind, undivided-minded — then the seeing of reality's meaning.

vbt-p10-v99 — Causeless may be the knowing, supportless, error-natured: truly not anyone's — thus becoming Śiva, O dear.
