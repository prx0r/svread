vbt-p12-v111 — Having whirled, whirled — by the body swiftly, falling to earth: by the ceasing of agitation-power, the supreme state is born.

vbt-p12-v112 — On supports or by power, by knowing, or from mind-dissolving: born at power-penetration's end — the Bhairava form.

vbt-p12-v113 — This tradition, O Goddess, hear — rightly I tell: even freedom is born at once, when the eyes are unwinkingly fixed.

vbt-p12-v114 — Contraction of the ears having been made — at the lower gate likewise: unsmeared, unploughed, meditating, one may enter eternal brahman.

vbt-p12-v115 — At a well and such, a great pit — having stood above, from looking: of undivided mind rightly, at once mind-dissolving, plainly.

vbt-p12-v116 — Wherever the mind goes — outside or inside, or even so: there, there Śiva-state — by pervasiveness: where would it go?

vbt-p12-v117 — Wherever by the eye's road, consciousness is manifested of the pervader: of him, from only-that-natured, from cit-dissolving — full-selfness.

vbt-p12-v118 — At hunger's end and such — in fear, in grief, in the abyss, or fled from battle; at curiosity-thirst's end: reality-being-full state.

vbt-p12-v119 — In things being remembered, when seen — in place, the mind should leave: one's own body unsupported made, the lord spreads forth.

vbt-p12-v120 — Anywhere, on a thing placed — slowly the gaze should withdraw: that knowing, mind-with — O Goddess, void-abode may it be.
