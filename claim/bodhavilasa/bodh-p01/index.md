**bodh-p01-v01** — Oṃ. Salutation to Netra. Salutation to Śiva constantly — maker of the five acts, manifest as supreme-self bliss.
**bodh-p01-v10** — One, everywhere, pure, awareness-selfed, the great Lord. From mis-knowing that I-ness, freedom sits in the palm.
**bodh-p01-v02** — Consciousness is free, the universe's cause — so called; freedom-formed, the knower — and powers too she bestows.
**bodh-p01-v04** — That world's variety stands through object-difference — manifold-variety-yoked, high-and-low-numbered.
**bodh-p01-v05** — From consciousness-tightening comes mind — though knower, even the great Lord. Like a banyan seed, there the world-form — that the wise know.
**bodh-p01-v06** — Knowing is the self's pure form — stainless, utterly stainless. That same, rule-bound, is called mind — knowing.
**bodh-p01-v07** — Object-faced, awareness has come to rest in mind. From tightening-dominant-self, the wise know knowing as mind.
**bodh-p01-v08** — The māyā-knower, knowing-formed, one with that, abides among embodied beings — clinging to objects, void of true discernment.
**bodh-p01-v09** — Ever one, the established mind — by kalā, sattva, guṇa and the rest, by seven-knowerhood, by thirty-six count.
