tal-a34-v00 — Now is told the entry into one's own nature, as the sequence requires.

tal-a34-v01 — This āṇava — taught in many ways for the attaining of Śiva-hood: having entered into the intervals therein, let him rest in the proximate station.

tal-a34-v02 — Then, by abandoning even the āṇava, let him take refuge in the Śākta plane; then likewise the Śāmbhava — clearly, in graded succession.

tal-a34-v03 — Thus — a sharer in the Bhairava-state whose outpouring is filled with the great rays of sequentially-dawned awakening — in the end, with utter disregard for means, he enters forever his own self, the universe gestating within.

tal-a34-v04 — Told is this entry into one's own nature — by the Supreme One.
