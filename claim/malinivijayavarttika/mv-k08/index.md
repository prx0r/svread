**mv-k08-v1168** — That yogin, filled with wonder, obtains awareness of his own self and remains in a state that does not depart even amid the arising and passing of various objects.
**mv-k08-v1169** — Water lying low in a tank does not flow elsewhere, even with effort, because it is incapable of doing more than filling that place alone.
**mv-k08-v1170** — But when it is filled with savor by a stream of water flowing through the inner door, then streams filled by that overflow in all directions.

**mv-k08-v1173** — For the limitation born of conceptualization may afterward show division; that very division is māyā, the power of the free one that reveals the vision of difference.

**mv-k08-v1176** — Even the means is not a means, because it stops effortful activity; it is free from exhalation and inhalation and from the regulation of body and breath.
**mv-k08-v1177** — Thus this mind carries the self across from the ocean of difference, even when it is sinking while still in the taste of objects.
**mv-k08-v1178** — A gourd without holes does not become soft inside; likewise, those who forcibly restrain the mind's own path, as if holding back a horse, do not make it inwardly receptive.

**mv-k08-v1180** — Through knowledge one can become dispassionate even toward pleasure, discerning 'this is this'; for the teacher has taught this in many ways in his own scripture.
**mv-k08-v1181** — The activities of the senses fall away through indifference and dispassion alone; but as long as they are forcibly controlled, they keep acting turbulently.
**mv-k08-v1182** — Even that so-called withdrawal which is applied to the net of the senses becomes, for one whose bondage is already firmly set, a binding as hard as diamond cement.

**mv-k08-v1184** — When the self, the great light of consciousness, is present in the heart, and the senses and objects such as taste are also present and active, the yogi abides very clearly in a fullness whose arising is free of difference; let that reality be accepted.

**mv-k08-v1187** — Wherever union with bliss arises, there one's own nature is full; if the delusion of difference is understood, one may reach peace there. **mv-k08-v1188** — Just as one who sets out to dig a well feels accomplished only when water is reached, while another is satisfied with water already in hand, so the way of attaining the supreme is varied.
**mv-k08-v1189** — This is without means; so how, by the usual relation of means and goal, or in any other way, could a person be freed from the bondage of difference?

**mv-k08-v1191** — Just as children are clearly led into the real nature of sound through written letters, so the deluded are gradually led by those provisional means.
**mv-k08-v1192** — For that very purpose, even in the nondual supreme reality, this scripture respectfully teaches something appropriate, such as worship and meditation.
**mv-k08-v1193** — Whatever delights the mind, in any state of the senses, can be joined to the abode of true awareness, to the very opening of Brahman.
**mv-k08-v1194** — Resting on absorption in true being that follows the self, one naturally and without effort comes to function as an instrument in the worship of the wheel of the supreme Kula lord and his powers.
**mv-k08-v1195** — Having made the support-ground cleansed by a moment of sprinkling with the essence of wonder, I worship you, O God, together with the Goddess day and night in the temple of the body, using flowers of my own nature taken from the mind and filled with their own fragrance, through the method of the priceless offering-vessel of my own heart overflowing with the nectar of bliss.
**mv-k08-v1196** — Thus, wherever one may stand, by the very nature of one's own reflective awareness, there the entry of the three powers shines forth in a threefold way.
**mv-k08-v1197** — When that shining appears ninefold, arising from the stated twelve wheels, its repose occurs in a single moment and takes the form of a state numbering one hundred and eight.
**mv-k08-v1198** — That state is clearly discerned as one alone, numbering a hundred; and in this way, whatever speech at all may arise from this yogin belongs to that same state.
**mv-k08-v1199** — That very speech becomes mantra practice on the unsurpassed path, constantly and without any need to rely on the presence of inner fuel.
**mv-k08-v1200** — If one reflectively understands that all beings enter into that which blazes with hundreds of foremost flames spreading forth from all the streams of the senses, then that very place is where their entry occurs.
