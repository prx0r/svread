**mv-k07-v971** — To that extent, this path of words, resting on the level of the object, becomes the first sprout within the mass of conventions bound up with worldly existence.
**mv-k07-v972** — Thus it is ascertained that objecthood, the state of being the knower, and the state of being the means of knowledge all belong to the thirty-sixfold collection of principles.
**mv-k07-v973** — There, all this shines forth in the unchanging supreme Lord in the form of a reflection, and not as something external.
**mv-k07-v974** — In the sky of consciousness, in Śiva alone, such divided cognitions of various bodies appear to worldly beings, just as a snake and the like appear in a rope.
**mv-k07-v975** — Because the settled view before the body's death belongs to the realm of dream, what kind of other body or bodily arising after death could there really be?
**mv-k07-v976** — In a dream, particular details appear by the force of the unfolding of mere appearance, but they are not produced as independent realities in that way.
**mv-k07-v977** — Just as some śālagrāma stones contain varied forms within them, so the inner delineations of the levels from māyā downward belong to the variegated heart of consciousness.
**mv-k07-v978** — Cities, oceans, mountains, and the like follow that will; they are not by themselves entities that independently function as existent or nonexistent causes and effects.
**mv-k07-v979** — Whether by cutting off or setting in motion a long-established rule, or even where no rule has yet taken root, this Bhairava of the sky of consciousness remains independent.
**mv-k07-v980** — In that state which shares nondifference with Bhairava, full of one consciousness alone, the absence of the recognition 'I am thus' is what differentiates the realm of beings.
**mv-k07-v981** — Just as no other agent is established in a dream by any means of knowledge, so too the creation should be understood as belonging to one's own independent awareness.
**mv-k07-v982** — One who plays in the mind's variegated city and garden knows: 'I alone abide as all these beings that have come to be, and they are nothing but consciousness.'
**mv-k07-v983** — The varied experiences of 'I am born' and 'I am dead' appear in the unborn and deathless, on the wall of the mind, as projections of one's own imagination.
**mv-k07-v984** — Being this world or another world is only a matter of awareness; even amid apparent divisions of time, part, awareness, and place, it remains undivided.
**mv-k07-v985** — If this creation were merely material and not made of consciousness, then no one could perceive even a single mountain from within it.
**mv-k07-v986** — When one examines the elements, subtle elements, and the rest in terms of support and what is supported, in the end it is the consciousness-power, identical with Śiva's own nature, that upholds them.
**mv-k07-v987** — Therefore cognition itself is the agent; that manifestation is Śiva in his own self. For that reason, this power is called the common ground.
**mv-k07-v988** — Even what is imagined and seems without support does not fall away, because the universe rests in its own supporting power; reflect on it in this way.
**mv-k07-v989** — From the fixation of the dense 'I' and so on arises the state beginning with earth, until at last the recognition 'I am consciousness' is fulfilled as the identity of Bhairava.
**mv-k07-v990** — Like blue and other colors in a jewel or in a rainbow's light, so appearances are in Śiva; but in ultimate truth they have no real arising or passing away.
**mv-k07-v991** — To say that creation happens in space or in time is incoherent, for the creation of the conscious Lord includes space and time as well.
**mv-k07-v992** — Within what is taken as waking, in a span of three and a half cubits and a single watch of time, separate dreams arise with their own varied measures of space and time.
**mv-k07-v993** — Therefore we do not regard a moment as anything fixed, for even within a single moment of action there are many swift actions.
**mv-k07-v994** — Therefore those who hold that the contraction of beings ends in a moment surely rely, through this narrow channel, on a view grounded in emptiness.
**mv-k07-v995** — Therefore the one who, by his independence, is able to make existent beings appear empty and likewise make the nonexistent appear clearly is the supreme Lord.
**mv-k07-v996** — Thus the supreme Lord is proclaimed as having the universe as his form; no separate entity such as earth or the rest can be established as independently real in that way.
**mv-k07-v997** — This has already been said before, so it is not examined again in detail; many refutations of external objects have already been composed.
**mv-k07-v998** — But if things are so, how can karma be connected with its fruit in birth and death? Has the previously stated freedom of consciousness been completely forgotten?
**mv-k07-v999** — For this free consciousness makes things shine forth just as it wills, and so they truly appear; it contains all forms within itself, and this is firmly established.
