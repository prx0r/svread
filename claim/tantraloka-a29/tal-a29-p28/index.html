tal-a29-v271 — Touching nonduality in all wheels through supreme piercing, the teacher should make supreme Shiva, giving Shiva state.

tal-a29-v272 — Thus determined in the Shri Viravali lineage: the indivisible middle is to be known in every way; knowing it, one is not stained.

tal-a29-v273 — In that division order, the accomplished teacher liberates bound beings; the disciple enters before the teacher, and mouth pierces mouth.

tal-a29-v274 — Form in form unifies with objects; when mind becomes unified, the two attain a beyond-mind state.

tal-a29-v275 — By mind movement of both, at that time one becomes initiated; in moon-sun union the living being goes to that identity.

tal-a29-v276 — Here gods from Brahma, desiring liberation, restrain their own ray wheel for both enjoyment and liberation.

tal-a29-v277 — If that initiation is proclaimed as Shiva's, this motionless liberation is told for all beings.

tal-a29-v278 — From the striking union of fire and moon parts, pulsation removes external breath gone outward through darkness-form union.

tal-a29-v279 — Gone out through hair pores, wandering with all causes, taking the unmarked middle, it makes wander and releases.

tal-a29-v280 — By union and uprooting method, pierce the five knots; contemplate the union activity pair in the middle abode.
