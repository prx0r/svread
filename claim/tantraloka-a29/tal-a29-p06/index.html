tal-a29-v51 — The inner twelve is to be worshipped, then eight and eight, or four, as desired; what number indeed in the rays?

tal-a29-v52 — Maheśi, Vairiñci, Kaumāri, and Vaiṣṇavi are in the four directions; Aindrī, Yāmyā, Muṇḍā, and Yogeshi are in the corners.

tal-a29-v53 — The eightfold set from wind-end to aghora is then worshipped in order by the view of union-bliss, becoming paired.

tal-a29-v54 — In the eight-eight rite too, in the manifold-named rite, this rite is prescribed in many ways; its number may be a lamp garland.

tal-a29-v55 — In the Ratnamala treatise, letter numbers are lamps; the wise should count the letters of the chief worshipped vidya.

tal-a29-v56 — One may perform family worship together with pithas, fields, and so on, as spoken by the supreme lord in the Madhava family.

tal-a29-v57 — The fourfold set named creation, preservation, destruction, and order, together with pithas and cremation grounds, should be worshipped for enjoyment and liberation.

tal-a29-v58 — One should remember this rite for self or for Shakti's wheel, placing it in the body called pitha, supreme-lordly.

tal-a29-v59 — Attahasam is at the crown place, Caritra in the ear hole, Kaulagiri in the ears, and Jayantika in the nostril holes.

tal-a29-v60 — Ujjayini is at the brows, Prayaga in the mouth, Varanasi in the heart, Shripitha at the shoulder pair, and Viraja in the throat.
