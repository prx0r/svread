tal-a29-v169 — Because of secrecy, it is not read with clear words; again, the curious one should infer it from the stated scripture recitation alone.

tal-a29-v170 — That wheel which all always worship, and which god and goddess possess, is supreme and causes presence at the beginning of goddess worship.
