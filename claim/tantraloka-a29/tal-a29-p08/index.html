tal-a29-v71 — Worship triad upon triad, joined with future own-triad — heart-coil, brow-center, this indeed, three in sequence.

tal-a29-v72 — Cremation grounds in sequence, field-born, with yogini host — eight-finger-high, upward-round, slender-waisted.

tal-a29-v73 — Red-circulating hearing-seeing lamps make with ghee — whatever else, in the middle worship own practice.

tal-a29-v74 — Non-duality indeed, not duality — command of the supreme Lord; declared by Siddhanta Vaishnava and the rest, mantras are impurity-joined then.

tal-a29-v75 — So long as light-endurance — lifeless they would be here in non-duality: vessel, eye-binding and the rest, circle, ladle small-ladle fire.

tal-a29-v76 — Abandoning here — success in good middle, in the vessel in the middle sacrifice the thin — this day-and-night sacrifice performing, and others by day.

tal-a29-v77 — With heroic feast done, subjugation — mantras succeed effortlessly; read here the seat-hymn in sacrifice, fortune-bringing invocation.

tal-a29-v78 — Forms or pair-forms, hero-formed — shaken-off, conductless — the wise should worship in order.

tal-a29-v79 — One indeed then the Kula-lord, himself having become so many — powers by couple-yoga satisfy like the universal form.

tal-a29-v80 — No sequence called would be in light-made consciousness — absence of consciousness indeed is not at all — therefore untimely, but satisfaction.
