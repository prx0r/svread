tal-a29-v231 — This foot's intent: he grants siddhis — the guru who was practitioner before; the other gives liberation enough.

tal-a29-v237 — Piercing-initiation in many ways there determined — and by the practitioner to be done through ascending entry.

tal-a29-v238 — Of the disciple cakra-breaking conviction arises, certain — by which anima and the rest: accomplishment, in Srimala too it is taught.

tal-a29-v239 — On attaining upper-wheel state, that is indeed demon-possession — of mantra-sound-bindu-power-serpent-supreme-self.

tal-a29-v240 — Sixfold in the glorious Cave, piercing-initiation prescribed by the supreme Lord — fiery kula as own-scripture states, cakra beginning with eight stars.
