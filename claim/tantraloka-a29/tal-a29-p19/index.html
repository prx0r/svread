tal-a29-v181 — The goddess-circle, situated in consciousness alone, is to be satisfied by the wise through universal enjoyment, from the mirror of consciousness.

tal-a29-v182 — Where all elements dissolve and burn, see that consciousness residing in the body, equal in splendor to the fire of time.

tal-a29-v183 — In this void-formed cremation ground, served by yoginis and siddhas, a play-place, greatly fierce, where all forms are set.

tal-a29-v184 — Filled with circles of sound-rays, with darkness continuously destroyed, free from all conceptual distinctions, alone in the state of bliss.

tal-a29-v185 — In the cremation ground filled with countless consciousness, terrifying consciousness, support of all deities, who entering does not become accomplished?

tal-a29-v186 — In the glorious Viravali scripture, Bhairavi spoke thus: having first performed sacrifice, for one fit for such propriety.

tal-a29-v187 — Having discerned the family, initiate one's own disciple in such a rite; by Rudra-power sprinkle him and place him near the deities.

tal-a29-v188 — Having observed his arms, kindle them by Rudra-power; with that same power offer flower to his hands anointed with scent.

tal-a29-v189 — Having placed his two unsupported arms, one should think of them as drawn by Rudra-power in the form of a blazing hook.

tal-a29-v190 — Then he himself takes cloth and becomes blindfolded; he himself drops the flower, and from that fall one discerns the family.
