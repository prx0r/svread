tal-a29-v01 — Then, for the suitable adept, the secret rule is stated; then all worship is said to be kula procedure.

tal-a29-v10 — Here in worship, whatever substance is forbidden in scriptural tradition, the wise one should use it immersed in left nectar.

tal-a29-v02 — Thus, for guru and disciple established in flow, the supreme lord has spoken the essence in ordered worship.

tal-a29-v03 — What arises in one month for one appointed to the accomplished order would not arise in a thousand years through various floods of mantras.

tal-a29-v04 — Kula of the supreme lord is power, capacity, elevation, freedom, vigor, virility, mass, and consciousness-body.

tal-a29-v05 — For one seeing all classes of beings by such nature, with masses of doubt destroyed, worship is indeed of that kind.

tal-a29-v06 — For establishing such form, by the path of mind, speech, and body, whatever the hero practices is called kula worship.

tal-a29-v07 — Outside, in power, in yamala, in body, in breath path, and in mind: thus kula worship is sixfold, differentiating according to distinction.

tal-a29-v08 — Bathing, circle, pot, and the like, or sixfold placement and the like, are not used here; nothing done is for breaking.

tal-a29-v09 — Free from six circles, devoid of all coverings, consisting of knower and known: this is declared as kaula in the traishirasa doctrine.
