## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a29-v281** — One should not join self, space, outside, mantra, and body; this initiation gives Shiva state to all beings.
**tal-a29-v282** — At initiation end, having cooked lamps, the offering should be eaten with all adepts by clan teachers destroying great sin.
**tal-a29-v283** — Thus in Shri Ratnamala, the extra rite indeed pacifies that sin, and this pacification is proclaimed.
**tal-a29-v284** — Next day the teacher's sacrifice should be done by him, because without it the rite does not reach completeness; do that with effort then.
**tal-a29-v285** — By which the teacher is pleased, that should be offered to him; in the circle rite interval, movement is called the rite.
**tal-a29-v286** — Having seen the well-filled bee vessel placed in the hero lord's hand, supreme Brahman, that should be drunk by the teacher's command.
**tal-a29-v287** — Having satisfied beings, one should offer to the teacher; having made the teacher on earth, bowed and taken, satisfying sky-goers.
**tal-a29-v288** — Having saluted own mantra, and that, female messenger, Ganapati, teachers, field protector, hero group, then by teacher order.
**tal-a29-v289** — The substance touched by the hero should be drunk by oneself, never otherwise; those who know supreme Brahman are free from coming and going.
**tal-a29-v290** — This should not be done together with those possessed of greed, delusion, pride, anger, attachment, and deceit, by one seeking welfare for oneself.
