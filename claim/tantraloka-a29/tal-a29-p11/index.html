tal-a29-v101 — Make her such by that — but no varna-etc. expectation; by worldly-otherworldly two-self association, through identity-superiority.

tal-a29-v102 — Action-cause-co-arisen is stated threefold in the guru's teaching — from direct-lineage-union equal to that, thus again three ways.

tal-a29-v103 — In the heart of all-conduct this is summarized — these six powers declared, giving enjoyment-liberation fruits.

tal-a29-v104 — By two — creation and dissolution — therefore the supreme gathering: having brought her, worshipped mutually, satisfied mutually.

tal-a29-v105 — By inner sequence indeed, the principal circle's worship — which indeed is bliss-abundance, of consciousness indeed inner.

tal-a29-v106 — That principal would be wheel-from-wheel supreme — from expansion, from satisfaction, from bond-cutting, from action-power.

tal-a29-v107 — The wheel of Kasescaka by rite, and of Karota, so spoken — sacrifice and libation outwardly, and its expansion is declared.

tal-a29-v108 — From the imagined-powerful going between wheel and wheel — from breath-going, from bliss-flowing consumption, conduct.

tal-a29-v109 — Thus, from the external overflowing of consciousness, satisfaction is made in the inner cakras with suitable portions of objects, along with incense, perfume, garlands, and the rest.

tal-a29-v110 — Let them do this mutually here for making the main cakra one; this is said in the Triśira Tantra, within the range of the pure seat.
