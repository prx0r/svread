tal-a29-v171 — The body indeed is the supreme mark, containing all principles, Shiva, joined with the goddess wheel, the best abode of worship.

tal-a29-v172 — That same circle is principal, trident-lotus-wheel-space; there indeed the goddess wheel is always worshipped outside and inside.

tal-a29-v173 — After recalling one's own mantra, with those births and juices abundant in joy, by the rite of creation and destruction, one should touch.

tal-a29-v174 — The consciousness wheel aroused by the impetus of touch, its lord obtains the supreme abode, having satisfied all deities.

tal-a29-v175 — By the prescribed follow-up rite, with heart-stealing substances, likewise from one's own desire recollection within, one should fully satisfy.

tal-a29-v176 — Having made support-bearing, with mind-taken wonder-juice instantly sprinkled, with nature flowers abundant in own fragrance, from the sequence of the invaluable cup of one's own heart full of bliss nectar, I worship you together with the goddess in the body temple night and day.

tal-a29-v177 — From the boundary of Sri Vira-avali onward in the scripture collection, this supreme sacrifice is praised by the moon-crested one.

tal-a29-v178 — Or, seeing all the group of deities situated in breath function by the previously stated method, the teacher should worship them there indeed.

tal-a29-v179 — Satisfaction of the breath-supported goddesses is by brahma-nose and other distinctions, by hand holes entering the apana moon wheel.

tal-a29-v180 — Thus by breath sequence indeed one should satisfy the deity group; soon by its grace one then attains knowledge accomplishments.
