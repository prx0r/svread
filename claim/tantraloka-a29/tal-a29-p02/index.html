tal-a29-v11 — In Shri Brahmayamala it is said: wine is Shiva essence; without it there is no enjoyment or liberation; but with flour, honey, and jaggery it is that.

tal-a29-v12 — Female, neuter, and male forms give prior and later enjoyment; but grape-born is supreme radiance, Bhairava free from imagination.

tal-a29-v13 — This itself is the pure essence of light, bliss, and consciousness, beloved of the deities and eternal; therefore one should always drink it.

tal-a29-v14 — In the glorious Krama secret, by the supreme lord, argha-vessel, sacrifice-abode, and lamp are called three.

tal-a29-v15 — The secret in Kaula sacrifice: there argha comes from Shakti union, the abode is named earth-garment-body-seat, and excellence follows in order.

tal-a29-v16 — Lamp is ghee-born; cows are indeed remembered as earth-wandering deities; knowing this, the Kaulika should become diligent in those three.

tal-a29-v17 — Knowing the primacy of the argha-vessel, doubt may indeed arise about substances stated by Shambhu, and that doubt is defiling.

tal-a29-v18 — Having entered the fragrance-rich sacrifice house, facing east or north, one moves from reverse to proper order by supreme impurity.

tal-a29-v19 — One should perform purification, burning and nourishing, in order from fierce-gentle distinction, or from mother-presence mantra.

tal-a29-v20 — If desiring initiation, plan purification of the path; then, purifying things, by Shakti alone lead them to immortality.
