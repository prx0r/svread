## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a29-v111** — But in the middle of the six letters, the rudra place may enter, in the reflection of one's own nature manifesting enjoyment and non-enjoyment.
**tal-a29-v112** — Gradually the inner-cakra goddesses go to the middle consciousness cakra; but they, having abandoned what is established in the body, do so because of another whose body is well.
**tal-a29-v113** — They dwell thus: that is not full, nor does it overflow, the power gained by filling rays made of the inner-cakra deity.
**tal-a29-v114** — That pair of power and power-holder becomes mutually facing; disturbance is born from the contact of entering the upper place of that pair.
**tal-a29-v115** — It disturbs even the inner cakras; those then become consisting of that, not separate. Thus this yamala discussion of dissolved difference, whatever it may be.
**tal-a29-v116** — From gradual difference union, that itself indeed is the consciousness emission collision, the eternal supreme place, the great bliss of the world of both natures.
**tal-a29-v117** — Neither peaceful nor risen, the cause of peaceful and risen birth, the supreme kaula should always make consciousness one's own, desiring her uninterrupted foot.
**tal-a29-v118** — In the ultimate sense, indeed, the form of the consciousness goddess is uninterrupted, by discussion of such and such pacification, arising, state, and dissolution.
**tal-a29-v119** — The uninterrupted natural abode may be entered; the fortunate one, then the pair consisting of peaceful and risen arises simultaneously from power and power-holder.
**tal-a29-v120** — The risen form gone to each other's abode, the peaceful form indeed gone to self; both in reality are yamala, thus risen and peaceful.
