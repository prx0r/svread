tsa-a13-v20 — There in the middle the Lord; above, his is the north-eastern face; below, the underworld face; in the east-and-rest four directions, the so-called Tatpuruṣa, Aghora, Sadyas, Vāma; in the four-direction-middle, the other four.

tsa-a13-v21 — Thus indeed awareness-glory alone, formed, shines forth direction-difference — thus direction is not another principle.

tsa-a13-v22 — As, as the shadow is to be overstepped — being, before, before, it becomes — so reaching the Lord's centrality: being all-presiding alone is centrality — thus it is said.

tsa-a13-v23 — Thus as the Lord is the divider of directions, so the sun — for it indeed is the Lord's knowledge-power, thus said: there, there, there the east is of the manifest from the east; and just where, so there — thus self-ruled even, facing one's own place, is in front.

tsa-a13-v24 — Thus by self-sun-Lord-triad-unification is direction-discussion — thus Abhinavagupta's gurus.

tsa-a13-v25 — Thus standing, facing north one should sit, burning body-ness in body-city-eight and the rest by I-feeling abandonment; from nearness as another's body, from bodilessness — then mounted on the waveless steady abode, from gaze-nature — which indeed is the first throb-digit — that alone is the form; thereupon is the as-taught worshippable-deity-wheel-placing; chiefly too here the powers are to be worshipped.

tsa-a13-v26 — From being that seat, of the Lord-nine-self and the rest, of power alone and of worship-worthiness — thus the gurus.

tsa-a13-v27 — There five states: waking and the rest; and sixth called unsurpassed, nature-state to be contemplated.

tsa-a13-v28 — Thus sixfold is the placing.

tsa-a13-v29 — There from the presiding of causes, Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra-Īśa-Sadāśiva-power-formed, each from thirty-six-principle-group, world-transcended in Bhairava-Lord-non-difference-course placing, from fullness is Bhairavī-being; hence this has no opening.

tsa-a13-v30 — Which, they said: gained in waveless mounting, again what with principle-emission through placing and the rest — thus.

tsa-a13-v31 — So long indeed that waveless Bhairava-formed, which in own self shows emission-dissolution-variety-peak.

tsa-a13-v32 — Thus by mutual-meeting-yoga, Lord-made breath-body-mind contemplated, outside and within with flowers-smoke-satings as possible, one should worship.

tsa-a13-v33 — There in the body, in breath, in mind accordingly one should do the trident-lotus-placing — as at base-power-root: root, bulb, to-root-pith, at the hanging-end, to kalā-principle's end the staff; Māyā-natured knot; four-natured pure-knowledge lotus; right there the Lord Sadāśiva; he alone the great corpse; through excellence dissolved, from awakening, chiefly, from knowable-bodied body-decay, and from sound-touch-awareness-nature.

tsa-a13-v34 — That risen from the navel, come out of the three head-openings at its top, abiding inside sound, power-pervader, equanimity-formed and the rest, the six-end; thereupon is the pure lotus-triad, beyond-mind; on this universe-made division made seat, presiding as pervading-being, the desired become-supported deity having fashioned — which right there, in same-disposition-full self, universe-being-offering: that alone is worship; which alone becoming-that: that is meditation; which inner-deliberation-being-shaking as of such: that is murmur; which by such-deliberation-order-awakened great-splendour, by that force alone, is universe-self-making: that is oblation; that very thing done thus, the retinue, from that alone, like sparks from a fire-heap, having meditated, just so one should worship.
