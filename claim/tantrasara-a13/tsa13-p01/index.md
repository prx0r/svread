## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a13-v01** — Now the glad-hearted should go to the worship-place; for wherever the heart is gladdened and made fit for absorption into the Lord, there it becomes — that alone is the mark, nothing else; for even in the saying, meditated identity alone is the cause, and that too is from being-glad alone — nothing else is the place.
**tsa-a13-v10** — For here, for firmness of realization of non-difference with the Lord, the worship-act is made the example: there, of all factors, thus is Lord-ness — there, from the sacrificer's place-purity-bringing-doings, from vessel-purity, from placing, from the sacrificer's body-placing, from the worshippable's altar-placing.
**tsa-a13-v11** — Thus, even by rite-order alone, making all factors the Lord's, seeing all rites with just this view, without chief knowledge and yoga — one becomes the Supreme Lord.
**tsa-a13-v12** — Thus, having placed on the vessel, having worshipped with flowers, smoke and the rest, one should sprinkle the rite-essence and the flowers and the rest with its drops.
**tsa-a13-v13** — Then in the halo-circle, on ground or in sky: oṃ to the outer retinue, hail — thus one should worship.
**tsa-a13-v14** — Then at the door-place: oṃ to the door-deity-wheel, hail — thus one should worship.
**tsa-a13-v15** — But when there is an unguarded outer place, having entered, on the maṇḍala-altar-front alone one should do the outer-retinue-door-deity-wheel worship, the aforesaid placing and the rest — not outside.
**tsa-a13-v16** — Then too: phaṭ phaṭ phaṭ — thus, having thrown the weapon-muttered flower, having removed the obstacles, having meditated, having entered within, with gaze kindled by the Lord's ray, one should see all around the worship-house.
**tsa-a13-v17** — There the release-seeker should stand facing north, so that by the Lord's Aghora-radiance swiftly indeed, his bonds burnt, he may become.
**tsa-a13-v18** — There the Lord's freedom alone, by form-shining, shines forth the direction-principle.
**tsa-a13-v19** — There awareness-light alone is the middle; thence, other-division arising: what is taken as light is above, what is not thus is below; what faces the light is east, the other is west; from having become facing-light, the next is that-light-stream-rise-place; south from favour, facing it, but from to-be-shone, north — thus the direction-four.
**tsa-a13-v02** — But seat, mountain-top and the rest in scripture is to be understood as aimed at pointing out this place.
**tsa-a13-v03** — In these seats and the rest, by the Lord's ordainment, powers entered by the Lord take body — like noble lands for the pious, like barbarian lands for the impious; the teaching of the mountain-top, through solitude, from distraction-avoidance, is the state of one-pointedness — thus.
**tsa-a13-v04** — There, before the worship-house, outside alone, one should do the general placing — first on the hands, then on the body.
**tsa-a13-v05** — hrīṃ na pha hrīṃ, hrīṃ ā kṣa hrīṃ — thus; by these two, expressing power and the power-bearer, the two Mālinī-alphabet mantras; with each one singly — first power, then the power-bearer — in release from foot-tip to head-end; in enjoyment everything is reversed.
**tsa-a13-v06** — For Mālinī the Goddess is the chief Śākta form, milking all wishes through the collision of seed and womb.
**tsa-a13-v07** — And meaningful is this name: garlanded with Rudra-power garlands, flowered in fruits, the bee of the sound that dissolves the winter of transmigration, holding attainment and release, endowed with the power to give and to withhold — since ra and la are remembered as one.
**tsa-a13-v08** — Hence very, for even a broken-rite mantra becomes complete through this placing; even a stained one — Gāruḍa, Vaiṣṇava and the rest — having reached stainlessness, becomes release-giving.
**tsa-a13-v09** — After the body-placing, this same placing is to be done on the vessel.
