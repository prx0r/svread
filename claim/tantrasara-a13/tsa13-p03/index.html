tsa-a13-v35 — Remembering the twelve-end, this foremost trident, from the root.

tsa-a13-v36 — Leaving sequence, going to goddess-wheel-front attains sky-going-ness.

tsa-a13-v37 — From root-support, of twice-six-end-sky-peak-filling nature.

tsa-a13-v38 — Of sky-going-ness, from sky-faring-rest and from sky-nectar-eating.

tsa-a13-v39 — Thus by inner rite alone, in reality, done-what-was-to-be-done.

tsa-a13-v40 — Truly for the then-entered, still outer is to be done, to remove limitation alone — and who too thus does not partake immersion: his chief is the outer rite; from its practice, immersion-gain; since for him too, but the beast-veiling inner rite, when mounted on that too, of his thought-force is purity-giving.

tsa-a13-v41 — Now when one wishes to do initiation, then for preparation: ground-taking, Gaṇeśa-worship, jar-and-vessel worship, altar-worship, oblation — one should do.

tsa-a13-v42 — But in daily-and-occasional: altar-and-rest worship-oblation alone.

tsa-a13-v43 — There is initiation-preparation, the disciple's refined-fitness-placing, like sharpening of teeth; the God's toward-work-upturned-ness-grasping, the guru's taking of that.

tsa-a13-v44 — Of implement-things, by being inside the worship-house, through the Lord's radiance-swelling, is bestowing of worship-implement-fitness — thus.

tsa-a13-v45 — There, having arranged the worship-house full of all implements, having remembered the Goddess Mālinī or Mātṛkā, bearing the taken, full of her-letter-radiance-mass, one should cast the flower-handful.

tsa-a13-v46 — Then the stated weapon-muttered mustard-grain-parched-rice and the rest, radiance-formed, having scattered, in the north-east direction gathering in order — thus is ground-taking.

tsa-a13-v47 — Then having given the seat up to pure-knowledge's end, Gaṇeśa's worship; then having filled the jar, filled with bliss-substance, adorned, one should worship; then the worshippable next, having placed betel, there the chief mantra, as all-presiding, remembering with rule-precedence, with that 108-times-muttered, that jar one should make.

tsa-a13-v48 — In the second vessel, weapons for obstacle-quelling one should worship.

tsa-a13-v49 — Then in own directions the world-guardians with weapons one should worship.

tsa-a13-v50 — Then to the previously-initiated disciple's hand one should give the weapon-vessel.

tsa-a13-v51 — And oneself the guru should take the jar.

tsa-a13-v52 — Then making the disciple pour the stream around the house for obstacle-quelling, with jar the guru should follow, reciting this mantra: O O Indra! by you in your own direction for obstacle-pacifying.

tsa-a13-v53 — With attentiveness, to be done to rite's end, by Śiva's command.

tsa-a13-v54 — In the three-syllabled name, 'bho' is one alone — thus.

tsa-a13-v55 — Then in the north-east direction one should place the jar.

tsa-a13-v56 — Upon the scattered, the weapon-vessel.

tsa-a13-v58 — Then in the altar-middle Supreme-worship.

tsa-a13-v59 — Then the fire-pit as Supreme-power-formed having contemplated, fire having kindled there, with heart-inner-awakening-fire made one, with mantra-deliberation-accompaniment, the blazing Śiva-fire having contemplated, placed there, having worshipped, one should sate the mantras with ghee and sesame.

tsa-a13-v60 — And with the vessel, sprinkling alone is sesame-ghee and the rest's refining.

tsa-a13-v61 — And of ladle-and-spoon, non-difference-view alone indeed is refining.
