tsa-a13-v100 — In mantra-practice, daily worship, hearing, study, one is entitled; but in the occasional, everywhere the guru alone one should request.

tsa-a13-v62 — Then having offered as able, having arranged ladle-spoon up-down-facing as power-Śiva-formed, mutually facing, having stood even-footed, contemplating the stream of supreme nectar fallen, risen in the sky of the twelve-end, risen full-Śiva-moon, and uttering the vauṣaḍ-ending mantra, until ghee's end one should stand — thus is full oblation, mantra-wheel-sating.

tsa-a13-v63 — Then the sprinkled caru having brought, on altar-vessel-jar-fires share by share having offered, one share having left over, to the disciple one should give a share.

tsa-a13-v65 — Its falling in fire-Yama-Nirṛti directions and below is not auspicious — thus.

tsa-a13-v66 — There oblation by weapon-mantra is to be done.

tsa-a13-v67 — Then for distraction-avoidance, for fitness to see the future mantra, having brought in the blindfolded disciple, having made him kneeling, one should have him cast a flower-handful.

tsa-a13-v68 — Then suddenly, unfastened-eye-bound, through descent-graced instrument-ness, seeing the present mantra in that place face-to-face, he becomes made of that; of graced instruments mantra-nearness is visible, since as it were of trembling beings.

tsa-a13-v69 — Then in one's own right hand with blazing, having worshipped the deity-wheel, placing it burning the disciple's bonds on head-heart-navel.

tsa-a13-v70 — Then in the left with gentleness, having worshipped, fattening with pure principle — then one should bow.

tsa-a13-v71 — Then the being-deity-direction-offering, full of wine-meat-water and the rest, outside one should give, having sipped.

tsa-a13-v72 — Then oneself having eaten the oblation-meal, with disciple-self attaining oneness, awakened-natured one should stay.

tsa-a13-v73 — Dreaming too, at dawn if the disciple tells an inauspicious dream — that to him one should not explain.

tsa-a13-v74 — For doubt and fear would be his; merely with the weapon one should make its expiation.

tsa-a13-v75 — Then just so having worshipped the Lord, entered before him by the disciple's breath-course, touching the six-cause-touching in heart-throat-palate-brow-cleft-twelve-ends, each eight refinements contemplating, some time resting the disciple-breath right there, again one should descend.

tsa-a13-v76 — Thus effected-forty-eight-refinements-upon-made-Rudra-part-attainment becomes a samayin.

tsa-a13-v77 — Then to him the worshippable mantra with flowers and the rest one should bestow.

tsa-a13-v78 — Then the rules to him one should lay down.

tsa-a13-v79 — In the guru with whole self, devotion; so in scripture; in God; aversion toward their opponents; like the guru, of the guru's son and the rest; of the knowledge-tied made, of the before-initiated and the rest, seeing; of the womb-tie, for its honouring, not by oneself — thus to be deemed.

tsa-a13-v80 — Of women, of the barren that-loathing-cause one should not do.

tsa-a13-v81 — Deity-name, guru-name, and mantra, apart from worship-time one should not utter.

tsa-a13-v82 — What the guru has enjoyed — bed and the rest — one should not enjoy.

tsa-a13-v83 — What games and the rest are worldly, that in the guru's nearness one should not do.

tsa-a13-v84 — Apart from that, elsewhere one should not make excellence-thought.

tsa-a13-v85 — Everywhere, in śrāddha and the rest, the guru alone one should worship.

tsa-a13-v86 — And in all occasionals, śākinī-and-the-rest words one should not say.

tsa-a13-v88 — With those of lower sight, Vaiṣṇavas and the rest, company one should not make.

tsa-a13-v89 — Abiding in this teaching, one should not see with former-birth thought.

tsa-a13-v90 — When the guru's circle has come home, as able one should do worship.

tsa-a13-v91 — A lower-path abider, a Vaiṣṇava and the rest, having even made him guru, from his teaching-clan one should abandon.

tsa-a13-v92 — Even then one should not see with excellence-thought.

tsa-a13-v93 — With mark-bearers no conduct-meeting one should make; them only as able one should worship.

tsa-a13-v95 — In the wheel, standing, of last-and-first division, birth-made, one should not fancy.

tsa-a13-v96 — Apart from the body, no other abode-ford and the rest with high regard one should see.

tsa-a13-v97 — Constantly one should remember the mantra-heart — thus the disciple, having heard, having bowed, having accepted, the guru with wealth-wife-body-up-to gift pleasing, and the before-initiated and poor-lordless and the rest sating.

tsa-a13-v98 — And with the future rule, the image-wheel one should sate.

tsa-a13-v99 — Thus one becomes a samayin.
