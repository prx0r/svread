tsa-a22-v01 — Now this entire worship is taught through the glorious Kaula method, for the fit ones mounted on firm resolve of exactly that awakened kind. On this point the Yogasaṃcāra and the rest declare: bliss is Brahman, seated in the body, established threefold at lips and lowest end.

tsa-a22-v10 — For so it is: from Māyā, person, nature, and intellect beginning, down to earth, there are twenty-seven tattvas — kalā and the rest included therein — and likewise, in the knowledge-power in the middle state, the qualities of the Brahman-pentad, beginning with instant-ness, born-ness, and become-arisen-ness, number just twenty-seven in form. So declare the glorious Lakulīśa masters.

tsa-a22-v11 — In the highest too the Lord is five-powered: to each power belong five forms, making twenty-five powers. Mutually undivided, they are a single power, still undivided — hence by a pervasion twenty-seven-formed one should bind the buddhi-and-breath-formed flame of consciousness-fire with a single utterance alone, so that Paramaśiva alone stands bound and it hastens toward nothing beyond the tantra. Thus penetrated, one should render the present offering-substance, dwelling, directions, and supports made of that very stuff; then one should fill the offering vessel by the tuft-binding pervasion alone and worship it, worship the altars with its drops, and — joining the left ring-finger with the thumb — worship and satisfy the mantra-wheel upon the body’s wheels with its essence. Then, up to breath; then fashion the trident-formed seat ending in the three powers upon the altar — up to Māyā, in the lettered auṃ-syllable fashion the seat ending in the three powers — up to Māyā in the lettered auṃ-syllable; and thereupon the power to be worshipped, deliberation-formed. Thus, by a single utterance alone, having effected the placing of support and supported, one should behold the universe right there — even in the consciousness that has become the supported — and that too as made of consciousness. So the universe through consciousness, and through that, her mutual enclosure comes about: arisen from consciousness, it comes to rest right there — for the universe rises from the knowable, and consciousness rests right there. Just this much is the consciousness-principle, attained through the twofold enclosure.

tsa-a22-v02 — Whoever abandons it, not abiding in Brahman, is cut off from bliss.

tsa-a22-v03 — And sacrificers of the wheel who feed on blissless, artificial food —

tsa-a22-v04 — both sink into dreadful hell. Therefore one should hold to this stance.

tsa-a22-v05 — So in this stance there is the Kula sacrifice, and it is sixfold: in the outer, in the Śakti, in one’s own body, in the couple, in breath, and in consciousness.

tsa-a22-v06 — Here each later one is higher than the last, and each earlier one exists to give a taste for what follows.

tsa-a22-v07 — For one who seeks powers, the second, the fourth, and the fifth are to be carried out in every way; for one who seeks release the sixth is chief — yet even he must perform the second and the rest on occasional rites as far as possible, and to complete the rite.

tsa-a22-v08 — Among these, the outer consists of the bliss-full altar, the hero’s cup, a red cloth — or else the liṅga and the rest as mentioned before.

tsa-a22-v09 — There, with no need at all for bathing and the rest of the duties — purity being gained by resting in full bliss alone — one should first contemplate the oneness of breath, consciousness, and body, and then utter the mantra twenty-seven times over crown, face, heart, secret place, and body, in forward and reverse order, since consciousness itself has Paramaśiva’s form: the fullness of the universe’s course made complete in the Supreme Lord — in the lower, in the middle, and in the highest alike.
