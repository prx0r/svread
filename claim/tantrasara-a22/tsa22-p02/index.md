## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a22-v12** — So it has been said: having fully enclosed emission.
**tsa-a22-v13** — Then, having offered incense, smoke, wine, flowers, and the rest, down to self-surrender, having murmured in one’s own rest and gathered it all in, one should cast it into water.
**tsa-a22-v15** — Now, in the Śakti. There — the goddesses and heroes of the Śakti-circle being mutually of both natures, from the awakening of Śiva and Śakti that runs from play's beginning to emission's end, through mutual activity, by the Supreme Lord's ordainment, in pure form — preeminence lies there. By this, even the distinguished wheel's Śakti-ness stands explained. There, worship is by the tuft-binding pervasion alone, upon the seat ending in the three powers; in the triangle, in the middle, the emission-power — but in the pervasion lies the distinction.
**tsa-a22-v16** — So too in one's own body: in that very wheel there; then in the sub-wheels beginning with the crown-aperture.
**tsa-a22-v17** — Now, in the couple. This is the mark of the Śakti: like that — non-difference. Then, disregarding age, birth, and the rest, out of non-attachment; for identity is born of the transgressive pair.
**tsa-a22-v18** — From being arisen together with cause and effect, threefold — directly, or else in arranged fashion — the two, having worshipped one another, draw bliss near by satisfying.
**tsa-a22-v19** — One should worship the wheel according to its fitness, and likewise the sub-wheel that follows — outwardly with flowers and the rest, ending inward, and with perfumes, foods, wines, and the rest.
**tsa-a22-v20** — Such is the state surging with the play of that bliss-mass. And the host of sub-wheels dissolves through identity with the wheel.
**tsa-a22-v21** — In the relish of own-being at the time when each enjoyment unfolds, gradually the sub-wheel goddesses enter the middle consciousness-wheel.
**tsa-a22-v22** — That whose power is won by filling the host of rays embodied in the sub-wheel goddesses — having the nature of that power, like that — becomes mutually facing.
**tsa-a22-v23** — The agitation born of the throbbing entry into the upper abode of that pair agitates the sub-wheels as well — and then they are made of that, not separate.
**tsa-a22-v24** — Such is this couple, whose talk of difference has melted away: just when it is so, then, through gradation and excellence, she indeed is the collision of consciousness and emission.
**tsa-a22-v25** — The steady supreme abode of that — the both-natured, vast world — bliss is neither the calm nor the arisen: the womb-cause of the calm and the arisen, the supreme Kaula.
**tsa-a22-v26** — Desiring the undivided station, one should make her — consciousness — one's own; then the pair, calm-natured and arisen-natured, rises at once for power and for power-bearer.
**tsa-a22-v27** — From the interpenetration of self and other, when there is calm and other — of the two, through two-naturedness — power, though arisen like that, nourishes emission; its bearer does not.
**tsa-a22-v28** — In her lies the excellent Kula. Then, through her, among men, come the taught collisions of yoga. Then — in the twofold emission, in this calm-arisen abode — those who contemplate —
**tsa-a22-v29** — In the eastward one, mounted in the undivided station as emission-being — the arisen, too, is received mutually from mouth to mouth, from the chief mouth outward into the mouth.
**tsa-a22-v30** — The goddess-wheel becomes satisfied, bestowing powers, knowledge, and release. In the practice of calm, one attains the calm Śiva — which here is the wheel of deities.
**tsa-a22-v31** — The void, devoid of bliss — from bliss's own abode the offering — and the humming-thrilled ones, through the taste of relishing outward states filled with one's own essence.
**tsa-a22-v32** — The sub-wheel surging with inner fullness reaches the wheel; then that too surges as before. Thus this emission is threefold in its implied sense.
**tsa-a22-v33** — In this emission-abode, through relish, mantra-power — in just three ways — bears each fruit in each consciousness-womb.
