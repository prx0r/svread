## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a18-v01** — One well-practised in knowledge should be consecrated, to be a practitioner or a teacher — for even devoid of all marks, the knower alone is entitled to practitionerhood and to bestowing grace; not another, even if consecrated.
**tsa-a18-v02** — Having handed over his own authority, a teacher who performs no initiation incurs no fault; but before that, bondage itself, through fault, through authority's bond, through the giver of the Vidyeśa state, is his non-performance of initiation. So consecrated, for the success of mantra-deity identity, six-monthly, daily, through the conduct of muttering, fire-offering, and special worship, let him perform the knowledge-vow. Thereafter, having become made of that, he is entitled in initiation and the rest. Therein: let him not initiate the unfit, nor let him pass over the fit; let him test even the initiated in the giving of knowledge; having known even deceit-grasped knowledge, let him overlook it; and herein, through the splendor of consecration, god-worship and the rest.
