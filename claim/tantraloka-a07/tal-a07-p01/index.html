tal-a07-v01 — Now this supreme secret, the rising of the cakras, is told.

tal-a07-v10 — Nineteen hundreds sixty-four in Śiva-syllable; eighteen hundreds would be rising in twelve-syllable.

tal-a07-v02 — Thus effortless spoken; effort-born but is described: seed-syllable-natured all, of consciousness vibration-nature.

tal-a07-v03 — Bringing supreme consciousness means thus described, like waterwheel machine bucket flow.

tal-a07-v04 — By single concentration effort wonderful machine-rising should obtain; by single concentration force arisen in mantra-rising night.

tal-a07-v05 — That mantra-deity by effort-identity becomes pleased; in sky taste-one-eye in eternal-rising, half of that in two-seed-lump.

tal-a07-v06 — In three seven thousands two-hundred thus rising held; in four but thousands five and indeed four-hundred.

tal-a07-v07 — In five-syllable ocean-thousands three-hundred twenty so; in six three-thousands six-hundred rising would be.

tal-a07-v08 — In seven three-thousands but six-eighty-excess remembered; by hundreds but by twenty-seven in eight-syllable variation.

tal-a07-v09 — By twenty-four hundreds but in nine-syllable rising would be; by sixty-one-twenty of hundreds in ten-syllable.
