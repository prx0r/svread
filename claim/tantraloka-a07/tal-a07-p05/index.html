tal-a07-v41 — In word-mantra-syllable in cakra division śakti-principle-going; in words having made mantra-knower in japa and the rest would become fruit-holder.

tal-a07-v42 — Two-three-seven-eight-numbered should omit śakti-rising; thus śakti-abiding mantras vidyā or cakra-leaders.

tal-a07-v43 — By word-lump own form having known to be joined always dear; in eternal-rising in great-principle in rising-abiding in Sadāśiva.

tal-a07-v44 — Unjoined in śakti-path but not recited by rising who; they not succeed by effort recited by hundreds of millions even.

tal-a07-v45 — In garland-mantras in all mental recitation is said; or whispered śakti-rising of them not should arrange.

tal-a07-v46 — In word-mantras in all as far as that word-śakti-going is able; constantly by joined so much to be recited but by sādhakas.

tal-a07-v47 — So much in them indeed number in words called-word; so much rising having made from three-word-statement first in order.

tal-a07-v48 — In twelve-named in twelve cakra with-half hundred would be; rising that indeed with four-forty-hundred would be.

tal-a07-v49 — In sixteen-named in twelve in two-ninety-excess in hundred by four-half equal said; hundred twelve-excess.

tal-a07-v50 — In sixteen-named in sixteen would be eighty-four; rising two-hundreds that indeed fifty-six-excess.
