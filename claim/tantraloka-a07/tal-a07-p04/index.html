tal-a07-v31 — Knowing even without thought thought in object-domain; other-vibration not as long as that risen so long indeed he.

tal-a07-v32 — So long one thought would be various object imagining; who but thus not knowing of them thought does not arise.

tal-a07-v33 — He indeed one not would be someone even in three worlds ever; by word-attachment knowledge thought indeed is called.

tal-a07-v34 — She and would be sequential thus; what how who should think; pot even thus should be led would be thought; what story in state.

tal-a07-v35 — Not thought and someone is who abiding in measure and nonmeasure; not and knowledge-collection is from their non-simultaneity.

tal-a07-v36 — Thereby set indeed this transaction thought-born; therefore other-vibration as long as not rises so long one.

tal-a07-v37 — Knowledge that thought-self-attribute-apex even should touch; from eighty-one-place great-śakti-reflection-natured.

tal-a07-v38 — Thought Śiva-state-giving before indeed described; as ears I make dance thus by effort so would be.

tal-a07-v39 — From cakra movement going effort so that cakra indeed intellect; of japa homa worship and the rest from breath equality therefore rule.

tal-a07-v40 — In Siddha-doctrine Kuṇḍalinī-śakti breath-equal-transcendent said; and in Yoginī-Kaula that this by Supreme Lord.
