## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a15-v01** — When again, near death, a power-descent arises of himself or through a kinsman's mouth — then for him immediately perform the exit-initiation.
**tsa-a15-v02** — Having placed the whole path in the disciple and purified him in order, and placed the blessed Kālarātrī, the vein-cutter, by her, joint by joint — having split the vein-nooses, let him make the disciple's consciousness dwell at the brahmarandhra.
**tsa-a15-v03** — Then, by the aforesaid order, let him give the full-offering for yojanā's sake — so that at the full-offering the departed soul becomes one with Paramaśiva.
**tsa-a15-v04** — And for the desirer of enjoyment, the second full-offering.
**tsa-a15-v05** — In the place of enjoyment, for yojanā, and at that time in his jīvalaya — no remaining-turning here. Or let one recite Brahma-knowledge in the ear: she, of parāmarśa-nature, instantly makes vimarśa awakened in the consciousness of the already-awakened paśu.
**tsa-a15-v06** — Also in this recitation there is authority from samayya and the rest.
**tsa-a15-v07** — But if he shows a with-conviction, seedless initiation to a fool and to one whose power-descent has come — then at Śiva-hand-giving-time this rite: contemplating in the right hand a triangular, āgneya, flame-terrible, repha-spark, outward-vāta-wheel-rolling mandala, casting a little seed therein in the hand itself, by the up-down repha-revealed phaṭkāra successions he burns her generative-power. Doing thus, he casts that hand onto the disciple's head. Thus for both this seedless initiation destroys the capacity for doing one's own work — because even inanimate things are stated as initiatable. Let him contemplate the disciple installed inside the vāyupura, shaken, become-light — by which, by comparison, he is seen as light.
