tsa-a08-v65 — Stirring necessarily indeed in between to be assumed: thus proved Sāṃkhya-seen separated guṇa-principle.

tsa-a08-v66 — And that stirring from category-lord-presiding alone — otherwise toward fixed man, thus would not succeed.

tsa-a08-v67 — Then from guṇa-principle mind-principle, where man-light and object offer reflection.

tsa-a08-v68 — From mind-principle I-maker, by which in mind-mirrored object-contact turbid, in man-light, in non-self is self-pride, like silver-pride in shell.

tsa-a08-v69 — Hence indeed making-ness by this is said of it; Sāṃkhya's but that does not fit — for he does not wish self's I-feeling-nature; but we wish makership too of it.

tsa-a08-v70 — And that pure deliberation alone, non-rivalled, own-self-wonder-formed I — thus.

tsa-a08-v71 — This is of it, of I-maker: instrument-heap.

tsa-a08-v72 — But nature-heap of it alone is threefold, by lucidity-and-rest difference.

tsa-a08-v73 — There the lucid, from which mind and the five mind-senses — and there in mind, in the generable, endowed with all-subtle-birth power, it is the begetter.

tsa-a08-v74 — In ear, distinguished by sound-birth power — thus; up to nose, fit with smell-birth fitness — thus elemental is not fitting: since I hear and the rest following, clearly I-maker-ness; and by instrument-ness necessarily maker-part-touching-ness; otherwise, if another instrument is joined, from regress-and-rest befalling.

tsa-a08-v75 — And the maker-part is I-maker alone; by that two chief instruments: in knowing knowledge, in doing kalā — of the blind and of the lame; and from non-leaving of I-formed knowing-doing, from excelling in subtle-part, but from just the lucid I-maker, the five action-senses — I go, thus distinguished as I-maker, fit for effect-instrument; the foot-sense, its chief seat is outer, elsewhere too that is indeed — thus even of the crippled there is no going-break.

tsa-a08-v76 — And not from duty-confusion-freed alone, for the reason action is instrument-effected; and chief of goings and the rest is action-ness, not of form-and-rest grasping; of it, in Kaṇāda's system, since quality — hence necessarily to be admitted is the action-sense group.

tsa-a08-v77 — And it, the pentad, from so-much-ness of joining.

tsa-a08-v78 — Thus indeed: outside — first for giving up, or joining; for taking, or for both, or by both-less-ness for own-nature rest — there in order: anus, hand, foot, lap — thus.

tsa-a08-v79 — But within, in breath-seat-action joining, the speech-sense; by that, in sense-seat, in hand, whatever going, that too is foot-sense's alone doing — thus to be deemed; by that action-endlessness too does not bring sense-endlessness; to this extent of the rajasic clinging — thus they say.

tsa-a08-v80 — Others: from rajasic, mind — they say.

tsa-a08-v81 — Others: from lucid, mind; and from passionate, senses — thus.

tsa-a08-v82 — But from enjoyer-part-coverer, from darkness-ruled I-maker, the subtle elements, knowable-one-formed, five.

tsa-a08-v83 — Of sound-particulars, for stirring-natured, which one, unstirred-natured, pre-existent, common, undistinguished-natured — that is sound-subtle.

tsa-a08-v84 — Thus up to smell too is to be said.

tsa-a08-v85 — There from stirred sound-subtle: space-giving-business ether; of sound, since bearing word-superimposition-room.

tsa-a08-v86 — Sound-subtle stirred is wind; but sound of it is by ether from non-absence.

tsa-a08-v87 — Form stirred is fire; the two earlier as before.

tsa-a08-v88 — Taste stirred is waters; the earlier three as before.

tsa-a08-v89 — Smell stirred is earth; the earlier four as before.

tsa-a08-v90 — Others: by sound-touch, wind; in and-the-rest order, from five, ground — they think.

tsa-a08-v91 — Mere heap of qualities is earth; no other quality-bearer at all.

tsa-a08-v92 — And in this category-cluster, the higher-higher-qualified is pervading; the lower-qualified, pervaded.

tsa-a08-v93 — It alone is the excellence of quality: that without it, another quality does not arise; by that, earth-principle, from Śiva-principle beginning, is pervaded by water-principle — thus water by fire, and the rest, up to power-principle.
