## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a08-v18** — So far directly Śiva is maker; but the impure path Aghoreśa, also called Ananta, emits — by the Lord's will-sway, for the enjoyment-perfection of atoms wavered by craving.
**tsa-a08-v19** — There the waverer, not-full, other-natured form, throbbing, actionless, mere longing — will be future fitness for bounding, thus: not impurity of man, nor another category.
**tsa-a08-v20** — But passion-principle is longing bounded by action.
**tsa-a08-v21** — But action there is mere action; mind-nature is passion, varied by action-difference — thus the distinction will be told.
**tsa-a08-v22** — It is this impurity from the Supreme Lord's self-veiling wish — nothing else at all a thing; and that too by that Lord-wish-nature alone is the thingness of earth and the rest.
**tsa-a08-v23** — And it, impurity, existing in the knowledge-kevalin, facing destruction — thus does not fatten its own effect, action.
**tsa-a08-v24** — But of the dissolution-kevalin, merely yawning it has been — thus impurity-strengthened action is the cause in transmigration-variety enjoyment — thus for the enjoyment-perfection of atoms pierced by enjoyment-latency of that, glorious Aghoreśa emits — thus it is said fittingly: and the stirring of impurity is by Lord-wish-force alone, for of the inert by itself, anywhere at all, is inability.
**tsa-a08-v25** — An atom, indeed, is called mere shining of conscious-unconscious-form; of it conscious-form is lordliness alone, unconscious-formedness alone is impurity. And of it emitting, by Lord-wish-made, and thence always of the ever-to-be-emitted-thing-abiding form's to-be-shown-as-inert-ness, inert, all-effect-pervading-formed and pervasive is Māyā-called principle, the material cause; and showing it forth is the Supreme Lord's power named Māyā, quite other than that.
**tsa-a08-v26** — Thus of kalā-and-rest principles to earth-ended too two-formedness is to be described.
**tsa-a08-v27** — And here proof too have said Abhinavagupta's gurus in two-formedness.
**tsa-a08-v28** — Which in resolve shines, that separated too exists outside, with clear form — like a pot.
**tsa-a08-v29** — And thus of Māyā-kalā-and-sky-flowers and the rest too this very course — thus the purely affirmative reason.
**tsa-a08-v30** — And by this, those who enjoy knowing of direct-realization-form with Māyā-kalā-nature-mind and the rest as object — they too are perfected, perfected alone.
**tsa-a08-v31** — Thus standing, from Māyā-principle is world-birth.
**tsa-a08-v32** — And it though indeed non-sequential alone, yet by the stated view sequence appears — thus.
**tsa-a08-v33** — And that too is told: there per self the kalā-and-rest group is divided.
**tsa-a08-v34** — From its effect's makership-strengthening and the rest, by self-difference found — but that group sometimes might become one too, by the Lord's wish, like social selves — there all this kalā-and-rest group is pure, which by Lord-objectedness, fit for gaining its nature, doing own work, from world-opposition.
**tsa-a08-v35** — And it becomes thus by the Supreme Lord's power-descent sway — thus we shall tell in its showing.
**tsa-a08-v36** — But the impure is opposite of that.
**tsa-a08-v37** — There from Māyā kalā is born, which yokes the sleeping-placed atom with slight makership; and she, as if swelling, from the union of the two, Māyā and atom, though arisen deforms Māyā, not the undeformable atom — thus Māyā-effect-ness of her.
**tsa-a08-v38** — Thus from mutual clinging, indistinguishable inwardness of man-and-kalā.
