## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a08-v01** — Which this world-born glory-natured, said to womb endless varied enjoyer-enjoyed — there whatever pervades as great-light-formed, that is like the great common, Supreme-Śiva-formed.
**tsa-a08-v10** — Thus the free-awareness-natured Supreme Lord alone, world-thing-bodied, is the maker of pots and the rest — since potter-awareness, from not being greater, and the potter's body placed amid the thing-mass — how is the potter-body's maker-pride? Thus — if Lord-made even that will be, like pots and the rest.
**tsa-a08-v11** — Hence the assemblage-doctrine too strengthens makership of awareness alone, the world-bodied.
**tsa-a08-v12** — For a pot such as would be there on Meru would not be.
**tsa-a08-v13** — Thus in the constructed caused-ness, the many-typed-ness toward categories' cause-effect-ness in scriptures — that too fits — from dung, worms, from yogin's wish, from mantra, from herbs: like scorpion-arising.
**tsa-a08-v14** — There by own-system view we show the constructed.
**tsa-a08-v15** — There the Supreme Lord, full with five powers — thus said; he by freedom manifesting power after power chiefly stands fivefold.
**tsa-a08-v16** — Where awareness leads: Śiva-principle; where bliss leads: power-principle; where will leads: Sadāśiva-principle — for will, of knowing and doing, is of even-acceptance nature; where knowledge-power leads: Īśvara-principle; where action-power leads: knowledge-principle — thus.
**tsa-a08-v17** — And here the category-lords: Śiva, Śakti, Sadāśiva, Īśvara, Ananta — like Brahmā in withdrawal; of these common-formed, the particulars with pervasion for object are five — namely Śāmbhavas, Śāktas, Mantramāheśvaras, Mantreśvaras, Mantras — thus the pure path.
**tsa-a08-v02** — But whatever form pervading only some differences, that is a category — as earth called of glow-hardness-bulk-and-rest form, pervading all world-eggs ruled by world-lords from Kālāgni to Vīrabhadra.
**tsa-a08-v03** — There of these categories cause-effect-ness is shown; and it is twofold.
**tsa-a08-v04** — Ultimate, and of emission —
**tsa-a08-v05** — There the ultimate cause-effect-ness is this much: namely, of the agent-natured, free Lord, with such from-Śiva-to-earth-ended body, non-different in nature and resting in own nature — the showing-forth.
**tsa-a08-v06** — But the constructed cause-effect-ness, made by the Lord's will with necessity-breath: and as long as, when fixed before-after appearing, being even in the more, following own nature, only so far — by that from yogin's wish too, sprout from seed too, in dream and the rest, from pots and the rest, even thus.
**tsa-a08-v07** — And there too, of the Supreme Lord non-loss of agency — thus even unconstructed, that ultimate stands firm.
**tsa-a08-v08** — For with the ultimate wall-like standing form, all this drawn is fitting, not otherwise. Hence by the assemblage alone is caused-ness fitting.
**tsa-a08-v09** — For it, made of the weave of all beings, by free-awareness greatness, thus of fixed own place-time-thing-mass nature — each thing-nature completed, being thus; of being-thus, being-otherwise — as it increases, so the effect's other-kind-ness by degrees nourishes.
