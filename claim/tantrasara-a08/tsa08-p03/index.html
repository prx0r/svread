tsa-a08-v39 — Māyā-womb officers, but by some lord's grace, when all action ends, Māyā-man discrimination comes about — by which above Māyā sits the knowledge-kevalin; never ever below Māyā is kalā-man discrimination, by which one stands above kalā.

tsa-a08-v40 — Or nature-man discrimination, by which below the chief one would not transmigrate.

tsa-a08-v41 — But in impurity-man discrimination: Śiva-likeness.

tsa-a08-v42 — But when man's fullness is seen, Śiva-ness alone.

tsa-a08-v43 — Thus kalā-principle alone is slight-makership-giving, not —

tsa-a08-v44 — makership of the unknowing — thus.

tsa-a08-v45 — Slight-knowing-giving impure knowledge is born from kalā; and knowledge sees mind, and grasps its-lying parts and pleasures and the rest with discrimination.

tsa-a08-v46 — Of mind, of quality-confused form, with discrimination to grasp, from inability.

tsa-a08-v47 — Hence the mind-mirrored being is discerned by knowledge.

tsa-a08-v48 — Slight-makership, to perfect some part, somewhere alone makership — in this sense it comes down; but somewhere too in this part is passion-principle's business.

tsa-a08-v49 — Not from non-dispassion-made is that — even of non-dispassion, seeing no-attachment.

tsa-a08-v50 — In dispassion, even in duty and the rest, attachment is seen.

tsa-a08-v51 — Of the sated in food and the rest, though non-dispassion absent, from unremoved inner passion.

tsa-a08-v52 — By that, without again: from non-dispassion-non-arising contingency.

tsa-a08-v53 — And time, effect reckoning, reckons makership too bounded by that — in the like, in some-where-ness, this alone makership — thus in this sense is necessity's business.

tsa-a08-v54 — In cause-effect-ness too hers alone is the business; by that from kalā alone this fourfold is born — this very, slightly now knowing, clinging, I-do — thus-formed awareness, gone to body-city-eight and the rest, is called beast.

tsa-a08-v55 — That this Māyā-and-rest six is called six sheaths.

tsa-a08-v56 — Of awareness by Māyā, from being set aside, of kalā and the rest falling above, like sheaths from standing.

tsa-a08-v57 — Thus slight-makership which is Māyā-effect; there the slight, but, undistinguished which makership to be qualified; there engaged kalā is the cause of knowledge-and-rest birth — thus described.

tsa-a08-v58 — Now the qualifier part which is called slight is to be known, and the effect — and as long as she, kalā, makes apart from own self, so long this very, of enjoyment-particular interwoven with pleasure-pain-delusion-nature, of mere common, of that-guṇa-sameness-alias, of nature-principle is the emission — thus of enjoyer-enjoyed pair together dependent on kalā-principle is emission.

tsa-a08-v59 — Here, and of these by the real path, emission is sequence-barren — thus said; sequence-appearing too is, and-is-thus, too said indeed.

tsa-a08-v60 — And sequence of knowledge-passion and the rest, though varied, is seen — someone loving knows, who knowing is loved, and the rest.

tsa-a08-v61 — By that, describing differently, even in Raurava and other scriptures is uncontradicted to be deemed — but that very, become-stirred enjoyed-common: guṇa-principle.

tsa-a08-v62 — Where pleasure is enjoyed-form-light: lucidity; pain is light-unlight-swing-natured, hence very action-formed passion; delusion is unlight-formed darkness.

tsa-a08-v63 — The triad too, this enjoyed-formed.

tsa-a08-v64 — Thus from stirred chief comes to-be-done-arising, not from unstirred — thus.
