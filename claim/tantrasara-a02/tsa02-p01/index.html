tsa-a02-v01 — First of the four ways in: the way with no way. We shall expound it.

tsa-a02-v02 — When grace has struck firm, a person hears the teacher's word once — and then discriminates for himself. From that point his immersion needs nothing and never sets.

tsa-a02-v03 — Here "yoga-limb" means one thing only: thinking it through. But what is there to think? The Lord, who is self-shining, is yourself. So what could a means do? It can't get you your nature — you have it eternally. It can't inform you — you shine already. It can't strip a covering — there is none. It can't take you inside — there's no outsider to go in.

tsa-a02-v04 — And the means itself, as something separate from you, is equally impossible. So: all this is one reality, consciousness and nothing else — time can't measure it, place can't bound it, qualities can't fade it, shapes can't rule it, words can't point it out, proofs can't build it up. By its own will it is what lets everything from time to proof arrive at its nature; free, thick bliss; and that is I, with the universe mirrored inside me. Who sees this steadily is immersed in the Lord always, without means — no mantra binds him, no worship, no sitting, no observance.
