**tal-a13-v323** — The supreme teacher makes fruitful that which is above; even one below, seeing such, serving the teacher, may become he.

**tal-a13-v327** — Scriptural, thought-born, and meditation-born knowledge exist, but meditation-born knowledge beyond them is greater because it alone is the cause of liberation.
**tal-a13-v328** — How could one extract from principles or make connection at any time without supreme meditation-born knowledge?
**tal-a13-v329** — Even a yogi who has attained perfection in principles up to the highest place beginning with Sadashiva becomes a connector only through habituated knowledge.
**tal-a13-v330** — Even firm perfection born of yoga in lower principles is not a means for liberation, like a wealth-tree.
