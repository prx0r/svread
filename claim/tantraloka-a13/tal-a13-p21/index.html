tal-a13-v201 — Having said thus, he speaks again of the intense-intense topic — oneness with ignorance ceases for someone.

tal-a13-v206 — Abandoning such appearance, there is indeed no time at all — but from freedom, in such appearance, let time-power blossom.

tal-a13-v207 — But not for questioning is she, in Shiva by his greatness glorified — indeed the Shaiva great power, bound with selves, stands; true, covering-natured, but peaceful — this one self-nature-seeing.

tal-a13-v208 — Agitation indeed is division; oneness only — calmed, identified with that, then.

tal-a13-v209 — By that peace bound, situated, power-nature-sharing — abandoned-atom-state he becomes, Shiva, from that power-firmness.

tal-a13-v210 — There too, from gradation and the rest, quick slow and the rest — his body-fall would be; or log-etc. sameness.
