tal-a13-v245 — Much-little enjoyment-yoga and body-abundance-smallness course — in intense-mild, middle-mild, mild-mild, desire to enjoy.

tal-a13-v246 — In order chiefly, by rule to the end, Shiva — others' desire-to-go texts, prior-text-consistent.

tal-a13-v247 — They make middle-intense-named power-descent object — when intuition-entered even this, concord-joining.

tal-a13-v248 — Desiring desiring-to-go becomes, then would be led to true guru — not all intuition-entered by power would be led to true guru.

tal-a13-v249 — Thus speaks: going-desire-ness must be stated, not otherwise, certain — rudra-power-entered is led toward true guru.

tal-a13-v250 — Thereby attained-discrimination-born knowledge-complete mind — firmness-concord-growth-from, desiring-to-go becomes, clearly.
