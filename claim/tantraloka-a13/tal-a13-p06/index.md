**tal-a13-v58** — Destruction is not seen for eternal being whose birth is not cause-dependent; prior absence is nonexistent, so be it.
**tal-a13-v59** — Now its ripening is named boundness to own power toward all; so it would be obstructed power, like poison-fire.
**tal-a13-v60** — If power arises again, its own effect would arise like poison-fire; even liberated ones would not be liberated; we do not accept its power.
