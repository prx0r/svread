tal-a13-v351 — There unauthorized, but who, is called that other guru — as seated in that circle toward the other-circle king.

tal-a13-v352 — Own-circle desiring to conquer, fully serving, perishes — so higher-higher knowledge-siddhi desiring, resorting.

tal-a13-v353 — Lower-than-lower teacher goes to destruction — so indeed higher-abiding, from agama, siddhi-desiring.

tal-a13-v354 — Maya-scripture devoted attains destruction — and said in glorious Bliss: action, having resorted, from being.

tal-a13-v355 — Despises that, and in that resorts elsewhere to the fruitless — day by day decreasing thus, is cooked in Raurava and the rest.

tal-a13-v356 — Who upward-path seeking, lower guru-agama abandoned by descent — he blessed, made forward-facing.

tal-a13-v357 — Therefore indeed here, in scriptures, in Shaiva only is determined — toward other-scripture-meanings despairing, the purifying rule.

tal-a13-v358 — Therefore also supreme Shaiva — who elsewhere fallen, he indeed here to be graced; upward and upward, but of the guide, fallen sometimes.

tal-a13-v359 — Therefore indeed, for by omniscient Brahma-Vishnu-and-the-rest in own teaching, not handed down — linga-uprooting and the rest, anything.

tal-a13-v360 — Thus Vishnu-and-the-rest, Shaiva-ultimate-sole knowers — toward some, so initiated; from delusion, dissent resorted.
