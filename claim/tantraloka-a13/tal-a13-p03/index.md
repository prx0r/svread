**tal-a13-v21** — Of ignorance, how loss — in prior absence indeed of consciousness? Ignorance is that prior absence — not, from impossibility of future production.
**tal-a13-v22** — Why not future that knowledge — indeed from body-and-rest birth? Thus this all-party-destroyer, sharp-wheel whirling.
**tal-a13-v23** — Now destruction indeed is this ignorance, situated always — in liberated atoms thus; in them let Maya-effect expansion be.
**tal-a13-v24** — Now ignorance is not non-existence but false knowledge — their view; that very, of actions in own duty, is co-cause.
**tal-a13-v25** — What action should be declared, when it produces its own fruit, if causality is obtained only through false knowledge?
**tal-a13-v26** — Now, if false knowledge is truly present at the moment action is performed, what fruit comes from such action?
**tal-a13-v27** — In the prior view, after dissolution and at the beginning of creation, false knowledge has no possibility anywhere because body and the rest are absent.
**tal-a13-v28** — In the latter view, whenever and wherever action is done, all of it is accompanied by ignorance.
**tal-a13-v29** — Necessarily, for anyone, destruction of karma would not occur, even if, having become a knower, one performs some action.
**tal-a13-v30** — Action would be fruitless, but what course remains for the prior mass of karma, even at dissolution, through union with conscious nature?
