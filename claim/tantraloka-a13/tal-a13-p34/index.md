## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a13-v331** — He who has arisen with perfection of all paths is indeed Sadashiva; how would this directly present one liberate mortals while going?
**tal-a13-v332** — As said in the Malini Tantra, considering one joined with knowledge, the wise declare the possessor of habituated knowledge to be liberating.
**tal-a13-v333** — Therefore habituated knowledge alone is the one mark of a guru; this distinction, declared by me, is shown by Shambhunatha.
**tal-a13-v334** — One intent on liberation-knowledge should make the habituated knower guru and abandon even an already obtained other; thus it was said by Shiva.
**tal-a13-v335** — As a bee seeking fragrance goes from flower to flower, so a disciple seeking knowledge may go from guru to another guru.
**tal-a13-v336** — Having obtained a powerless guru, how could one rely on him for liberation-knowledge, O goddess? From a tree with destroyed root, whence flowers and fruits?
**tal-a13-v337** — Seeing higher-and-higher excellence-fortune, yet situated in the lowest — who in place? Therefore who else would be fate-burnt?
**tal-a13-v338** — But who enjoyment and liberation and desires knowledge only — self-practiced knower, yoga-accomplished: he should resort to guru.
**tal-a13-v339** — In that absence, resort to the knowledge-liberation knower — in enjoyment-share, the yogin who would be able to give that fruit.
**tal-a13-v340** — In fruit-giving-incapable, danger-only-teaching yogin — better the knower who shows means and would liberate.
