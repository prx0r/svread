tal-a13-v71 — If both are obstructed, birth and lifespan would not be; therefore the body would fall; if cessation is only of enjoyments, indeed?

tal-a13-v72 — If Shankara obstructs impurity in presence of karma giving birth and lifespan, what does he fear from enjoyment-giving karma?

tal-a13-v73 — Even gathering hundreds of states free from heat and cold, devotion-rasa immersion is not seen in abundance.

tal-a13-v74 — Even so, considering the greatness of time, the supreme lord acts thus; what kind is that time?

tal-a13-v75 — What of this beginningless enjoyment and beginningless karma with person? Hence enjoyment-cycle time is unequalled for all.

tal-a13-v76 — If there were a beginning for someone, variety would arise from that class; enjoyed by this and by this aeon, but the pair.

tal-a13-v77 — From so much enjoyment cycle there would be equality of karmas; by this method-seed I think the cause of variety.

tal-a13-v78 — The world's fixed karma does not fit thus; atoms covered by beginningless impurity are cognition-action-self.

tal-a13-v79 — How are all equal yet resort to variegated karma succession by enjoyment craving? Variegated indeed, whence?

tal-a13-v80 — If again beginningless karma impression variety is to be said, that same variety, whence of necessity and attachment?
