tal-a13-v41 — Therefore, even by Samkhya view, this ignorance is not fitted; without ignorance, bondage and liberation have no order.

tal-a13-v42 — They are fitted, but that is not established by stated reasons, even if fear, action, and gods' will exist.

tal-a13-v43 — There is no union of bondage and liberation because a difference cause is impossible; therefore by the word ignorance, knowership and agency natures are meant.

tal-a13-v44 — The covering of conscious atoms is something to be spoken by the wise, established as covering nature, non-different from its own form.

tal-a13-v45 — In difference and absence of proof, that one in all selves; whence is it produced? If from maya, how indeed is that?

tal-a13-v46 — Somewhere only, well-traversed not this; in liberated self thus this prior further question: would a cause be obtained if not?

tal-a13-v47 — From absence of origination by that, it is eternal and does not perish; from that, in oneness only, and from commonality of other self.

tal-a13-v48 — Not from being cause of a real thing, not from concealment of mind, and not union of selves by this self, because of its impossibility.

tal-a13-v49 — Therefore one thing, always bound with selves, inert impurity, that ignorance, is the cause of samsara's sprout.

tal-a13-v50 — When its obstructing power rests, by Shiva's rays the soul is touched, and its own knowledge and action become clear.
