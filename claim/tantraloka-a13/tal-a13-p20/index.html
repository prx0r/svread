tal-a13-v191 — Knowledge is not attained without mind and intellect, dear one; from the supreme state that subtle power principle is called.

tal-a13-v192 — Discrimination of all states arises from pure state in the great soul; the intellect principle has three gunas: high, low, and middle.

tal-a13-v193 — Gone to anima and the rest, binding too, inert-sensed — surely from intuition, in liberation, by initiation: what, in Shiva-rite?

tal-a13-v194 — Said knowledge indeed: in initiation, children fools women — from bond-cutting freed, awakened in Shiva-rite.

tal-a13-v195 — Therefore initiation becomes among these as cause, O beautiful one — but by initiation, in bond-liberation, from pure state, discrimination-born.

tal-a13-v196 — Thus this read text — who by themselves unable to understand, their, from concord with Shiva-words, understanding may go to firmness.

tal-a13-v197 — In glorious night-attack, and by self-guru-scripture-power threefold — knowledge principal: self-perception, ferrying across the conceptual ocean.

tal-a13-v198 — Doubt with mantra-self-become-substance hope, divine-tattva-etc. scope, rooted in concepts — ceases by own conviction.

tal-a13-v199 — Having grasped this inner-situated meaning — in Malini view, thus of this self, at some time from fitness.

tal-a13-v200 — The Shaiva power is bound, peaceful, liberation-fruit-giving — by that bond, then someone that moment is released.
