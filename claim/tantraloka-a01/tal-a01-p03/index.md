## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a01-v21** — Examine this heart-lotus of Abhinavagupta, whose contraction has gone through the radiance of falling at the feet of Shri Shambhunatha sun, for the worship of Mahesha.
**tal-a01-v22** — Here the first statement sung in all scriptures is: ignorance is the cause of samsara, knowledge is the only cause of liberation.
**tal-a01-v23** — They desire impurity-ignorance as the cause of samsara's sprout; thus it is declared in Shri Malinivijayottara.
**tal-a01-v24** — By qualification established in mind, after samsara, removing possibility in its absence, liberation is declared.
**tal-a01-v25** — Ignorance is not just the absence of knowledge, because that would apply too widely. For such absence exists even in a clod of earth, yet a clod does not undergo transmigration.
**tal-a01-v26** — Therefore, ignorance is knowledge itself that fails to manifest the knowable reality in its fullness; this is what has been taught in the Shiva Sutras.
**tal-a01-v27** — In the two sutras, 'Consciousness is the Self' and 'Knowledge is bondage,' the intended meaning is shown through their mutual relation of connection and separation.
**tal-a01-v28** — The word 'consciousness,' by its form, always signifies sheer freedom without implying any particular distinction, as used in the ancient sutra.
**tal-a01-v29** — But the second sutra, by speaking of action or instrument, introduces duality into that which is essentially mere consciousness.
**tal-a01-v30** — The manifestation of duality is ignorance; because it is insubstantial, it is called bondage, and for that very reason it is shown to be something that must be completely cut off.
