## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a01-v81** — The great lords of mantra, the lords of mantra, the mantras headed by Shiva, and the two akala classes together with the sakala class are all powers of Shiva himself.
**tal-a01-v82** — The unfailing essential nature of the whole group of principles is the Self alone, for as the revered Trishiro doctrine states, the Self is their very nature.
**tal-a01-v83** — By the term 'group of principles' is meant that which stands in the heart, in all bodies, and in its own nature, which is very subtle, and which is the collective totality of the principles.
**tal-a01-v84** — The Self alone is said to be the essential nature: flooded with the nectar of Shiva, it is knowledge abiding in light, beyond the midst of being, nonbeing, and the like.
**tal-a01-v85** — Abiding in one's own place should be understood as being the seer, free of covering, purified by the pure insight that reveals the distinct reality.
**tal-a01-v86** — When the functioning of the group-nature is understood, everything is accomplished; abandoning the lower and entering the higher, one becomes established in delight in the middle region.
**tal-a01-v87** — Movement and rest, dream and waking, opening and closing, running and leaping, effort, and the feeling of power are all included.
**tal-a01-v88** — The divisions of intellect, states, designations, and manifold actions are all pervaded here by that delight, which is Shiva, the supreme cause.
**tal-a01-v89** — One whose mind is worn down by impurity can, through the mere restraint of memory, meditate on the supreme object of meditation that is present within the state of going and coming.
**tal-a01-v90** — One reaches the supreme Shiva, called Bhairava, even through repetition; but true repetition is of that very nature, having fallen away from the states of being and nonbeing.
