## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a01-v231** — In the end, within knowledge accompanied by means, the entire expanse of action will be included in a very clear form.
**tal-a01-v232** — Action is not something other than knowledge; it is knowledge that has taken on a sequential form through the force of means, and this was stated earlier as action.
**tal-a01-v233** — Right knowledge, abiding in both self and other, is the sole cause of liberation, because the divisions of self, other, and so on are nothing more than constructions.
**tal-a01-v234** — Even when all things are equally constructed, a teacher whose nature is the flashing forth of unity is called one-natured, accomplished, and liberated.
**tal-a01-v235** — As far as that continuum extends, it is declared to be the teacher; and because it is made of right knowledge, one is liberated by one's own self through it.
**tal-a01-v236** — For that very reason the knower carries his own continuum across; this is well established by reason and scripture, because to that extent the sage is one.
**tal-a01-v237** — Therefore some object here: if liberation comes from knowledge, how could initiation and other ritual actions bring about liberation?
**tal-a01-v238** — If action is of the nature of knowledge, the objectors ask whether knowledge liberates only where it is located and what happens in liberating another; but they are dismissed at the root, and the text turns to what others have established.
**tal-a01-v239** — Some say impurity is a substance, like a film over the eye, and initiation is the action that removes it, like applying collyrium.
**tal-a01-v240** — We will refute that view later, since it is condemned by reason and scripture, and we will also show the true status of impurity, māyā, and karma.
