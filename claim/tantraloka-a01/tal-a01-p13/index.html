tal-a01-v126 — For the self-manifest there is no injunction prior; indeed deity-creation has arisen from power-cause.

tal-a01-v127 — But awareness is eternal and self-manifest in I-form; injunction is three-part command, and meditation is injunction-nature.

tal-a01-v128 — Indra and other deities are accomplished through injunction, but I-knowledge is not so; they take knowable form.

tal-a01-v129 — Seeing him only submerged, even knowing, they do not know; as said, they do not know me truly, therefore they wander.

tal-a01-v130 — But wandering is only attainment of limited form, as in 'gods and sacrificers go,' and by that it was described.
