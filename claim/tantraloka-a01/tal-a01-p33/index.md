**tal-a01-v329** — Thus the method of enunciation has been declared for the sake of easy comprehension. Now, in examining its characteristics, they will be explained in due order.
**tal-a01-v330** — The self abides as partless consciousness-light, and its own nature is the host of powers of consciousness. It appears bound by concealing its own greatness, and its liberation comes through revealing its luminous nature. This manifold meaning has been briefly revealed here.
