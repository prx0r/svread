tal-a01-v321 — The divisions of festivals, their specifics, circle worship and its performance, and the purpose of worship on guru days and other special beginning and end days are to be explained.

tal-a01-v322 — The examination of death, the procedure for gatherings of yoginīs and others, the method of exposition, the method of hearing, and the procedure for guru worship are to be described.

tal-a01-v323 — In the twenty-eighth chapter, called the revelation of occasional rites, are established the division of the qualified practitioner and the sequence of the lineage of siddhas and their consorts.

tal-a01-v324 — In the twenty-ninth chapter are the procedures of worship and of the messenger, the sequence of the secret upaniṣad, initiation, consecration, and awakening.

tal-a01-v325 — In the thirtieth chapter, the nature of mantra and its power are explained. In the thirty-first, the division of the trident and lotus and the explanation of the sky, the swastika, and so on are to be set forth in detail.

tal-a01-v326 — These matters are to be explained in detail in the thirty-first chapter. In the thirty-second chapter are set forth the distinctions of quality and principality, their own nature, and the worship of their power.

tal-a01-v327 — The division of kalā is declared in the full revelation of the seals, because from the thirty-two principles up to Īśa it becomes clearly manifest there.

tal-a01-v328 — Since there is no separate division here, no additional topic is stated in this overview. Even so, that matter is treated because it is primary and must be known in the sequence of another section.

tal-a01-v329 — Thus the method of enunciation has been declared for the sake of easy comprehension. Now, in examining its characteristics, they will be explained in due order.

tal-a01-v330 — The self abides as partless consciousness-light, and its own nature is the host of powers of consciousness. It appears bound by concealing its own greatness, and its liberation comes through revealing its luminous nature. This manifold meaning has been briefly revealed here.
