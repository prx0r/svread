tal-a01-v261 — Because each newly determined aspect of a property becomes the basis for what follows, examination proceeds again and again in the form of indication and definition.

tal-a01-v262 — In perception, inference, comparison, trustworthy testimony, and the rest, the triad of indication, definition, and examination shines forth in the cognition of all living beings.

tal-a01-v263 — Indication is non-conceptual, definition is conceptual construction, and examination is a further series of conceptual constructions, even within direct perception.

tal-a01-v264 — Saying 'this is a mountain' is indication; saying 'it has fire because there is smoke' is definition; and the whole network of knowledge about pervasion and the like is examination.

tal-a01-v265 — This enunciation is like a cow; the remainder becomes a definition in examination.

tal-a01-v266 — Such an enunciation, with its own desire, defines the sacrifice command; agnishtoma and the remainder are examined.

tal-a01-v267 — The creation of a thing, tolerating unwanted parts, is called enunciation.

tal-a01-v268 — Then only does one discern; future creation, arranged timelessly, connects all time.

tal-a01-v269 — The creation of what will be created and withdrawal of the previously created part, when duty is declared, is called the mark of consciousness.

tal-a01-v270 — The examination resting from creation and withdrawal is said, with a mother-approved fixed limit.
