## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a01-v71** — In the highest sense, Shiva is nothing other than manifestation itself. There is no real thing apart from that shining, and there is no ultimately real difference between his power and the one who possesses it.
**tal-a01-v72** — Whatever brings forth the surge of a reality's own power is not different from that reality, because it is identical with it. That is power, and in this way the Goddess appears with another form even while remaining the same reality.
**tal-a01-v73** — Shiva, whose majesty never fades, appears as if created within the sprout of his own awareness, through his freedom in acts such as contemplation.
**tal-a01-v74** — Therefore, although the reality is partless, it shines through particular aspects; that aspect is called its power, yet the power is the reality itself, so the sequence of power and possessor is only an apparent distinction.
**tal-a01-v75** — In the revered Kirana scripture, through a question-and-answer discussion, it is stated that neither experience nor mental conceptual thought exists in Shiva, for mind itself is not there.
**tal-a01-v76** — To the question, 'How can initiation happen without knowing Shiva?', the answer is that it is not through ordinary experiences like hunger, nor through mental conceptual thought.
**tal-a01-v77** — Just as one can recognize a tree from its form even without perceiving its taste and other qualities, so too one can come to know Shiva through indicators such as nada, bindu, and the like.
**tal-a01-v78** — Shiva is said to have manifold powers, and from that arises the vast expansion of divisions such as kala, tattva, body, syllable, atom, word, and so on.
**tal-a01-v79** — Creation, maintenance, concealment, withdrawal, grace, and even the fourth state are all the unfolding of the Lord's manifold powers.
**tal-a01-v80** — Waking, dream, deep sleep, the fourth, and even what lies beyond them are all waves in the abundant freedom of that Lord.
