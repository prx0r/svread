tal-a01-v111 — He has a ninefold nature, the power of the ten directions, and an elevenfold nature; thus Bhairava is the lord of the great twelve-spoked wheel.

tal-a01-v112 — Thus, in the wheel of the universe—whether thousand-spoked or even uncountably spoked—the Lord, the great sovereign whose power is the universe, expands.

tal-a01-v113 — Even among those wheels, the differences belonging to each wheel are explained, case by case, through a unity that follows the nature of its own group.

tal-a01-v114 — In the Traiśirasa doctrine, by the reckoning of four, six, two, and two, the Lord—whose own form is wondrous—is said to have sovereignty over six wheels.

tal-a01-v115 — There, the names of the goddesses of the wheel are determined according to differences of function and by etymological conception of the yoginīs associated with gentle or fierce forms and meditations.

tal-a01-v116 — For the one lord of consciousness, the inner body is indeed creative intuition; as gentle or in another measured form, the wheel of waves of consciousness is worshipped.

tal-a01-v117 — With the intention, 'May this be nourished,' the goddess-consciousness is nourished by such meditation mixed with articulate expression, and also by external activity.

tal-a01-v118 — When it becomes clear, it shines and grants him a corresponding fruit. Nourishment is like a dry place becoming a lake; therefore there is clear water.

tal-a01-v119 — Then, having followed that, meditation centered on it is extended; and there are phonemes which by their own nature flow with essence, as follows.

tal-a01-v120 — Those phonemes, mostly dental, labial, and dental, formed together with certain other sounds, attain the state of a seed and make that consciousness clear.
