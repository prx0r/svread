tal-a01-v301 — In the examination of the student's fitness and related matters, the conception of places divided by location, the division of general nyāsa, the offering vessel, and their purpose are covered.

tal-a01-v302 — The fitness of materials, worship, outer-door worship, sequential entry, the nature of initiation, and the purification of body, breath, and so on are outlined.

tal-a01-v303 — The variety of special nyāsa, the special offering vessel, worship of the body, and nyāsa and worship on the paths of breath, intellect, and consciousness are described.

tal-a01-v304 — The superiority over other scriptural groups, worship of the wheel on all sides, taking possession of the site, the five cow products, and worship of the earth and Gaṇeśa are included.

tal-a01-v305 — Worship of the weapon mantra, the fire rite, consecration related to fire, oblations of satisfaction, preparation of the rice offering, and rites ending with the tooth-stick are treated.

tal-a01-v306 — The rite of Śiva's hand, preparation of the bed, rites relating to dreams, and the observances are summarized.

tal-a01-v307 — In the fifteenth chapter, on the rite of becoming an initiate, contemplation of the self of the maṇḍala and the elaboration of the offered victim are covered.

tal-a01-v308 — Satisfaction of the fire, illumination of one's own nature, the rite of nyāsa on the paths present in the student's body, and the variety of what is purified, the purifier, and so on are treated.

tal-a01-v309 — In the sixteenth chapter, the division of initiation, the supreme nyāsa, the purpose of the being of mantra, and the division of yojanikā and related matters are presented.

tal-a01-v310 — In the rite of the son's initiation, the preparation of the thread, purification of the principles, burning of the bonds, application, and the division of the paths are stated.
