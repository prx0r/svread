tal-a01-v141 — Indeed, manifestation of world-nature as one self-form is supreme knowledge for atoms; other knowledge is inferior and much.

tal-a01-v142 — That, manifesting by direct means and by that means etc., differs here by various breaks.

tal-a01-v143 — There also, because self-other door-gate-ness is partial, greater difference arises by obstruction and non-obstruction.

tal-a01-v144 — The means of knowledge is not called ignorance; knowledge only is subtle, supreme, and of will-nature, according to opinion.

tal-a01-v145 — In knowledge, the distinction between means and goal is a coarse level. This very same thing is the power of action, which alone causes both bondage and liberation.

tal-a01-v146 — In the first mode, which is self-awareness resting in a single non-conceptual abode, whatever flashes forth clearly and directly is called will.

tal-a01-v147 — Just as an object can appear clearly to someone whose perception has flashed forth, even without any deliberate mental connection, so too for some people the state of being Śiva shines forth directly.

tal-a01-v148 — But when recognition does not come immediately, they call it the means of knowledge: through repeatedly working with conceptual elements in a steady sequence of certainty, one comes to reflective awareness.

tal-a01-v149 — But what is taught as the means of action works by accomplishing externalized objects shaped by conceptual thought. Because it remains within difference, it does not by itself lead to liberation.

tal-a01-v150 — For in the teaching of the revered scripture, there is no action separate from knowledge. Knowledge itself, when firmly established in that way, reaches the culmination of yoga.
