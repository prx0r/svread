tal-a01-v331 — False knowledge produces defects of vision like a cataract, so that even what is pure seems to be a place of impurity. But when that cataract-like defect in vision is recognized and removed, how could any suspicion of impurity remain?

tal-a01-v332 — O host of beings, you forcibly seize people's hearts, make them dance with many gestures, conceal the heart of the self, and sport in that way. Whoever calls you insentient is himself insentient and poorly taught in thinking himself sensitive; I would even take his insentience as a kind of praise, since it suggests a likeness to you.

tal-a01-v333 — Here, teachers whose impurities have fallen away, who know the higher and the lower, and who are grounded in the true being of Shiva are authorized to undertake investigation. Without that qualification, inquiry is fruitless and leads only to hatred, blame, loss, and begging.
