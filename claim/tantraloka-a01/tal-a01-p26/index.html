tal-a01-v251 — Again, doubt arises in the 'what' aspect because two forms that could be reflected on remain unopened; conceptual construction, by contrast, is clearly different.

tal-a01-v252 — Therefore, the first manifestation of a being whose nature is not yet unfolded is here called indication, question, and indeed doubt.

tal-a01-v253 — As self-awareness expands along the path by which forms and beings that were not yet fully revealed come forth, it becomes the questioner and takes on the condition of the disciple.

tal-a01-v254 — Because the certainty of inner reflective awareness is then concealed, the questioner also partakes in the rise of withdrawal that comes immediately after expansion.

tal-a01-v255 — To whatever extent there is a clearly manifest outward expansion in which her own form has not yet fully opened, to that same extent she is called the questioner.

tal-a01-v256 — In this way, awakening itself takes the form of question and answer; so even in the roles of teacher and disciple, the difference of bodies is not ultimately real.

tal-a01-v257 — Because awareness is of the nature of awareness, it makes the many forms that abide within it appear outwardly, just as both the general and specific features of a grape are manifested.

tal-a01-v258 — When some property is projected only in a general way, while a further specific aspect is still awaited, that general projection has the nature of doubt.

tal-a01-v259 — When the awaited specific aspect finally settles, determination arises; it comes from the inclination of the cognizing subject, and not in any other way, not even through countless imagined alternatives.

tal-a01-v260 — Then, because of the skillful force with which that object is appropriated in its own nature, it opens up; and by that act arise determinations that follow upon its definition.
