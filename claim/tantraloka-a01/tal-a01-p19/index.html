tal-a01-v181 — This has already been answered, for what has been grasped is then followed by conceptual thought. Determinate cognition shines forth very clearly, so the objection does not stand.

tal-a01-v182 — By the force of unity with the nonconceptual sense of I grasp, the object is apprehended. In nonconceptual self-awareness, it is simply the flashing forth of the object that is known.

tal-a01-v183 — That flashing forth is not established by conceptual thought, since conceptual thought does not directly depend on the object. It belongs only to awareness, which rests in its own clarity or lack of clarity.

tal-a01-v184 — Both independence from and dependence on conceptual thought can occur in the same self. For example, even at night a jewel expert may see gems revealed only for the brief moment of a lightning flash.

tal-a01-v185 — He can discern the particular features of many jewels. If awareness is clear, this happens through the force of prior practice.

tal-a01-v186 — This will be discussed later as arising from the will of the unrestricted Lord. The immersion has been described as having fifty forms.

tal-a01-v187 — This division is based on the clear distinctions within the thirty-six principles. Among them is a triad made of self, knowledge, and power.

tal-a01-v188 — In order, the triad called self, knowledge, and power is divided into four, ten, and three forms. This is because that triad is all-pervading.

tal-a01-v189 — So this triad is counted in distinction from the nonpervading principles. It also stands in mutual relation through impurity, the process of purification, and purity.

tal-a01-v190 — The elements are established by direct perception and can also be inferred from cause and effect. For that reason they are treated as separate from the group of principles.
