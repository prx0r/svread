tal-a14-v21 — And from renewed grace, expiation-and-the-rest being performed: accordingly, in initiation and the rest performed, he would be Śiva-natured.

tal-a14-v22 — Concealed — even the departed-breathed: kin, friends, teachers having resorted to, through śaktipāta, through initiations is graced.

tal-a14-v23 — There too, diversity of quick-slow-time and the rest: so comes śaktipāta — it, by which he comes to Śiva-nature.

tal-a14-v24 — Thus creation, maintenance, dissolution, concealment, grace: in the five, doership is Śiva-hood, of consciousness-self.

tal-a14-v25 — Proud of five-act-freedom-complete-selfhood: the yogin's worship-japa-meditation-yogas, let them stand united, ever-risen.

tal-a14-v26 — In magic-show business one should never delight: even Sadāśiva — which enjoyment is bondage — that too, for the unfit.

tal-a14-v27 — Knowerness alone is Śiva-hood, is freedom: that is here called; like a potter, but, doership is not primary, from His superintendence.

tal-a14-v28 — Thus having known, to be grasped, never at all, ever, refutation: 'I am Śiva' — if following my will, why is not the world — thus the doubt.

tal-a14-v29 — 'Let them follow my will' — here in the I-knower, may it flash: Śiva, or the Supreme Lord — body and the rest, then, fashioned.

tal-a14-v30 — Of Śiva, for now is this — the body — but this, so fashioned by you: what other bodiness of his? Then what would be a speakable state?
