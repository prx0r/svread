tal-a14-v31 — Told, and, in the Siddhasantāna venerable Ūrmi-Mahākula: wind-wandering-breath-scattering-and-the-rest-made toilers.

tal-a14-v32 — In frauds and the rest, who deluded — they: deluded in the supreme state; everywhere with honor which even departure — for liberation.

tal-a14-v33 — Taught, she in the essence-śāstras, as enjoyment-means arisen: if all-pervading is God — tell! having departed, where will he go?

tal-a14-v34 — But if not all-pervading, then pot-like then he would be: the departure-rite-yoga — this partially — is taught.

tal-a14-v35 — In the partless Śiva-principle, but, how departure-fittingness? As from earth-and-the-rest to wind-end, by cliff-water-fire fasts.

tal-a14-v36 — Of the self joining in the void, likewise departure-procedure: therefore one should not make the soul depart out of longing for the supreme principle.

tal-a14-v37 — But in the venerable Pūrvaśāstra, whatever of departure the mark was told: not that, as a means to liberation, but for loss of enjoyment, from such desiring.

tal-a14-v38 — Perfected by japa-meditation-and-the-rest, through freedom, from śaktipāta: toward enjoyment detached, if thus: the body should abandon — quoted.

tal-a14-v39 — Even of self-willed death, what from Bhīṣma onward is heard, indeed: on arrival at enjoyment-distaste, approaching life's end.

tal-a14-v40 — By yoga-mantra-nectar-substance-boon-and-the-rest, a siddhi-partaking body: to abandon — for not otherwise is possible without the stated-sequence-practice.
