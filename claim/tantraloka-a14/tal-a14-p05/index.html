tal-a14-v41 — Told, and, in the Mālinītantra, by the Supreme Lord, likewise: all — even, or else — enjoyment deeming ugly.

tal-a14-v42 — Thus-and-the-rest, by one who says: by all, the inner-reality-unseen reality, teaching of one's own freedom in creation-and-the-rest duties.

tal-a14-v43 — Which very chief initiation should be of the disciple, Śiva-giving: told in the venerable Niśicāra, and by Bhairavī energy, radiance.

tal-a14-v44 — Pervaded, the universe they behold, the thought-abandoned-minded; but the thought-joined-minded: from body's fall, Śiva reaches.

tal-a14-v45 — By outer-initiation-and-the-rest-practice, through conduct-samaya-constructs, thought-free just so now indeed: living-liberated, no doubt.

tal-a14-v46 — By thought made like the root-mass of the world-worn-out tree, inwardly, of the ultimate fire: may there be spark-fragments; even if at its end, blazes intensely the pure-awakening-fire-mass.

tal-a14-v47 — Thus the initiation-approach — this, shown, scripturally approved.
