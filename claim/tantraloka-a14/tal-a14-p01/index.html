tal-a14-v01 — Now the nature of concealment, as it is told, let it be examined: from own nature the Supreme Lord touches the rule-bound and rule-free order.

tal-a14-v10 — Who on your teaching-path, initiated, falling through delusion into dissent: surely of them there is not your sun-appointment — can lotuses contract from sunbeams?

tal-a14-v02 — Touching, he shines forth; thereby he is called self-willed: having resorted to the rule of action and fruit — this Great Lord.

tal-a14-v03 — He ordains creation, preservation, destruction — the intermediate states; and again, in the great creation, in the endlessness of creation and destruction.

tal-a14-v04 — One, that God, universe-self, the Lord arrived at abandoning rule; and whatever intermediate creation — and stasis here too is unforced.

tal-a14-v05 — He does not abandon the form; niyati-abandoned, standing here: that. But by niyati alone, when, through the self-concealing procedure.

tal-a14-v06 — He enjoys suffering, delusion and the rest; then the action-fruit order — but having abandoned the karmic restraint, enveloped in suffering-delusion.

tal-a14-v07 — Desiring to shine forth, sits this one, in concealment disregardful; as from light's freedom, the awakened like the unawakened.

tal-a14-v08 — So sits, uncrossed yet as-if-crossed, behaving; as the awake one her fool-conduct performing even while hating it.

tal-a14-v09 — In the heart sits the fool — thus indeed of the awakened is the conduct; and the venerable Vidyā-lord said, in the Mānastotra, such as this.
