**tal-a14-v11** — Known-and-knowable, standing in the creator's place though being, who going wrong from your path, they too are not right: mostly of them, arisen from emblem-mind-and-the-rest, false awakening like snake-fat lamplight.
**tal-a14-v12** — Since pierced, with lead foremost: now copper — that which again to its own nature would not return; nor drunk by earth-dwellers, that nectar of whom thirst-hunger-suffering-pains again herein.

**tal-a14-v14** — Nor is it his past action's might, such that thereby thus he has come; indeed, what would that action be? Or why here would the procedure be the same as with the earlier?
**tal-a14-v15** — Hence that, the Lord's will, by which deluded, so, endless-time-felt suffering-vesselhood he seeks.
**tal-a14-v16** — There too, if through will-variety, both-here-and-beyond-natured: of suffering too there is division, made slow-or-swift, so.
**tal-a14-v17** — Of Kāla, Kāma, Andhaka and the rest, of Paulastya-city dwellers, and of others: concealment — so much suffering, indeed hereafter too.
**tal-a14-v18** — Another too is concealment, consisting in samaya-violation: which was said by the Lord in the venerable Ānandagahvara.
**tal-a14-v19** — From samaya-violation, O Goddess: carrion-eaterhood for a hundred years; there too, through mild-intense-and-other-divisions, manifold is the course.
**tal-a14-v20** — And through the Great Lord's freedom, though concealed, this one himself: through another's door, or comes back — again grace too, amply.
