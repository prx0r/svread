mpa-p01-v01 — Homage to the world-formed, whose form is beyond the world — to the ever-divine-shining, to the self of unending power.

mpa-p01-v10 — Waking, dreaming, deep sleep and the rest — holding them as your own rays. By your own will you shine like an actor, and yet you are partless in truth.

mpa-p01-v02 — You alone are you; I alone am I. You alone are; I am not at all. As for you and me both being — where is there homage, homage?

mpa-p01-v03 — Inside the body, sought by me always — you as self, and the self is never seen. Whatever is seen is you alone, that.

mpa-p01-v04 — Of your devotee who well knows your form, now again: having seen you as the self's form — to you homage, to me homage.

mpa-p01-v05 — What finesse of going is there, what should be done, thus, from the root? Of yours, made of your magic power — by what, in what, from where is the dissolving?

mpa-p01-v06 — I, you; you, I — thus there is no duality of us anywhere. Only the wish to grasp samādhi: that is what the abiding of difference is.

mpa-p01-v07 — You, I, and so on — so endless, ever in you, they find no room. Words, then — whence the world?

mpa-p01-v08 — Enough of difference-talk. From chewing the sap of devotion to you — all this is one, at peace, though even to say so is shy.

mpa-p01-v09 — When your own form expands — you and I and the whole world. When its withdrawal is born — neither you, nor I, nor indeed the world.
