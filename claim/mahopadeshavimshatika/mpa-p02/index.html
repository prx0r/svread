mpa-p02-v16 — Food-offering for the ever-content, betel for the Lord — whence? Circumambulation for the endless, obeisance for the non-dual — whence?

mpa-p02-v18 — How is there sending-off for the full, inside and out? And how is there oblation for the difference-free, everywhere?

mpa-p02-v20 — Just so, indeed, is the highest worship, in all states, always: with nondual mind, yoke the mind to God as Lord of all.
