tal-a37-v57 — Mother supreme relative thus saying; affection intensifies indeed bonds; in root bond in dissolved indeed of him I think; standing of living indeed liberation.

tal-a37-v58 — By father he in word jungle; made entry logic ocean wave drop purified mind; literature dense taste enjoyment devoted; by Maheśa devotion by self grasping pride taken.
