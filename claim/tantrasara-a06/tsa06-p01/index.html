tsa-a06-v01 — It itself is called by the word seat-fixing. There the seat is threefold — breath-wind, body, and the outer. There first the method in breath: all that course to be stated is reckoned as resting in breath; of it, counting in sequence and non-sequence alone is time. And it shines within the Supreme Lord alone; its shining is the God's power named Kālī; but with division, its appearing in sequence and non-sequence is breath-movement.

tsa-a06-v10 — Two fingers and a quarter is called tuṭi; in four of them a watch; half tuṭi, half tuṭi — there twilight. Thus in going out day, in coming in night: thus the lunar-day rise.

tsa-a06-v12 — There day is the dark fortnight, night the bright. There the first half-tuṭi and the last half-tuṭi are untimed rest; but the middle fifteen alone are tuṭis, lunar days. There light is rest and — thus these alone are day and night.

tsa-a06-v13 — There the light of knowable-ness is day; of the knowable in the dissolver, dissolution is night. And they — light and rest, through quick-slow variety in endless difference; in sameness with that, equinox.

tsa-a06-v14 — There in the dark fortnight, in the breath-sun, the in-breath-moon offers each single fattening digit, until on the fifteenth, in the tuṭi near the twelve-end, the moon whose digit-spread has thinned and separated dissolves into the breath-sun alone.

tsa-a06-v15 — Right after that, what half-tuṭi there is — that is the fortnight-joint.

tsa-a06-v16 — And of it, of the half-tuṭi, the eastern half is new-moon, the second first-day.

tsa-a06-v17 — There on the first-day, in that part, when, from cough-effort-attention-made tithi-cutting, it enters — then there is seizure. And there the Māyā-knower-Rāhu, co-born with the object-moon — by nature unable to dissolve, merely capable of covering — drinks the lunar nectar gone to the sun.

tsa-a06-v02 — For consciousness itself takes a form apart from its objects; hence indeed, through being bounded, going to knowability: the void. Then through freedom, falling on the object with eagerness to seize, action-power-led, breathing-formed, living-natured — since with five forms it fills the body, then it shines as if conscious.

tsa-a06-v03 — There in power, action is the time-course; in the eastern part, but in the northern, of varied form, is the place-course; there, of letter, mantra, and word courses, the standing in time-course, through beyond-subtle-gross-formedness.

tsa-a06-v04 — But the standing of the place-course, as tattva-person-ray-natured — thus it will be in its own place.

tsa-a06-v05 — There, though even in the body, with outer and inner, breath is warp-and-woof-formed — yet of distinctly sensible effort, that, from the heart beginning — thus from that alone this is to be described.

tsa-a06-v06 — There lord-power, self-power, and effort — thus a triad — are the cause in driving breath, through secondary-primary states.

tsa-a06-v07 — There, from the heart to the twelve-end's end, by one's own fingers, of all is the thirty-six-fingered breath-course, in going out and in coming in, since of each is fitted strength, effort, and body.

tsa-a06-v08 — There the water-clock, the lunar day, the month, the year, and as year-group-natured — thus the entire time is completed. There, in the finger and a fifth, the finger, caṣaka — thus by the rule is the ghaṭikā-rise; for a ghaṭikā is by sixty caṣakas; hence seventy-two-fingered it is.
