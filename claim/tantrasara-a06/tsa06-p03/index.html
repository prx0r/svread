tsa-a06-v44 — Again Gahaneśa emits.

tsa-a06-v45 — Thus which unmanifest-time — having multiplied it by ten half-highests one should tell Māyā-day, that much the night.

tsa-a06-v47 — Māyā-time multiplied by a hundred half-highests is a day in Īśvara-principle.

tsa-a06-v48 — Here breath emits the world, that much the night. Where breath-quiet — even in breath and in Brahman-cave-abode, in the quiet, which consciousness — even there is sequence.

tsa-a06-v49 — In Īśvara time multiplied by a hundred half-highests, whatever the number — that is Sadāśiva day, that much the night; that alone is the great dissolution.

tsa-a06-v50 — Sadāśiva, when own time ends, having ascended drop-half-moon-blocker, dissolves in resonance; resonance in power-principle, that in the pervader, and she in the Unsupported.

tsa-a06-v51 — By power-time multiplied by ten-million half-highests — Unsupported-day.

tsa-a06-v52 — The Unsupported in the like-minded station — which, that likeness, sameness; that is Brahman.

tsa-a06-v53 — From this likeness, from the partless, from time, by mere winking measure — the stated wheel-whirl rise of all time's spread and re-dissolution.

tsa-a06-v54 — One, ten, hundred, thousand, ten thousand, hundred thousand, million, ten million, hundred million, billion, ten billion, hundred billion, thousand billion, ten-thousand billion, ocean, end, middle, half-highest — thus in order ten-multiplied, eighteen: thus the counting-rule.

tsa-a06-v55 — Thus countless emissions-dissolutions in one great-emission-formed breath — it too in consciousness, she in the adjunct, it in bare awareness, of bare awareness alone this pulse which is called this time-rise.

tsa-a06-v56 — From that alone, in dream-fancy and the rest, variety of this brings no contradiction.

tsa-a06-v57 — Thus as in out-breath is time-rise, so too in in-breath, from the heart to the root-seat's end.

tsa-a06-v58 — And as in heart-throat-palate-brow-cleft-twelve-ends the six causes called Brahmā-Viṣṇu-Rudra-Īśa-Sadāśiva-Unsupported, just so too in in-breath, in heart-bulb-bliss-contraction-expansion-twelve-ends — these childhood-youth-age-rebirth-release lords.

tsa-a06-v59 — Now in equal-breath is time-rise.

tsa-a06-v61 — But moving up and down, in three channels it does going-coming.

tsa-a06-v62 — There on equinox-day, outside, at dawn-time, a ghaṭikā and a quarter it carries in the middle path.

tsa-a06-v63 — Then nine hundred breath-castings — thus by counting, outside two and a half ghaṭikās — left, right, left, right, left — thus five passages.

tsa-a06-v64 — Then when the five-passage has elapsed, in less-a-quarter fourteen passed ghaṭikās, at southern autumnal equinox-midday, nine breath-hundreds.

tsa-a06-v65 — Then too right, left, right, left, right — thus a five-passage, nine hundred each — thus even at night — thus.

tsa-a06-v66 — Thus on equinox-day and its night, twelve and twelve passages.

tsa-a06-v67 — Then in day wax-wane, passage wax-wane.

tsa-a06-v68 — Thus in one equal-breath-wind two years, from absence of in-out-breath union.

tsa-a06-v69 — Even here twelve-year-rise and the rest as before.

tsa-a06-v70 — But in up-breath the course is twelve-end-limited, of mere-pulse-natured time.
