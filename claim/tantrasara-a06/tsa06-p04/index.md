## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a06-v71** — Even here as before the rule.
**tsa-a06-v72** — But in pervading-breath, from pervasiveness, even in non-sequence, by subtle-surging-ness is time-rise.
**tsa-a06-v74** — There in half-watch, in half-watch, class-rise is even at equinox; of the letter, of the letter, two hundred sixteen breaths; outside thirty-six caṣakas: thus this rise, effortless-born letter-rise.
**tsa-a06-v75** — But effort-born is mantra-rise: like a water-wheel-device drawing, by single-attention force — wondrous mantra-rise day and night keeping joined — with the mantra-deity gains identity.
**tsa-a06-v76** — There in the ever-arisen, by breath-course count alone is rise-count explained; when that doubled, its half, and the rest in order; in the hundred-and-eight wheel, two hundred the rise: thus in order. In gross-subtle course-nature of the rested one, when breath-course has thinned, when time is devoured come about, complete, one, just this awareness, wonder-power-filled, shines.
**tsa-a06-v77** — Time-difference alone divides awareness, not object-difference — like peak-abiding knowledge. Of knowing, as much as the abiding-time, that alone is the moment. And in one breath-rise, one knowing alone necessarily — and this: otherwise there would be no single thought-knowing at all, since word-sequenced-stained, since even the measure is sequential.
**tsa-a06-v78** — Which has said: the raised, half-short — thus.
**tsa-a06-v79** — Therefore until between pulses has not arisen, so long one knowing alone.
**tsa-a06-v80** — Hence very eighty-one, when remembering the word, through entering various natures — that one alone, having the Lord as object, that thought, when time is devoured, results as thought-free-natured alone — thus.
**tsa-a06-v81** — Thus seeing the entire time-course in breath-rise alone, and the various countless emissions-dissolutions, noting right there, by self alone recognizing lordship — one is freed alone — thus.
**tsa-a06-v82** — Seeing the awareness-formed self's breath-power, the form going there and beyond time together, with emission-stay-dissolution wheels, ever-ready attains Bhairavī-being.
