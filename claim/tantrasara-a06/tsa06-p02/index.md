## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a06-v18** — Since making the knower-measure-known triad non-divided, that time is holy, other-world-fruit-giving.
**tsa-a06-v19** — Then, entering breath, the awareness-sun with each single digit fills the in-breath-moon, until the fifteenth tuṭi is full moon; right after, fortnight-joint and seizure — thus as before. But this is this-world-fruit-giving: thus the month rise.
**tsa-a06-v21** — There the dark fortnight alone is the northern course; in six and six fingers is the passage, from Capricorn up to Gemini's end.
**tsa-a06-v22** — There per finger five lunar days; even there day-and-night division. Thus in coming in the southern course — womb-ness, birth-wish, about-to-be-born-ness, will-be-born-ness, birth-beginning, being-born-ness, birth-and-six-changes — thus in order among Capricorn and the rest.
**tsa-a06-v23** — Just so worship here does fitting fruit.
**tsa-a06-v24** — And here Dakṣa and the rest up to Grandfather, Rudras and powers — twelve lords: thus the year rise.
**tsa-a06-v25** — Per finger sixty lunar days, thus in order at passage a year — thus by this order, in coming and going, twelve-year rise. Per finger of lunar days three hundred in finger-and-a-fifth — a year where before caṣaka was said: thus by counting, at passage five years — thus by this series, in one breath-out-in time sixty-year rise. Here twenty-one thousand six hundred: thus of lunar days the number.
**tsa-a06-v26** — That many alone in day and night is the breath-count: thus not beyond sixty-year rise is examined, from endlessness.
**tsa-a06-v27** — There the human year is the gods' lunar day; by this order twelve thousand divine years are four ages.
**tsa-a06-v28** — Four, three, two, one — thus from kṛta beginning, by so many hundreds, eight twilights.
**tsa-a06-v29** — By seventy-one four-ages a manu-period; by fourteen manu-periods Brahmā's day. At Brahmā-day's end, in the triple world burnt by time-fire, and elsewhere in the triple world smoke-lulled, all people swiftly, fire-driven, in people-world having become pralaya-kevalas, stand.
**tsa-a06-v30** — But awakened Kūṣmāṇḍa, Hāṭakeśa and the rest play in the great world.
**tsa-a06-v31** — Then at night's end Brahmā's emission.
**tsa-a06-v32** — By this measure a hundred years is Brahmā-lifespan.
**tsa-a06-v33** — That is Viṣṇu's day, and that much the night; of him too a hundred is the lifespan.
**tsa-a06-v34** — That day; above that, O Rudra-world-lord, Rudra's; that much night; as before a year. That hundred too — and the limit.
**tsa-a06-v35** — There at Rudra's end, going to Śiva-ness; Rudra's stated office-limit. Of world-egg bearers that day; of a hundred Rudras night that much; of them too a hundred is the lifespan.
**tsa-a06-v36** — When a hundred Rudras end — world-egg destruction.
**tsa-a06-v37** — Thus from water-principle up to the unmanifest, this alone in order is the Rudras' lifespan.
**tsa-a06-v38** — The earlier's lifespan is the later's day: thus.
**tsa-a06-v39** — And then Brahmā and Rudras are unobstructed officers standing in the unmanifest: thus.
**tsa-a06-v40** — And then Śrīkaṇṭha-lord is the withdrawer.
**tsa-a06-v41** — This is intermediate dissolution; on its ending, emission.
**tsa-a06-v42** — There other scriptures, stated, too are emitted.
**tsa-a06-v43** — But Śrīkaṇṭha-lord's own lifespan — that is the sheath-dwellers' Rudras' day, that much the night; their lifespan — that is Gahaneśa-day, that much alone the night; and in her the entire alone dissolves in Māyā.
