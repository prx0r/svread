## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a16-v01** — Now indirect initiation is twofold: the dead one, and the jīva-part.
**tsa-a16-v10** — Outside too — thus how does it not happen, from drawing-and-rest without practice, if asked: by passion, hatred and the rest yoked, in its operation, from the aiśvarya-immersion yoke.
**tsa-a16-v11** — Then, from being necessarily controlled, dependence on practice-and-rest would indeed be.
**tsa-a16-v12** — Here, however, from immersion in grace-natured Lordhood, such is the state.
**tsa-a16-v13** — For the Supreme Lord himself, by the door of the guru's body-abode, graces those to be graced.
**tsa-a16-v14** — And he, of unthinkable glory — it is mostly said.
**tsa-a16-v15** — Thus drawn by the jala-practice, the jīva, having entered dārbha, jasmine-fruit-and-rest or the body, becomes; nor does he tremble, from the absence of mind-breath-and-rest equipment; but by the force of that contemplation he trembles even so — as before: the purification-and-rest consecration, within the full-offering's yojana-end.
**tsa-a16-v16** — Here, further, by the full-offering, dissolution of the dārbha-and-rest form in the supreme fire is to be done.
**tsa-a16-v17** — Thus raised out, he is released by the full-offering itself — if he stands in the hell-ghost-animal classes.
**tsa-a16-v18** — A man, just then, obtains knowledge, yoga, initiation, or discernment.
**tsa-a16-v19** — From being an eligible body — thus the raising-of-the-dead.
**tsa-a16-v02** — Therein [the command]: one who has served the guru — whether dead, or banished, or killed by sorcery and the rest, or killed as an infant, or arisen at the death-moment as willed, or one whose power-descent has come through another mouth — or likewise one to be initiated: such is the command.
**tsa-a16-v20** — Even for the living, in indirect [initiation], when power-descent arises: this very sequence — excepting the dārbha-form-imagination jīva-drawing.
**tsa-a16-v21** — The consecration of the one established by mere meditation itself.
**tsa-a16-v22** — Initiation gives both the enjoyment and the enjoyer.
**tsa-a16-v23** — From the strength of one's own habit, and the unthinkability of severing the enjoyment-habit: by many, in initiation, the higher-doctrine consecration is strong; another would be for that consecration.
**tsa-a16-v24** — Likewise for the initiated from outside — the manifestation of knowledge-and-rest, it is said.
**tsa-a16-v03** — And in the dead-initiation, adhivāsa-and-rest is not used.
**tsa-a16-v04** — In the mandala — where abundant rite, the supreme instrument, flower-and-rest seat, pedestal-and-rest mandala, trident-lotus-and-rest form, the specific meditation-object mantra, itself radiant — of the meditation-devoted yogi, of him rich in one-devotion-immersion, and of the knower: these are the presence-causes, stated in order.
**tsa-a16-v05** — In their conjunction, what talk would there be? — thus it was said by the Supreme Lord.
**tsa-a16-v06** — Then, having worshipped the god and established his kuśa-and-rest form in front, by the guru-derived order of knowledge-teaching let him see it; and he, rising from the muladhara, having crossed by seminal power the spread-out endless nerve-path-staff to the nose-sky, scattering — let him seek to pervade.
**tsa-a16-v07** — As long as, by smoke favorably heaped in fine-flame mesh, the path-wheel is covered and the desired jīva is brought in — thus the great jala-named practice.
**tsa-a16-v08** — By this, the to-be-covered goes into another's power — facing first; afterwards the whole is brought, if — or then even from the path's middle, as he wishes.
**tsa-a16-v09** — Two-drawn or two-raised, or within a dead person's scope, that-which-is-to-be-drawn in the jīva: the yoga gone through blessed Śaṃbhunātha's tradition — jala-named, told by me.
