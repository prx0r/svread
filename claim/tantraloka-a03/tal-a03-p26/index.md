**tal-a03-v250** — From the stirring of those powers, six immaculate cognitions are declared. They arise from the immersion of those same powers as the power of action.
**tal-a03-v251** — Twelve cognitions are declared, and in them all is contained. This is the principal wheel of powers of the God of gods.
**tal-a03-v252** — To this extent, the God of gods, Bhairava, possessed of full power, is of the nature of reflective awareness and is united with emission and projection.
**tal-a03-v253** — Because of knowledge that measures limitation, these powers are called Kalikas in some places; and in the revered Sara scripture, the supreme one-syllabled power is said to be in the middle.
**tal-a03-v254** — One should worship the power called the Bhairava-self, surrounded by twelve yoginis; from them the circle of powers extends up to sixty-four.
**tal-a03-v255** — Beginning from the syllable e, it unfolds up to the thousand-spoked; and in the scripture these powers are given many names according to their different functions.
**tal-a03-v256** — Their modes of worship, in dual, nondual, and mixed forms, have been explained at great length in the revered Traishira.
**tal-a03-v257** — It is not written here, for fear of prolixity and because it is not needed; those same powers are proclaimed as stainless, pure, and not-terrible.
**tal-a03-v258** — Because they are the source of the terrible and more terrible forms, they are also of that nature, appearing in creation, maintenance, dissolution, and in the transcendence of those three conditions.
**tal-a03-v259** — The abiding form of those same powers is divided in many ways, but the teachers say that the form beyond limiting conditions is twofold.
