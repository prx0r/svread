## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a03-v150** — Because it is necessarily of the nature of the five powers, this is clear in each case, as the power of will, abiding in its own nature, appears from a single form.
**tal-a03-v151** — The ca-group is of the nature of the five powers and consists of gradual clear manifestation. Then, through connection with a brief activity that shares in the stain of the knowable, the next stage arises.
**tal-a03-v152** — From that twofold power are born the pair of groups beginning with ṭa and ta. But from unfolding comes the pa-group, because through it the universe is completed.
**tal-a03-v153** — This, having the form of the knowable and extending up to the twenty-fifth, is clearly manifest. Because it is knowable and thus clear, this much is declared to have the form of the stop consonants.
**tal-a03-v154** — And the power of will, which is twofold as stirred and unstirred, turns toward a portion of a different power and goes to the state of ya.
**tal-a03-v155** — That very same power, more quickly tinged by the stain of the knowable, and turned toward what is different, goes to the state of ra and to the state of la.
**tal-a03-v156** — In the same way, the twofold unfolding power, when it moves toward the power of otherness, becomes the letter va, which pours forth the essence of creation.
**tal-a03-v157** — Will itself, having reached supreme bliss, is called air when joined with swiftness; through shining it becomes fire, and through firmness it becomes earth.
**tal-a03-v158** — This group of four is said to be inner because it is contained within will and the other powers and abides in its own completion.
**tal-a03-v159** — The joining of the same-kind powers, beginning with will, is said to be a form of agitation, and this also applies to those whose nature involves both agitation and nonagitation.
