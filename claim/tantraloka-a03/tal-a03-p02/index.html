tal-a03-v10 — For beings, the obstructive body-maya nature belongs to them only; true-knowledge nature is non-obstructive.

tal-a03-v11 — Thus revealing an appearance of both forms, the boon-giver shines everywhere by image and reflection view.

tal-a03-v12 — Someone may argue that rays from the eyes strike a clear surface, bounce back, and thereby grasp one's own face; such a person is now questioned.

tal-a03-v13 — If the self, presiding over light outside the body, could know by that very light, then what need would there be for a mirror?

tal-a03-v14 — If the reversed rays became the graspers, the form would be seen on one's own face, not inside the mirror.

tal-a03-v15 — If it were really one's own face, that form would appear on one's own face, like touch, as 'mine'; but the mirror image is not tangible and has the nature of being only something seen.

tal-a03-v16 — The reflected image is only the shape of visible form; the real thing, joined with touch, smell, taste, and the rest, has those other qualities suppressed in the reflection.

tal-a03-v17 — Suppression is due to the absence of graspability, but that does not prove the thing is absent; and that non-graspability arises because there is no real contact with the object and because the image does not actually reside in the mirror.

tal-a03-v18 — For this reason, properties such as heaviness are not observed in the image; it is not actually situated in the mirror, which serves only as a means for seeing it.

tal-a03-v19 — Therefore, what appears does not exist there as something separate; the mirror is called its support only conventionally, while lamp, sight, and awareness are, in sequence, the real means of its appearance.
