## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a03-v240** — Absorption into that same sound is perceived suddenly. Those who do not become absorbed in it remain sunk in the body and similar limitations.
**tal-a03-v241** — Those who do not know remain with their awareness sunk and not centered in the heart. For them, the sound that comes from a skin-covered drum or the like is just an external resonance.
**tal-a03-v242** — Because it is both clear and unclear, that gross form is Madhyama. Madhyama also contains an undivided aspect, which is described as its coloration.
**tal-a03-v243** — Where sound is made of undivided tones, it is delighting. Non-division brings fulfillment, as can be seen in the recitation of rhythm.
**tal-a03-v244** — One feels satisfied in that unmanifest sound during musical playing. Yet that same level can also serve as the cause for the arising of clear letters.
**tal-a03-v245** — That gross level is Vaikhari, whose function is mainly sentences and other articulated speech. Even within this gross triad there is a connecting awareness like the first.
**tal-a03-v246** — When that triad is taken separately, it is called subtle. It is seen in experiences like 'I make the note shadja,' 'I play sweetly,' and 'I speak words.'
**tal-a03-v247** — A threefold connecting awareness is indeed experienced separately. Of that triad, the first aspect is a form without limiting condition.
**tal-a03-v248** — That is the supreme triad. There Shiva is of the nature of supreme consciousness, and in the manifestation of division three principal powers appear.
**tal-a03-v249** — The three principal powers are Anuttara as supreme will, the power situated as supreme-and-non-supreme, and the unfolding power called knowledge, which is declared as the lower.
