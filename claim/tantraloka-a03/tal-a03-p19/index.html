tal-a03-v180 — The wise say that the phonemes from ka to ha are the support of agitation; when that too is joined in the form of the womb, it moves into a further agitation.

tal-a03-v181 — Through that indication, the fiftieth phoneme arises, well established as before in the level known as the twenty-fifth.

tal-a03-v182 — There are two tetrads, in sequence, moving in difference and non-difference; the first tetrad is skilled in joining together the divisions of consciousness.

tal-a03-v183 — The other tetrad is the sole cause by which difference becomes established within non-difference; thus, whatever collection of phonemes there is, all of it was originally made of vowels.

tal-a03-v184 — A consonant becomes manifest through articulation, but its life-breath is the vowel; indeed, in the whole stream of phonemes, the root is only the group of six vowels.

tal-a03-v185 — Those six deities are the principal rays of the sun, and within those very solar rays there is a lunar digit.

tal-a03-v186 — Therefore the triad of long vowels is clearly a lunar body; and the moon is nothing other than the enjoyable and the enjoyer—there is no third thing apart from them.

tal-a03-v187 — The enjoyer alone becomes established as twofold by appearing as the enjoyable; for a pot does not have enjoyability as its own nature—rather, that belongs to the subject.

tal-a03-v188 — Therefore, the establishment that occurs in the subject is called the object's enjoyability; the unsurpassed appears through the states of being the object of awareness and the subject of awareness.

tal-a03-v189 — The enjoyable—will and the rest—attains the form of a collision; likewise, when will and the other powers rest on the ground of unsurpassed bliss, they become enjoyable.
