**tal-a03-v65** — Therefore, let the Lord's powers be the instrumental cause in such cases. Thus this whole universe is a pure reflection in the clear sky of Bhairava-consciousness, not by the grace of anything else.
**tal-a03-v66** — That power of the Lord, independent of anything else, by which he is the self of the universe, is declared to be the supreme, unsurpassed goddess Pratibhā.
**tal-a03-v67** — Of this Lord Akula, the supreme power is Kaulikī, who shines in the spreading forth of Kula; the Lord is never separated from her.
**tal-a03-v68** — The pure united form of those two is remembered as saṃghaṭṭa. That same union is called the power of bliss, because from it the universe is emitted.
**tal-a03-v69** — This goddess is declared to be the principle beyond the supreme and the non-supreme. She is its essence and its heart; that supreme emission is the Lord.
