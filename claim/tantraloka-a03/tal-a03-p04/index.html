tal-a03-v30 — Facingness is explained as the reflection's being situated in non-difference from the mirror-like condition in which it appears.

tal-a03-v31 — Therefore, in the hollow space of a well or jar, the speaker's space is reflected together with sound and appears as if another speaker were there.

tal-a03-v32 — Just as someone standing behind a mirror does not directly know the face, someone standing behind such a reflecting space does not directly know the original sound.

tal-a03-v33 — An unmanifest sound is not reflected; its manifestation and hearing occur at the same time, in the second moment.

tal-a03-v34 — In that moment, the sound's being reflected and its being heard occur at the same time, just as one does not ascertain a hand and its shadow at different times.

tal-a03-v35 — Now that the true nature of reflection has been shown here, we return to the main topic, for reflection properly applies there.

tal-a03-v36 — Sound is reflected beautifully in space, and touch too can appear as something external, like a blow, pain, or cold, yet it agitates one's own body because it is a reflection.

tal-a03-v37 — This reflected touch is not the primary one, because it does not reveal an independent chain of effects of its own.

tal-a03-v38 — In the same way, smell appears within the organ of smell, and taste appears clearly in the moisture of the mouth.

tal-a03-v39 — Just as a reflected form is not seen without the eyes, taste, touch, fragrance, and the rest are not perceived without their proper sense organs, even if they are present.
