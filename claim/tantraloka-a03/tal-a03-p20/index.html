tal-a03-v190 — The arising of the juncture phonemes is said to be the form of the enjoyer, and the god who is made of unsurpassed bliss is said to be the enjoyer alone.

tal-a03-v192 — Therefore the triad, essence of six, of consciousness-will-opening nature, that same threefold, they call Bhairava's supreme greatness.

tal-a03-v193 — That triad of the supreme lord is the full power sung; by it the universe is drawn, because in this worshipped.

tal-a03-v194 — Worship born in a cut and barren universe-power: thus this greatness is only so much, and that much is not possible.

tal-a03-v195 — Who would make a limit to the power of the unlimited? Therefore the god is supreme by freedom and supremacy.

tal-a03-v196 — Endowed with emanation power, completed as universe-formed; thus the great lord has fifty-reflection full power.

tal-a03-v197 — Reflection-self is one; indeed other powers are established in this only; the eighty-one-footed goddess will be included here.

tal-a03-v198 — In one reflection nature, sound-mass is Bhairava; by reflection-shadow and union, that same power is mother-power.

tal-a03-v199 — She is different in womb from sound-mass collision; Malini is as before ninefold reflection, having separate class nature.
