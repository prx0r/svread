## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a03-v40** — The object of the cognition of an external touch is not located inside the inner field of touch.
**tal-a03-v41** — When one's nearby sense organs and inner instruments are properly engaged, the reflection received in the sense organ produces a corresponding activity.
**tal-a03-v42** — An action does not arise from a remembered mental object, because such action truly belongs to the present. Therefore, the excellent touch is situated in that sense organ, having arrived there and being known, and it acts accordingly in the present.
**tal-a03-v43** — When an external object is impossible, that same excellent touch, reflected within one's own self, produces that pleasurable sensation. It does so within some continuum of subtle channels.
**tal-a03-v44** — Therefore, by offering the universe as his own self in the mirror of consciousness, this declares the pure universal nature of the Lord.
**tal-a03-v45** — Just as smell, form, touch, taste, and other qualities, when reflected, shine in a sword by taking on the coloring of that support, like a face and other images reflected there.
**tal-a03-v46** — Likewise, this universe, reflected in consciousness, should be understood to rest upon its expansive qualities, such as luminosity and freedom.
**tal-a03-v47** — Just as a reflection from all sides may appear in a crystal that is clear on all sides, so too reflections appear in consciousness, which possesses complete clarity.
**tal-a03-v48** — Absolute transparency is the non-manifestation of one's own form. Therefore consciousness is the most transparent, unlike a jewel, which grasps and displays its own form.
**tal-a03-v49** — A reflection is projected by an external original image, but if everything is a reflection of consciousness itself, what external original image could possibly remain?
