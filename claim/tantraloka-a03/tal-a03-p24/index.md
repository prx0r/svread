## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a03-v230** — That vital power is strength, vigor, the vital breaths, and loveliness. From it offspring arise, and it is also spoken of in ritual acts.
**tal-a03-v231** — In sacrifice and other rites, in rain, in plants, again in vital power, and then in offspring, the same power circulates. Thus emission takes on the form of the whole universe.
**tal-a03-v232** — That whole mass of sound is called Matrika. Because it enters a state of being both stirred and stirring, it is also declared to be Malini.
**tal-a03-v233** — Malini, beautiful through the union of seed and womb and the rise of emission, is indeed determined to be the supreme power that takes the form of the whole universe.
**tal-a03-v234** — In reality this supreme power is one alone and draws forth time. Yet through its connection with the differentiation of the power-holder, it takes on the form of a pair.
**tal-a03-v235** — That reflective awareness, whose nature is the perfect I, abides in its own self and, by its freedom, manifests division within itself.
**tal-a03-v236** — When division appears, this awareness is said to take a threefold body: Pashyanti, Madhyama, and the gross level called Vaikhari.
**tal-a03-v237** — Each of those three also has a threefold form: gross, subtle, and supreme. Among them is a form beautiful with flowing tones, having the nature of sound.
**tal-a03-v238** — That gross aspect is indeed Pashyanti. Since letters and other divisions have not yet appeared there, its single undivided form is called sweetness and power.
**tal-a03-v239** — Clearness and harshness arise from the friction of articulatory place, breath, and so on. Yet in this sound-form they function in close proximity to consciousness.
