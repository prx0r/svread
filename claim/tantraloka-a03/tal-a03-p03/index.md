## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a03-v20** — Lamp, eye, and cognition do not produce a separate mirror-like appearance, because they lack hardness and are supremely pure and clear on all sides.
**tal-a03-v21** — The God of gods revealed this for the understanding of the deluded: a thing may appear even elsewhere than where it is, yet not fully or truly there.
**tal-a03-v22** — The compassionate teacher explained that the image is not solid, not independent, and not simply stable or unstable; rather, it is the special power of some clear medium.
**tal-a03-v23** — The teaching on the mirror declared that a reflection has no fixed place, no independent form, no time, no measure, no mutual contact, no removal, and no solidity; it is not simply unreal, nor does it have an essence of its own. By understanding this, delusion is dispelled.
**tal-a03-v24** — In the account of reflection presented here, the reflection of sound is what is called an echo.
**tal-a03-v25** — The echo is not produced by the original sound coming back, because although it is heard as if returning, the speaker does not hear it in that way and distant listeners may not hear the original sound at all.
**tal-a03-v26** — Because an echo changes according to the hollows or openings it meets, the reflection of sound behaves like the reflection of a face or other visible form.
**tal-a03-v27** — Just as a mirror shows the form of another object, an echo is heard as something spoken by another.
**tal-a03-v28** — Because a reflection must face its original, the perceivers who stand between the source and the reflecting place are the ones who hear the echo.
**tal-a03-v29** — A reflection can be perceived even without perceiving the original directly, just as one may see in a mirror the body of a beloved person standing behind oneself.
