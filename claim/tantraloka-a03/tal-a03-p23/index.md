**tal-a03-v224** — The teacher's characteristic is this much—beginning and end—and he should know that the venerable one is like 'so'ham', a knower, Bhairava, of divine nature.
**tal-a03-v225** — Whatever verse, gāthā, or the like is joined with beginning and end, therefore, knowing thus, one sees all as mantra indeed.
**tal-a03-v226** — In the Vedanta called Aitareya, the Supreme Lord has ascertained in detail that the visarga-power of the universe is its cause.
**tal-a03-v227** — That which is red is fire; its vigor is sun-and-moon-bodied; thus the letter 'a' is supreme Brahman, whose nature is the arising of that collision.
**tal-a03-v228** — That seed too has a supreme vital power made of the five elements. As something to be enjoyed it takes the form of food, consisting of sound, touch, and taste.
**tal-a03-v229** — Sound too is sweet because it increases vital power. That vital power is supreme and pure, and it is understood to have the nature of a desire to emit.
