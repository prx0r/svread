tal-a09-v301 — Sound-born cognition arises when sound comes; even a faint drum sound, if heard, counts.

tal-a09-v302 — How can perception occur at a distance? Not so: sound-born sound is uttered from far and far.

tal-a09-v303 — What is in ear-space has far-and-far nature; that first sound is not pervasive, and this is fitting.

tal-a09-v304 — With those there, the intense-natured is heard by this; but how would the faint be heard? Not so, since it rests on another quality.

tal-a09-v305 — This was refuted by others in many ways; vain again. Who indeed is not a scholar in giving a blow to the fallen?

tal-a09-v306 — But of these, earth and the rest, as many tattva-group as before, standing by greater quality, pervaded, so much shines.

tal-a09-v307 — The pervaded-pervader relation shown of the tattvas is established by greater quality, not by causality nor by simplicity.

tal-a09-v308 — Even without cause, attachment is clearly pervaded by knowing and so forth; without that, what is called pervaded would not be, since.

tal-a09-v309 — No simplicity indeed exists here in own view; by greater quality the pervaded-pervader relation is known clearly.

tal-a09-v310 — Who indeed is superior in quality from that is called above; aboveness is pervader-ness, clear in Sri Malinivijaya.
