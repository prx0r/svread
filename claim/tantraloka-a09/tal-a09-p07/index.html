tal-a09-v61 — Lord-will-agitated enjoyment-craving consciousnesses, to distribute — Aghora-lord creates here white and other.

tal-a09-v62 — Of atoms longing named, actionless, desired — incomplete-thinking knowledge is impurity, abandoned with limitation.

tal-a09-v63 — Mere fitness only, this, for to-be-limited summary — by that impurity of this, no separate-reality-state exists, like attachment.

tal-a09-v64 — But unlimited-action-part-measure-limitation — that attachment in man, of intellect the nature: action-difference-variety.

tal-a09-v65 — Incomplete otherness, and this such-form appearance — of the independent one's Shiva-will: pot-form as pot.

tal-a09-v66 — Own-self-covering desire only, become thing, thus impurity — just as of the non-distinct, earth-and-rest become self-natured.

tal-a09-v67 — Thus also its, in scriptures distinct situated impurity — distinct, independent, not anyone, like cart and the rest.

tal-a09-v68 — That second, with impurity, Shiva-liberated-follower — what obstructing power of impurity exists? And that, unliberated-going.

tal-a09-v69 — Thus reason-abandoned doctrine, faith-only-imagined — the obstructing power of the inert by itself indeed does not proceed.

tal-a09-v70 — By itself in activity the universe would be thus — and what measure? Of impurity the obstructing, that power, if the Lord joins, that.
