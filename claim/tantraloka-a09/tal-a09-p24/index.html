tal-a09-v231 — By that agitation-formed fivefold wind impelled — for life; otherwise death again.

tal-a09-v232 — Therefore indeed from pure-self-freedom-I-nature, this from uncreated — but the other, so said, from the word krti.

tal-a09-v233 — Thus this ego's instrument-aggregate is determined — threefold its nature-aggregate: sattvic rajasic tamasic.

tal-a09-v234 — Clearly from sattva-predominant ego touching enjoyer-portion — mind buddhi senses sixfold born; but difference is stated.

tal-a09-v235 — Mind, which is all-object, thereby here intended to be stated — all-subtle-element agency is ego's qualifier.

tal-a09-v236 — Buddhi ego mind, they say, in awakening-quest is instrument — by outer gods not, nor even by inward-facing made.

tal-a09-v237 — And breath is no inner instrument, from inertness — of impelling nature; as cause of effort-desire-cognition portions — certain.

tal-a09-v238 — Determination and egoism, imagination and the rest, are not one-formed action — therefore three-ness, fitting in inner action, clearly.

tal-a09-v239 — And buddhi is not insentient — instrumentality is mind's, as with pradhana; the like-pradhana-insentient-buddhi doctrine is rejected.

tal-a09-v240 — Distinguished by sound-tanmatra-causality, that ego-principle — in ear as sense-organ, up to nose where smell-ness is indicated.
