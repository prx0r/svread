tal-a09-v01 — Now the classification of principles is described in detail, as arrived in order.

tal-a09-v10 — Inertness is dependence on a knower; even self-realization has no causality except through agency.

tal-a09-v02 — The forms formerly spoken shine as the supreme light-dense god, one Shiva; again from the essence of his freedom, when difference shines forth from Shiva-status, that supreme manifold-following form becomes this principle of the lord in teaching.

tal-a09-v03 — Thus indeed, from the time-abode to the end of Virabhadra city, earth-nature manifests from firmness, hardness, weight, and similar qualities.

tal-a09-v04 — Thus in water and other principles, up to Sadashiva, it is to be stated: in its own effect, in a flood of qualities, or also in its own similar quality.

tal-a09-v05 — Each principle stands in common form through pervading nature, in order, from earth-pradhana to purusha, Shiva, and others.

tal-a09-v06 — No consequence would arise for bodies and worlds; this was stated by the supreme lord in the Matanga scripture and others.

tal-a09-v07 — There, the cause-effect relation imagined by Shiva's will is shown as seen in the view of siddhayogīśvarī.

tal-a09-v08 — In reality Shiva, supreme lord of all beings, alone acts; dependent agency never arises.

tal-a09-v09 — Freedom belongs to the pure-consciousness-bodied supreme lord, while inertness is not free; thus they mutually contradict.
