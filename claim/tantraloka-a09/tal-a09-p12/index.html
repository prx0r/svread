tal-a09-v111 — Real speech would not be known to another by you; if the enjoyer's intention were as in oneself, it would differ.

tal-a09-v112 — That wrong action would necessarily be of another too; in knowing another's intention, one's own intention becomes firm.

tal-a09-v113 — In absence of intention there is no fruit-connection; the convention 'wrong action is mine' is for fruitlessness.

tal-a09-v114 — In cutting another's intention, not intended by self, fruit would not be for both due to destruction-cause arrangement.

tal-a09-v115 — In pleasure-cause and pleasure of this, from common intention even from undifferentiated, just enjoyment of virtue-etc fruit.

tal-a09-v116 — Suffering or suffering-cause would be thus mine; but common intention would not be of that unrighteousness, nor scripture.

tal-a09-v117 — We speak the topic of the described limited knower: with non-ego union all action impressions are dissolved.

tal-a09-v118 — Therefore for this one action does not exist; impurity may do cooperation for anyone, and by that this one attains destruction-nature.

tal-a09-v119 — With destroyed impurity, controlled by inner Shiva possession, even ego-state goes, not action-dependent condition.

tal-a09-v120 — Said in holy previous scripture by the supreme lord: they desire impurity as ignorance, cause of the world sprout.
