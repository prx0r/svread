tal-a09-v51 — Named Shiva Shakti Sa Desha Avidya: five tattvas — in each one also, in this tattva, full of all powers.

tal-a09-v52 — By each-predominance-union that difference is determined — thus indeed by own-independence-fullness, the all-pervader.

tal-a09-v53 — By countless many, by forms shining, avoiding limitation — Shambhava ones, Shakti-born, mantra-great-lords, mantra-leaders.

tal-a09-v54 — Mantras thus pure would be these five groups in order — in each respective group shines whatever form, connected.

tal-a09-v55 — Therein reality thus said, like time-fire and earth — by that, what they call by narrative-similarity, mocked.

tal-a09-v56 — Guru-worship, without book-desired views — Brahma, Nivritti-lord, as separate tattva is not counted.

tal-a09-v57 — Sadashiva and the rest are counted separately — what principle? In the self supported by Brahma Vishnu Hara Ishana Susiva.

tal-a09-v58 — In six, cause-named, half-old this — whence? Thus from its root destroyed; counted, not indeed as cause.

tal-a09-v59 — As earth-lord king is no other reality — so those various lords separately, how another reality?

tal-a09-v60 — Thus this pentad is defined pure-path — there directly Shiva-will only, doer-appearing differentiator.
