## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a09-v251** — Therefore, even when mind and action senses are lacking, form and motion do not shine for the blind or lame.
**tal-a09-v252** — But by common instrument power, even the blind attains perception of common form.
**tal-a09-v253** — Then from ego, beyond the subtle elements, action organs such as speech and hands are born.
**tal-a09-v254** — Actions like speaking and taking are ego actions; an action organ is that capable of action.
**tal-a09-v255** — Even when the hand is cut, the action-organ nature remains; its chief seat is the five-fingered hand.
**tal-a09-v256** — Even when taking by mouth, the established instrument is the hand; without it, action would not arise.
**tal-a09-v257** — Even with mind senses, seeing depends on an instrument because of action nature.
**tal-a09-v258** — Others do not accept going as an instrument, yet going and lifting are chief perceptions of action.
**tal-a09-v259** — Again, action is a quality; in the grammarian view, action is preceded by an instrument through pervasion.
**tal-a09-v260** — Knowledge is not taking; therefore action organs are chiefly called skin and so on, pervading their fields.
