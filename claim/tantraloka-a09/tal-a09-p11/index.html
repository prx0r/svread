tal-a09-v101 — In the enjoyer-self, this difference-form arrangement exists; thus it resorts to cooperation in the production of its own work.

tal-a09-v102 — Impurity is the cause as power-manifesting of action; but if action exists, how can impurity also exist?

tal-a09-v103 — Limited knowledge has contraction; such contraction indeed exists; even destroyed contraction becomes firm by meditation.

tal-a09-v104 — Thinking 'I am not the doer,' one abandons action impression; the firm disposition in the self says this action will fruit.

tal-a09-v105 — That impression is for fruit here, not for causing remembering; even in undestroyed contraction, meditation on 'I am not the doer' acts.

tal-a09-v106 — Fruit does not arise for the intoxicated or deluded, nor when penance is done; austerity performed by me today would clearly be for him.

tal-a09-v107 — Action of an intentional agent does not fruit from intention; but intention-named action in the mental sphere is impression.

tal-a09-v108 — Producing fruit-stained action becomes fit for fruit attainment; whoever even there with firmness abandons fruit impression.

tal-a09-v109 — He enjoys a special fruit made by abandoning that fruit; by this method one abandons all action-continuity.

tal-a09-v110 — With non-ego union one should abandon; even the limited one with contraction should do so; but some wrong action is own and intentional.
