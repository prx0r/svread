tal-a09-v211 — One does not desire to abandon it, because worldly pleasure-cognition-bliss is present; the ignorance-stained one is enabled by the force of the three-quality covering.

tal-a09-v213 — Thus in the kala-named principle, in a distinct part marked by some agency, agency is discussed as preceded by enjoyer.

tal-a09-v214 — As distinction, some part arises here as only-knowable, clear, and distinct; kala produces the primary.

tal-a09-v215 — For kala equally produces the enjoyable and the enjoyer, not separated by difference-intention, mutually.

tal-a09-v216 — Enjoyer-enjoyed nature would not be from separation, nor mutually in the dissolved state; maya too would not be anything.

tal-a09-v217 — Indeed from glorious Raurava onward, the raga-vidya-natured pair produces — kala indeed simultaneously from that manifest, thus the doctrine.

tal-a09-v218 — Said here: this order shines, true — thus indeed enough: being colored one knows all, and knowing all here one is colored.

tal-a09-v219 — Nevertheless reality-existence here by us determined — and in that no sequence at all; or if any, that too from inversion.

tal-a09-v220 — Therefore admit no disagreement about the scripture-stated rule — thus only the experienced, which from pleasure-pain-delusion.
