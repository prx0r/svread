tal-a09-v271 — Said: sense-group from egoism, but from rajas — from tamas-predominant egoism, of enjoyer-portion-covering nature.

tal-a09-v272 — The five tanmatras named from bhuta, cause of gross elements — mind intellect sense action-organs group, tanmatra group.

tal-a09-v273 — Thus here rajas-ego-yoga is connector in the two — others call mind born from rajas-ego, because.

tal-a09-v274 — Skilled in all-sense movement, light, speedy — others: from sattvic, mind; but intellect-action-organs.

tal-a09-v275 — From rajas, grasper-grasped-portion-touching, they think — planet-guardians think action-organ group, clearly.

tal-a09-v276 — Born of rajas-ego, for rajas is action-ness, because — in Sripurva scripture mind from rajas, but from sattvic again.

tal-a09-v277 — All senses fittingly — and this shines to us; for thus, of external-function senses, in function-shining.

tal-a09-v278 — In perceiving, power in inner joining, of mind again — as said by the guru, mind would do following-discrimination, true.

tal-a09-v279 — That-duality-supported, mother-activity-essence action, thus — but the tanmatra group, of darkness-predominant ego.

tal-a09-v280 — Here no dispute of all: graspable beginning indeed — in earth, fragrant-variegated, in smell-sphere.
