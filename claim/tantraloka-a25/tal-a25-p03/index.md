**tal-a25-v25** — 'Liberation comes from discrimination of the tattvas — if from initiation, from yoga, how could it come from mere conduct?' — to this, a fitting answer.
**tal-a25-v26** — Laughing, the Lord said: 'Whence your delusion about the Supreme Lord? For his all-gracing nature is well-established — look!'
**tal-a25-v27** — For one facing death, poison, disease, weapons and the rest are indeed the cause; whether small or great, that, in that manner meditated upon, is a cause of liberation.
**tal-a25-v28** — For liberation's sake these outer marks are employed; knowing thus, there is no doubt — so it is to be done by the wise.
**tal-a25-v29** — 'How could liberation come by just this much?' — one should resort to supreme devotion. Told is the śrāddha-rule, crusher of delusion, poison, and dread.
