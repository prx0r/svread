## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a25-v01** — Now the śrāddha-rule told in the venerable Ṣaḍardha is proclaimed.
**tal-a25-v10** — But for him whose darkness is destroyed by the sun of tattva-knowledge, none of this — funerary rite, śrāddha rule and the rest — is ever of use.

**tal-a25-v03** — On the third day, on the fourth day, on the tenth, in the month, in the month, in the first year, year by year, at all times it is to be performed by his own as before.
**tal-a25-v04** — There as before he should worship the god, offer in fire likewise; then the food-offering itself, first taking it in hand's reach.
**tal-a25-v05** — The guru — the food-made, growing, potent-formed śakti — having meditated, by her the pervaded: that target let the wise one visualize.
**tal-a25-v06** — Then his fettered part, of enjoyable form — that he should offer; in the enjoyer, through identity-state: thus the disciple becomes Śiva.
**tal-a25-v07** — Enjoyableness and the other, body, frame — these are held fetter-natured; in śrāddha, in corpse-uplifting, in the final sacrifice: their Śiva-making.
**tal-a25-v08** — Though fulfillment might come by a single rite, yet for the accomplishment of identity-state he should perform the whole rite.
**tal-a25-v09** — But for the enjoyment-seeker, in practice-abundance, in fruit-abundance as cause — therefore corpse-uplifting, śrāddha and the rest he should perform for him.
