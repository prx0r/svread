tal-a25-v11 — But for them — guru, his group, group-mates, fellow celibates, lineage-followers — the unity-day is a festival day.

tal-a25-v12 — For when there is a surge of awakening, then — O festival day! — from fulfillment: birth-day and unity-day are therefore festivals through accomplished awakening.

tal-a25-v13 — And when the putraka may be of help to someone, then the mother's, father's, and śakti's channels running in the left-right interval —

tal-a25-v14 — he should make flow, to the deity he should offer the offered food; and in the venerable Bharuṇa tantra that was set forth by Śiva.

tal-a25-v15 — And regard for their flowing-time is to be observed for the accomplishment of that form; then by spontaneity he should effect that accomplishment through the forthcoming rite.

tal-a25-v16 — And in anyone's śrāddha, guru-deity-fire libation with wheel-sacrifice should be śrauta; but no fettered rite should there be.

tal-a25-v17 — And so it is said in the venerable Maukuṭa: established even in the Śiva-teaching, one who trusts in the Vedic rite is like a broken bell — nothing at all.

tal-a25-v18 — And by the rite from stated god-worship to wheel-sacrifice, having reached Rudrahood, this creature fully enjoys divine pleasures.

tal-a25-v19 — Now we speak clearly: for the venerable accomplishment, the channel-course — whichever channel one wishes to set flowing, that very one he should visualize.

tal-a25-v20 — In visualization-identity that channel clearly flows; or, to set flowing whichever is desired — that limb, with that hand.
