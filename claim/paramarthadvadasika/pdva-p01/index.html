pdva-p01-v01 — Enjoy peace, always. With few words, stop the chatter-course. By the way of its stopping ask: what is this, how, who am I — and do not doubt. Whatever shines in unbroken sequence, making being and nonbeing shine apart — that is void, Śiva's abode, the highest real thing, brahman. Then what grasping is here?

pdva-p01-v02 — If you reach reality only after first rejecting non-reality, that itself is non-reality. But if non-reality is then reality itself — that is not reality, and not like that.

pdva-p01-v03 — Nothing that shines stands apart from shining. If you say even difference shines — in shining, it does not shine. So give up the grasping at its breaking-ways. In dream, having become dream itself — mere play, no maker of fear: sword-blows, plunges into water, fire-eating, thunder-claps and the rest.

pdva-p01-v04 — First resolve upon knowledge, action, and measuring. Whatever you declare, sir — what sets it apart from the inert? Though quivering, even the inert — is it not the abode of nondual awareness, beginningless, eternal, ungraspable, true-formed?

pdva-p01-v05 — You who illumine beings — if you are smeared by them, what is that to you? If it shines — hail! — your greatness stands unbroken even there. If not, it is not. Even so, in both cases — unrestrained, unfettered, with delusion unbroken, ever content in greatness — sir, you are ever awake.

pdva-p01-v06 — Send your sight outward, beyond the path of the target. Lest mimicking Bhairava be mere fraud. For the sky of nondual waking there is no outside; no inside for the abode of spaceless unfolding.

pdva-p01-v07 — When the agitation of desire's spread rises — whatever flashes, watch just that. In beginning, middle, and ends, if there — you shine, you shine; all dissolves into you.
