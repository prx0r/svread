## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**pdva-p02-v10** — Whatever lights there are — abiding in me, having found room in the highest sky — they shine in me, in eternal glory, undistinguished. I am He — unfeigned, eternal, of unbroken measure, infinite, true, free — dispelling the darkness of the foe-pair, nondual and not, beginningless waking light.
**pdva-p02-v11** — Let time, reckoning the measures, reckon on. Let the creator eagerly create. Brought under another's sway by command, let love churn. Play-pomp, sky-abiding as it were, in the sequence of self-drawn lines — let it rest on body and the rest. In the evil of distorted great delusion — what do I not see?
**pdva-p02-v12** — Who is here, sir? Whom shall I devour? Who is here, sir? Whom shall I suddenly slay? Who is here, sir? Whom — frenzied-bodied in devouring the supreme waking-abode — shall I drink?
**pdva-p02-v13** — The shattering of becoming-born fear, the scaring of the affliction-jackals. Even the wise on wisdom's yoke — for once, what an awakening! From the upsurge of beyond-contentment in wandering the dense forest, Hari Rāmyadeva made the non-difference-elephant trumpet.
**pdva-p02-v08** — Delusion — dense with suffering, conjecture, and piled-up reasoning, with chains of error teeming one after another from rising causes, like a mirage-city. If it does not shine in the sky of consciousness, the ground of the refuge of dual and nondual thought — where else could the highest rest stand, even for the many-selfed?
**pdva-p02-v09** — In dream, after all, the unreal itself is the road, and there is display in deep sleep's abode. But beyond that, in the conditionless sky of consciousness — what grasp does he have? In waking, if the heap of things stood firm as earth even for a moment somewhere — then through knowledge, past that, this as apart: what refutation even there?
