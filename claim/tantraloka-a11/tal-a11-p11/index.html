tal-a11-v101 — Just as another agent in a dream is not established by any means of knowledge, so everywhere let it be understood that self-awareness alone is self-established.

tal-a11-v102 — Who knows 'I alone am situated' would thus play in the wonderful garden-city of mind, with cities of elements, beings, and truths.

tal-a11-v103 — Thus the thoughts 'I am born' and 'I am dead,' along with all varieties of birth and death, shine as self-created appearances in the wall of mind, within the unborn and immortal.

tal-a11-v104 — In reality, for awareness there is no place or time in either world; awareness is only of other and this world.

tal-a11-v105 — This creation would not have become material if not consciousness-made; that was seen from its middle—by whom, one also earth-bearer?

tal-a11-v106 — At the end of the support-and-supported order of the elements and subtle elements, the power that consists of consciousness, indeed of Śiva's form, is the supporter.

tal-a11-v107 — Therefore perception itself is the doer and sustainer; she is Shiva. From that, states are there as states; power is their supporter; then.

tal-a11-v108 — Even supportless, the imagined does not indeed fall downward; thus the universe, rested in its own support-power, let be considered.

tal-a11-v109 — Of this, the convention 'dense I' and so on is indeed the state of earth and the rest up to the end; 'I am consciousness' is accomplished; Bhairava-self-ness.

tal-a11-v110 — Just as blue and other colors shine in a jewel and a rainbow, so in Shiva, in ultimate reality, these neither arise nor ever decay.
