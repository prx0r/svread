**tal-a11-v01** — The path of art will be spoken according to glorious Shambhava knowledge.

**tal-a11-v06** — Others say that at the beginning of initiation, for the purpose of easy collection, Shiva devised a class called kala, which is samaya-dependent.
**tal-a11-v07** — The covenant made by the God of gods does not reach ultimate reality; yet it is not false, and no other covenant arises.
**tal-a11-v08** — Cessation belongs to the earth-principle, establishment to the manifest perceptible domain, knowledge to the end of night, and peace to the end of power; this egg is four.
**tal-a11-v09** — In the Shiva-principle beyond peace, supreme Shiva is beyond kala; here there is no classification, convention, or even counting.
