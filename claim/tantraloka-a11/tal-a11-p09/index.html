tal-a11-v86 — That sovereignty, that abandonment, that knowledge of Shiva-selfhood, that absorption, that rejection—all of this occurs by order and disorder.

tal-a11-v87 — Therefore those mantras, purifying and of varied forms, spread a variegated purification in the siddhanta, vama, daksha, and related streams.

tal-a11-v88 — The mantras of the sequence called the unsurpassed triad are indeed all all-giving; but primacy belongs to one of them somewhere.

tal-a11-v89 — Therefore, in the scripture called Śrīpūrva, the arrangement of the mantras beginning with parāpara is set forth in the mantra-path, in a purifying manner.

tal-a11-v90 — Purifying-ness applies to the impurity of the goddesses, the triad, the three deities, and the eight faces/limbs.
