tal-a11-v41 — The disciple is delighted by Shambhu because the hope of enjoyment has departed; difference is expanded by Shambhu's self-willed power.

tal-a11-v42 — The Lord always rests in the non-different self; thus the threefold path differs as gross, subtle, and supreme.

tal-a11-v43 — The one who has entered the measurable part is called the city-principle-phase self; now the form situated in the maternal part is determined in three ways.

tal-a11-v44 — The measure-natured form of the path that reaches the mother-part is called a word, because comprehension merges into self-nature.

tal-a11-v45 — That indeed is the word, the mantra, fallen from agitation, when the secret-speaker becomes silent and established, because the mother.

tal-a11-v46 — Nevertheless, he does not abandon the form of the self of reflection; by that he is the self of measure and reflection, not like a man subject to agitation.

tal-a11-v47 — In the Trika teaching, the mantras and words spoken by him have an own form that is indeed undivided, supreme in agitated non-vibration.

tal-a11-v48 — When indifference is abandoned and agitation descends, the path of phonemes comes into play; in the Mother-portion that valid cognition would be the one stated before.

tal-a11-v49 — She is, however, non-different because of her full essence; therefore, in truth, each letter is one, beginning from patience.

tal-a11-v50 — Having done [the rite] in supreme Śaiva, the sixteen-syllabled [mantra] is declared from visarga; there, so much śakti-vibration is determined beforehand.
