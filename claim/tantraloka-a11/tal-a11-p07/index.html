tal-a11-v61 — The path that proceeds by calculation from yoga is declared to consist of parts; in pure union of measurability it has a share of subtle and gross.

tal-a11-v62 — In the state of the reality-path and world-path, the guru follows in order the form situated above of the measurable, the means, and the knowers.

tal-a11-v63 — Let the sound-self indeed be seen by yogins who taste rest in rising consciousness, abiding in meditation on the supreme self only.

tal-a11-v64 — From the power of all designations, unrestrained powers are created; in the meaning that arises together with the self, they extend as far as earth.

tal-a11-v65 — Considering in the ground of consciousness, those streams of letters bear consciousness as true valid cognition, without distinction among so many objects.

tal-a11-v66 — Children and animals are also cognizers; even those who share no convention have, through innate refinement, this very self-awareness.

tal-a11-v67 — Having resorted to various distinct things, they attain variegated cognizer-hood; rootedness is in this natural, infinite consciousness of varṇas.

tal-a11-v68 — Conventions pass away; even if they enter an unsymbolic state. But without this, all conventions are made again and again.

tal-a11-v69 — By unceasingness they would make an insurmountable regress; a child is instructed there by that, through seeking convention.

tal-a11-v70 — Even when this is pointed out with a finger, the mind is not nonconceptual; conceptualization is rooted in word, and word lives by convention.
