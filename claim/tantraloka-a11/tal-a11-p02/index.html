tal-a11-v11 — For the establishment of ultimate reality through instruction and immersion in that, the god who brings understanding is clearly manifested.

tal-a11-v12 — Because even in the Shiva principle a union of kalās is declared; and the cosmic egg is named the cause of the establishment of world-divisions.

tal-a11-v13 — They call 'covering' that which ends in power, as long as it exists; although before, even in the principle called Śiva, there is the world-path.

tal-a11-v14 — It has been said; yet even in the unobstructed, there is no occurrence of veiling in this. But thus, having left earth, [one is] in the powers of prakṛti and māyā.

tal-a11-v15 — Even in non-obstruction, how does the egg originate? Here our teachers say it is the five beginning with earth.

tal-a11-v16 — This appears directly, and nothing else exists; in measurability, gross and subtle distinctions arise through instrumentality.

tal-a11-v17 — From the exultation of the agent, in the state of agency, the thirty principles are clearly set forth as having the nature of difference; their non-difference is considered night.

tal-a11-v18 — When distinctions such as effectness and instrumentness dissolve, in the state of supreme independent expansion, they sang the fivefold ending in Shiva.

tal-a11-v19 — It is declared abundantly in the glorious Kalottara and other texts; thus these five principles indeed pervade the whole world.

tal-a11-v20 — In the five-mantra body, sadyojata and the rest are said up to Ishana; there, from earth to the end of sky.
