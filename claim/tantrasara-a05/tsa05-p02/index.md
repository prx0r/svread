## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a05-v20** — Therefore in these articulation-levels, resting each separately two-wise or wholly, he makes the rest-tattva attainable, distinct from that body, breath and the rest.
**tsa-a05-v21** — Keeping in mind that very secret of the articulation of the creation-dissolution seed, let him impress the concept; and in these rests, each separately, five states become, from the depth of entry.
**tsa-a05-v22** — Therein first bliss, from contact with the portion of fullness; then arising, a moment, in the embodimentlessness of the habitual; then tremor, from self-power overpowering, loosening body-identity; then sleep, from the dissolution of outwardness.
**tsa-a05-v23** — Thus, the non-self-being dissolved into self-being: from the all-pervasiveness of the self, the non-self-being dissolves in the self — therefore whirling, from the arising of great pervasion.
**tsa-a05-v24** — These are the levels beginning with waking, up to the limit beyond the fourth.
**tsa-a05-v25** — And these levels occur in the entry of the triangle-root-heart-pace-upward-kuṇḍalinī-wheel.
**tsa-a05-v26** — Thus in articulation-rest: what is supreme pulsation, the knowable dissolved into rest; what is dawning knowable; what is already-dawned knowable — that triad of marks we will say at the proper time.
**tsa-a05-v27** — And the supreme mark here is the yogini-heart.
**tsa-a05-v28** — Therein the primary pulsation-form, by contraction-and-expansion nature, arising as pair-form, from gaining the rest of the visarga-portion — enough.
**tsa-a05-v29** — Non-luminous: here entry.
**tsa-a05-v30** — First in self-knowing, then resting in the knowable, let him complete the knowable.
**tsa-a05-v31** — In the complete, here he rests — the distinction of measurer and measured; quickly let him withdraw.
**tsa-a05-v32** — Having pervaded, then he rests; those would be, together with emptiness, the six means-levels.
**tsa-a05-v33** — Breath and the rest are not-after-vyāna; and dissolved in it, the manifestation beginning with waking.
**tsa-a05-v34** — Established in practice here, in the abode of creation-dissolution contemplation, let him grow before long.
**tsa-a05-v37** — In this articulation flashes a sound, a letter, almost an imitation of the unmanifest; its primary form is in the creation-dissolution seed — from its practice comes the gain of supreme-consciousness. For instance: in kā, at the end of mā, in sācke, in anacke, or articulated within, remembered — the touch of consciousness-pulsation, equally distinguished, complete because it disregards convention; even for those who regard convention, words producing its meaning like a mirage and the rest; from the touch of unsurpassable consciousness, let him unify the heart-throat-lips, the pair of twelve-ends and the heart — the letter-secret.
**tsa-a05-v38** — The white-yellow-and-rest inner letter, born immediately after the contemplation of inner touch, being manifested, causes consciousness to be experienced — so some say.
**tsa-a05-v39** — By the absence of the expressible, from consciousness-pulsation, by the two checks of moon-and-sun motion.
**tsa-a05-v40** — Of which, by equal entry, complete in the form of the consciousness-seed-mass letter.
**tsa-a05-v43** — The operation of mudrā-revelation we will say.
