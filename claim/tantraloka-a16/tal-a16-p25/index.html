tal-a16-v241 — By necessity, the mind's activity is only in the body; beyond that there is no continuity, because it is constructed through one mind and united senses.

tal-a16-v242 — Local activity and knowledge of the self occur there; that object-knowledge does not deserve continuity in other bodies.

tal-a16-v243 — When that mind's body activity certainly becomes pure through yoga, mantra, and rite, then the knower acts.

tal-a16-v244 — As pure mind sees quickly even when far, so in initiation of understanding there is seeing of those worlds.

tal-a16-v245 — When one wishes initiation free from birth and so on, then the plan 'I lift from this' is proper deliberation.

tal-a16-v246 — Even without purification, the purifier rite there by mantra performs the birth sequence free from element union.

tal-a16-v247 — In the single purifier rite excluding birth and so on, only that purifier container should be imagined.

tal-a16-v248 — In initiation by substance union, sesame, ghee, unbroken rice, and grains are offered by respective mantra for birth union and separation.

tal-a16-v249 — When knowledge initiation is done to the disciple, then intensely by that mantra speech force one sees from the non-conceptual.

tal-a16-v250 — Concept is indeed speech-composed; that reflective one is mantra-self, and reflection is pure and bondage-natured.
