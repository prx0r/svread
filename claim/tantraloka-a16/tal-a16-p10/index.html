tal-a16-v100 — This threefold rite has limits at the forehead, the hole, and the twelve-end.

tal-a16-v91 — By that great light, all this world is in me, and it is not projected by anyone like an external reflection.

tal-a16-v92 — I am maker of this; it is not dependent on another and is established by me; thus purified by experience of great pervasion.

tal-a16-v93 — A creature having gone to equality with me is called liberated; by succession of heating, rubbing, pouring and so on, fire-nature.

tal-a16-v94 — Like an iron ball goes, the guru thus to Shiva-nature; then standing before, or a previously manifested form.

tal-a16-v95 — In indirect initiation, or fashioned by kusha etc, the dead disciple having been seen and worshipped, one should place his path with flowers and so on.

tal-a16-v96 — By which path the teacher primarily desires initiation, having placed him in the body, there other should be included; this is the rule.

tal-a16-v97 — When the path to be purified has been placed, the purifier mantra is placed there as desired, according to the propriety of the thing purified.

tal-a16-v98 — Sometimes, without placing the thing to be purified, mere placement of the purifier makes it pure itself; what is purified becomes pure by the purifier's power.

tal-a16-v99 — By wish one may use another, parapara, or para rite; having resorted according to their combination, one performs nyasa of the six paths.
