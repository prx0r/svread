tal-a16-v171 — Because direct initiation depends on continuity between two, its result is full; disagreement brings ruin.

tal-a16-v172 — In indirect immortal initiation, the guru alone, by the glory of action and knowledge, leads the disciple to the desired abode.

tal-a16-v173 — When action and knowledge are undivided, their continuity is likewise; action is again divided because it is of that nature.

tal-a16-v174 — The holy Svachanda treatise says result differs by impression distinction for disciples and guru, even when action and the rest are undivided.

tal-a16-v175 — Variety exists in enjoyment because continuity between purifier and purified produces such variety, and variety is the life of distinction.

tal-a16-v176 — Thus the path purified by those same mouths is joined; wishing to enjoy some principle, he becomes enjoyer endowed with that power.

tal-a16-v177 — Here, if auspicious actions exist, enjoyment variegation is such; in action purification, variegation is otherwise.

tal-a16-v178 — Enjoyment is joined by immediate exit or by body, then by practice or at body's end; thus it is fourfold.

tal-a16-v179 — The previous eight-division is said to become thirty-two through yoga; liberation, though one, is the seed called convention.

tal-a16-v180 — For child and known quick death, devoid of power; for aged or powerful, aiming; purification is twofold by purification or non-purification.
