tal-a16-v41 — Then, by the yoga of uprooting and unwrapping, revolving left, one becomes the nourisher of one's own breath, full of kundali nectar.

tal-a16-v42 — The filament of nectar from the heart lotus, taken by leftward circuit, melts the tissue by gaining ascent to the heart center.

tal-a16-v43 — Taking the pure moon essence, slightly red and yellow, into the breath-body that is like the tip of an elephant's trunk.

tal-a16-v44 — Having exited suddenly by the left path of one's own self, one should enter, nourishing the heart-lotus moon wheel called apāna.

tal-a16-v45 — That situated deity wheel should be nourished by that essence; by this method, all rasas, blood, and the rest are nourished.

tal-a16-v46 — One should gather the tissues in group sequence, one by one, or only in the middle of the collision of sun, moon, and fire.

tal-a16-v47 — Then, taking the living being that is light-form called breath-power, one should equalize the living being by the goddess wheel through meditation.

tal-a16-v48 — That itself is the chief nourishment: the identity of enjoyer and enjoyed; that great one is the fire-casket, blossoming, three-cornered, time-self.

tal-a16-v49 — When, in a half-ghatika sequence, rest-yoga occurs amid blood and similar essence-flow movements, the mass is present.

tal-a16-v50 — By a hundred repetitions, pacification of the bound soul occurs; having practiced there for some time with undivided mind.
