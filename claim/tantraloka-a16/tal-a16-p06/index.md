## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a16-v51** — As stated in the Cintamani, by that form the yogi obtains siddhi without doubt; that secret is clear like prana.
**tal-a16-v52** — Even for an unseen bound soul, the rite is thus; entered into the sacrificial ground, he is killed there and accomplished.
**tal-a16-v53** — One joined to the cakra there is called a hero-victim; but one killed elsewhere is treated by totality or partly.
**tal-a16-v54** — Offered to the deity, that is indeed held to be an external victim; kingdom, gain, stability, devotion to Shiva, and identity with that follow.
**tal-a16-v55** — Shiva-knowledge, attainment of the mantra-world, retinue, and union with that belong to the bound soul by equality from external or hero dharma.
**tal-a16-v56** — Flowers and the like also share that gain; by Shiva worship, one means, the lord of gods is of the nature of blessing the universe.
**tal-a16-v57** — By sacrifice alone he blesses; what is not moving and still? Therefore even a non-hero, though joined with doubt and the like, is compassionate.
**tal-a16-v58** — Never adopt a harming thought in the victim rite; this is a great benefit to the bound soul, though it may be unpleasant at that time.
**tal-a16-v59** — Disease-cutting, medicine, austerity, and use are an example here; as stated in the glorious Mrtyunjaya, bond-cutting is done for the bound soul.
**tal-a16-v60** — By separation from the three impurities the body does not grow; by cutting the mass of merit and demerit the body falls away indeed.
