tal-a16-v161 — By whichever mantra creation in this tantra is made, by that alone the mantra-knower should initiate; thus is the supreme command.

tal-a16-v162 — Thus seventy kinds are declared by purifier difference; when initiation with these mantras occurs without purifying nyasa.

tal-a16-v163 — Then it is to be known seventyfold, devoid of birth and so on; the purifiable difference is then to be stated, and that too is told in brief.

tal-a16-v164 — Tattva is fourfold from differences of one, three, five, six, and thirty-six; path and likewise four egg groups are from five and one differences.

tal-a16-v165 — Thus the purifiable is tenfold; thirtyfold from the threefold rule of that; by purifiable-purifier difference, hundreds and twenty-one.

tal-a16-v166 — Here too, by nyasa union, in purifiable sound and action, one hundred twenty-one kinds, devoid of birth and so on, becomes.

tal-a16-v167 — Consisting of birth and so on, thus in hundredfold scripture, scripture would be seventy plus; that also from substance-knowledge difference.

tal-a16-v168 — Twofold; eighty-five would be hundreds plus eleven; from enjoyment-liberation inquiry twofold, that is declared.

tal-a16-v169 — Purification of both inauspicious and auspicious things makes enjoyment twofold, with distinction across three times.

tal-a16-v170 — Through one, two, or all powers, enjoyment becomes seven or eightfold, and guru-disciple order divides it twofold.
