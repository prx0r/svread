## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a16-v301** — Knowledge becomes manifest in the fitting way; the yogin who takes refuge in yoga becomes united and attains identification with that reality.
**tal-a16-v302** — Then all states depend on guru initiation, mantra, and scripture, especially for wicked beings among past, present, and future.
**tal-a16-v303** — Purification of actions should be done; it is not for one desiring enjoyment, while good souls are distinguished from those seeking worldly pleasures like kingdom and heaven.
**tal-a16-v304** — Having abandoned transcendent enjoyment, one desiring his enjoyment performs there a material initiation with ghee, sesame, and similar offerings, even in auspicious matters.
**tal-a16-v305** — The best teacher should offer, saying 'I purify his action'; but in knowledge-filled initiation, that becomes pure by union.
**tal-a16-v306** — By the force of the guru rooted in self-awareness, destruction occurs; when his inauspicious actions become pure, then auspiciousness follows.
**tal-a16-v307** — By relying on one's own gradation in the path, this production arises, from the completion of enjoyment of fruits obtained by auspicious ripening sequence.
**tal-a16-v308** — Where this one is placed and stands, when future action is destroyed, it concerns future beings differing between the first body and another body.
**tal-a16-v309** — When the inauspicious part is purified, there is no decrease of enjoyment itself; this enjoyer constantly enjoys from the abode of maya to the end.
**tal-a16-v310** — Nothing gives painful fruit even in the path beginning with the body; then in the abode of maya all pleasant enjoyments are enjoyed.
