**tal-a16-v111** — The rise is twelve angulas beyond the body, equal; then seventy-two ten and two are in the body from the head end.
**tal-a16-v112** — Starting from the feet, O fair-hipped one, up to the anahata region, even beyond the body, by rest of consciousness, by force of imagination.
**tal-a16-v113** — Body-ness thus; therefore the rise should be twelve angulas; thus to determine here this is said one hundred eight.
**tal-a16-v114** — Then city nyasa up to the ankle: earth; here sixteen cities; therefore by one-angula pervasion each from Lakula onward.
**tal-a16-v115** — Up to the two testicles, three angulas; but goat-testicle then in oceans; in eight deva-yogas two indeed from each angula foot.
**tal-a16-v116** — Thus up to pradhana forty-six angulas; fifty-six cities thus; but in eastern ground sixteen.
**tal-a16-v117** — Then by half-angula pervasion, six cities in three angulas; four, pair; in one one; and city in angula.
**tal-a16-v118** — In attachment, male cities lord number thus; in six angulas; in anger-lord city one in pair; and this anda; and know.
**tal-a16-v119** — Of Samvarta and Jyotisha thus, of kala and tattva in order, of Shura and Pancanta cities fixed, and one-pair-ness.
**tal-a16-v120** — In Shri Purva Shastra that is said by supreme lord Shambhu: from northward order two-one difference in vidya etc three.
