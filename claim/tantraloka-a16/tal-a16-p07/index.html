tal-a16-v61 — By that this killing is not so called — initiation is variegated-formed; of the fetter-bound, separation from breaths is indeed killing.

tal-a16-v62 — But this is yojana only — of the beast for the god, in offering; therefore relying on the god's word: give many beasts, thus.

tal-a16-v63 — Offered, body regained, offered again — six times thus — whoever here is the six-birth supreme beast.

tal-a16-v64 — As gold purified by the firing process is proclaimed — what perfection would he not himself bestow, or is he not liberated?

tal-a16-v65 — It is said in the Ananda scripture: who abandons mantra-consecration, reviles initiations, gives goddesses and mantras without rule, leads astray.

tal-a16-v66 — Having gained initiation-mantra and the rest, deluded by son and the rest he abandons — then attaining humanity, he does so again.

tal-a16-v67 — Thus from one to seven births — twofold, two-footed or four-footed — the beast is born for goddess-oblation purpose.

tal-a16-v68 — Given by the giver, through that door he goes from union to Shiva — thus considering, strange is the employment of beasts.

tal-a16-v69 — The desired-to-be-enjoyed should not be made into that beast elsewhere — nor is this one suitable — so understanding, remove it.

tal-a16-v70 — But if desire is for that beast in particular, then offer it — give so many beasts, as said by Maheshin.
