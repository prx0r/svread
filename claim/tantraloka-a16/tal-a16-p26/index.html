tal-a16-v251 — Because eternal beginningless boon shiva identity is constructed, by its union the teacher's concept may attain shivahood.

tal-a16-v252 — In shri sara shastra this is spoken by the supreme lord: comprehension of meaning is object-subject-formed.

tal-a16-v253 — That very mantra-power, spread out in the mantra continuum, is declared as mantra-power of reflection-nature.

tal-a16-v254 — Reflection is twofold and divides mantras by purity and impurity; this is said in the holy Pauṣkara, and Brahma, Vishnu, and others are egg-born.

tal-a16-v255 — The primary attached beings are sattvic, rajasic, and tamasic; through their impure reflection the teacher becomes absorbed in that.

tal-a16-v256 — Vaishnava and others are declared bound souls, not fit for the lord's rule; mantras standing on the pure path, such as Shiva-bhattaraka and others, are fit.

tal-a16-v257 — The teacher seen by holy Matanga and others is absorbed in that and is Shiva; but can independent speech union alone produce reflection?

tal-a16-v258 — From where before? If from reflection, from where? In determination, the first would give unwaveringness from the teacher.

tal-a16-v259 — If there is transmitted speech, it too comes from another teacher; in previous order, before, it is from the first teacher.

tal-a16-v260 — Speech is indeed transmitted and exists today also; let it be grasped, even if such speech by force is independent.
