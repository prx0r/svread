tal-a16-v181 — Mahesha speaks a command in the glorious cave concerning the guru's duty when death is near.

tal-a16-v182 — Seeing a disciple afflicted by old age and disease, the guru should transfer him and place him in the supreme principle.

tal-a16-v183 — Thirty-five distinctions of the guru, or of guru and disciple, are declared; seventy arise from the stated twofold calculation.

tal-a16-v184 — Having destroyed those previously stated initiation distinctions, the guru speaks enumeration for attainment of practiced knowledge.

tal-a16-v185 — The stated number, increased by forty, should be divided by seventy, and one should calculate initiation distinctions.

tal-a16-v186 — Here five lakhs, ninety-seven thousand, and eight hundred initiation distinctions are given in the Malini Tantra.

tal-a16-v187 — Seventy-fold and thirty-fold groups, purification, one principle and beyond, attendants, six rites, and duties yield four distinctions.

tal-a16-v188 — That knowledge composed of substance, free from birth and endowed with that, is thirty-five-fold from the union of enjoyment and liberation.

tal-a16-v189 — Enjoyment is thirty-two-fold by auspicious, pure, impure, and time distinctions; liberation is threefold; duty distinctions are seventy.

tal-a16-v190 — Initiation is divided in many ways by distinctions of purifier and purified; without the purified, it is seventy-fold.
