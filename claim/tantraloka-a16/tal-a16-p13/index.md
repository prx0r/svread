## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a16-v121** — Because order lacks essence at the beginning, niyati comes before kalā, or the two principles are designated by mutual names.
**tal-a16-v122** — The arrangement of Ekavīra, Śikheśa, and Śrīkaṇṭha before time is described as niyati.
**tal-a16-v123** — Thus the six principles beginning with puruṣa are seen designated by mutual names and arranged in eighteen angulas.
**tal-a16-v124** — Then the eight principles standing in māyā, each half an angula, occupy four angulas up to thumb measure.
**tal-a16-v125** — Thus twenty-eight cities are in two eyes; three cities come from puruṣa at night, two are less by one-third angula, in order.
**tal-a16-v126** — Two and two make five cities in vidyā in four angulas; then eight aiśa cities follow in four half-angulas in order.
**tal-a16-v127** — Then three in two and two thus in four; sādāśiva is fivefold, and one vasu is in the sun.
**tal-a16-v128** — Sixteenfold rasa-viśikha, two vasu, vasu-moon thus; city groups are four, rasa-ocean, pair-eye, and suns there, with angulas in order.
**tal-a16-v129** — One hundred eighteen cities are arranged in this body measured as eighty-four; thus in the remainder is the pervading śiva principle.
**tal-a16-v130** — Thus another method, called parāpara, is described; its enumeration of eight arrows, made by the sky sage, is to be known in the next method.
