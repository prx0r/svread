## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a16-v01** — Now the rite described by Shiva is set forth for attaining disciplehood: when a teacher wishes to appoint one established in the vow into disciplehood, guruhood, or adept-hood.
**tal-a16-v10** — Ganapati, guru, Parama, Paramesthi, and the prior-siddha speech-field-lord, thus seven are named in the guru-row rite to be fully worshipped by our gurus.
**tal-a16-v02** — After performing consecration, on the second day one should draw the mandala in the collective rite and elsewhere as stated.
**tal-a16-v03** — By the count of six, eight, and double twenty-four, the five-wheel system is called in the scripture named Shri-prior.
**tal-a16-v04** — Thirty-two and its double, in the Shri-mat-Traishira view, and the connection with countless wheels, are described in the Shri-Siddha beginning.
**tal-a16-v05** — Therefore, as the sacrifice is measured by the wheel, one should worship accordingly; by that, here one should draw three tridents.
**tal-a16-v06** — In the three tridents, the three goddesses, by rotational course and by middle-left-right distinction, should become fully worshipped.
**tal-a16-v07** — Maintenance before the mandala will be taught briefly: having drawn the mandala, one wipes it with scented cloth.
**tal-a16-v08** — Having bathed, the guru, before the mandala here, should worship the external deities and the door deities at the door place.
**tal-a16-v09** — In the front part of the mandala, from the northeast direction in order up to the southeast, one should fully worship Ganesha and the others up to the field protectors.
