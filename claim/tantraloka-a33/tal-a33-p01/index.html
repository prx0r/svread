tal-a33-v00 — Now the unification, arrived in due course, is discussed.

tal-a33-v01 — Since it was said the triad is to be worshipped together with the cakra-divisions: here, in this manner, is their unification.

tal-a33-v10 — Arghīśa and Bhārabhūti, Sthiti, Sthāṇu, Hara, thus; Jhaṇṭi, Bhautika, Sadyoja, Anugraha, Krūra, Sainika.

tal-a33-v02 — Viśvā, her Mistress, Hāraudrī, Vīranetrī, Ambikā, and Gurvī, thus: the goddesses shown in the Siddhāvīra, in the six-spoked wheel.

tal-a33-v03 — Māheśī, Brāhmaṇī, Skāndī, Vaiṣṇavī, Aindrī, Yamātmikā, Cāmuṇḍā indeed, and Yogīśī — thus eight, or the Aghorī-group and the rest.

tal-a33-v04 — Joined with the twelve — the mothers of Fire, Nirṛti, Wind, and Īśa: Nandā, Bhadrā, Jayā, Kālī, Karālī, Vikṛtānanā.

tal-a33-v05 — Kroṣṭukī and Bhīmamudrā, Vāyuvegā, Hayānanā, Gambhīrā, and Ghoṣaṇī, thus: the rule in the twenty-four-spoked wheel.

tal-a33-v06 — Siddhi, Vṛddhi, Dyuti, Lakṣmī, Medhā, Kānti, Sudhā, Dhṛti, Dīpti, Puṣṭi, Mati, Kīrti, Susthiti, Sugati, Smṛti.

tal-a33-v07 — Suprabhā — sixteen in all, thus: the Śrīkaṇṭha-headed powers. Bali and Balinanda, Daśagrīva, Hara, Haya.

tal-a33-v08 — Mādhava in the six-spoked wheel; but in the twelve-spoked these are remembered: Dakṣa, Caṇḍa, Hara, Śauṇḍī, Pramatha, Bhīma and Manmatha.

tal-a33-v09 — Śakuni, Sumati, Nanda, Gopāla and Pitāmaha; Śrīkaṇṭha, Ananta, Sūkṣma, and Trimūrti, Śaṃbareśvara.
