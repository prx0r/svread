tal-a33-v21 — But in the division of those two, in the fashioning of śakti and her possessor: the sound-mass and Mālinī, of agitation-nature, such a body.

tal-a33-v22 — Thus in the splitting of inner awareness, really the triad called Anuttara, Icchā, Unmeṣa — from which the universe is awareness.

tal-a33-v23 — But in union with Ānanda, Īśa, Ūrmi, that is declared the hexad; through semivowel-sibilant union that is declared the octad.

tal-a33-v24 — Then, in the absence of the four amṛta-vowels, it would be a dodecad; in union with them, called sixteen — thus until innumerability.

tal-a33-v25 — Since it clearly bears the universe from single-awareness-bearing on, bearing part-of-part awarenesses at the end.

tal-a33-v26 — Hence the fifty's oneness, vowel-manifest-variety, eight vargas, letter-division, eighty-one kalā-rise.

tal-a33-v27 — Thus shown before, from half-mora-bearing; for even vowel-half exists, of the svarita a half-mora.

tal-a33-v28 — From its beginning the udātta, that said by the pada-knower; thus this consciousness, of sacrificand-form-awareness-nature.

tal-a33-v29 — Unbroken from consciousness, this wheel-horizon of wheels; by lordship-and-veil division employ it manifoldly.

tal-a33-v30 — Parāparā, Parā, and the other: creation, maintenance, concealment; but the fourth, Mātṛsadbhāva-formed, is called repose.
