tal-a35-v01 — Now the union of all scriptures is told: here is the whole ancient practice.

tal-a35-v10 — If come from the east, that — this certainty — of former time; and not consciousness-serenity alone, the whole sequence of practice.

tal-a35-v02 — Having sought prasiddhi, that very certainty is called āgama; for conjunction and exclusion depend on prasiddhi.

tal-a35-v03 — In dependence-on-self of the two's manifestation-groups, what would be their course? Even direct perception is born of eye, self, lamp, object and the rest.

tal-a35-v04 — It requires prasiddhi at the root — thus-natured, wrapped all around: born, one alone, hungry — a child.

tal-a35-v05 — What should he do, what take, by what see, what approach? Indeed, even in a hundred-things-strewn place, whatever is his —

tal-a35-v06 — of one seeing, or even smelling, of one touching: consciousness is serene, taking that very principle at once — it bears conjunction and exclusion.

tal-a35-v07 — Well! Even the serene consciousness, whatever goes with specific meaning, it too is previously fashioned by vāsanā-shaped reflection.

tal-a35-v08 — Not born of direct-perception-inference-etc-outer-proof-serenity; dependent-on-vāsanā previously — this is mere intuition — not.

tal-a35-v09 — Not the desire for eating-mud of a man, a child's, arises; dependent-on-vāsanā previously — if reflection — she and vāsanā.
