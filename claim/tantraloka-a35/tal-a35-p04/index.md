## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a35-v31** — Of it, whatever is the attainable highest state, that is called Trika; through non-severance of all-differences, that very kula is called.
**tal-a35-v32** — As high-low-marked, in divided body-parts, one living being should be; so Trika, in all, by the scriptures.
**tal-a35-v33** — In the venerable Kālī-kula, and it is said, devoid of five streams, of ten-and-eighteen-difference, this essence is declared.
**tal-a35-v34** — In the flower is the fragrance; in the sesame is the oil; in the body is the soul; in the water is the nectar; as, so indeed, of scriptures kula within is established.
**tal-a35-v35** — That one alone āgama — this, varied, in varied authority-holders; so indeed she, prasiddhi — for, goes in own-flock and other-flock.
**tal-a35-v36** — Sāṅkhya, Yoga, Pāñcarātra, Vedas — and indeed, not should one censure; because all are Śiva-born: thus in the Svacchanda teaching.
**tal-a35-v37** — From one āgama — and these, in pieces plucked out, in the world should be āgamas; and by them the people wander, deluded.
**tal-a35-v38** — In many-āgama sides too, subject-difference is speakable, necessarily held in high-low-ness, for establishing validity.
**tal-a35-v39** — Otherwise not at all anyone's validity is established, surely; permanence-and-noncontradiction, thus, for us are no proof-cause.
**tal-a35-v40** — In this part too — of that very one, validity would be — from that utterance; otherwise in non-realization, in fixed non-being, in preference.
