tal-a35-v21 — As long as Śiva-hood is not in him, that long the self-following — that very — her, this certainty — he does not doubt.

tal-a35-v22 — In another doubting, she would there much esteem; thus, in future-Śiva-hood — this, prasiddhi — esteem, surely.

tal-a35-v23 — One alone āgama — and this, by the Lord, the all-seer, shown, which on the path, in activity and in cessation, standing.

tal-a35-v24 — In dharma, artha, kāma, mokṣa, through complete-incomplete-and-other-differences, in various fruits: one means, the Śāmbhava āgama.

tal-a35-v25 — In that — from subject-variety — in variably-fruit-giving, even the varied-means-teaching brings no contradiction.

tal-a35-v26 — Worldly, Vedic, Sāṅkhya, Yoga and the rest, Pāñcarātra, Buddha-Arhat-logic-scriptures, category-sequence-texts.

tal-a35-v27 — Siddhānta-tantra-Śakti and the rest, all Brahman-born, because in the venerable Svacchanda and the rest taught through Sadyoja-and-other-differences.

tal-a35-v28 — As in one place even, in Veda and the rest, going-to-that-that-āśrama, another-saṃskāra — here too; so the liṅga-uddhāra and the rest.

tal-a35-v29 — And as there, in the earlier āśrama, not from the later āśrama fruit comes; so from Pāñcarātra and the rest, not Śiva-nature.

tal-a35-v30 — One alone āgama — then there, from the worldly-scripture, from the beginning — from Vaiṣṇava, from Buddhist, from Śaiva — all indeed is established.
