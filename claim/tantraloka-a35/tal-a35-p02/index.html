tal-a35-v11 — Root, prasiddhi — that is proof: 'everywhere indeed' let it be accepted; in seeking dependence-of-the-prior, she sometimes herself.

tal-a35-v12 — For in that one all-knowing-nature, without doubt it shone before; practice is nowhere entire in a proof.

tal-a35-v13 — Hence — by mere non-all-knowing-priority, this is not established; in many-all-knowing-priorities no proof there is at all.

tal-a35-v14 — Gleaming with a hundred certainties of pleasure-release-and-their-causes; of that reflection-nature — it: Bhairava, the Supreme Lord.

tal-a35-v15 — Then too, from part-part union — she, certainty, tradition, scripture, or, having resorted to, spread, should make worlds practice.

tal-a35-v16 — By that very childhood, all delighting in carrying practice, being, co-depend on the Śaiva indeed, the first āgama.

tal-a35-v17 — But the incomplete others: hence not shareholders of liberation-fruit, co-depend; as far as — but that far shareholders of that fruit.

tal-a35-v18 — Even at childhood's end, whatever to-eat — this food is set going: by that certainty alone, not from perception, not from inference, not from impossibility.

tal-a35-v19 — And not anywhere here, from fault-hope-doubt: cessation; and certainty, born-of-non-cognition: understanding, word-natured.

tal-a35-v20 — Of the mother, nature — which, in her, one doubts — not this, ever; by self-making alone, of the all-knower — for: the doubter.
