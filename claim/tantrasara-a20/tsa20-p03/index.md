## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a20-v36** — Here, having placed the universe-made vessel on the base, having sated the wheel of deities, one should sate oneself with the saluted vessel. If no vessel: auspicious folded shell-hands; or with the right hand shaped as a vessel — auspicious — with the two hands, the right laid above, made jointless, folded shell-hands; with the falling drops the vetālas and guhyakas are pleased, by the flow Bhairava. Here entry is to be given to no one; if by mistake one has entered, one should make no doubt; having made up one's mind, one should perform the wheel-rite twice over. Then fried foods, meals, and the rest should be scattered freely before them, as desired — in a hidden house or, without code-names, one should join all by the word 'deity': thus is the hero-mixture rite.
**tsa-a20-v37** — Then at the end with gifts, betel, clothes, and the rest one should satisfy them: thus this is the most excellent image-rite.
**tsa-a20-v38** — Even the unseen-maṇḍala one, by the image-rite worshipping the festival days, within a year alone gains the fruit stated for putrakas — without twilight practice and the rest. Thus for the old, the enjoyers, and women is this rule: when power has descended, to be taught by the guru.
**tsa-a20-v39** — Now the sacred-thread rule.
**tsa-a20-v40** — And it, in the Ratnamālā, the Triśiromata, the Siddhāmata, and the rest, is preceded by rule and fulfills the Lord's command; and this is said in the Tantrāloka: without the sacred thread, all is fruitless.
**tsa-a20-v41** — Here, from bright Āṣāḍha up to the Kula-full-moon day's end, the sacred thread is to be done; Kārttika's dark fifteenth fulfills the Kula-wheel and the daily wheel: so say the knowers of the Nitya-tantra.
**tsa-a20-v42** — Māgha's bright fifteenth: so say the knowers of Bhairavakulormi.
**tsa-a20-v43** — The southern-course-end fifteenth: so say the knowers of the Tantrasadbhāva.
**tsa-a20-v44** — Here, according to one's means, having worshipped the God, having sated with oblation, one should give the sacred thread: from gold-, pearl-, and jewel-made beginning, one should make it even down to cloth, thread, cotton, and kuśa-grass-wombed.
**tsa-a20-v45** — And that with tattva-number knots, and with pada, kalā, world, letter, and mantra-number knots: one knee-ending, another navel-ending, another throat-ending, another on the head — thus four sacred threads to the God and to the guru, contemplating forms completely full of all paths, one should give; to the rest, one.
**tsa-a20-v46** — Then a great festival is to be held — the four-month rite, seven days and three days: thus chief and fallback courses. When there are means, monthly the sacred thread, or in the four months, or once. In not doing it, one should murmur expiation. Even the knower, even possessed of means, incurs by not doing; in greed-covered non-doing of knowledge, from the disgrace of knowledge.
**tsa-a20-v47** — When, even having attained wisdom, the Lord's teaching is defiled — then one needs expiation.
**tsa-a20-v49** — Thus: this is the sacred-thread rule.
**tsa-a20-v50** — At knowledge-gaining and the rest, at the end of worldly festival too — everywhere, excess of consciousness-blaze and the nearness of the deity-wheel especially come about: thus, considering such excess, just such a distinction, in after-rite and the rest, one should perform.
**tsa-a20-v52** — One should request the guru, complete in all scriptures, for the sake of exposition — he too, for his own disciple or even another's, should expound the scripture fit for fit refinement; even for one abiding in a lower teaching, from compassion, his heart made hopeful by the prospect of a descent of power manifested by the Lord's varied will, one should expound — excluding secret instruction.
