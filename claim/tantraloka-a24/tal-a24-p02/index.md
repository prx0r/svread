## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a24-v11** — Some said even that is to be done in the mortuary: having worshipped the Omnipresent, he should perform the entire nyāsa as before.
**tal-a24-v12** — By dissolution-sequence, from the foot to the head-end, just so should he awaken him — with rite, knowledge, and absorption.
**tal-a24-v13** — With bindu he should block the tattva, with the śakti-seed pierce; at the nāda-place he should rub, and with the trident strike.
**tal-a24-v14** — With what lies inside suṣumnā indeed, with emission again and again he should strike all the kalās; then this bound soul trembles.
**tal-a24-v15** — He should raise the left hand — or then join him to the Supreme; without proof, liberation is indeed unbelievable to the deluded.
**tal-a24-v16** — For this purpose this is taught — but not as giving what serves liberation; thus said the Supreme Lord in the venerable Kulagahvara teaching.
**tal-a24-v17** — The to-be-proved, like liberation, is to be inferred through proofs, since beyond the senses; and in the Dīkṣottara the puryaṣṭaka-class offering is taught.
**tal-a24-v18** — Its rite: on the ear-petal lotus, in the middle the god Sadāśiva, the Īśa-Rudra-Hari-Brahman tetrad beginning with the east quarter.
**tal-a24-v19** — Having worshipped, hearing and touch, taste, smell, the twofold body, intellect and ego, and mind — to Brahmā and the rest he should offer in order.
**tal-a24-v20** — Having made their libation, with a hundred fire-offerings the guide effects this renunciate initiation, purifying the city-of-eight.
