param-p11-v100 — Practising this truth-path, missing yoga though, by name — god-world-pleasure-sharer, glad-minded, rejoices long.

param-p11-v92 — Heaven-made, hell-made — this person between bodies; when that breaks, by fitness joining another body.

param-p11-v93 — As in knowing-time the self once shone for him, so then — on body-falling, otherwise it does not become.

param-p11-v94 — Senses stolen, memory lost, breath choked, cut off, vitals pained — enjoyment born of body-impressions.

param-p11-v95 — How could he, being embodied, not be so? By that, deluded though — at death-time the knower falls not from self-truth.

param-p11-v96 — This truth-path, quickly, when gained from the teacher's mouth — by keenest grace-fall, then indeed, unblocked, Śiva.

param-p11-v97 — All-crossed form, by steps resorted, on gaining root in supreme truth — at the end, Śiva-madeness.

param-p11-v98 — But his truth-made stream, unreached, middle-rested — though mind yearns for that step, death may sometime come.

param-p11-v99 — Fallen from yoga, the books call him wonder-enjoyment-world-lord; rested by stage-power, become, in next birth Śiva-becoming.
