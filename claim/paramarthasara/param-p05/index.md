## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**param-p05-v38** — In calm, calm; in glad, glad — so the fool is deluded; amid principles, the Lord is truly not so.
**param-p05-v39** — First removing the shining of that-form in non-self, the supreme self shatters delusion of the non-self-form in self.
**param-p05-v40** — Thus, for one whose goal is gained in cutting delusion-pair at the root, no weighing of other duties ever, for the supreme yogin.
**param-p05-v41** — Earth, nature, Māyā — this triad, fallen into known-form — by nondual-contemplation power becomes mere-being remainder.
**param-p05-v42** — As girdle, earring, bracelet are seen as gold by leaving difference, so in leaving difference all shines as mere being.
**param-p05-v43** — That brahman — supreme, pure, peaceful, nondual-natured, even, whole, deathless, true — rests in shining-formed power.
**param-p05-v44** — Desired, known, done — by the shining-formed: untouched, yet it takes sky-flower-ness.
**param-p05-v45** — By grasp-yoga of power's trident, the whole, in the supreme Lord, in Śiva-name, in supreme truth, is emitted by god of gods.
**param-p05-v46** — Again too, by five-power-spreading order, outside, that varied egg-three created by outer-self-gaining.
