param-p04-v29 — So dharma and adharma, heaven and hell, birth and death, pleasure and pain — caste, life-stage and the rest — though unreal in the self, arise by delusion's force.

param-p04-v30 — This is that darkness: shining in things, the conceit 'I am not the self' arises even where the self is unsurpassed.

param-p04-v31 — Darkness upon darkness — a great burst upon the cheek: taking body, breath and the rest, non-selves, for self.

param-p04-v32 — By the yoga of body, breath, reflection, mind, knowing, and sky-expanse, the self wraps itself — a wonder — in a net, like the net-maker.

param-p04-v33 — By the yoga that shines with one's own knowing glory, unwrapping one's own self: thus supreme Śiva spreads the wondrous play of bondage and release.

param-p04-v34 — Creating, keeping, dissolving; waking, dreaming, deep sleep — they shine in him, in the fourth abode, yet unveiled he shines.

param-p04-v35 — Waking is the universe, through difference; dreaming is splendor, through light's glory; the knower is the sleep state, through massed knowing; beyond that, the fourth.

param-p04-v36 — As cloud, smoke and dust do not soil the sky's surface, so the supreme person stands untouched by Māyā's deformings.

param-p04-v37 — When one jar-sky is pervaded by dust, the others do not get soiled: so these souls, enjoying pleasure-pain differences.
