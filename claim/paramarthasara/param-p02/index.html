param-p02-v11 — Free of every thought, pure, at peace, beyond rising and dissolving — in that supreme principle shines the world, thirty-six in nature.

param-p02-v12 — As towns and villages, varied yet undivided in a mirror's orb, shine out in division, each from each and from the mirror too.

param-p02-v13 — From the purest knowing of supreme Bhairava: though void of division, the world shines forth divided, each from each and thence.

param-p02-v14 — Śiva, power, Sadāśiva, then the Īśvara-Vidyā-formed principle-state — the five powers, in divided nature, He lights up.

param-p02-v15 — Supreme is the freedom that effects the hard thing, the great Lord's own; the goddess, Māyā-power, veils Śiva's self — that is it.

param-p02-v16 — Grasped by Māyā, knowing turns stained and the person becomes a bound beast, fettered by time, art, limit, passion, ignorance.

param-p02-v17 — Here and now I know but little, with my whole self: six sheaths with Māyā are the atom's inner limbs, so it is said.

param-p02-v18 — Like husk settled on rice-grain, separate yet undivided, purity comes by turning to Śiva's path with longing.

param-p02-v19 — Mere pleasure, pain, delusion — from resolve, will, and I-sense; then nature, then the inner organ: intellect, mind, ego in turn.
