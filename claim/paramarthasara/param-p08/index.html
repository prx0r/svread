param-p08-v65 — Beyond direction-time-touch, firm, changeless, the full Lord — sole maker of hosts of powers dissolving and rising.

param-p08-v66 — Awakening to the self as Śiva-made, maker of creation's rules — how could he wander? Spread out, whence, where, what refuge?

param-p08-v67 — Thus proved by reasons: the knower's deed is fruitless. 'Not mine, this — but his': firmly, no fruit in the world.

param-p08-v68 — Thus awakened by contemplation-wind, sacrificing all thoughts in the blazing self-flame, he becomes flame.

param-p08-v69 — Eating this or that, covered by anything, calmed, dwelling anywhere — the all-beings-self is freed.

param-p08-v70 — Though doing horse-rites by hundred-thousands, brahmin-killings by hundred-thousands — the truth-knower, by merits not, by faults not touched, stainless.

param-p08-v71 — Leaving pride, joy, anger, lust, sorrow, fear, greed, delusion; no praise, no formula; dull as if — let him move, opinion-free.

param-p08-v72 — The pride-joy host arises from difference-delusion: how could the nondual-self-awakened be touched by it?

param-p08-v73 — Nothing to praise or offer, separate from him, at all: pleased by praise, freed — then no bow, no formula.
