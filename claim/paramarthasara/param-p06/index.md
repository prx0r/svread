**param-p06-v49** — I indeed, universe-formed, hand-foot-and-all-natured as the body; in all I indeed shine, light-natured.
**param-p06-v50** — Seer, hearer, smeller — bereft of body-senses though, a non-doer too: varied systems, texts, reasonings I indeed compose.
**param-p06-v51** — Thus, duality-thought dissolved, crossing enchanting Māyā: as water in water, milk in milk — dissolved in brahman may one be.
**param-p06-v52** — Thus, in the principle-host, by contemplation attaining Śiva-madeness: what sorrow, what delusion, for the brahman-beholder?
**param-p06-v53** — Deed-fruit, good or bad, comes only from contact with false knowing; hard indeed is contact's fault — like a thief's company, though one is no thief.
**param-p06-v54** — World-convention-makers who tend ignorance here, fools, go to birth and death, bound by duty's locks.
**param-p06-v55** — Yet even duty-formed deeds, heaped in ignorance-time, long-heaped like cotton, perish by knowing-flame-power.
