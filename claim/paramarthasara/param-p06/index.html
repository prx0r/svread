param-p06-v53 — Deed-fruit, good or bad, comes only from contact with false knowing; hard indeed is contact's fault — like a thief's company, though one is no thief.

param-p06-v54 — World-convention-makers who tend ignorance here, fools, go to birth and death, bound by duty's locks.

param-p06-v55 — Yet even duty-formed deeds, heaped in ignorance-time, long-heaped like cotton, perish by knowing-flame-power.
