**param-p03-v24** — The supreme covering! Impurity here: subtle — the Māyā-sheath; gross — the outer body-form; wrapped in three sheaths stands the self.
**param-p03-v25** — Joined to darkness-ignorance, the one self, its own nature, perceives itself manifold through grasper-grasped variety.
**param-p03-v26** — As cane-juice in molasses, sugar, lumps: so every divided state of the supreme self, Śambhu.
**param-p03-v27** — Knower, inner ruler, breath, the all, the born — mere convention, in truth not at all.
**param-p03-v28** — No snake in the rope, yet fear kills till death: delusion's great power no one can tell apart.
