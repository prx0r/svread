tsa-a14-v01 — Now the child-initiation rite.

tsa-a14-v10 — In non-being wealth, the great-mandala-sacrifice is not to be done — indeed.

tsa-a14-v11 — And living animals let him offer.

tsa-a14-v12 — They too become graced thus — by compassion in the animal-rite one should not doubt.

tsa-a14-v13 — Then in fire, having satisfied the Supreme-Lord by sesame-ghee and rest, before him another animal for vapa-hom let him do; the deity-wheel let him satisfy by its fat; again the mandala let him worship; then approaching the Supreme-Lord, contemplating the self all-non-different-whole-six-path-complete, the disciple placed in front let him make.

tsa-a14-v14 — In indirect initiation, of-living-dead-form, let him contemplate him in front, or establish a likeness made of darba-cow-dung-and-rest in front.

tsa-a14-v15 — Such a disciple — sprinkled with offering-vessel-drops, worshipped by flowers and rest — having done, the whole path in his body let him place.

tsa-a14-v16 — Then thus let him consider: for the enjoyment-seeker, the auspicious — let him not cleanse.

tsa-a14-v17 — For the liberation-seeker, the auspicious-and-unauspicious — both even.

tsa-a14-v18 — In the seedless, the covenant-nooses also let him cleanse; she is to-be-done for near-death, even most-foolish — thus the Supreme-Lord's command; and for him, from mere devotion-faith to teacher-deity-fire, attainment.

tsa-a14-v19 — Herein, everywhere, habit-capture is indeed the distinguishing [factor], by habit-conformity of mantras — from that-and-that work-doing.

tsa-a14-v02 — It is expanded, determined from the Tantrāloka.

tsa-a14-v20 — Thus having traced habit-distinction, by main-mantra-consideration-special, the whole path in-own-body-located, by Siva-two-nature-contemplation let him cleanse.

tsa-a14-v21 — Thus in order, from foot-thumb up to twelve-end, of the disciple whose body-consciousness is made non-different from self-body-consciousness, attained — right-there, in the lake of infinite bliss, in the essence of freedom-sovereignty, in the Lord of the wheel of deities full of all will-knowledge-action-power, bearing all paths, in the mandala of world-being mere-consciousness-residue — by union in such form with the disciple-self, having-become-one, rest let him cause-to-attain; thus non-different from the Supreme-Lord he becomes.

tsa-a14-v22 — Then if an enjoyment-seeker there be: where just in-tattva his enjoyment-desire becomes, right-there by whole-and-scattered-manner let him join.

tsa-a14-v23 — Thereafter, for residual-function, from the Supreme-Lord's-nature, suddenly having-emerged, made-of-pure-tattva body for him let contemplate — thus this all-noose-releasing initiation.

tsa-a14-v24 — Then the disciple the guru by fees, as-before, let worship.

tsa-a14-v25 — Then in fire the disciple's rite let him do: the Sri-Para-mantra — of-so-and-so, so-and-so tattva I-purify — thus ending-with-svaha, per-tattva three oblations; at-end complete ending-with-vausad.

tsa-a14-v26 — Thus Siva-end-tattva-purification; then by-yojanika-told-order full-offering.

tsa-a14-v27 — For the enjoyment-seeker, in the enjoyment-place, for-yojanika-sake one [offering]; another for-pure-tattva-creation-sake, one-more.

tsa-a14-v28 — Then worship of the guru by fees — thus this child-initiation.

tsa-a14-v29 — In-which ongoing one [tattva] — having-excluded past and future — act is purified.

tsa-a14-v03 — But it is stated condensed.

tsa-a14-v04 — Having done the samaya-ending rite on the third day, let him worship the group-sacrifice in the trident-lotus mandala; therein let him worship the outer-retinue, the door-deity-wheel outside; then from the mandala's front-part, beginning at the Aiśa-corner up to Agneya, in row-order — Gaṇapati, guru, paramaguru, parameṣṭhin, former-teachers, the yoginī-wheel, Vāgīśvarī, Kṣetrapāla — let him worship.

tsa-a14-v05 — Then, taking the fitting command, having placed the whole path from trident-root up to white-lotus, let him worship; then in the middle trident's middle-axis the Goddess Śrīparābhaṭṭārikā with Bhairavanatha, in the left-axis likewise Śrīmadaparā, in the right-axis Śrīparāparā, in the right trident's middle Śrīmadaparā, in the left trident's middle Śrīparāparā — two each in their own places.

tsa-a14-v06 — Thus by all-place-enshrinement of the Goddess, all becomes complete from her enshrinement — thus.

tsa-a14-v07 — Then in the middle-trident's middle-axis, let him worship the whole deity-wheel up to the lokapāla-missile by non-difference-alone; from its enshrinement, worshipped everywhere.

tsa-a14-v08 — Then in pot, kalasa, mandala, fire and own-self — by non-difference-contemplation let him join the five-grounds' conjunction; then by enrichment of the Lord two-nature-juice, flowers and rest, special-worship let him do.

tsa-a14-v09 — Why bother? Complete with satisfaction-food-offering, without wealth-deception — let him make the sacrifice-place.
