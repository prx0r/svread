**tal-a08-v251** — From the eight divine wombs to the thumb-sized end, eight Mahadevas may be, and this intellect principle is declared.
**tal-a08-v252** — At the end of the eight Mahadevas, the eight yoga is declared here; there Shrikantha, lord of Uma, is declared.
**tal-a08-v253** — Auspicious forms, the hero, the eight Mahadevas, and the body are named; nature's gunas are above and below intellect.
**tal-a08-v254** — There agitated gunas produce intellect; inequality not attained is not a cause for producing effects.
**tal-a08-v255** — Nature may be cause through guna equilibrium, but without agitation it still has unequal gunas.
**tal-a08-v256** — How would instability arise in the first agitation? This is Sankhya's fault, if by that those gunas.
**tal-a08-v257** — The unmanifest is desired; equilibrium is mere contact, not otherwise, and our view joins it with the independent lord's will agitation.
**tal-a08-v258** — The unmanifest is cause of intellect principle; agitated gunas are involved, and nature's agitation comes from the lord's will before.
**tal-a08-v259** — That indeed may be the intellect principle; what need of other imagined gunas? It is not obstructed by cause-form reflection.
**tal-a08-v260** — Then other agitation produces effects like seed and sprout, and rows of heavy ones are established in tamas, rajas, and sattva.
