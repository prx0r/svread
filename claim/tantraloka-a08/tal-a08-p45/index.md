## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v441** — In the water principle, Prabhāsa and Sureśa are present for each self together with their retinues, along with Bhairava, Kedāra, Mahākāla, Madhyāmra, and Jalpa.
**tal-a08-v442** — In the principle of radiance is this secret group of eight: Śrīśaila, Hariścandra, and those named Bhīma, Indra, Aṭṭahāsa, Vimala, Kanakhala, Nakhala, Kuru, Sthita, and Gayā.
**tal-a08-v443** — This most secret group of eight is associated with wind, with the tanmātras, and with the witness. Its members include Sthāṇu, Suvarṇa, Bhadra, Gokarṇaka, and Mahālayaka.
**tal-a08-v444** — Avimukta, Rudrakoṭi, and Vastrāpada complete that pure group. In space are Sthūla, Sthūleśa, Śaṅku, Śruti, Kālañjara, and the bearer of the circle.
**tal-a08-v445** — In the ego-principle there is a group of eight including Mākoṭa, the two Aṇḍas, and Chagalāṇḍa. Others also say that the tanmātras and the senses extend up to ahaṅkāra.
**tal-a08-v446** — In intellect the group of eight sources is taught, and in primal nature the group of eight yogas beginning with Akṛta. Thus the established order consists of seven groups of eight worlds, extending from water up to the root.
**tal-a08-v447** — In the human principle are Vāma, Bhīma, Ugra, Bhaveśa, Vīra, Pracaṇḍa, Gaurīśa, Aja, Ananta, and Ekaśiva. In vidyā there is the pair Krodha and Caṇḍa.
**tal-a08-v448** — In kalā and niyati are Saṃvarta and Jyotiratha, and also Sūra and Pañcānta. In kāla is the triad named Vīra, Śikhīśa, and Śrīkaṇṭha.
**tal-a08-v449** — These eight, led by Bhuvaneśa, are situated in the night division and in the thumb: Vāma with Mahātejā, Bhavodbhava, Ekapinga, Īśāna, and the rest of that group.
**tal-a08-v450** — Vidyā has twenty-eight worlds, extending from puruṣa to the end of night. There are Hālāhala, Rudra, Krud, Ambikā, Aghorikā, and Vāmā.
