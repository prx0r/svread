tal-a08-v31 — In the eight patalas, one each, Hatuka is lord in the eighth; Shrikantha appoints selves per world and forces many.

tal-a08-v32 — Thus that one gives powers in the raurava rule to vow-keepers and those who perform forbidden conduct.

tal-a08-v33 — Even initiated persons who lose their vow and do not perform penances become corrupters of left conduct in that place.

tal-a08-v34 — Those living by shares of god, fire, and substance stand highest; others below are devoted to garuda and other mantras.

tal-a08-v35 — They are various servants before Hatuka; but if there they worship the god-lord with devotion, they are transformed.

tal-a08-v36 — They dissolve in that lord-principle and gradually reach supreme Shiva; otherwise they remain engaged in that enjoyment.

tal-a08-v37 — They, devoted to crying under time-fire torment, dissolve in the quality-principle and then return again at creation's mouth.

tal-a08-v38 — They are cast by mothers with terrible torment into lowest-low bodies, according to their own action.

tal-a08-v39 — Among humans there, some mantra-knowers are freed in order, but others are bound according to prior deed.

tal-a08-v40 — Thus this group account, named hulahula and so on, is spoken by the blessed one in the shrimad ananda-adhika rule.
