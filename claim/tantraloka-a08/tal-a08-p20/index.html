tal-a08-v195 — The supreme goddess gives liberation through seedless initiation; she performs for people as much initiation as corresponds to the vidyesha veil.

tal-a08-v197 — Those who remember the great lord and die in water, desert, fire, or by beheading reach his city called Vijaya.

tal-a08-v198 — They go to knowledge-Ishana and radiant Virabhadra, in a region spread by crores above Virabhadra, in three ways.

tal-a08-v199 — The water-bearing rudra egg, bow-shaped, is situated from Virabhadra's world and likewise from Bhadrakali's abode.

tal-a08-v200 — Adorned by thirteen other worlds, then earth with its mountain is defined by holding the smell tanmatra.
