tal-a08-v81 — Even beings without present impressions may appear in Bharata or elsewhere, and through firm prior impressions or by the Lord's will they may still experience good and bad results.

tal-a08-v82 — Action is also found in other places, as the ancient tradition says; there the time is always Treta, but in Bharata the full cycle of four ages prevails.

tal-a08-v83 — In Bharata there are nine divisions separated by ocean water; the land measures five hundred and the water measures five hundred as well.

tal-a08-v84 — The divisions are Indra, Kasheru, Tamrabha, Naga, eastern Gabhastimat, Saumya, Gandharva, Varaha, and the one called Kanya, extending up to the ocean.

tal-a08-v85 — In the ninth division, Kanya island, there are six sub-islands situated to the south in the middle of the ocean, and the island is adorned by seven chief mountains.

tal-a08-v86 — The mountains are Anga, Yava, Malaya, Shanku, Kumuda, and Varaha; Agastya dwells on Malaya, and Lanka stands on Trikuta—these are the six sub-islands.

tal-a08-v87 — The islands and sub-islands are inhabited mostly by various foreign peoples and are rich in pearls, gold, and gems, as stated in the teaching of Shri Ruru.

tal-a08-v88 — Whatever action is done in Bharata can be exhausted there, and by it one may reach from the lowest hell up to Shiva, or even attain liberation—especially in the region called Kanya.

tal-a08-v89 — In Bharata dwell Mahakala and a crore of Rudras, and there flow the Ganga and five hundred other sacred streams; therefore birth here is extremely hard to obtain.

tal-a08-v90 — In other regions beings pass through their karma by mere enjoyment, like animals, but for those born in Bharata even attainments beyond desire are possible.
