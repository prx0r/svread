## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v261** — Three, thirty-two, one, thirty-one, and twenty-one are listed; by their own knowledge, yoga, and power, the best teachers play.
**tal-a08-v262** — Three-eyed ones, freed from bonds, do grace; intellect and guna extend to both, seven more than a hundred.
**tal-a08-v263** — Among Rudras and worlds, chiefly others, the eight yoga is declared between them, in the guna mass and again in Shiva's body.
**tal-a08-v264** — Having transcended the womb, in the secondary mass, yoga givers would be unmade, made, vibhu, Virincha, Hari, Guha, and then goddess by order.
**tal-a08-v265** — From the unmanifest arise the organs, the subtle powers like smallness, the effects, the manifold of cognitions, and the qualities beginning with sattva.
**tal-a08-v266** — These givers of yoga, arising from the qualities, bestow on souls merit, knowledge, dispassion, lordship, and their various fruits.
**tal-a08-v267** — Beyond them is the world of the god Śrīkaṇṭhanātha, who is seated on the qualities beginning with sattva and is the lord and consort of the one mother of the entire universe.
**tal-a08-v268** — By his power, taking a seizing form, he presides over the beings in the worlds of Umā, Guha, Nīla, Brahmā, Ṛbhukṣa, Kṛta, Akṛta, and others.
**tal-a08-v269** — When he wishes to withdraw the worlds, he enters the lotus of the four-faced Brahmā, burns the four worlds, and then creates the human worlds again.
**tal-a08-v270** — By his will, Rudrāṇī, whose body consists of the qualities beginning with sattva, brings forth in each cycle, and there she is worshiped at the altar in every cycle.
