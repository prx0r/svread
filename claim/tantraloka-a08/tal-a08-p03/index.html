tal-a08-v21 — The ruler of the Kataha Rudras, who are devoted to desire, is Ananta; he is mounted on a vessel within the waters, reeling from drinking liquor.

tal-a08-v22 — Meditating on the god Bhairava and surrounded by serpents, he is associated with the world of Kalagni above, which rises to a height of one crore yojanas.

tal-a08-v23 — For fear of reducing the worlds to ashes, he does not look upward; and although he pervades the universe, because he burns this earth he is understood in that lower position.

tal-a08-v24 — Because he was made clear earlier in relation to the hells, he is regarded as below them; the mighty one's flame extends ten crores, and above it there is an empty space of half that extent.

tal-a08-v25 — Above that level stand the lords of hell, feeling only pain in graded order, with some in the middle and others above, surrounded by intervals of difference.

tal-a08-v26 — Among them, in order, are avici, kumbhipaka, and raurava, eleven and eleven and ten, ending at arrow-fire.

tal-a08-v27 — For each of these there is a crore height interval, a lakh measure, and a duration between sky-void mouth counts.

tal-a08-v28 — Above kushmanda, those who perform dark action contrary to scripture are placed in a lakh-less-crore station.

tal-a08-v29 — There, terrible world-persons torment them until enjoyment ends, except those who once took refuge in supreme Shiva with one-pointed mind.

tal-a08-v30 — They do not go hell-yoked; their dark result gives only small pleasure, with thousand-nine heights and one interval following in order.
