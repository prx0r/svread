tal-a08-v321 — In the Sri Sara teaching, again this is declared as six sheaths; this knot-principle is the work of maya, then maya.

tal-a08-v322 — The maya principle is indeed pervasive, deep, formless, place of all dissolution; there world-division is not fit, that knot therefore.

tal-a08-v323 — The endless lord of maya principle simultaneously agitates collected atoms; she produces night by endless own enclosures.

tal-a08-v324 — By that, the declared veil net from kala to earth end is countless and various; maya alone is one and to be distinguished.

tal-a08-v325 — It is said in the Sri prior scripture: the earth-manifest pair is countless, called night-power, but one-natured.

tal-a08-v326 — Bonds previously said, om-syllables, five-eight, sage's family and womb, speech-goddess; one born in her is not born again.

tal-a08-v327 — At initiation time, in earth-path purification, all that goes the earth path of the endless is fully established near him.

tal-a08-v328 — Accomplishable, giver, subduer, meditator, ash: five dots, with formation, marked by five-meaning secret rudra goad heart.

tal-a08-v329 — Attraction and mirror, this eight of measures; all with unlost powers are authorities of maya principle.

tal-a08-v330 — Those with maya bodies enjoy their own enjoyment; at the end of dissolution, indeed by the endless, they are withdrawn, but at day's beginning.
