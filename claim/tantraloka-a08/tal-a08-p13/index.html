tal-a08-v121 — Between bhuvarloka and the sun there are ten wind paths, each separated by ten thousand units.

tal-a08-v122 — The first wind path above is ritarddhi, fifty yojanas above.

tal-a08-v123 — The nourishing wind is followed by pracetas, higher by a hundred.

tal-a08-v124 — Mute lightning clouds stand a krosa from earth and shower rain.

tal-a08-v125 — Above them are clouds showering frogs and beings, and higher poison-water clouds.

tal-a08-v126 — Skanda-born clouds and pisacas occur in the flood wind; fifty above are maraka clouds.

tal-a08-v127 — Mahadeva-born vinayakas there take away the actions of unperfected men.

tal-a08-v128 — Fifty above is vajranka wind, with stone clouds and lowest vidyadharas.

tal-a08-v129 — Those who died with siddhi are situated in vajranka wind.

tal-a08-v130 — Fifty above vajranka are electric rain clouds, apsarasas, and merit-doing men.
