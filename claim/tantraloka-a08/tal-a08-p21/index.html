tal-a08-v201 — The dead go to that supreme earth; the wise go to the ocean city, attainable by holding the taste tanmatra.

tal-a08-v202 — Then Shri's city is reached in Rudra's play descents, especially by special death at Prayaga and Shri mountain.

tal-a08-v203 — Sarasvati city is the abode of knowers of sound-Brahman; those suitable to Rudra are situated there, while others stand apart from Rudras due to primacy.

tal-a08-v204 — In many cities Ganga, Shri, and Sarasvati appear; god-lords range from Lakula to Amarasha, with eight in the waters.

tal-a08-v205 — Then the fiery principle is the abode of Shiva-fire here; those who have resorted to fiery holding go to this fire.

tal-a08-v206 — In the fiery principle there are eight leaders from Bhairava to Hari; breath has a world, and air is divided in ten ways.

tal-a08-v207 — Having meditated or abandoned breaths and made holding there, great souls enter that, becoming air and sky-bodied.

tal-a08-v208 — The eight from Bhima to Gaya go to the air principle; the sky world is attainable by holding that space.

tal-a08-v209 — From cloth-foot-end to pillar-etc and eight gods are in the space principle; uninitiated beings claiming the Shiva principle are among beings.

tal-a08-v210 — Even those lacking knowledge, despite strong holding, go outside from the egg; earth, ocean, fire, air, and space goers, and initiated ones too, are named.
