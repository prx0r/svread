## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v371** — Sukshma, Sutejas, and Sharva are these ten Shivas. Then, beginning from the east, come Vijaya, Nihshvasa, Svayambhuva, Vahni, Vira, and Raurava.
**tal-a08-v372** — Mukuta, Visara, Indu, Bindu, Prodgita, Lalita, Siddha, Rudra, Santana, Shiva, Para, Kirana, and Paramesha are remembered as the Rudras.
**tal-a08-v373** — All these Rudras have knowledges that bear names equal to their own. They are surrounded by crores of mantra-sages and joined with Vibhu, Vama, and the Rudras together with their powers.
**tal-a08-v374** — Sushiva's seat is a very white lotus with countless petals, endowed with Tara and other powers. The group of powers and Rudras is present both in his retinue and on his seat.
**tal-a08-v375** — In each one's own retinue there are countless crores beyond measure. They are freed from the impurity of maya, yet remain established only in their office.
**tal-a08-v376** — In the covering of Sushiva, the Rudras are all-knowing and full of all powers. When the bondage of their office dissolves, they become peaceful, take on the form of Shiva, and are no longer subject to rebirth.
**tal-a08-v377** — Above is the radiant covering of bindu. There is a moon-bright lotus, and there Shiva as Shantyatita is adorned, resting in the lap of his power.
**tal-a08-v378** — He is surrounded by the retinue of the kala group beginning with Nivritti, and by crores of cities of countless Rudras and their powers.
**tal-a08-v379** — In the revered Matanga scripture, the highest principle called Laya is, by that technical name, identified here with bindu.
**tal-a08-v380** — That bright principle, made of four forms and both with parts and without parts, is where enjoyment is said to take place. This is how it is described here.
