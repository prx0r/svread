tal-a08-v111 — When it is midnight in Amaravati, it is sunset in Yama's city, midday in Varuna's city, and sunrise in Soma's city.

tal-a08-v112 — When it is sunrise in Amaravati, it is midnight in Yama's abode, sunset in Soma's city, and midday in Varuna's city. Thus the times change with the sun's movement.

tal-a08-v113 — Beyond the Lokāloka mountain lies darkness extending thirty-five hundred crores, nineteen lakhs, and forty thousand yojanas.

tal-a08-v114 — Beyond Lokāloka lies the Garbhoda ocean, the king of oceans, equal in measure to the seven oceans. In its womb rests the entire earth.

tal-a08-v115 — According to the Siddhanta scripture, on the shore of the Garbhoda ocean is a region called Kausheya. There Garuda lives surrounded by accomplished birds.

tal-a08-v116 — There they sport on mountain tops, and there are nine principal mountains. Beyond them are thirty hot-water rivers that flow down to the netherworld.

tal-a08-v117 — Then comes the Naimi garden in the four directions, always attended by yoginis. After that are Meru, the serpents, the clouds, and then the golden egg.

tal-a08-v118 — Enclosed by the shell of Brahma's egg, the world called Bhurloka extends fifty crores of yojanas in the ten directions.

tal-a08-v119 — This earth-world is filled with six kinds of beings: beasts, birds, deer, trees, humans, and reptiles. It is also inhabited by pishachas, rakshasas, gandharvas, and yakshas.

tal-a08-v120 — It is also the abode of vidyadharas, and in short, for the whole creation of beings it serves as a place of enjoyment and residence according to their desire.
