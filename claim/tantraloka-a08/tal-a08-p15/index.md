**tal-a08-v141** — The sixth level has garuda, bull, and lord, with daksha; the ninth is established by brahma power.

**tal-a08-v145** — From the earth up to Saturn, the wise know a set of five planets, each at its own interval. From the sun, the realm of the Seven Sages is one lakh away, and from there the Pole Star is placed at the same distance.
**tal-a08-v146** — The unmoving Pole Star is Brahma himself in a lower form, stationed in Brahma's place. It has become the central pivot of all celestial cars and stands above them all.
**tal-a08-v147** — Here, all things are understood to be bound within the sphere of wind. There are seven heavenly wind masses, chiefly beginning with the clouds and the like.
**tal-a08-v148** — Those who perform sacrifices and other rites but lack knowledge go to heaven. When that merit is exhausted, they return to the human world because of the merit that still remains.
**tal-a08-v149** — Thus, from the earth up to the Pole Star the distance is fifteen lakhs. From heaven up to the Great World it is two crores and eighty-five lakhs.
**tal-a08-v150** — In the Great World dwell seers, sages, and perfected beings such as Markandeya. Gods whose duties have come to an end also abide there.
