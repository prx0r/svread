## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v01** — Now the path of space is also taught, through contraction and expansion, according to the method seen in the Shaiva scriptures.
**tal-a08-v10** — Rudra rules in the knot, and Isha in Maya up to the range of Sadakhya; above them is the unsupported Shiva who pervades all that, and beyond him is the supreme who pervades even that.
**tal-a08-v02** — The path of time, made of the Lord's power of action, has already been examined; now the path of space, connected with the variety of forms, is being defined.
**tal-a08-v03** — This entire path is established in consciousness alone; whatever does not rest there is as unreal as a flower in the sky.
**tal-a08-v04** — Created through the door of consciousness, the path is situated in the void, in the intellect, in the vital airs, in the circles of channels, and also in the outer body.
**tal-a08-v05** — This path should be defined in this way, because by continually attending to its proper sequence the yogi can quickly attain the state of Bhairava.
**tal-a08-v06** — When, by the mere desire to see, one abides pervading all things, what more needs to be said? So it is taught in the Spanda scripture.
**tal-a08-v07** — Having known the entire path, one should dissolve it into its lords, and then, as before, melt them in sequence into the circle of body, breath, and mind.
**tal-a08-v08** — All of that should be dissolved into one's own consciousness; when contemplated, that consciousness, full in its own nature, becomes the fire that dissolves the ocean of worldly existence.
**tal-a08-v09** — In the revered Mahikshottara, the teacher declared these lords of the path: Brahma rules from Ananta up to Pradhana, and Vishnu from Purusha up to Kala.
