## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v281** — Reasoning, scriptural word, and study are three means that remove suffering; gaining friends and giving are also included, making the eight accomplishments. The obstacle before accomplishment is threefold.
**tal-a08-v282** — Above the worlds beginning with aṇimā there are three rows connected with teacher and disciple. Their threefold division is explained by the influence of the three qualities.
**tal-a08-v283** — Above those rows is the eightfold set of nāḍī-knowledges beginning with iḍā. In the soul there is established a power made of sound, called expansion.
**tal-a08-v284** — A soul that is not an agent cannot truly be established as the doer. Even if the soul is not an agent, it may still stand as an assisting cause.
**tal-a08-v285** — Otherwise one would have to treat the soul as part of the effect, which would undermine the doctrine that the effect pre-exists. Therefore, in such a case, there is a specific power belonging to the soul.
**tal-a08-v286** — Taking on that many forms, that power attains fullness. Above the eight nāḍīs, an eightfold set of forms is taught.
**tal-a08-v287** — At the level of the puruṣa principle there is an eightfold embodied set: effect, cause, suffering, pleasure, knowledge, what is to be accomplished, the instruments, and the means.
**tal-a08-v288** — Beyond the eightfold forms is the world of the ten bodily observances: non-harm, truth, non-stealing, continence, freedom from deceit, freedom from anger, and duties toward the teacher.
**tal-a08-v289** — In the principle of the person are the ten declared qualities, including service, purity, contentment, and straightness. There too is situated a sixteenfold set ending with smell.
**tal-a08-v290** — Starting from the world called the body-bond, the qualities of intellect appear. There the ego is present in eightfold and threefold forms, along with bonds beginning with desire.
