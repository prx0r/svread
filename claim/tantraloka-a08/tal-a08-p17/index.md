## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v161** — Sharva, Rudra, Bhima, Bhava, Ugra, the great lord, and Ishana are the seven Shiva-lords of the worlds, ruling upward from the earth world.
**tal-a08-v162** — Seven worlds are formed from gross particulars, while the higher worlds are formed from subtle ones. The teacher explained this clearly in the Ruru scripture.
**tal-a08-v163** — In Brahma's first creation, five gross particulars called the abundant ones were made from his own body. With a seventh formed from them, these became the basis of the seven worlds.
**tal-a08-v164** — Beyond that, the worlds are supported by subtle coverings: great elements born from the tanmatras, upheld through subtle bodies and mutually connected by smell and the other subtle qualities.
**tal-a08-v165** — From the fire of time up to the staff-bearer the distance is eighty-eight crores. Above that, within the cosmic egg, is a solid shell one crore yojanas thick.
**tal-a08-v166** — There are fifty crores above the earth's surface and fifty crores below, making the earth sphere one hundred crores in all. From that it is pictured like a golden grain of rice.
**tal-a08-v167** — The mantra extending to the hundred Rudras, with the syllables hum and phat, splits that hard-to-pacify boundary. Ten by ten in every direction, there are a hundred Rudras outside.
**tal-a08-v168** — That which supports the cosmic egg does so by its own power on all sides. The teachers, in the Raurava and other scriptures, have said that it has the form of an egg.
**tal-a08-v169** — The cosmic egg is the mass of reality that has turned toward manifestation, emerged from the form of power, and remains undivided by any addition or removal.
**tal-a08-v170** — The upper shell of the egg is sattva mixed with a trace of darkness, while the lower shell is sattva mixed with activity and associated with darkness.
