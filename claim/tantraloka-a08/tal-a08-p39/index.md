## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v381** — Because Nivritti and the other kalas are extremely subtle, embodiment beginning from earth does not take shape in the usual way. Since the Mother is absorbed in flashing great knowledge, it is not distinctly perceived.
**tal-a08-v382** — Just as earth particles in gold do not appear separately because its fiery nature predominates, so too the elements in the bodies of the higher and lower Rudras do not appear separately.
**tal-a08-v383** — Above bindu is the half-moon. From that come kala, moonlight, radiance, splendor, and the pure one. These five are the obstructing powers.
**tal-a08-v384** — The best of teachers declare that in Nirodhini there are five kalas: Rundhani, Rodhani, Roddhri, Jnanabodha, and Tamopaha.
**tal-a08-v385** — The half-measure is called bindu, which has the form of space and four phases. Half of that is the half-moon, and by an eighth part of that comes nirodhikā.
**tal-a08-v386** — Rodhikā blocks the causal lords beginning with Brahma; one should abandon it. Then, after piercing this nirodhikā, one reaches the supreme world called Sadakhya.
**tal-a08-v387** — There, in supreme form, abides the one whose great body is made of the five mantras. Thus, above the covering of bindu that ends in the half-moon and nirodhi, there is a great presence.
**tal-a08-v388** — Nāda is like the filaments of a flower and is surrounded by great beings. There are four worlds in the directions and a fifth in the center.
**tal-a08-v389** — Indhikā, Dīpikā, Rodhikā, and the upward-going Mocikā are there. In the middle is a lotus, and there the upward-moving one is surrounded by its powers.
**tal-a08-v390** — Above nāda is the suṣumna realm. There the lord who bears its powers is surrounded on the left and right by Piṅgalā and Iḍā.
