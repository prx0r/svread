## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v431** — In the subtle elements there are five, and there are the Viśvedevas and then a group of eight. A fifth grouping is connected with the senses, egoity, intellect, and the guṇas, including a group of eight gods.
**tal-a08-v432** — In the root there is a group of eight yogas called Krodha, associated with time and with niyati. Patadruga and others, and Aṅguṣṭhamātra and others, belong to the tattva of attachment.
**tal-a08-v433** — In vidyā there are twelve lords beginning with Ekaśiva, and in kalā there are ten. There is also a group of three hundred beginning with Vāmā, described by a numerical formula.
**tal-a08-v434** — Some Śaiva beings up to this point are summarized as belonging to Śrīkaṇṭha. When someone desires the enjoyments of others, the appropriate application should be made there.
**tal-a08-v435** — When something is removed, purification should be done, and the rest should be treated as included within. This is shown as a scriptural variant set forth for that purpose.
**tal-a08-v436** — Teachers may also work out many other variants by their own understanding. In the revered former scripture, however, a count of one hundred and eighteen is stated.
**tal-a08-v437** — Here the text briefly states the chief matters to be purified. The list begins with Kālāgni, Kūṣmāṇḍa, Narakeśa, Hāṭaka, and Bhūtalapa.
**tal-a08-v438** — The list continues with Brahmā, the lord of the sage-world, and Rudras stationed in five intermediate regions. In the lower Ananta and in the east are those named Kapāli, Vahni, Anta, Nirṛti, and Bala.
**tal-a08-v439** — The sequence runs upward through the named lords as far as Śambhu, together with the lord of Vīrabhadra. In the outer division of the Brahmāṇḍa there are eleven, and likewise five in the intermediate division.
**tal-a08-v440** — Thus this group of sixteen cities is to be considered in nivṛtti and kalā. The list begins with Lakulīśa, Bhārabhūti, Diṇḍi, Āṣāḍhi, Puṣkara, and Nimeṣa.
