tal-a08-v421 — In the tattva of restriction there are ten Śaṅkara worlds; in time there are ten Śiva worlds, making two cities. In attachment there are the well-delighted world and the teacher-disciple city, and in the pair of knowledge and limited agency there are further worlds.

tal-a08-v422 — World after world is set out, including the three sheath-cities, the city of speech, and the city of measure. Thus vidyā, together with the seven tattvas beginning with puruṣa, contains twenty-seven cities.

tal-a08-v423 — In the pure domain are Vāmeśa, Rūpa, and Sūkṣma; then pure knowledge and the measure of Śakti’s radiance; then Suviśuddhi and Śiva, and the worlds named Mokṣa, Dhuva, Īśa, Saṃbuddha, Samaya, and Sauśiva.

tal-a08-v424 — The peaceful level has seventeen cities. It includes the three cities of the Vidyeśas and Sadāśiva, the principles Bindu, Half-moon, and Nirodhikā, and reaches up to the supreme Sauśiva and the Indhikā and other cities of the central channel.

tal-a08-v425 — There are the supreme sound, the cavity of Brahman, the upper Kuṇḍalinī joined with Sūkṣma and others, and Śakti; then come five cities supported by Vyāpin, Vyoman, Ananta, and Anātha.

tal-a08-v426 — Then there is a sixth, supported by Paramamanas. Alternatively, one may speak of the sixteen-world group of Samanā, or of the enclosure of Bindu, the supreme Sauśiva, and the five worlds beginning with Indhikā.

tal-a08-v427 — The central-channel domain, the cavity of Brahman, Kuṇḍalinī, the five Vyāpins, and Samanā together make up this sixteen-world group. This is the pair of tattvas belonging to the level beyond peace.

tal-a08-v428 — In the revered Mataṅga scripture this sequence of cities is given: beginning with Kālāgni and the hells, it amounts principally to one hundred and forty.

tal-a08-v429 — Kūṣmāṇḍa, the seven netherworlds, the seven worlds, and Maheśvara make up the middle of the cosmic egg. Outside that, a hundred Rudras are situated.

tal-a08-v430 — There is a level of two hundred places joined with fifty-seven more. From the middle of a group of five eights, thirty-two are assigned within the four elements.
