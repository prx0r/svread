## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v181** — The remaining Rudra-lords are Shighra, Nidhisha, Vidyesha, Shambhu, and Virabhadra; they surround the Brahma egg like bees covering honey and clusters of kadamba filaments.
**tal-a08-v182** — In the same way, the Shiva-Rudras, with countless attendants, surround the Brahma egg; their arrangement is described in vast numbers, such as forty niyutas and a crore.
**tal-a08-v183** — Presided over by Shrikantha, these Rudras create and withdraw, and they hold lordship over the gods, as stated in the Raurava Vartika.
**tal-a08-v184** — But in the Siddha Tantra, beyond a hundred crores from the golden egg, there is a sequence of a hundred more eggs, successively doubled and associated with silver and other substances.
**tal-a08-v185** — In those eggs, Brahmas exist in sequence with doubled lifespans; they perish in order, and at the end of that series a higher principle is reached.
**tal-a08-v186** — From earth upward come water and the rest in order; up to ego the scale increases tenfold, and beyond that intellect increases a hundredfold.
**tal-a08-v187** — Above intellect, manifest nature is a thousandfold, purusha is ten thousandfold, and niyati is a hundred thousandfold, leading to a millionfold beyond it.
**tal-a08-v188** — Up to kala the scale is ten millionfold, Maya is a hundred millionfold, Ishvara is a billionfold, and beyond that it reaches a thousand crores.
**tal-a08-v189** — Sadakhya pervades at that vast scale, and together with its multitude of powers the all-pervading goddess is established as pervading the entire path.
**tal-a08-v190** — Beyond all that is the pure, supreme, immeasurable Shiva principle; from water up to Shiva-tattva, no one but Shiva has truly seen it.
