tal-a08-v231 — Below these, their bodily enjoyments are declared to be abundant, and there are forty worlds with corresponding regions of equal enjoyment.

tal-a08-v232 — These are described only as groups of eight and five because of differences in means, and they are reached through devotion, yoga, the giving up of life, and so forth.

tal-a08-v233 — Among these, Umapati alone is the lord, possessing independent senses yet being without organs; from him, by gradation, the octad of divine wombs is to be recognized.

tal-a08-v234 — The senses of the worlds are instruments that delimit objects; through a sequence increasing by one from smell up to mahat, sovereignty arises.

tal-a08-v235 — Knowing thus that powers like anima operate in this range from the paisaca level up to Virinca, one should purify the intellect together with the eight constituents of the subtle city and the senses.

tal-a08-v236 — They know the octad of Krodhesas, beginning with Samvarta and led by Anila, and then the octad of fires arising from the wrathful octad beginning with Baladhyaksa.

tal-a08-v237 — Then the octad of yogas beginning with Akrta is declared in the intellect in the Svacchanda teaching, but in the teaching beginning with Sri it is placed in the root.

tal-a08-v238 — At the level of the yoga octad, in Soma, there is what belongs to Srikantha; then comes the city of Maya, which is again spoken of as belonging to Srikantha.

tal-a08-v239 — Therefore, a second world is spoken of separately for those two; there, in the city of Maya, the universe is presided over by a goddess.

tal-a08-v240 — In each cosmic age that great goddess is described by different names; the city of the lord of Uma is then surrounded by the Mothers.
