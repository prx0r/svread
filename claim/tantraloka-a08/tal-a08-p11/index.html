tal-a08-v101 — There are always unbarred doors there for entering the netherworld. This is taught by the teachers in the revered Raurava scripture.

tal-a08-v102 — Thus the salt ocean has been described. Beyond it lie six continents, and each one is surrounded by its own ocean.

tal-a08-v103 — The six continents, each successively doubled in size and ruled by six sons of Manu, are Shaka, Kusha, Krauncha, Shalmali, Gomeda, and Pushkara. Their six oceans are of milk, curd, ghee, sugarcane juice, liquor, and sweet water.

tal-a08-v104 — Medhatithi, Vapushmat, Jyotishmat, Dyutimat, King Havi, and Samvara rule Shaka and the other continents, and Agnidhra was appointed over Jambu continent.

tal-a08-v105 — The five continents beginning with Shaka are each divided into regions by seven mountains. Pushkara is divided into two parts, and there Hari, Yama, Varuna, and Indra rule in the east and the other directions.

tal-a08-v106 — From half of Meru up to the end of the sweet-water ocean, the total measure is two crores, fifty-three lakhs, and fifty thousand yojanas.

tal-a08-v107 — Beyond the seventh ocean lies a golden land extending ten crores of yojanas. After that is the Lokāloka mountain, one hundred thousand yojanas high and ten thousand yojanas broad.

tal-a08-v108 — Eight Rudras with the world-lords are stationed in the eight directions of Lokāloka. Some say the sun moves only within Lokāloka and never goes beyond it.

tal-a08-v109 — The paths of the ancestors and the gods, going south and north, are the sun's two tracks. Teachers say these are the sun's southern and northern courses.

tal-a08-v110 — Meru is north of all and Lokāloka is south. In this way one should understand the sun's rising and setting.
