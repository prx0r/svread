tal-a08-v301 — In the Shaiva scripture they are placed in the principle of maya, under Ananta: skull-vow observers, those who offer their own limbs, and practitioners of harsh austerities.

tal-a08-v302 — Fearless and observing the sword-edge vow, knowing the principles, they gradually ascend through each principle until they reach the place where the lord is called Ananta.

tal-a08-v303 — And it is said that beyond him is the abode of the lord of Ananta, the god who performs creation, maintenance, and dissolution, and who guards the door of the cave and the bhaga.

tal-a08-v304 — Drawing them out again and again from the cavity of maya, he bestows on persons merits, powers such as minuteness, knowledges, austerities, pleasures, and yogic attainments.

tal-a08-v305 — Empowered by his energy, all the lords from Ananta onward, down to those beginning with Shatabhava, shine like lamps dispelling darkness in the office of the cave.

tal-a08-v306 — Presiding over the unmanifest, Shiva brings forth the world by the command of Shambhu, because he is the cause of authority over both the pure and impure streams.

tal-a08-v307 — Those first established in that great station endowed with Shiva's qualities, beginning with Ananta, become the agents of the world's creation, maintenance, and dissolution.

tal-a08-v308 — This has been called the cavity of maya. Beyond it is the cave, the womb of the world, where generation occurs as the stirring of the lord's power takes effect.

tal-a08-v309 — In the womb-openings, rich with various desires and called bhaga, when the lord desires the goddess who follows his will, the generative process begins.

tal-a08-v310 — In each bhaga, offspring beginning with the unmanifest are born from her. They are so extremely subtle that their nature is not described in the prescribed texts.
