tal-a08-v341 — Above the knowledge principle is the aisha principle, ascending in order from Shikhanda to Ananta, joined with eight cities.

tal-a08-v342 — Shikhanda, Shrigala, Murti, one-eye, one-rudra, highest Shiva, subtle Rudra, Ananta, and the eight Vidyeshvaras are named.

tal-a08-v343 — In the ascending upward placement of the seven, Ananta is the leader and lord, to be meditated and worshipped increasingly.

tal-a08-v344 — Three crores are established with the chief mantra lords; these leaders are thereby Vidyeshas and world rulers.

tal-a08-v345 — The gurus said this in the Shiva-body teachings: a god with eight shakti pairs sits on the guha throne.

tal-a08-v346 — From the deep beginning to the end of hell, he creates, assigns Rudras, and uplifts men through Manonmani, remaining middle-standing.

tal-a08-v347 — Those uplifted by him enter supreme principle contemplation; he is again appointed by Shiva among path dwellers and atoms.

tal-a08-v348 — After the completed lord assignment, Ananta lord enters that abode with many soul mantra crores, while subtle Rudra extinguishes.

tal-a08-v349 — The lord establishes the first atom in Shikhanda's place; thus eight proceed in order until the abodes, by the single guru.

tal-a08-v350 — Those eight, seated on lotus thrones with eight shakti yoga, make liberation for countless beings.
