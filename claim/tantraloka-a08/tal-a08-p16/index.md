**tal-a08-v151** — In the great intermediate space there are others who still hold office. Eight crores above the Great World is the Jana world, where Kapila and others dwell.
**tal-a08-v152** — Many happy Sadhyas dwell there as well. One crore above the Jana world is the Tapas world, where Sanaka and others of great wisdom reside.
**tal-a08-v153** — There the lords of creatures born from Brahma hold their office. Above the Tapas world, sixteen crores away, is Satya, the abode of Brahma.
**tal-a08-v154** — There the self-born Brahma dwells and manifests this universe. In the Satya world are the Vedas and others who have been refined by action and meditation.
**tal-a08-v155** — There, above, one crore higher, are those established in bliss at Brahma's seat. Two crores above Brahma's seat is the city of Vishnu.
**tal-a08-v156** — Devotees of Vishnu reach that abode through meditation, worship, and recitation. Seven crores above Vishnu's world is the world of the supreme lord.
**tal-a08-v157** — Rudra is the maker of creation and dissolution within the cosmic egg. Those who are devoted to worship of the linga but lack initiation and knowledge are connected with his realm.
**tal-a08-v158** — They go to Rudra's city within the cosmic egg and never fall back down. All who dwell there eventually go to Shiva, becoming Rudras initiated by Srikantha.
**tal-a08-v159** — When their office comes to an end, they ascend together with the group of Rudra's daughters, going from city to city above Rudra in ever higher stages.
**tal-a08-v160** — Below the cosmic egg and above Rudra is the city of the staff-bearer. By Shiva's will he holds the egg firm and also opens the path of liberation.
