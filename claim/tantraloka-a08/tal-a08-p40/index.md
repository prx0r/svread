## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v391** — The goddess Suṣumnā, resting in the lord's lap and shining like the moon, strings together the whole path, both above and below.
**tal-a08-v392** — The support of nāda and suṣumnā pierces this entire universe; it goes out below by the downward power and above from the crown by the upward power.
**tal-a08-v393** — Absorbed through the channel in the cavity of Brahma, that unmanifest, resonant, imperishable sound resounds in all beings, empowered by Śiva's śakti.
**tal-a08-v394** — Above Suṣumnā is the covering called the brahma cavity. There dwells Brahmā, white, three-eyed, bearing a trident, five-faced, and crowned with the moon.
**tal-a08-v395** — In his lap sits the supreme goddess Brahmāṇī, who moves on the path of liberation and both obstructs and grants release. Piercing through her, one comes to the upward Kuṇḍalinī.
**tal-a08-v396** — That śakti is like a sleeping serpent and is called the support of the universe. Within her are the subtle, the very subtle, Amṛtā, Amitā, and other powers.
**tal-a08-v397** — In the middle is Vyāpinī, and within her is Vyāpīśa, the bearer of Vyāpinī. This is the śakti principle, whose manifestation extends all the way down to earth.
**tal-a08-v398** — Then comes the Śiva principle. Established in the four directions are Vyāpī, the sky-natured lord Ananta, and those who share in his powers.
**tal-a08-v399** — In the middle there is the unsupported one: the god of gods, Anāśrita, bearing his śakti in his lap and shining with the radiance of a hundred crore suns.
**tal-a08-v400** — Above the Śiva principle is the supreme śakti called Samanā. She abides as the active agent of all causes.
