**tal-a08-v361** — Above the very-pure covering there is a single Shaiva city. Above the Shiva-covering, they say, lies the covering called the liberation-covering.
**tal-a08-v362** — In this liberation-covering, eleven Rudras are described. Above the liberation-covering there is a single-city covering called the fixed one.

**tal-a08-v364** — Above the will-covering is the one called the awakened covering, frequented by the eight directional Rudras. Above the awakened covering lies the great covenant-covering.
**tal-a08-v365** — The covenant-covering encloses the covering of Ananta within five worlds. Above it lies the auspicious-Shiva realm, and there is the great world called Sadakhya.
**tal-a08-v366** — In that world dwells the god Sadashiva. His powers of knowledge and action are on his left and right, while his supreme will rests in his lap.
**tal-a08-v367** — By that will, Sadashiva performs the five acts beginning with creation. With him are the five Brahmas, the six limbs, the group of eight beginning with Sakala, and other Shivas.
**tal-a08-v368** — The auspicious Shiva is surrounded by those ten and eighteen Rudras. The five Brahmas are Sadyojata, Vama, Aghora, Purusha, and Ishana, and the heart is named among them.
**tal-a08-v369** — They declare the six limbs to be head, tuft, armor, eye, and weapon, together with the heart. Along with them are Sakala, Akala, Shunya, Kaladhya, Kha, and Kshapana as the last.
**tal-a08-v370** — The throat-and-lip one is the eighth, and this group of eight Saktalas is traditionally transmitted. Then are named Omkara, Shiva, Dipta, Hetu, Isha, Dasheshaka, Sushiva, and Kala.
