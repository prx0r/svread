## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v291** — The bonds are threefold: adventitious, of the Ganeshas, and of the Vidyeshvaras. They are present in the person and block the path to liberation.
**tal-a08-v292** — Whatever is other than the supreme Shiva, whose nature is nondual consciousness, freedom, and beauty, is called a bond.
**tal-a08-v293** — Thus, even though the supreme Lord remains full, when personhood is assumed there arises a wondrous non-recognition of his own nature; from that come persons.
**tal-a08-v294** — All the bonds, whether stated or unstated and described in the Paratantra, are present in the person. By purifying them there, one is freed from worldly becoming.
**tal-a08-v295** — Above the person, in niyati, dwell ten Shankaras, golden and very white. In the principle of time dwell ten Shivas.
**tal-a08-v296** — In raga is the world of Viresha, a city of teachers and students, with ten million and sixteen thousand attendants for each.
**tal-a08-v297** — In impure knowledge there is a world shining with nine Shaktis, beginning with Vama and extending up to Manonmani.
**tal-a08-v298** — In kala is the supreme world of the three great gods. Beyond that is maya, a threefold domain filled chiefly with countless millions.
**tal-a08-v299** — That maya is pervaded by cavities called bhaga, as taught in the Shaiva scripture. Again, there is a group of eight great gods extending to the measure of a thumb at night.
**tal-a08-v300** — Likewise, in the teaching of Shrimalini, the powers beginning with Vama are described as ruling the eight circles; in the earlier sacred teaching they are said to begin from the level of the person.
