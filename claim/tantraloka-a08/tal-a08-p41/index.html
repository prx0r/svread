tal-a08-v401 — Empowered by Śiva, she bears many cosmic eggs. Resting upon her, Lord Śiva performs the five acts.

tal-a08-v402 — Samanā is the instrument of that great causal lord. The unsupported principle, in activity, is the occasioning cause and is called the cause.

tal-a08-v403 — Through her the all-pervading lord presides over the five causes. This unsupported, lordly, endless, sky-bodied one does so always.

tal-a08-v404 — He impels the pervading lord by his own śakti as instrument. Below that abides Māyā in the form of karma, and below that is the śakti Kuṇḍalinī.

tal-a08-v405 — The arising of the world as effect begins with nāda, bindu, and so on. In the teaching, this includes everything from the earthly level up to Sadāśiva.

tal-a08-v406 — All that is declared to be produced and subject to arising and destruction. Now I will present the measure of all the worlds as it was taught to me by my own gurus.

tal-a08-v407 — That will now be explained briefly so that the mind may quickly move through it. Within the cosmic egg are Ananta, Kāla, Kūṣmāṇḍa and Hāṭaka, and Brahmā and Hari.

tal-a08-v408 — There are one hundred Rudras with their hosts. Outside is Nivṛtti, which has one hundred and eight worlds, distributed individually across the sevenfold group of water, fire, wind, space, ego, intellect, and root.

tal-a08-v409 — The kalā level is declared to have fifty-six worlds, and so it is called pratiṣṭhā. Some, however, say that eight of its own lords are to be purified there.

tal-a08-v410 — Others say that all the worlds are to be purified. The teachers led by Śrī Bhūtirāja Miśra, however, say that a hundred Rudras are outside.
