## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a08-v411** — This nivṛtti consists of eight Rudras within, together with Śarva. The Rudras named here are Kālī, Vīra, Dharā, Abdhi, Lakṣmī, Sarasvatī, and Guhya.
**tal-a08-v412** — Thus the group of eight is in water. In fire there is a pair beyond Guhya; in wind there is the world of Vāyu. In space there are the group beginning with Gaya, Vyoma, the eight Pavitras, and a pair of worlds.
**tal-a08-v413** — In ego there is the group of eight beginning with Chagala. Then, within the domain of space and ego-maker, there is the group of eight worlds of the tanmātras, sun, moon, and śruti, belonging to the gods of intellect and action.
**tal-a08-v414** — There are ten worlds in the tanmātra group, and others in the sense group and in mind and ego; altogether there are twenty-two worlds.
**tal-a08-v415** — In the intellect there are eight divine ones, a triad called Kṛt, Tejas, and Yoga, then Umā and her lord, then the eight Mūrtis, the twelve Suśivas, and Vīrabhadra.
**tal-a08-v416** — Then come the eight Mahādevas; in the intellect the count is seventeen. In the guṇa tattva there are three sets of five, and altogether they know fifty-six worlds.
**tal-a08-v417** — Although the eight Krodheśvaras are in mūla, the state of balanced guṇas, they are not counted again in pratiṣṭhā because they are treated as purified in the intellect.
**tal-a08-v418** — Thus, in pratiṣṭhā, from the water tattva up to mūla, there are twenty-four tattvas, along with the satisfactions beginning with Ambā, those beginning with Tārā, and the siddhis beginning with Aṇimā.
**tal-a08-v419** — There are teachers, teachers with disciples, groups of seers, and those beginning with Iḍā, together with the eight bodies. There is also the world of modifications beginning with smell, the eight qualities of intellect, ego-activity, and the qualities of the sense objects.
**tal-a08-v420** — There is also the incidental group of twenty-seven beginning with Kāma, and likewise the groups of Gaṇeśas and Vidyeśas. Thus, among the bonds there are three worlds, and here in the puruṣa there are sixteen worlds.
