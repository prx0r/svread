stav-p06-v51 — Homage to Soma master — his longing drawn in, desire stretched long, the moon his crest.

stav-p06-v52 — Crying 'incapable, what can I do?' with nowhere a resolve to stand on — Śaṅkara's grace that befriends all is where I hide.

stav-p06-v53 — You stand past the strands, yourself proclaimed sheer wonder — in what corner, Bhava, could a second you be found?

stav-p06-v54 — Nondual you are, boundless self, O lord — and still your fathomless glamour fools us now.

stav-p06-v55 — Powers from the minute on, undying lordship, becoming's end — Bhava, these are green shoots of the prayer-tree rooted in you.

stav-p06-v56 — No direction lacks you; all hours are your stuff — won already, yet when, lord, shall I win you? Speak.

stav-p06-v57 — Homage, Śambhu — lone tenant of lucid honest hearts, ash-white limbs, high swan.

stav-p06-v58 — Scatter the surging gloom, burn every stick of becoming — be my lamp of waking, lord, my lamp of love.

stav-p06-v59 — You set the triple-world drama teeming with live seed — Hara, to strike the set, what playwright but you has skill?

stav-p06-v60 — Homage to turn the real and the fake to nought or to aught — to the bound and the free, who wears wasting and sway as one.
