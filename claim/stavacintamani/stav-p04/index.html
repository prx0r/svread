stav-p04-v32 — This whole of three worlds fruits from mere will of yours alone — who, O lord, knows the reach of your workings?

stav-p04-v33 — To him I bow, by whose stairway of works even Brahmā and the rest climb higher and higher to win the abode.

stav-p04-v34 — 'Here is Brahmā, here the Great Indra, here sun and moon' — so his power-vine stands in bloom: may Bhava, that one, guard us.

stav-p04-v35 — Deluded hearts find no delusion in him; far-rangers never reach his end — hard ground at dusk: Mṛḍa we praise.

stav-p04-v36 — Homage in hymn and memory, in meditation, sight, touch, and likewise in arrival — to the beloved mass of bliss, the matted-haired.

stav-p04-v37 — Thinking in mind, 'why this pride?', O highest Lord — 'proud, I am made of you' — ah me, not in myself: what joy is this?

stav-p04-v38 — Pondered though crores of duties be, through mind's restlessness — I rest, O Bhava, and revel deeply in the bliss of mind in you.

stav-p04-v39 — Subtle though you be, how is this triple world a mere fragment of yours? Gross though you be, why do even Brahmā and the rest not plainly see you, O lord?

stav-p04-v40 — Had it never been that 'you alone speak them all' — which sage, O god, would ever have strained through tangled words?

stav-p04-v41 — Through what practice, what rite, what wisdom does one behold you, O lord — teach me, and let your favour fall on me.
