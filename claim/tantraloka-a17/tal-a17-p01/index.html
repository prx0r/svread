tal-a17-v01 — Then I speak the procedure that bestows identity with Bhairava-nature, thus in five: mandala, pot, fire, disciple, and self.

tal-a17-v10 — Having invoked and worshipped maya-shakti with one's own mantra, one should satisfy it; the matrika syllable is used in invocation, and the malini syllable in worship.

tal-a17-v02 — Having taken unity of pervasion, one places the path and the student's course; the three impurities—karma, māyā, and anu—are placed on the arm, on the throat, and likewise.

tal-a17-v03 — And the teacher should cast into the crest by thread-knot union; its appearance as other than its own form is impurity; that knot is called impurity.

tal-a17-v04 — For the firmness of the reply 'iti,' an external knot is arranged; the arms are the seat of action; the Vishnu-maya self dwells in the throat.

tal-a17-v05 — Thus imagination, made from the downward-flowing flame-atomicity, produces manifoldness in the triad called nara, shakti, and shiva.

tal-a17-v06 — To speak, one should arrange a ninefold thread for knotting; fire, water, and food are threefold, each in three ways.

tal-a17-v07 — At the end of hearing, some from this entered the white, black, and red; then in fire, having satisfied all mantras, they became consciousness-space only.

tal-a17-v08 — In the common form of the principles, one should practice gradual purification; there, by union with one's own mantra, one should first invoke earth.

tal-a17-v09 — Having worshipped with flowers and the like, one should then propitiate with ghee, sesame, and the like the deity pervading each principle, and afterwards the presiding deity of the māyā principle.
