tal-a17-v61 — Meditate on the earth principle, purified and pure-formed, mixed with pure water fit for purification; then it becomes purified.

tal-a17-v62 — Thus, by increasingly mixing the preceding principles, the entire series of principles becomes Shiva; otherwise, if pure, they remain separate.

tal-a17-v63 — Separateness too is an impurity; it originates in what is called māyā; even when karma is exhausted, liberation would not be like that of the vidyeśvaras.

tal-a17-v64 — Then also, all invocation and the rest concerning the water element continue until the mixing of tejas in fire, in space, and in consciousness itself.

tal-a17-v65 — Thus, in due order, in the pure kala-principle, the kala should cut the bond resting on the arm; for that kala is of the nature of opening some agency.

tal-a17-v66 — He would burn up that knot, whose nature is the expansion of karma-named impurity, together with what goes to the spoon's tip, in the radiance of the fire-mantra, by a full oblation.

tal-a17-v67 — The mantra is all-form; by the power of refuge it becomes manifest form, and then in fire it burns bonds.

tal-a17-v68 — Burnt and with dissolved nature, the bond toward him, like Shambhu, attains only the remainder of the supreme lord's great radiance.

tal-a17-v69 — When the karma-bond is to be offered, all of his good and bad, inauspicious, present-past, and future—everything—is to be offered.

tal-a17-v70 — I burn; three phats; vauṣaṭ thus; the full offering should be thrown; thus, in the purification of māyā's end, the throat-noose also should be offered in fire.
