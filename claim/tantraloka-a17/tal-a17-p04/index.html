tal-a17-v31 — Having done that, one should touch the heart with Shiva's hand; then, by one's own nadi path, one indeed reaches the child's heart.

tal-a17-v32 — Having gone to unity with the disciple-self, and having taken him folded in the heart, the one called swan-form, there by the dissolution seal.

tal-a17-v33 — He should make the illuminating one situated in his own heart [enter] the disciple's body by rays, separated only by rays.

tal-a17-v34 — Because one moves from the place of bondage, gains an independent place, and remains dependent on one's own action, one deserves birth everywhere.

tal-a17-v35 — Having first made the matter brought by that soul-heart, then in illusion he would create his bodies of that earth-principle.

tal-a17-v36 — There, for him, the conception rite is proper, together with the male-child rites and so on; one should perform a single sacrament up to birth from the womb.

tal-a17-v37 — Birth and the state of being enjoyer of enjoyments, united in one place, are purified; then, in those enjoyments, it makes his dissolution into that same identity.

tal-a17-v38 — Then one should practice severing those bonds of principles; and in this fourfold, impressions are further distinguished as higher and lower.

tal-a17-v39 — Of the mantras, the fifteen-fold [set] is supreme, or one should join in order eight beginning with pivanya and six weapons and the like; thus [it is] supreme.

tal-a17-v40 — These fifteen come about in order in the purification by dissolution; having first spoken the lower mantra, one proceeds from the mouth of the Self — and so the rite is joined.
