**tal-a17-v49** — O lord of truth, by Shiva's command, you should not make an obstacle to this son's going to the disease-free place.
**tal-a17-v50** — Then, if the mantrin wishes to purify separately the fivefold path beginning with the world, which enters the interval of the earth principle.
