tal-a17-v41 — The teacher, saying 'I perform the impregnation rite,' should again utter that very mantra ending in svāhā and give three oblations.

tal-a17-v42 — Thus, of one born from the self of supreme-inferior mantra, I make supreme-inferior enjoyment-enjoyership.

tal-a17-v43 — Beforehand, having pronounced svāhā, let him offer three oblations; having uttered the drinking mantra of the self, thus then...

tal-a17-v44 — In enjoyment, I perform dissolution; then again the same mantra; and three oblations ending in svāhā should be given with ghee, sesame, and the like.

tal-a17-v45 — This indeed is the rule at the beginning of vomiting and at the end of fifteen: before, the mantra of the named self is supreme-self-related; thus then.

tal-a17-v46 — The supreme mantra is 'I do bond-cutting'; then, again from that, uttering hum, svaha, and phat together, one should give three offerings.

tal-a17-v47 — In this tetrad of saṃskāras, I have told the mantras; in them, before the karma term, one should speak the earth-principle term.

tal-a17-v48 — Then, having invoked and satisfied the lord of the earth principle, the guru, filled with devotion to Shiva, should instruct thus.

tal-a17-v49 — O lord of truth, by Shiva's command, you should not make an obstacle to this son's going to the disease-free place.

tal-a17-v50 — Then, if the mantrin wishes to purify separately the fivefold path beginning with the world, which enters the interval of the earth principle.
