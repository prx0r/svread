## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a17-v51** — Without mantra, as before, one should offer three and three oblations and give the city, endowed with the reasoning 'I purify,' with a serene intellect.
**tal-a17-v52** — Thus, in kalā, mantra, pada, and varṇa too, the expert should give three oblations each, separately or collectively.
**tal-a17-v53** — Then, having given the full oblation with the supreme vauṣaṭ-ending, one should draw the disciple from another mantra, from one's own heart, and lead him.
**tal-a17-v54** — When, however, through contemplation of inclusion in one pure principle, one should not separately purify beyond hearing the lord of truth.
**tal-a17-v55** — Then, scattering the full [substance], one should lift the atom into the self and join it for the yoga of the self abiding in that state, with that other oblation.
**tal-a17-v56** — Some think the guru would give it by an action-word, but other teachers say such a thing is made of contemplation.
**tal-a17-v57** — Here an external offering should not be given separately to the teacher again; yet if one gives it, there is no fault, and that is a means in meditation.
**tal-a17-v58** — Thus, when the former father's self is established, the guru should join; then the disciple's heart should be led; that self is of so many sounds.
**tal-a17-v59** — Pure, for the attainment of firmness of that, and full by the supreme again; first the great Pashupata for purification of the reverse.
**tal-a17-v60** — I offer again by weapon ending in vauṣaṭ; thus one should throw again the full [oblation]; then, having worshipped Maya, one should dismiss her.
