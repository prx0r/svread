tal-a17-v81 — That pure consciousness, gone into consciousness, is Shiva-formed; it is not different from the disciple's consciousness, nor from mantra, fire, and the rest.

tal-a17-v82 — Meditating, by the application as before, on Shiva who is with parts and partless, or two-natured, one may cast the full power by calmed faculties.

tal-a17-v83 — In the Traiśirasa Tantra it is said to be all-fulfilling; yet by the root-rising path it is flooded with the Shiva-moon.

tal-a17-v84 — But that knowledge-nectar which arises from the root stream in the cavity at the middle or end of birth and is agitated by the fierce rays of Shiva's sun.

tal-a17-v85 — By that, one should properly satisfy [him] with quieted senses; but [he is] radiant with rays of light in the middle of the lotus of the void-abode.

tal-a17-v86 — He is the grasp of the scripture of motionless knowledge of supported and support, and the striking impact of the one sphota of the world supported by birth.

tal-a17-v87 — Beginning from the root place and extending to the end of someśa, it stands like the sky; it is called thus only until tranquility.

tal-a17-v88 — It was said in the previous scripture: having filled the ladle with ghee and performed [the rite], the disciple, thus abiding in the self, remembers the root mantra.

tal-a17-v89 — Uniting Shiva, Shakti, oneself, the disciple, ghee, and fire, one slowly reaches the dvādaśānta with an undivided mind.

tal-a17-v90 — There, having adopted kumbhaka and meditating on the whole and the partless, one should stand undisturbed until the ghee is exhausted.
