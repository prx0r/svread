**tal-a17-v87** — Beginning from the root place and extending to the end of someśa, it stands like the sky; it is called thus only until tranquility.
**tal-a17-v88** — It was said in the previous scripture: having filled the ladle with ghee and performed [the rite], the disciple, thus abiding in the self, remembers the root mantra.
**tal-a17-v89** — Uniting Shiva, Shakti, oneself, the disciple, ghee, and fire, one slowly reaches the dvādaśānta with an undivided mind.
**tal-a17-v90** — There, having adopted kumbhaka and meditating on the whole and the partless, one should stand undisturbed until the ghee is exhausted.
