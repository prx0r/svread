tal-a17-v71 — Of that full one, the maya-called, bond-breaking manifestation-natured [thing], I burn with three phaṭ; with vauṣaṭ the guru throws the full.

tal-a17-v72 — If it is to be made seedless, then here indeed one should cast another full mantra, endowed with the word of burning the seed called samaya-pāśa.

tal-a17-v73 — Devotion should be shown to the guru, to God, and to scripture; this is not a condition for the descent of power, for this is its very nature, not something separate.

tal-a17-v74 — At the end of māyā, when purity comes, the speech-lord who was formerly māyā full of power indeed attains the state of knowledge-power.

tal-a17-v75 — Having summoned pure knowledge, one should employ knowledge-power; thus, in order, when purified, the Sadashiva state too is sufficient.

tal-a17-v76 — Having cut the knotted crest, one should burn the impure boy, because in Sadashiva there are two bonds named authority and enjoyment.

tal-a17-v77 — Thus said, the anu bond is here; but the māyīya bond lasts until night. The student should bathe and sip as appropriate, and the teacher himself [is also implicated in this purification].

tal-a17-v78 — When the impurity called atomic is burnt up in the downward-flowing flame-impurity, then one contemplates all the knowables mentioned before.

tal-a17-v79 — Having joined the disciple's body with one's own body and life, one should make the universe from one's own body and life and remember it within.

tal-a17-v80 — By the stated procedure, one who is firm-minded and of undivided intellect has the place, time, and distance pair situated in prana, and prana is power-going.
