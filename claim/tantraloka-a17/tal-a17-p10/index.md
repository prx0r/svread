**tal-a17-v100** — The speciality here is this: as long as life, the child's guru should, with full [power], create pure principle for the remaining function.

**tal-a17-v92** — If, after the body falls, one again desires enjoyments in some principles, then through the differences of all and partial principles at the end one attains the supreme state.
**tal-a17-v93** — Then, in that truth-ground, with undivided mind in that counting, one should again perform yoking between full offerings.
**tal-a17-v94** — The initiation proclaimed as granting liberation, or granting both enjoyment and liberation, may be twofold: seeded or seedless.
**tal-a17-v95** — Seedless initiation is to be performed for a child, for one whose death is unknown, for you when powerless, or by old age and the like, when the strength of power-descent arises.
**tal-a17-v96** — In seedless samaya, one should purify the bonds too; one who has undergone seedless initiation is devoted to the deity, fire, and guru.
**tal-a17-v97** — By this much indeed one may go to Shiva immediately, having enjoyed enjoyments, or, as stated in the Shri Dikshottara, in practice thirty-six angulas.
**tal-a17-v98** — One should abandon, stage by stage, the worlds and truths that extend from foot to head; what remains is only an atom, partless, bodyless, that supreme self.
**tal-a17-v99** — By power, there I cast him thus; but meditating, one should initiate. In a seeded initiation, however, one should not purify from time.
