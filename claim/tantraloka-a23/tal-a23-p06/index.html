tal-a23-v51 — If this is known here, the world would not be destroyed; knowledge should be bestowed; even if it is unknown, when it is known again, one should practice the removal of knowledge.

tal-a23-v52 — When it is known again and again yet lacks trust, then one should meditate before him, throbbing like the moon and sun.

tal-a23-v53 — Then one should meditate on the deluding left power, which rises from the sky of wisdom of one's own heart-lotus and is darkened by Rahu.

tal-a23-v54 — Having contemplated her as having emerged by the left-hand procedure and as proceeding toward the goal, one should contemplate him as light swallowed by her.

tal-a23-v55 — By this sequence-yoga, for one of deluded mind and evil soul, knowledge, mantra, science, and the like cause harm.

tal-a23-v56 — But how could knowledge situated in the self be taken away? Therefore how is knowledge-removal said to be preceded by śrī?

tal-a23-v57 — It is said: since the disciple's knowledge has fully matured, why should it be removed from one who possesses complete fitness?

tal-a23-v58 — But this fool is deeply made by the Lord's Vāmā power by nature alone; therefore his vidyā and the rest are harmful.

tal-a23-v59 — Again, the teacher is identical with Shiva; if the consecrated one performs the fivefold action, then his full authority is that.

tal-a23-v60 — So the guru is authorized over the fourfold task — creation and maintenance of the pure realities, their impurity and their dissolution — through the joining (of the paths) and grace.
