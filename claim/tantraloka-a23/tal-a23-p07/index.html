tal-a23-v61 — By non-difference from Shiva, he performs that; like the fifth, this too is the duty called disappearance; thus that one is of Shiva-nature.

tal-a23-v62 — Therefore, indeed, in Shiva, in scripture, in knowledge, and in the vessel of reassurance, even the abode of anger is not hidden by the guru's foolishness.

tal-a23-v63 — Indeed, one whose teacher is angered is called hidden, a worldly being; yet he is God indeed—not a teacher, nor a false knower.

tal-a23-v64 — Therefore, although the defamer of scriptures and the like is not burned by the angry guru, yet he is obscured.

tal-a23-v65 — This is the coming of our teacher: when he is hidden in a child, one should not be angry, nor should the wise one curse, for he is indeed always a benefactor.

tal-a23-v66 — If, impelled by the Lord's will, he strangles the bond in his throat, what is the teacher to do for him there? Would there be cooperation?

tal-a23-v67 — Even though the guru is non-different from Shiva, he is indeed the Lord's principal, grace-filled power; one devoted to that power should be graced by him in every way.

tal-a23-v68 — But scripture is spoken only to awaken awareness of freedom; it is not an action to perform. For those who are falling, a hand-support is acceptable, but making them fall is not.

tal-a23-v69 — Therefore, having purified him with expiations, the guru should initiate him with compassion, since he is again facing the desire for independence and the like.

tal-a23-v70 — Approaching with an upward gaze and then being well reassured, one approaches the supreme; even by approaching the lower scripture, one may not attain worthiness of good.
