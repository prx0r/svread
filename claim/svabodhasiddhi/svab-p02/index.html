svab-p02-v10 — No way but keeping to your own being. Keep to her, rest in yourself — whoever does is happy.

svab-p02-v11 — From noticing your own notions, from holding to pure knowing, from winning the highest joy — the sage grows one solid mass of waking.

svab-p02-v12 — Grip your notions still, and power comes to rest in the powerful; their oneness is what yogis are after.

svab-p02-v13 — Know your own form, drop the veil of principles, scatter the cause of striving — then, tradition says, you are called free.

svab-p02-v14 — With a fully wakened mind, hold to waking alone — until, by your own strength, you stand in yourself, alone.

svab-p02-v07 — How tell what cannot be named? If anyone reaches it at all, it is by the honest method of feeling oneself.

svab-p02-v08 — Taught the yoga of knowing, yet with no trust inside — trustless, how could he ever taste freedom's fruit?

svab-p02-v09 — The way to the goal is this: stay awake in your own awakening. From such waking the sage stands whole, undivided.
