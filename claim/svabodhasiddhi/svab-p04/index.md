**svab-p04-v29** — From wanting nothing, from self-waking, from dropping hope and pride, from cutting the road of real-and-unreal — no taking of a body again.
**svab-p04-v30** — Inner sense at peace, fancies thinned, delighting in the gain of his own form, drunk on the nectar of tasting himself — he goes to freedom, his work done.
**svab-p04-v31** — So, for reaching the goal — stainless, lighting consciousness, true, mother of freedom's rise — I have told the perfection of self-waking.
**svab-p04-v32** — This teaching can make even one who never won enough, and free him from doubt's fault — with such doubtless teaching, what business has the much-chanting scholar?
**svab-p04-v33** — All-blessing maker, wildfire to tangled deeds, shower of your own station, free of every notion, temple of all joy — to cut suffering's root I bow to the unmatchably lovely Śiva, taught from the guru's mouth.
