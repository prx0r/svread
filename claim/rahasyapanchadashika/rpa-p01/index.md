## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**rpa-p01-v01** — At god's hour, surrender to the Lord. Then stillness, restraint, and the twilight rite. Two watches of muttering and worship, then the other session; the rest of time is Śiva's remainder-conduct.
**rpa-p01-v02** — A-first-faced, KA-handed, ṬA-footed, PĀ-sided with ṂA in the middle; YA-hearted goddess, consciousness-formed — Sarasvatī wins.
**rpa-p01-v03** — Thought-jewel, wish-cow, wish-trees bear fruit — just men's desires. Unasked, unheaped, nourish man's aims, O mother's noble mood.
**rpa-p01-v04** — Without whom nothing at all is done, nothing known, nothing wanted — by the lord of awareness. To her, the highest, the mother of worlds — homage to Śivā, Śiva's beloved.
**rpa-p01-v05** — Ever-risen, blessed, all-auspicious, Śiva-giving, dwelling in Śiva's heart, O Śivā — moonlight opening the night-lotus: for the worshipping mind, make my going in heaven supreme.
**rpa-p01-v06** — Be gracious, all-auspicious Śivā, Śiva's beloved. Umā, Ramā, Sarasvatī — you alone are the supreme goddess.
**rpa-p01-v07** — O mother, O Ambikā, formless, nameless, Umā, Raudrī, Vāmā, Mahālakṣmī, Māyā — supreme goddess, sporting the five acts, auspicious Bhairavī, glorious: I surrender to you.
**rpa-p01-v08** — Māyā, Vidyā, Mātṛkā, Mālinī — you throb in every body as awareness-spark-self. How shall I meditate you, self-flashing? I meditate you as inward-resounding speech.
