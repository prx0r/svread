deha-p02-v10 — Seated in the southwest petal she worships Bhairava with flowers of sound — to hearing-formed her, ever, I bow, the Vaiṣṇavī power.

deha-p02-v11 — Standing firm in the western quarter's petal, with heart-delighting flowers of touch she delights Bhairava — her who wears the form of skin I honor: Vārāhī.

deha-p02-v12 — With choicer details of form, her body seated in the wind-quarter's petal — she who honors Bhairava, her Indrāṇī, sight-bodied, I praise.

deha-p02-v13 — Dwelling in the sprout of the wealth-lord, she who ever with foods of varied six tastes honors Bhairava — her called tongue I honor: Cāmuṇḍā.

deha-p02-v14 — Seated in Īśa's petal she worships Bhairava with manifold perfumes — to her called nose, Mahālakṣmī, I ever bow.

deha-p02-v15 — Worshipable in the six views, infused with the thirty-six realities, ever self-named — the field-lord, giver of siddhis, I praise.

deha-p02-v16 — The quivering pith of experience, within all, ever close at hand — thus ever arisen I hymn my own body's circle of gods.

deha-p02-v09 — She spreads Bhairava's worship, seated in the southern petal, with flowers of distinction — ever mind-formed Kaumārī, her, I praise.
