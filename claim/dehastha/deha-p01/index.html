deha-p01-v01 — The body is proclaimed a temple of God, the own self the eternal God; casting off the sweepings of ignorance, let him worship with the feeling I-am-He.

deha-p01-v02 — Him who is saluted by the hosts of demons and gods, engaged in bestowing wished-for boons, worshipable as foremost in a hundred rites, breath-bodied — Gaṇapati I praise.

deha-p01-v03 — Whose foot-pair is worshipped by choice heroes, troops of yoginīs, rows of perfected ones; who removes the pain of humble folk — Vaṭuka, named apāna, I praise.

deha-p01-v04 — By whose mind-strength the universe shines as Śiva's path for devotees — him, intent in attention, the stainless true guru, always I praise.

deha-p01-v05 — With feasts of their own sense-objects the sense-goddesses ever fully honor in the heart-lotus — whom, him, made of consciousness, bliss-Bhairava, I praise.

deha-p01-v06 — The play of the dawning, shining forth, and chewing over of the universe, which she performs unceasingly — bliss-Bhairavī, her, whose form is reflection, I praise.

deha-p01-v07 — Who worships Bhairava with flowers of resolve, standing in the petal of the lord — to intellect-formed Brāhmaṇī, to her, I ever bow.

deha-p01-v08 — She performs Bhairava's worship, standing in fire's petal, with flowers of I-making — ever of I-making form, I praise her, Śāmbhavī the mother.
