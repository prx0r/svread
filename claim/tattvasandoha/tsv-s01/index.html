यदयमनुत्तरमूर्तिर्नजेच्छयाखिलमिदं जगत्स्रष्टुम्।
पस्पन्दे स स्पन्दः प्रथमः शिवतत्त्वमुच्यते तज्ज्ञैः॥१॥
इच्छा सैव स्वच्छा सन्ततसमवायिनी सती शक्तिः।
सचराचरस्य जगतो बीजं निखिलस्य निजनिलीनस्य॥२॥
स्वेच्छाशक्त्युद्गीर्णं जगदात्मतया समाच्छाद्य।
निवसन् स एव निखिलानुग्रहनिरतः सदाशिवोऽभिहितः॥३॥
विश्वं पश्चात्पश्यनिदन्तया निखिलमीश्वरो जातः।
सा भवति शुद्धविद्या येदन्ताहन्तयोरभेदमतिः॥४॥
माया विभेदबुद्धिर्निजांशजातेषु निखिलजीवेषु।
नित्यं तस्य निरङ्कुशविभवं वेलेव वारिधे रुन्धे॥५॥
स तया परिमितमूर्तिः सङ्कुचितसमस्तशक्तिरेष पुमान्।
रविरिव सन्ध्यारक्तः संहृतशक्तिः स्वभासनेऽप्यपटुः॥६॥
सम्पूर्णकर्तृताद्या बह्व्यः सन्त्यस्य शक्तयस्तस्य।
सङ्कोचात्सङ्कुचिताः कलादिरूपेण रूढयन्त्येवम्॥७॥
तत्सर्वकर्तृता सा सङ्कुचिता कतिपयार्थमात्रपरा।
किञ्चित्कर्तारममुं कलयन्ती कीर्त्यते कला नाम॥८॥
सर्वज्ञतास्य शक्तिः परिमिततनुरल्पवेद्यमात्रपरा।
ज्ञानमुत्पादयन्ती विद्येति निगद्यते बुधैराद्यैः॥९॥
नित्यपरिपूर्णतृप्तिः शक्तिस्तस्यैव परिमिता तु सती।
भोगेषु रञ्जयन्ती सततममुं रागतत्त्वतां याता॥१०॥
सा नित्यतास्य शक्तिर्निकृष्य निधनोदयप्रदानेन।
नियतपरिच्छेदकरी कॢप्ता स्यात्कालतत्त्वरूपेण॥११॥
यास्य स्वतन्त्रताख्या शक्तिः सङ्कोचशालिनी सैव।
कृत्याकृत्येष्ववशं नियतममुं नियमयन्त्यभून्नियतिः॥१२॥
इच्छादित्रिसमष्टिः शक्तिः शान्तास्य सङ्कुचद्रूपा।
सङ्कलितेच्छाद्यात्मकसत्त्वादिकसाम्यरूपिणी तु सती॥१३॥
बुद्ध्यादिसामरस्यस्वरूपचित्तात्मिका मता प्रकृतिः।
इच्छास्य रजोरूपाहङ्कृतिरासीदहम्प्रतीतिकरी॥१४॥
ज्ञानापि सत्त्वरूपा निर्णयबोधस्य कारणं बुद्धिः।
तस्य क्रिया तमोमयमूर्तिर्मन उच्यते विकल्पकरी॥१५॥
वामादिपञ्चभेदः स एव सङ्कुचितविग्रहो देवः।
ज्ञानक्रियोपरागप्राधान्याद्विविधविषयरूपोऽभूत्॥१६॥
श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनजिह्वाघ्राणानि बोधकरणानि।
शब्दस्पर्शौ रूपं रसगन्धौ चेति भूतसूक्ष्माणि॥१७॥
अयमेवातिनिकृष्टो जातो भूतात्मनापि भूतेशः।
गगनमनिलश्च तेजः सलिलं भूमिश्च पञ्चभूतानि॥१८॥
श्रोत्रादिकरणवेद्याः शब्दाद्यास्तानि वेदकान्येषाम्।
वचनकरी वागासीत् पाणिः स्यात्करणभूत आदाने॥१९॥
गमनविसर्गानन्दत्रितये पादादिकं करणम्।
गन्धवती भूमिः स्यादापः सांसिद्धिकद्रवास्तेजः॥२०॥
उष्णस्पर्शमरूपस्पर्शो वायुरम्बरं सशब्दम्।
षट्त्रिंशत्तत्त्वमयं वन्दे कौलं कुलातिगं शम्भुम्॥२१॥

tsv-s01-v01 — That Anuttara-formed one, by his own desire to manifest all this world, vibrated; that first vibration is called the Śiva-principle by those who know it.

tsv-s01-v10 — Ever-full satisfaction, his very power, yet limited, being — ever coloring him toward enjoyments — has become raga-principle-ness.

tsv-s01-v11 — That eternity, his power — having drawn off by giving rise-and-fall — making fixed divisions, is arranged in the form of the time-principle.

tsv-s01-v12 — Which power of his called autonomy, rich in contraction — that very one, constraining him helplessly fixed in do-and-do-not, became restriction.

tsv-s01-v13 — The triad-collection of will and the rest — power, quiescent, of contracted form in him; but consisting, being, in the sameness of sattva and the rest whose nature is collected will and the rest.

tsv-s01-v14 — Deemed prakṛti: of the nature of citta whose nature is the fusion of buddhi and the rest; his will, as rajas-formed I-making effecting I-notion, was.

tsv-s01-v15 — Even knowledge, of sattva-nature, is the cause of determinative cognition — buddhi; its action, dark-formed-bodied, is called mind, the doubting-maker.

tsv-s01-v16 — With fivefold division beginning with Vāmā, that very god of contracted body — through knowledge-action-tinge predominance became of manifold-object form.

tsv-s01-v17 — Ear, eye, touch, tongue, nose are the knowledge-instruments; sound, touch, form, taste, smell — thus the subtle elements.

tsv-s01-v18 — This very one, most base-born, even as elemental self, is the Lord of beings; ether, air, fire, water, earth — the five gross elements.

tsv-s01-v19 — Beginning with sound, to be known by ear and other instruments — they are the knowns of these; speech was the uttering-maker, the hand would be the instrument-being in taking.

tsv-s01-v02 — Will itself — transparent, ever-co-inherent, being — is power: the seed of the entire world of moving and unmoving things, hidden in herself.

tsv-s01-v20 — In the triad of going, excreting, bliss — foot and the rest as instrument; earth would be smell-possessing, waters naturally fluid, fire.

tsv-s01-v21 — Hot touch and formless touch is wind; ether with sound. I praise the Kaula consisting of thirty-six principles, beyond the kula — Śambhu.

tsv-s01-v03 — Having fully covered with the Self the world emitted by own will-power, abiding — he indeed, devoted to all grace, is called Sadāśiva.

tsv-s01-v04 — Seeing the universe after himself with this-ness, the Lord has become all; that becomes pure knowledge, in which this and I are held non-different.

tsv-s01-v05 — Māyā is difference-notion toward all beings born of own portions; always his unbridled power — like the ocean shore — blocks.

tsv-s01-v06 — He, by her, of limited form with all powers contracted — this is the Person; like the sun red at dusk, powers withdrawn, unskilled even in self-shining.

tsv-s01-v07 — Complete doership and the rest — many are his powers; through contraction contracted, in the form of kalā and the rest, thus they ascend.

tsv-s01-v08 — That all-doership, contracted, directed merely at a few things — making him a doer-of-little — is called kalā by name.

tsv-s01-v09 — His all-knowing power, of limited body, directed merely at little to be known — producing knowledge — is declared vidyā by the ancient wise.
