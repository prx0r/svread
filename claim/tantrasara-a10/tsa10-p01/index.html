tsa-a10-v01 — Thus, for now, the tattva-path is stated.

tsa-a10-v10 — The autonomous, indeed, is the supreme tattva — that too, what is immeasurable, is beyond-kalā.

tsa-a10-v11 — Thus exactly five kalās make the thirty-six tattvas.

tsa-a10-v12 — Thus: prameyatva is twofold — gross-subtle; that makes ten.

tsa-a10-v13 — Karaṇatva is twofold — pure and agent-touching; that makes ten.

tsa-a10-v14 — By the distinctness of instrument-subordinate-agent-status: five pure-agent-status; five by undivided-division in expansion-facing; the Śiva-tattva, free of exhaustive-limitation, makes the thirty-sixth.

tsa-a10-v15 — And when it is taught or contemplated — that which is its station-place is the thirty-seventh; when that too is contemplated, the thirty-eighth. And there is no infinite regress of the to-be-contemplated: in the undivided-self-autonomy-joined knowing-making it ends in the thirty-seventh itself — the thirty-sixth, having transcended all tattvas, being comprehensible-within-limit; such is the five-kalā rule.

tsa-a10-v16 — The Ātma-kalā up to Vijñāna-kalā, Īśa-ending the Vidyā-kalā, the rest the Śiva-kalā: such the three-tattva rule.

tsa-a10-v17 — Thus the ninety-tattva-and-rest too should be inferred — it is said.

tsa-a10-v18 — Going-with-the-knowable-part — by gross-subtle-other-nature triply: the world-tattva-kalā-path-distinction is triply named by mātṛ-viśrānti; likewise triply: in its being-authority, the padā-dhvā; in the authority's agitation-wave-subsiding, the mantra-dhvā; in its subsiding, the full-knowership, the varṇa-dhvā — it, and this, in such viśrānti, becomes one whose own-nature is gained: thus the one's sixfoldness is warranted.

tsa-a10-v02 — The kalā-path, though, is now delineated: as in worlds something goes along as form — that is a tattva; so in the tattvas, class by class, whatever goes along as form — that is kalā, since it endures one-form classifications.

tsa-a10-v03 — As in earth, nivṛtti turns back — from which the tattva-creation comes, it is said.

tsa-a10-v04 — In the class beginning with water, pratiṣṭhā as cause — by nourishment and filling.

tsa-a10-v05 — In the class ending with māyā-of-puruṣa, vidyā — by the excess of consciousness over the disappearance of the knowable.

tsa-a10-v06 — In the class ending with Śuddha-Vidyā-Śakti, śāntā — by the subsidence of the kañcuka-waves.

tsa-a10-v07 — This very four-egg-group is the pārthiva-prākṛta-māyīya-śakti-named group.

tsa-a10-v08 — In these, of the earth-and-rest powers, by their standing in the power-tattva as far as mutual contact exists, and contact's being with-obstacle — so much is warranted as egg-hood.

tsa-a10-v09 — In the Śiva-tattva, śāntātiśayā — because of the auspiciousness of its teaching-contemplation-arcā-beginning.
