## Reading

Of things there is no arising; naturally, by your contemplation they shine; even the non-true partakes of truth for a moment, through experience's delusion. This universe-glory is will-born from you, birth from elsewhere it has none; hence by splendor you shine in worlds — one, yet many-selfed.
