tal-a26-v01 — Now is told the remaining activity useful to the living.

tal-a26-v10 — Those two natural and seedless who would speak for remaining activity; progressively identity means Guru worship devoted but those two.

tal-a26-v02 — Initiation manifold this ending in śrāddha which is declared; that for purification, for liberation, for enjoyment also, for both or.

tal-a26-v03 — There for accomplishment of purification which initiation directly not liberating; by intention which and directly liberating with-seed.

tal-a26-v04 — By those two initiated who, their life-long behavior to be spoken, of putrakas and the rest, for accomplishment of identity.

tal-a26-v05 — Of desirer of enjoyment or of desirer of liberation or, from own consciousness guru scripture by authority which, for purification initiation indeed by guru done.

tal-a26-v06 — Then he purified fit having known self in own doctrine; practice of that spoken thing for enjoyment for liberation and cultivates.

tal-a26-v07 — From Ācārya faith indeed who even would be enjoyment-liberation holder; for destruction of that obstacle emergence should speak to him also behavior.

tal-a26-v08 — Of own consciousness and guru consciousness similar faith holder even, by remaining activity to be instructed for cessation of that obstacle etc.

tal-a26-v09 — Who in every way having abandoned dependence on other but standing from own; from faith who even and from Ācārya faith indeed from only.
