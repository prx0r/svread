tal-a26-v31 — Purification having made of mantras in proper place placement; mudrā exhibition meditation from difference-nondifference nature.

tal-a26-v32 — In body breath intellect ether earth by mind there and worship; muttering and here according to power to god this offering.

tal-a26-v33 — For accomplishment of identity state at every juncture should practice; others but east north west south directions fourfold.

tal-a26-v34 — Of junctures they say this and Tantric not our opinion; which that in time chapter before juncture declared fourfold.

tal-a26-v35 — That very within having concentrated juncture-related should practice rite; making fourfold juncture or one disciple.

tal-a26-v36 — Should do study knowledge Guru duty etc devoted; from juncture meditation risen endless identity state union.

tal-a26-v37 — By that purification force all time would be identity indeed he; then at desired time he worship with flowers liquor and the rest.

tal-a26-v38 — On altar and the rest disciple should do according to capacity etc; well-purified with rite all having done inner muttering end.

tal-a26-v39 — Argha-vessel before as having made from own dear mantra; by that altar flowers etc all wise should sprinkle.

tal-a26-v40 — Then there indeed having intended door seat Guru succession; should worship Śiva-absorbed own-body-worship before.
