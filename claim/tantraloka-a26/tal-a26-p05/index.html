tal-a26-v41 — Then that altar having seen sky crystal pure consciousness-natured; having seen there own deity-group.

tal-a26-v42 — By reflection-ness by mirror-ness and from consciousness; this invocation principal like fan of winds.

tal-a26-v43 — All-going even as wind by fan sustained; all-pervading thing-doing mantra-cakra from rising so would be.

tal-a26-v44 — By fifty-four that this principle is said; in venerable Nirmaryāda-śāstra and that this by Lord spoken.

tal-a26-v45 — God all-going god boundless how Śiva is invoked is forgiven; thus asked spoke Lord.

tal-a26-v46 — By impression is invoked goddess impression and is dismissed; by ultimate truth of god no invocation dismissal.

tal-a26-v47 — Invoked by me god on altar and established; worshipped praised thus having rejoiced god should dismiss.

tal-a26-v48 — Of unawakened beings for generating satisfaction indeed; invocation etc of them activity how otherwise.

tal-a26-v49 — By time but know engaged in Lord's rule; progressively of god attainment world-preceding.

tal-a26-v50 — By knowledge lamp light destroyed all ignorance collection; whence is brought god where or is led even he.
