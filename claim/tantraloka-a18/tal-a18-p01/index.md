## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a18-v01** — Now this concise initiation is taught — the attainment of Śiva-hood: no powder-mandala, no eve-rite here, no taking of ground and field; but in whatever place, the guru worships Śiva.
**tal-a18-v10** — Thinking 'I have not become identical' — the rite lying scattered — joining it up, thinking 'through sequence identity is reached', he should perform the rite in its scattered form.
**tal-a18-v02** — Having meditated the path with the mind, the knower of the tattvas' far shore should initiate her who is freed from birth and the rest — by whatever path the guru proceeds.
**tal-a18-v03** — He should make it ending in the single tattva, steeped solely in the Śiva-mood; then with the Parā-mantra unto him: 'Now I purify the tattva.'
**tal-a18-v04** — 'Svāhā' thus for each tattva; upon purification he should cast the full oblation. So with all other mantras too — or else as told.
**tal-a18-v05** — Enclosed with Parā by name, ending in svāhā, ending in the first: a hundred, a thousand, or a hundred-and-eight — with that, with śakti indeed, he should offer.
**tal-a18-v06** — Then 'pūrṇā' — having purified the unbroken, highest, such-like — the initiation-rite taught here and there in scripture by the Great Lord.
**tal-a18-v07** — Singly each: with the Mātṛkā-pair letters he should purify the tattvas; or else with the condensed mantra — in all mantras this is the rule.
**tal-a18-v08** — And in whatever measure the guru's knowledge is well-practiced, his self made of identity-with-that — in that measure let him do the concise rite, not otherwise.
**tal-a18-v09** — And in the venerable Brahmayāmala it is said: even in the concise he should contemplate the pervasion common to all paths — only, in the sacrifice, no elaboration.
