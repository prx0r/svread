gītārthasaṃgraha 

ya eṣa vitatasphuradvividhabhāvacakrātmakaḥ 
parasparavibhedavānviṣayatāmupāgacchati | yadekamayabhāvanāvaśata 
etyabhedānvayaṃ sa śaṃbhuraśivāpaho jayati bodhabhāsāṃ nidhiḥ 
|| 1-1 || 

dvaipāyanena muninā yadidaṃ vyadhāyi sāstraṃ 
sahasraśatasaṃmitamatra mokṣaḥ | mādhānyataḥ phalatayā 
prathitastadanya- dharmādi tasya paripoṣayituṃ pragītam || 1-2 || 

mokṣaśca nāma sakalāpravibhāgarūpe 
sarvajñasarvakaraṇādiśubhasvabhāve | ākāṅkṣya virahite 
bhagavatyadhīśe nityodite laya iyānprathiḥ samāsāt || 1-3 || 

yadyapyanyaprasaṅgeṣu mokṣo nāmātra gīyate | tathāpi 
bhagavadgītāḥ samyaktatprāptidāyikāḥ || 1-4 || 

tāsvanyaiḥ prāktanairvyākhyāḥ kṛtā yadyapi bhūyasā | 
nyāyyastathāpyudyamo me tadgūḍhārthaprakāśakaḥ || 1-5 || 

bhaṭṭendurājādāmnāya vivicya ca ciraṃ dhiyā | 
kṛto'bhinavaguptena so'yaṃ gītārthasaṃgrahaḥ || 1-6 ||

## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**gita-a00-v01** — He who, made of the widely flashing wheel of diverse states, comes to be the objects of experience — whose one, given to contemplation, is thus the bond of nondifference: He, Śaṃbhura, woe-averting, triumphs — treasury of the lights of knowing.
**gita-a00-v02** — By the sage Dvaipāyana was this scripture expounded, measured by hundreds of thousands; here liberation is established as the fruit — and mādhānyataḥ [L0-fork], its other dharma-and-rest is recited to nourish it.
**gita-a00-v03** — Liberation, by name, complete-differentiation in form, all-knowing, auspicious-natured in all instruments and more — free from need, in Bhagavān the Lord, ever-risen: dissolution — this much, toward it [prathiḥ L0-fork], in summary.
**gita-a00-v04** — Although liberation by name is celebrated in other connections, yet the Bhagavadgītā is the giver of true attainment.
**gita-a00-v05** — In them, although earlier ones made many expositions, yet my attempt too is reasonable — revealing its hidden meaning.
**gita-a00-v06** — From the oral tradition of Bhaṭṭa Indurāja, examined long with thought, made by Abhinavagupta — this here is the Gītārthasaṃgraha.
