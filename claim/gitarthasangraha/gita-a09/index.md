**gita-a09-v10** — Those actions do not bind me, wealth-winner, seated like an indifferent one, unattached in those actions.
**gita-a09-v14** — Great souls, resorting to divine nature, worship with unwavering mind, knowing the imperishable origin of beings.
**gita-a09-v15** — Always glorifying, striving, vowed, bowing, and ever united, they worship with devotion.
**gita-a09-v17** — I am sacrifice, svadha, medicine, mantra, ghee, fire, and offering.

**gita-a09-v19** — The Lord is the goal, supporter, witness, dwelling, refuge, friend, origin, dissolution, abode, treasure, and imperishable seed.
**gita-a09-v21** — Knowers of the three Vedas, purified of sin, sacrifice and seek heaven; gaining merit, they enjoy divine pleasures in Indra's world.
**gita-a09-v24** — Even devotees of other gods, endowed with faith, sacrifice; they too worship me, Kaunteya, though without proper rule.
**gita-a09-v25** — I am the enjoyer and lord of all sacrifices, yet they do not know me truly and therefore deviate.
**gita-a09-v26** — God-vowed go to gods, ancestor-vowed to ancestors, being-worshippers to beings, and my worshippers to me.
**gita-a09-v28** — Thus, one who knows in the stated order finds even an offering to Indra and other gods to be an offering to the supreme lord; every other action too becomes worship of the great lord as one's own self. Therefore whatever you do, eat, offer, give, or practice as austerity, O son of Kunti, do that as an offering to me.
**gita-a09-v30** — I am equal among all beings; to me none is hateful, none dear. But those who worship me with devotion are in me, and I too am in them.
**gita-a09-v31** — Even if someone is of very bad conduct, if he worships me with undivided devotion, he should indeed be considered a saint, for he is rightly resolved.
**gita-a09-v33** — O Partha, even those of sinful birth, women, vaishyas, and shudras who take refuge in me indeed go to the supreme destination.
**gita-a09-v34** — How much more so holy brahmins and devoted royal sages; having obtained this impermanent, unhappy world, worship me.
**gita-a09-v06** — Just as the great, all-pervading wind ever abides in space, so know that all beings abide in me.
