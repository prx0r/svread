gita-a08-v01 — Now the eighth chapter. Arjuna said: What is that Brahman? What is the self? What is action, O supreme person? What is said to be concerning beings? And what is called the divine?

gita-a08-v13 — He who departs uttering Om, the single syllable Brahman, remembering me, goes to the supreme goal.

gita-a08-v17 — They know Brahma's day as a thousand yugas and night as a thousand yugas.

gita-a08-v18 — All manifest beings arise from the unmanifest at day and dissolve into the unmanifest at night.

gita-a08-v20 — Beyond that is another eternal being, manifest and unmanifest, which does not perish when all beings perish.

gita-a08-v21 — The unmanifest imperishable is called the supreme goal; reaching it there is no return, and that supreme abode is mine.

gita-a08-v24 — Departing knowers of brahman in the bright six-month northern course go to brahman.

gita-a08-v05 — At the time of death, whoever remembers me alone and leaves the body goes to my state; there is no doubt about this.

gita-a08-v06 — Whatever state one remembers when leaving the body at death, that is the state one attains, O Kaunteya, being always imbued with that state.

gita-a08-v08 — By yoga of practice, with a mind that does not wander elsewhere, one reaches the supreme divine Person, O Partha, by thinking of him; after the body breaks, freed from bodily pain, one then thinks of the Lord.

gita-a08-v09 — He who remembers the seer, the ancient orderer, subtler than an atom, the supporter of all, of inconceivable form, sun-colored, beyond darkness.
