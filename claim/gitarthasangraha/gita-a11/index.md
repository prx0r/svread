## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**gita-a11-v01** — Now the eleventh chapter: Arjuna asks to make directly seen the very meaning stated in the preceding chapter, since what is understood through teaching becomes clear when mounted by direct cognition; therefore these statements and replies are spoken. Arjuna said: for my grace you spoke the supreme secret called self-knowledge; by that speech this delusion of mine is gone.
**gita-a11-v10** — Many mouths and eyes, many wondrous sights; many divine ornaments; divine, many raised weapons.
**gita-a11-v11** — Wearing divine garlands and garments, anointed with divine fragrance, full of all wonders, divine and endless, with faces everywhere.
**gita-a11-v12** — If the splendor of a thousand suns were to rise at once in heaven, that might be like the splendor of that great soul.
**gita-a11-v13** — Then Arjuna saw the whole world, divided in many ways, in one place in the body of the god of gods.
**gita-a11-v14** — Then Arjuna, filled with wonder, hair standing on end, bowed with his head and spoke to the god with folded hands.
**gita-a11-v15** — Arjuna said: I see your gods in your body, O god, and all groups of beings, Brahma the lord seated on the lotus, sages, and all divine serpents.
**gita-a11-v16** — I see you with many arms, bellies, faces, and eyes, endless in form on every side; I see no end, no middle, and no beginning again, O lord of the universe, universal form!
**gita-a11-v17** — I see you crowned, mace-bearing, and discus-bearing, a mass of radiance on all sides, radiant; I see you hard to look at, all around, immeasurable in blazing fire, sun, and radiance.
**gita-a11-v19** — I see you, infinite in power and arms, moon-and-sun-eyed, with a blazing fire-mouth, scorching this universe by your own radiance.
**gita-a11-v02** — Birth and dissolution of beings have indeed been heard by me in detail from you, lotus-petal-eyed one; your greatness and imperishability too.
**gita-a11-v20** — Indeed, this interval between heaven and earth is pervaded by you alone, and all directions; seeing your wonderful fierce form, the three worlds are agitated, great soul.
**gita-a11-v21** — Indeed these god-hosts enter into you; some frightened ones, with joined palms, praise, saying svasti; and great-sage hosts hymn you with abundant praises.
**gita-a11-v22** — Rudras, Adityas, Vasus, Sādhyas, Viśve, Aśvins, Maruts, Uṣmapās, and Gandharva, Yakṣa, Asura, and Siddha hosts all behold you, astonished.
**gita-a11-v23** — Seeing your great form with many mouths and eyes, mighty-armed, with many arms, thighs, feet, bellies, tusks, and fangs, the worlds are agitated, and so am I.
**gita-a11-v24** — Seeing you, whose form touches the sky, blazing in many colors, with open mouth and blazing wide eyes, my inner self is agitated; I find neither steadiness nor peace, O Viṣṇu.
**gita-a11-v25** — Seeing your fang-terrifying mouths, like death-fire, I know no directions and find no comfort; be gracious, lord of gods, abode of the world.
**gita-a11-v26** — All these sons of Dhritarashtra, together with groups of earth-protectors, Bhishma, Drona, that charioteer's son, and our own warrior chiefs, are seen.
**gita-a11-v27** — Hurrying, they enter your fang-terrifying fearful mouths; some are seen stuck between the teeth with crushed heads.
**gita-a11-v28** — Men of various forms, being crushed, enter your mouth of inconceivable form; Yudhishthira's and Dhritarashtra's warriors, all cut by various weapons, enter indeed.
**gita-a11-v29** — Surely these are slain by your radiance; for thus these have entered your body, as many river currents flow toward the ocean indeed.
**gita-a11-v03** — Thus this is as you said: I wish to see you yourself, supreme lord, your divine form, O best of persons.
**gita-a11-v30** — Thus your world-heroes enter the blazing mouths around, as moths enter blazing fire for destruction with swift force; so too worlds enter your mouths for destruction with swift force.
**gita-a11-v31** — You lick and devour all worlds on every side with blazing mouths; filling the whole world with radiance, your fierce rays scorch, O Viṣṇu.
**gita-a11-v33** — The blessed Lord said: I am Time, grown and world-destroying; I am engaged here to gather worlds. Even without you, all warriors stationed in opposing ranks will not be.
**gita-a11-v34** — Therefore rise, gain glory; having conquered enemies, enjoy prosperous rule. These have already been slain by me; be a mere instrument, left-handed archer.
**gita-a11-v36** — Sañjaya said: having heard this speech of Keśava, the crowned one, with folded hands and trembling, bowed again and indeed said to Kṛṣṇa with faltering voice, greatly frightened, having prostrated.
**gita-a11-v39** — You are the primordial god, the ancient person; you are the supreme treasure of this universe; you are the knower and the knowable, the supreme abode; by you the universe of infinite form is pervaded.
**gita-a11-v04** — If you think that is possible for me to see, O lord, lord of yogis, then show me your imperishable self.
**gita-a11-v40** — You are air, Yama, fire, Varuna, the moon, Prajapati, and great-grandfather; beginningless, measureless, unmatched being; lord of all; O all-great manifestation.
**gita-a11-v42** — No other is seen in the three worlds; the lord of inconceivable action, endless vigor, and unmeasured valor pervades all, therefore he is all.
**gita-a11-v43** — Thinking you were my friend, I spoke forcibly, O Krishna, O Yadava, O friend; not knowing your greatness, I did this by carelessness or affection.
**gita-a11-v44** — Whatever disrespect was shown to you for mockery, in play, bed, seat, or food, alone or before others, O Acyuta, I beg your pardon, immeasurable one.
**gita-a11-v45** — You are father of the moving and still world, venerable teacher of this universe; none is equal to you, much less superior, in the three worlds of unmatched splendor.
**gita-a11-v46** — Therefore bowing and placing my body, I propitiate you, worshipful Lord; as a father bears a son, as a friend bears a friend, O god, you should endure.
**gita-a11-v47** — The sages remember your divine and wondrous former deeds; no other is doer of the world; you alone are sustainer, ordainer, and all-pervading Bhava.
**gita-a11-v48** — What wondrous thing of yours would be unbearable, or what could be possible from another? I will proclaim, since you are doer of all, O vibhu; therefore all this is you alone.
**gita-a11-v49** — Your action is extremely wondrous and not difficult; no comparison for your action is found; not even your qualities have measure, nor your splendor, strength, or glory.
**gita-a11-v05** — The blessed Lord said: see, O son of Pritha, my forms by hundreds and by thousands, of various kinds, divine, of various colors and forms.
