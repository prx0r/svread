gita-a16-v19 — I continually cast those hateful cruel lowest men into asuri wombs.

gita-a16-v02 — Non-harm, truth, non-anger, renunciation, non-attachment, non-slander, compassion, non-covetousness, gentleness, modesty, and non-restlessness.

gita-a16-v22 — Freed from these three dark gates, O Kaunteya, a man practices for self and then goes to the supreme destination.

gita-a16-v23 — This is not a human saying to disregard; scripture here is beginningless authority. One who abandons scriptural rule and acts from desire attains neither perfection, happiness, nor supreme destination.

gita-a16-v03 — Energy, patience, steadfastness, contentment, non-malice, and absence of excessive pride are the divine wealth of one born divine, Bharata.

gita-a16-v04 — Hypocrisy, pride, egoism, anger, harshness, and ignorance are the demonic wealth, Partha.

gita-a16-v06 — In this world there are two creature-creations, divine and demonic; the divine was spoken in detail, now hear the demonic, Partha.

gita-a16-v09 — Relying on this view, lost selves with small intellects arise as fierce actors, inauspicious for the world's destruction.
