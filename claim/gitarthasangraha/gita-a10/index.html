gita-a10-v01 — The Lord says he will speak his supreme word again to Arjuna, who is pleased, out of desire for his welfare.

gita-a10-v10 — To those constantly united and worshipping with love, I give that wisdom-yoga by which they reach me.

gita-a10-v11 — For their compassion, I, abiding in self-state, destroy ignorance-born darkness with the shining lamp of knowledge.

gita-a10-v12 — Arjuna said: you are supreme Brahman, supreme abode, pure, highest person, eternal, divine first god, unborn, all-pervading.

gita-a10-v13 — The sages all say this of you, and likewise the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala, and Vyasa; you yourself also speak to me.

gita-a10-v14 — I think all this is true, O Kesava, that you speak to me; for neither gods nor great sages know your manifestation, O Lord.

gita-a10-v15 — You yourself know yourself by yourself, O best of persons, creator of beings, lord of beings, god of gods, lord of the world.

gita-a10-v16 — You should tell completely the auspicious glories of yourself, by which glories you pervade and stand in all these worlds.

gita-a10-v17 — How may I know you, O yogi, while I consider you? In which and which states are you to be thought, O Lord, by me?

gita-a10-v18 — Tell again in detail your yoga and glory, O Janardana; for there is no satisfaction for me hearing this nectar.

gita-a10-v19 — The blessed Lord said: indeed, I will tell you my auspicious glories chiefly, best of Kurus; there is no end to my expansion.

gita-a10-v02 — Neither gods nor great sages know his origin; he is indeed the beginning of gods and great sages in every way.

gita-a10-v20 — I am the self, Guḍākeśa, dwelling in the hearts of all beings; I am the beginning, middle, and indeed the end of beings.

gita-a10-v21 — Among Ādityas I am Viṣṇu, among lights the sun with rays; among Maruts I am Marīci, and among stars I am the moon.

gita-a10-v22 — Among Vedas I am the Sāmaveda; among gods I am Vāsava; among senses I am mind; among beings I am consciousness.

gita-a10-v23 — Among Rudras I am Śaṅkara; among Yakṣas and Rākṣasas I am the lord of wealth; among Vasus I am fire; among peaks I am Meru.

gita-a10-v25 — Among great sages I am Bhṛgu; among speeches the one imperishable syllable; among sacrifices the sacrifice of muttered prayer; among immovable things Himalaya.

gita-a10-v26 — Among all trees the sacred fig tree; among divine sages Nārada; among gandharvas Citraratha; among perfected ones Kapila the sage.

gita-a10-v27 — Know me as the swift one among horses, Airāvata among lordly elephants, and the lord of men among men.

gita-a10-v28 — I am the thunderbolt among weapons, the wish-cow among cows, Kandarpa as procreation, and Vāsuki among serpents.

gita-a10-v29 — I am Ananta among serpents, Varuṇa among aquatic beings, Aryaman among ancestors, and Yama among controllers.

gita-a10-v03 — Whoever knows me as unborn, beginningless, and great lord of the world, being undeluded, is freed from all sins among mortals.

gita-a10-v30 — I am Prahlāda among daityas, time among those who reckon, the lion king among beasts, and Vainateya among birds.

gita-a10-v31 — I am the wind among purifiers, Rama among weapon-bearers, the makara among fishes, and the Ganga among rivers.

gita-a10-v32 — Of creations I am the beginning, the end, and the middle too, O Arjuna; of knowledges, self-knowledge; of debates, I am the speech among speakers.

gita-a10-v33 — Of letters I am the a-sound, and the dual among compounds; I am imperishable time, and I the ordainer facing all directions.

gita-a10-v34 — Death is all-seizing and the origin of I among future things; fame, prosperity, and speech belong to women; memory, intelligence, steadfastness, and forgiveness are also named.

gita-a10-v35 — Among the samans I am the Brihat-saman; among meters I am Gayatri; among months I am Margashirsha; among seasons I am the flower-maker.

gita-a10-v36 — I am gambling among the fraudulent, splendor among the splendid, victory, resolve, and goodness among the good.

gita-a10-v37 — Among the Vṛṣṇis I am Vāsudeva; among the Pāṇḍavas, Dhanañjaya; among sages, Vyāsa; among poets, Uśanas the poet.

gita-a10-v38 — I am the rod among punishers, policy among conquerors; and indeed I am silence among secrets, knowledge among knowers.

gita-a10-v39 — Whatever is the seed of all beings, that I am, Arjuna; nothing exists without me, no being moving or still.

gita-a10-v40 — There is no end, Parantapa, to my divine glories; this extent of glory has been spoken by me briefly.

gita-a10-v41 — Whatever being is glorious, splendid, or mighty, know that very thing, you, to be born of a spark of my splendor.

gita-a10-v05 — Non-harm, equanimity, contentment, austerity, giving, fame, and infamy become various states of beings from me alone.

gita-a10-v06 — The seven former great sages and four Manus are born from my mind-born nature, and these creatures of the world are theirs.

gita-a10-v07 — Whoever truly knows this glory and yoga is united by unshaken yoga; there is no doubt here.

gita-a10-v08 — I am the source of all; all proceeds from me; thus the wise, endowed with devotion, worship me.
