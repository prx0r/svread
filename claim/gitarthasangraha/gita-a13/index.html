gita-a13-v16 — It is outside and inside beings, moving and unmoving; because of subtlety it is unknowable, far and near.

gita-a13-v17 — Undivided in beings, yet as if divided, it stands; bearer of beings, that to-be-known, devouring and ruling.

gita-a13-v02 — The blessed Lord said: This body, O son of Kunti, is called the field; he who knows this, the knowers of that call the field-knower.

gita-a13-v25 — Some see the self by meditation, others by sāṃkhya, yoga, or karma-yoga.

gita-a13-v28 — He who sees the supreme lord standing equally in all beings, perishing with the perishing, truly sees.

gita-a13-v31 — When one sees distinct beings as unified, then brahman is attained.

gita-a13-v32 — The beginningless, qualityless supreme self, though in the body, neither acts nor is stained.

gita-a13-v04 — Hear from me in brief what that field is, of what kind, with what modifications, from what, what it is, who its knower is, and of what nature.

gita-a13-v08 — Humility, nonpretense, nonviolence, patience, straightness, service to the teacher, purity, steadiness, and self-restraint.

gita-a13-v09 — Dispassion and non-egoism in sense objects, and seeing the faults of birth, death, age, disease, and suffering.
