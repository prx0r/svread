## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**gita-a06-v01** — The lord said: one who does duty as action without relying on its fruit is both renouncer and yogi, not one who merely abandons fire or action.
**gita-a06-v12** — Having established a firm seat for oneself in a pure place—not too high and not too low—with cloth, deer skin, and kuśa grass on top.
**gita-a06-v14** — Holding body, head, and neck even and motionless, steady, looking at the tip of one's own nose, and not looking at the directions.
**gita-a06-v17** — Yoga is not for one who eats too much, nor for one who fasts one-sidedly, nor for one who sleeps too much, nor for one who stays awake too much, Arjuna.
**gita-a06-v21** — Where the mind ceases through yoga practice, and the self sees itself in the self, there it is satisfied.
