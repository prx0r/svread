tal-a32-v21 — Avadhūta, without conduct, not-I-am thus meditating, mantra-single-devoted, seeing body-dwelling all deities.

tal-a32-v27 — This Karaṅkiṇī goddess; Jvālinī hear now; jaw and forehead, hands spreading fingers clear.

tal-a32-v28 — Should move by wind force, having made inner eyebrow, wise; having opened mouth with tongue and, hāhā-sound but should cause.

tal-a32-v29 — This Jvālinī in fire-wheel; by her and hundred-and-eight hundred should recite if; then would succeed three worlds with moving and nonmoving.

tal-a32-v30 — In other bodies self, other from own body; should see moving, from loss and gain standing in coming-going place.
