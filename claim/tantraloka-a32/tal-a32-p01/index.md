**tal-a32-v00** — Now I speak here the procedure of mudrās praised in guru-scripture song.

**tal-a32-v05** — Karaṅkiṇī, Krodhanā, and Bhairavī, Lelihānikā, Mahāpretā, Yogamudrā, Jvālinī, Kṣobhiṇī, Dhruvā.
