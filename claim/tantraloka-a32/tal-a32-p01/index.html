tal-a32-v00 — Now I speak here the procedure of mudrās praised in guru-scripture song.

tal-a32-v01 — Mudrā, reflection-natured, is said in the venerable Devyāyāmala, by hearing bimbodaya, from discernment of two predicates.

tal-a32-v10 — There with full form I describe Khecarī alone; having bound lotus-seat, yogī should cast navel-ākāśa-īśvara.

tal-a32-v02 — Emergence from bimba of whom thus said reflection-ness; of bimba of whom emergence thus said instrumentality thereof.

tal-a32-v03 — Delight, called own-nature-gain, through body-gate of selves bestows at night; which by that mudrā in śāstras described.

tal-a32-v04 — There principal venerable Khecarī, deity-natured, known in undivided state; in divided state Triśūlinī.

tal-a32-v05 — Karaṅkiṇī, Krodhanā, and Bhairavī, Lelihānikā, Mahāpretā, Yogamudrā, Jvālinī, Kṣobhiṇī, Dhruvā.

tal-a32-v06 — Thus many varieties this, venerable Khecarī alone is sung; others her limb-born but, Padmā and the rest, in Mālinī doctrine.

tal-a32-v07 — Of them, by abundance and non-principality, not here description; venerable Khecarī-absorbed, whatever place should resort.

tal-a32-v08 — For goddess presence that would be enough; what with vain pomp; in desired rite they and would be principal of someone ever.

tal-a32-v09 — Mudrā fourfold by division of body, hand, speech, mind.
