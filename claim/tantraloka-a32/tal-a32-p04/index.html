tal-a32-v31 — Nine-hole-dwelling and one, roaring, pervading, fixed; by this indeed sky-goer venerable yoga-movement is called.

tal-a32-v32 — Kulakuṇḍalinī having bound, within the atom unconscious; left which this in this universe of him intent on withdrawal.

tal-a32-v33 — In own place satisfaction having obtained, consisting of knowledge-nectar-taste; should go to Kanda-abode in middle, sound having made soundless.

tal-a32-v34 — As long as living, quadrangular body-support and desirous; there her having aroused but, movement having known progressively-come.

tal-a32-v35 — Both wheels bound but, branch-ends-supporting; from root place as, goddess, darkness-knot should tear.

tal-a32-v36 — Called Vajra by knowledge-born indeed; so from both branch ends, from triangle center emerged root of liṅga should penetrate.

tal-a32-v37 — There assembled by pair of wheels unity shines; by reversal but, having thrown twofold-condition, moves thence.

tal-a32-v38 — From thigh to big toe fire of time she should throw; in coming and going movement should move she, marked with liṅga.

tal-a32-v39 — There by union of those conditions grants opening; who knows, he would succeed indeed of reception and emission of sap.

tal-a32-v40 — This with union place wave opening supreme; this succession then another even out of succession, Khecarī supreme.
