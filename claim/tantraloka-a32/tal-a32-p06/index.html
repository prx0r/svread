tal-a32-v51 — Two-testicles-and-liṅga and, having obtained body, gone; but this should ascertain abode of Bhava and abode of non-Bhava by utterance.

tal-a32-v52 — Mental this but others, Padmā and the rest eight mudrikās; in Mātṛ-vyūha-kula they would be of her and attendants.

tal-a32-v53 — Body but entire which is form of supreme letter; this mudrā mahāmudrā of Bhairava thus in cave.

tal-a32-v54 — Well seated in lotus but, by fingertip rays, by suddenly rising rays turned away, by back dwelling.

tal-a32-v55 — Inner standing Khecarī this called contraction moon-marked; therefore indeed having raised arms and bent.

tal-a32-v56 — In sky properly from standing sky-called Khecarī held; from union of two fists in heart she called heart.

tal-a32-v57 — Called peace she two-hands up and down standing risen; to be seen with equal gaze and with hands joined outside.

tal-a32-v58 — This indeed śakti-mudrā if hands run downward; of ten fingers but after fist binding.

tal-a32-v59 — Khecarī by throwing goddess five-Kuṇḍalinī held; dissolution-mudrā and this indeed if upward is thrown indeed.

tal-a32-v60 — Exit-mudrā instantly indeed of bound beings bond-cutter; in chasm far away instantly own-self as-if-dropping.
