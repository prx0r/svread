tal-a32-v11 — Staff-shaped but him should lead until three cavities; having held there, that quickly should drive with three cavities but.

tal-a32-v12 — This having bound in sky movement would be thus in venerable Pūrva-śāsana; sound-light-wind-joined mind having rested and above.

tal-a32-v13 — By this practice-yoga, having pierced Śiva, supreme should go; hands below clavicle having made, left foot and on right.

tal-a32-v14 — Having opened mouth with little fingers, with middle fingers but nose; ring finger wise should crook eyebrow-bend two index fingers.

tal-a32-v15 — Tongue and mantrin should move, hāhā-sound and should cause; with trident by practice, Brahmarandhra present.

tal-a32-v16 — Foot having abandoned, that-only instantly abandons earth; in void-and-nonvoid-dissolution having made, one-staff blue and fire.

tal-a32-v17 — In three-śakti-bound, in three-presiding-deities, trident; should know that by which wise flies in sky.

tal-a32-v18 — Sky-nature having abandoned, being present as existence only; trident equal-taste having made, in taste taste like standing.

tal-a32-v19 — One-staff he having known, trident sky-going, dear; having bound but Khecarī mudrā, having meditated self and Bhairava.

tal-a32-v20 — Khecarī-cakra-joined instantly abandons earth; abandoned-portion, without conduct, without doubt, world-abandoned.
