tal-a31-v11 — Divide all each field by sixteen parts; then the middle of two brahma threads is clearly the brahma place.

tal-a31-v12 — Having made a boundary, then the target is the fourth thread from the beginning; then go transversely to the fourth thread thereafter.

tal-a31-v13 — In the square, if there are two moons, make the outer half-part; their attached brahma-sutra is situated at the third marman.

tal-a31-v14 — If so, in the half of the square, the inward-facing pair would be at the second marman of the hand, immovable.

tal-a31-v15 — Having made a pair of full moons, the wise one should turn them from the sixth brahma-sutra point to the third cross part.

tal-a31-v16 — Having made a half-square, rotate the thread hanging from the full moon tip, upward-facing, toward the broken-moon pair fire part.

tal-a31-v17 — Having abandoned the cross-part pair, from the western broken moon then to the corner, so it would be; make a broken piece with two rotations.

tal-a31-v18 — From that one very sharp curved tip a horn is born; the same rule is declared for the second; this rule is clear.

tal-a31-v19 — When the middle horn is to be made, in the third upper square and fourth half, an inward-facing pair of moon-halves would be.

tal-a31-v20 — And that one full moon, previously turned, should reach as needed; by mutual knot union a bound state arises.
