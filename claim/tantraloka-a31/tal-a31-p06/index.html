tal-a31-v51 — He knows conduct as guru in trika teaching; his foot-dust is to be borne on the head by one desiring Shiva.

tal-a31-v52 — Here worship creation, preservation, and destruction in order; the fourth, however, is all-filling from the middle, or in all.

tal-a31-v53 — Or perform the four-trident or hidden-staff sacrifice; there worship that fourfold sequence properly and clearly.

tal-a31-v54 — Thus this was told in the secret supreme sixfold heart, declared and indicated in Siddhayogishvari-mata.

tal-a31-v55 — Having threaded the mandala in front, it grants all desires, equipped with great trident and adorned with lotus wheel.

tal-a31-v56 — At each gate draw a trident, excluding the western; or in corners make a great trident with tree.

tal-a31-v57 — At the trident tip place trika; thus it is to be made eightfold, or threefold.

tal-a31-v58 — Thus the divine city of sky-goers is indicated; it is told in Siddha-tantra teaching, even in another place.

tal-a31-v59 — Lotus-born in the middle, with trident peaks by its half; the trident-marked mandala is to be constructed, and lotus-marked in filling.

tal-a31-v60 — Now the trident-lotus arrangement is in Shripurva, Trishira-mata, Siddha-tantra, Trikakula, and Devi-yamala-mala.
