tal-a31-v158 — Rotate from both sides so a shape arises at the tip, then draw the former line there with the thread.

tal-a31-v159 — Leaving four fingers by the previous far gate, the wise one destroys the line so a trident shape results.

tal-a31-v160 — Leaving half a hand at the trident tip, make lotuses below, with three horns and a lotus with pericarp in the middle of the hand.
