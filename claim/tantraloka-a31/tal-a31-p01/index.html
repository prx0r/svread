tal-a31-v01 — Then the existence of the circle is briefly described: having established the eastern direction, one stretches the thread uniformly.

tal-a31-v10 — We say that yoni mandala, shown by the order of true being; in the four-handed Veda-supported form, abandon three parts all around.

tal-a31-v02 — Having halved that, one marks again in middle, east, and west; then by half-of-half measure, east and west.

tal-a31-v03 — By that much one marks; with thread one gives the twin compass; the fish-joint pair thus becomes in south and north.

tal-a31-v04 — In its middle one drops the thread for south-north success; or, with an east-west equal thread, north-south.

tal-a31-v05 — One marks from the other mark and from the east too; likewise those are placed in the fish middle, throwing a long thread south-north.

tal-a31-v06 — By the measured field's half measure, from the middle one marks toward directions; with two threads arising from two directions, the fish becomes corner-placed.

tal-a31-v07 — In the fishes, the Vedas are threads; thus a square is proclaimed, from one onward, as a hundred-ending mandala.

tal-a31-v08 — In the Siddhanta tantra, a hundred mandalas are called that seat; what goes in its middle, the chief three mandalas, is remembered.

tal-a31-v09 — Middle trident, triple trident, and nine trident are clear; there the trident arrangement is said with infinite divisions.
