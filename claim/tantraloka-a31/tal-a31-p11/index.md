**tal-a31-v105** — Having made the trident-root seat in the middle measured by space-ocean-equal fingers, make the staff a trident divided into three parts all around.
**tal-a31-v106** — By eight-finger measure all around, the trident tip would be hand-measure; the middle and root become accordingly.
**tal-a31-v107** — The altar is to be made in the middle; at both sides it is six fingers and twelve fingers long.
**tal-a31-v108** — From four-finger height at the root, arrange the altar; at both sides it is thus half-moon shaped.
**tal-a31-v109** — Rotate the chalk thread around the waist, making it two fingers wide and four fingers long by measure, O goddess.
**tal-a31-v110** — Arrange the left side likewise as the right side, with one finger width from the trident tip in the middle, below and above.
