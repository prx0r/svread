tal-a31-v131 — At equinox, place the mantras in the supreme mandala; when this is worshipped, one attains the place of the all-lord.

tal-a31-v132 — With svastika the fitting rite is to be done; place the channels first, according to muhurta measure.

tal-a31-v133 — Twenty-nine lineages are located in the indra, varuna, south, and north directions, going straight and transverse.

tal-a31-v134 — Eight hundred forty-one marman points arise by lineages and sense counts in the lotus circle.

tal-a31-v135 — By taste counts the seat becomes a svastika giving all desires; by vasu counts the door paths are arranged.

tal-a31-v136 — Destroy the holes, brahmin, arrow, and fire points, outer and inner, in order, in four directions and middle doors, O beautiful one.

tal-a31-v137 — In cities of fire, earth, sage, sky, outer, and womb, destroy marman points devoid of inner channels.

tal-a31-v138 — In door and wall corners, channels of brahma lineage situated from eye and taste, including eye, fire, arrow, and seasons, are to be destroyed.

tal-a31-v139 — Destroy fire and eye-fire; vedas and eye-pair from taste; the moon-going eye; and east vedas fire from taste.

tal-a31-v140 — Abandoning the world-located small channel, eye vedas fires follow in order; also the arrow fire-going pair and eye fires from taste.
