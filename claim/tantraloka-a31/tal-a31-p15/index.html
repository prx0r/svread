tal-a31-v141 — From the eye, the east-going is named sumeru door; svastika and pleasant city are located in four directions, both.

tal-a31-v142 — Two hundred marman points, directions multiplied by sages, and eye-beginning parts are swept from the path middle, O beautiful one.

tal-a31-v143 — Made by three sages in the middle, by senses the pericarp becomes; eye-made vasus, petal, and eye are once divided.

tal-a31-v144 — Having made fire vasu-going, destroy moon-located points; in fires and sage middle, destroy up to seat and moon.

tal-a31-v145 — It speaks of removing brahman's eye-objects and omitting a small eye among eastward channels in the ocean.

tal-a31-v146 — It directs omission in the four corners of the seat from the head and eye triad.

tal-a31-v147 — Petals are white, filaments red and yellow, the pericarp gold-like, and leaf tips red.

tal-a31-v148 — The sky line is very white, and the seat is endowed with three lines: white, red, and yellow.

tal-a31-v149 — Four-colored swastikas lie toward fire and northeast; a coral-like path and weapons are outside.

tal-a31-v150 — It describes a sapphire-like thunderbolt, power, lotus-gem radiance, a gold-like staff, and a very red face.
