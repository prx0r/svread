tsa-a04-v34 — By which, with the self distinct from one another, by difference alone — that is his Śrīmadaparā-power.

tsa-a04-v35 — By which the threefold holding of this, in the self itself, by embracing, in the form of tracking, grasps — she is the Goddess Śrīparā herself, named by other words such as Śrīmat-mātṛ, sat-bhāva, kāla, karṣiṇī.

tsa-a04-v36 — These four powers, from autonomy, each operates threefold alone.

tsa-a04-v37 — In creation, preservation and dissolution — twelve they become.

tsa-a04-v38 — Thus consciousness first constructs the state within; then outside too by distinctness; taking reddened-nature right there; then, with the intention to reabsorb that state within; then it forms the doubt that is the obstacle to reabsorption, and grasps it; the doubt-grasped portion of the state it constructs in the self by reabsorption; then it constructs even the reabsorber-nature — my-this-form — as its own nature; then, in constructing the reabsorber-nature, the standing of some state by habit-nature, and of some, the residue-of-consciousness-merely; then, by being inseparable from the construction of own-nature, the instrument-wheel; then even the instrument-Lord; then it constructs the māyic knower-form too; it constructs the knower who is contraction-and-release-turned, expansion-grasping, taste-loving; therefore the expanded form too it constructs — thus these twelve Goddess-consciousnesses, directing one knower-object or even several, simultaneously or in order, two-wise, three-wise, even-standing, and arising-portioned, revolving like a wheel, outside too by the order of month, segment and asterism, and within too by the order of pot, cloth and the rest, shining, nourishing the autonomy of the wheel-Lord, denoted by the word Śrīkālī.

tsa-a04-v39 — And construction is motion, throw, knowledge, counting, enjoyment-making, sound, and self-dissolving too.

tsa-a04-v40 — As the Śrībhūtirāja-teachers say: from the throw and from the knowledge, Kālī — by subjection to construction, then.

tsa-a04-v41 — And this meaning is to be considered where it is spread in my composed commentary, in the treatise, hymn and the rest.

tsa-a04-v42 — Not too secret to be shown somewhere, nor to be hidden in every way — so our teachers.

tsa-a04-v43 — Thus what was said — offering, fire-offering and the rest: in this kind the Great-Lord alone is to be thought.

tsa-a04-v44 — All see Viṣṇu-to-Siva as the ground-form only of what is to be rejected, of what is to be accepted; and that false view is necessarily to be abandoned by unsurpassable yogins — for that very reason the knowledge-Lord's great fervor in the hymn of experience: in this kind, in offering and the rest, at the end of yoga, in the fivefold, each many-form establishment; as it becomes, so let him practice — not by discriminating edible and inedible, clean and unclean and the rest, by abandoning thing-qualities, by a fancy-merely-essence tormenting the self. For purity is not the form of a thing like blueness elsewhere, from the command of impurity on it; like the gift's command in initiation — if 'it is impure there' by another command too the same, other-command not-existing from being overridden — if not, the obstructive power of Siva's command alone is established by reason, and established by the omniscient, unsurpassable, endless scripture too — we will say.

tsa-a04-v45 — Therefore from the Vedic on, spoken in the Pārameśvara-system, tantra, clan, Ucchuṣma and other scriptures too, whatever restraint or injunction or prohibition — here it is established as of no effect whatsoever.

tsa-a04-v46 — Likewise it is said in the Śrīpūrva-and-rest, spread — from the Tantrāloka it is to be sought.
