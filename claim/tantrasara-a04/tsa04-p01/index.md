## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a04-v01** — When the concept impresses in order, for entry into the nature just told — then the contemplation-sequence, preceded by true reason, true scripture and the true teacher's instruction, has its use.
**tsa-a04-v10** — There, for one pierced by an excessively firm descent of power, true-reason arises by itself through self-attainment — he is called one initiated by the goddesses.
**tsa-a04-v11** — For another, by the order of scripture — with elaboration we will say this in the revelation of power-descent.
**tsa-a04-v12** — But the task lies in determining the teacher's scripture, and in the arising of the fitting concept that is born of the scripture's chain of kin: that chain of concepts is called true-reason; it is called contemplation as well, since from indistinctness an existing thing is contemplated as non-existent by being made distinct.
**tsa-a04-v13** — And here no other yoga-limb is a direct means apart from true-reason, the light-form of pure knowledge: of austerity and the rest, of the restraint-class, of nonviolence and the rest — the yama-type, of retention and the rest, the prāṇāyāma-class — what task is there in consciousness by being established in the knowable merely?
**tsa-a04-v14** — Withdrawal too should make the instrument-level excellent; meditation, concentration and samādhi too, performed successively by the order of practice, should impart identity-with-the-meditated-object to the meditator.
**tsa-a04-v15** — But practice in the supreme tattva, in the Siva-self, in one's own nature, is simply not possible.
**tsa-a04-v16** — For one rooted in consciousness, practice is the form of fixing breath, mind and body — like carrying a load, knowing the scripture's meaning, dancing-practice; but in the form of consciousness nothing is to be taken and nothing removed — how then practice?
**tsa-a04-v17** — If it is asked — 'by reasoning too?' — it is said: this is only the mode of removing the residence-in-duality, nothing else at all.
**tsa-a04-v02** — For by concept-strength alone beings mistake themselves to be bound; that misapprehension is the cause that obstructs saṃsāra — so the counter-pair concept arises: it tears the saṃsāra-causing concept, and is the cause of prosperity.
**tsa-a04-v03** — Such a concept, having crossed all the delimited-natured tattvas from Śiva to the limit, is undelimited-consciousness-merely-formed — that is the supreme truth, the place where thing-ordering stands, the world's essence; by it he breathes the world — that am I, world-crossed, world-self am I.
**tsa-a04-v04** — And this does not arise for the māyā-blind, since true-reason and the rest are absent.
**tsa-a04-v05** — For Viṣṇu-and-rest are limited in scripture by passion-tattva only, so even in higher vision they show no inclination toward it — therefore they are hostile to true-reason, true scripture and true teacher-instruction alone.
**tsa-a04-v06** — As said in the Pārameśvara: all Viṣṇu-and-rest are colored by knowledge-passion.
**tsa-a04-v07** — They do not find the supreme tattva, being devoid of omniscient knowledge.
**tsa-a04-v08** — Therefore only those pierced by the firm descent of Śāṃbhava-power, having impressed the concept by the order of true scripture and the rest, enter the supreme own-nature.
**tsa-a04-v09** — But then would the supreme tattva be of concept-form? Not so: the concept's work is fulfilled merely in breaking the residence-in-duality; the supreme tattva, however, is everywhere all-formed and self-luminous — so there is no service or refutation of it for anyone from concept.
