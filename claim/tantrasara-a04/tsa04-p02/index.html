tsa-a04-v18 — Even in ordinary practice: the manifesting of the desired form of body and the rest as consciousness-selfhood, and the contemplation of the absence of other forms — that alone is the purpose of practice.

tsa-a04-v19 — But in the supreme tattva nothing at all is to be discarded, it is said.

tsa-a04-v20 — The residence-in-duality too, mark well, is not some separate thing-existing: it is mere own-nature-manifestation — hence the rejection of duality is done by concept, from the saying.

tsa-a04-v21 — This supreme truth, own-nature shining as the form of manifestation, is taken by itself from autonomy; by the order — thrown off, turned toward expansion, then expanding, then expanded — it shines in this way; and the shining too is the Supreme Lord's own nature — so here the yoga-limbs are not the direct means.

tsa-a04-v22 — Reason may favor it, but true-reason alone is the direct means there; it is pure knowledge too, and is impressed in many forms: offering, fire-offering, recitation, vow, yoga. Therein the abode of all states is the Supreme Lord alone — nothing separate exists; for the establishment of conviction in concept, the offering of all states to the Supreme Lord itself is the offering — from heart-nature, the offering in the Supreme Lord is easy for those who themselves show entry into consciousness; hence the external use of flower, satisfaction, scent and the rest was declared.

tsa-a04-v23 — All states are made of the light of the Supreme Lord: for the attainment of the convicted concept, in the light of the Supreme Lord's consciousness-fire, in the desire to swallow all states — the sudden dissolution of all states into the residue of mere light: that is the fire-offering.

tsa-a04-v24 — Likewise, for the arising of the both-natured contemplation, by disregard alone of the states divided by outer, inner and other knowable forms: that supreme tattva is formed of one's own nature — the inner contemplation is the recitation.

tsa-a04-v25 — For the gaining everywhere-always of the impersonal identification with the Supreme Lord, by the identification of equality with the Supreme Lord: the viewing even of the body, even of a pot and the rest, is the vow.

tsa-a04-v26 — As said in the Śrī-Nandiśikhā: all-equality is the supreme vow.

tsa-a04-v27 — Thus, by various concepts formed of portions of pure knowledge, what concept-independent, natural, supreme-truth tattva shines — for establishment in being merely the eternal shining of that kind, the specific concept that tracks its own nature: that is yoga.

tsa-a04-v28 — Therein the Supreme Lord, of the nature of full consciousness — fullness is his power, clan, capability, wave, heart, essence, pulsation, glory; the triad, Kālī, Karṣaṇī, Caṇḍī, Vāṇī, enjoyment, vision, Nityā and the rest — is denoted by the scriptural languages that run according to each sense; by that, by that form, let it be in the hearts of the meditators.

tsa-a04-v29 — And she shines as fullness-consciousness by the seeing of the nature of the whole power.

tsa-a04-v30 — And his powers are countless.

tsa-a04-v31 — Why say more? The world — those are his powers; how can they be taught?

tsa-a04-v32 — In three the world is completed: by which this bears, sees and illumines up to the bearer-limit of Śiva as mere uncaricatured consciousness-form — the Supreme Lord; that is his Śrīparā-power.

tsa-a04-v33 — By which, like a mirror or an elephant and the rest, by difference and undifference — that is his Śrīparāparā-power.
