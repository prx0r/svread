## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a36-v00** — Now the descent of the scripture is told, come in due course.
**tal-a36-v01** — Pointed out by the venerable Siddhas and set forth by the gurus: Bhairava, Bhairavī the Goddess, Svacchanda, Lākula—Aṇurāṭ.
**tal-a36-v10** — And from Rāma, Lakṣmaṇa; from him the Siddhas; from them too the Dānavas; and the Guhyakas; then from them the Yogins, thence the kings.
**tal-a36-v02** — Gahaneśa—Abjaja, Śakra — the guru, from the crore-fund, studied in sequence from the nine the nine-crore-expanse.
**tal-a36-v03** — Then from these the guru distributed a quarter from each crore; both quarters to Dakṣa and the rest, and to Saṃvarta and the rest.
**tal-a36-v04** — And a quarter to Vāmana and the rest, half a quarter to Bhārgava; but a quarter-quarter to Bali, and the other quarter-quarter.
**tal-a36-v05** — Half to Siṃha; then from the remainder two shares to Garuḍa, a quarter to Vāsuki the serpent — and these are seventeen fragments.
**tal-a36-v06** — Half from heaven Rāvaṇa then took; Rāma took half too from this; beginning with Vibhīṣaṇa, obtained through the guru-disciple-rule sequence.
**tal-a36-v07** — Divided into nineteen fragments it then became; and each fragment into eight sub-fragments, through the stated quarter-division.
**tal-a36-v08** — Quarter, root-extracts in the latter Greater-Uttara, likewise the Kalpa; saṃhitā-kalpa-skandha-…-anuttara, the pervader — threefold, three.
**tal-a36-v09** — Here the goddesses are set forth in sequence in expansive form indeed; but in the ninth quarter no count is stated — in the undifferentiated.
