tsa-a07-v01 — Therein this whole — of-place-path-illumination-power-born, rested in consciousness: through its door, in void, mind, prāṇa, nerve-circles-within, outside in body up to liṅga-sthaṇḍila-image — the whole path is firmly fixed. Having melted that whole path into the body, the body into prāṇa, that into mind, that into void, in that consciousness — all-filled-full-consciousness is attained: knowing the thirty-six tattvas' own-form, seeing beyond-it consciousness Supreme-Siva-formed, even world-made consciousness would make-known — the beyond-portion-of-knowable just as some, taking-as-supreme the māyā-womb-qualified, Viṣṇu-Brahmā-and-rest. Therefore it must be by prakriyā-knowledge-endowed.

tsa-a07-v10 — From ahaṃkāra, hundredfold buddhi-tattva.

tsa-a07-v11 — Then thousandfold prakṛti-tattva — so much prakṛti-egg.

tsa-a07-v12 — That, like the cosmic-egg, is countless.

tsa-a07-v13 — From prakṛti-tattva, puruṣa-tattva too, ten-thousandfold.

tsa-a07-v14 — From persons, niyati, lakh-fold.

tsa-a07-v15 — From niyati, successively ten-lakh-fold up to kalā-tattva-end.

tsa-a07-v16 — As niyati, rāga, aśuddha-vidyā, kāla, kalā — and so.

tsa-a07-v17 — From kalā-tattva, crorefold māyā — so much māyā-egg.

tsa-a07-v18 — From māyā-tattva, śuddha-vidyā, ten-crore-multiplied.

tsa-a07-v19 — From vidyā-tattva, īśvara-tattva, hundred-crore-fold.

tsa-a07-v02 — That is said: knowledge-endowed-with-prakriyā is not [mere] prakriyā.

tsa-a07-v20 — From īśvara-tattva, sādākhya, thousand-crore-fold.

tsa-a07-v21 — From sādākhya, multiplied-by-group, śakti-tattva — so the śakti-egg.

tsa-a07-v22 — That power pervading, by which the world-path inside-outside stands — hence all-pervading.

tsa-a07-v23 — Thus these tattvas, standing as mutual-coverings: the later pervader, the earlier pervaded — by that standing they stand.

tsa-a07-v24 — Up to the all-power-tattva-end, the path is pervaded by Śiva-tattva.

tsa-a07-v25 — Śiva-tattva, further, is immeasurable, having-transcended-all-paths, pervading-all-paths.

tsa-a07-v26 — These, dwelling in the gaps-between-tattvas, the bhuvanas — their lords stand in earth, here.

tsa-a07-v27 — In those abodes, those who die — their course there, they grant.

tsa-a07-v28 — And successively upward-downward they impel, by the dīkṣā-order.

tsa-a07-v03 — Therein the earth-tattva, hundred-crore-extended, in-form-of-Brahma-cosmic-egg.

tsa-a07-v04 — Within it: Kālāgni, hells, pātālas, earth, heaven up to Brahma-world — so.

tsa-a07-v05 — Outside the cosmic-egg, a hundred of Rudras.

tsa-a07-v06 — Nor does a count of the cosmic-eggs exist.

tsa-a07-v07 — Then water-tattva, tenfold from earth-tattva.

tsa-a07-v08 — Then, successively, tenfold up-to-ahaṃkāra-end.

tsa-a07-v09 — As water, fire, wind, sky — the five-substances-included one-eleven-wombed ahaṃkāra — and so.
