tal-a02-v31 — By these two just, but from causes: not two-natured, nor two-abandoned; wholly, for, shines it: by what form, let it be mantred?

tal-a02-v32 — In the venerable Triśiras, told, supreme-knowledge-nature, by śakti dwelling within the womb, śakti-wombed, supreme state.

tal-a02-v33 — Neither being, nor non-being, nor the pair: by unsayable speech, unsayable-word-escalated, śakti-dwelling, śakti-devoid.

tal-a02-v34 — Thus, who rooted-knowledge-truth-purified, on the anuttara path, rooted: they, means-unruled.

tal-a02-v35 — Of them, this, shines together, everywhere, the being-circle, standing-forth just, melted in consciousness-Bhairava-fire.

tal-a02-v36 — Of these, pleasure-pain-share-doubt-fear-notions, nirvikalpa-entry-only-remainder come to.

tal-a02-v37 — Of these, no mantra, no meditation, no worship, nor even imagination: no, from samayin to ācārya, anyone, is a resting-place.

tal-a02-v38 — All-restraint-system-break-marks-bearing, not than grace beyond, in any remaining conduct, is the purpose.

tal-a02-v39 — Own, to be done, whatever, considering the world, this, from effort, not, for others' sake, toward, joins, any own activity, but if, whose every existence-stain is destroyed, full of Bhairavī-nature: the deed, his, clearly this just this-much world-duty merely.

tal-a02-v40 — Him, who see, by that-form-order, stainless-aware, they too, of his form, so much, just, of him, grace-naturedness.
