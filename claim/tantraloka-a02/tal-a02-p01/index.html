tal-a02-v01 — Now the first word, of unceasing highest-recognition-nature: to determine it, this second treatise I begin.

tal-a02-v10 — Awareness-principle, 'self-luminous' — in this, what? Now, by reasoning: in its absence, would the universe be non-shining from inertia?

tal-a02-v02 — For the means-less, whose nature: what point by teaching here? Once may teaching be; afterwards as means-less it is called.

tal-a02-v03 — This means-less reality — whence without means? By itself; but of them that such: what should we say, indeed, to them?

tal-a02-v04 — Which fourfold-told form of awareness — of the pervading, this: own nature alone is to be regarded; for ever-arisen is the pervading.

tal-a02-v05 — By so many uncounted natures it shines; some part-by-part, others enter partlessly through that.

tal-a02-v06 — There too, from means-and-the-rest-expecting otherness-yoga, of means too not to be barred: variety from its otherness.

tal-a02-v07 — There, who pure-souled, in Bhairavī, own awareness, means-less practicing: whose rule — is proclaimed.

tal-a02-v08 — There, for now, rite-yoga does not deserve means-hood; for from that arisen, rather it is distinctly discerned.

tal-a02-v09 — In awareness, means just would be — quoted; if awareness is called: luminosity in self-luminous — and that, thence, from another — how?
