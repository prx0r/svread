tal-a02-v41 — This tattva-recognition is chief, sacrifice-and-the-rest is called, up to initiation, by the Lord, in the Siddhayogīśvarī doctrine.

tal-a02-v42 — From the altar, the higher, loom, from the loom northward, the cloth, from cloth, meditation, then, the to-be-meditated, then, should be dhāraṇā higher.

tal-a02-v43 — Then too, yoga-born, form, then too, knowledge higher, by knowledge, for, greatly accomplished, should become a Yogīśvara — but, quoted.

tal-a02-v44 — He too, through freedom-abode, if even of the impure-aware, grace, desiring to grace that forthcoming one, he should resort to the rite.

tal-a02-v45 — According to the graced one, varied, it, and, is taught, through Parāparā-and-the-rest-means-flood-mixedness-division.

tal-a02-v46 — For that purpose alone, and of him too, of the Lord-formed one, that means-scripture-and-the-rest-hearing-studying-zeal.

tal-a02-v47 — For not, of him, of the free, anywhere, wherever, is refutation, not of the impure-minded, of man, grace, but meanslessness.

tal-a02-v48 — In the venerable Ūrmi great-scripture, Siddha-lineage-shaped: this told, so, by the venerable Somānanda and the teachers.

tal-a02-v49 — From the guru's word-and-the-rest, from wiping by heaped reasoning, from assurance — the śāstra, toward, from the arisen, or even, from the told: when dissolved, in the doubt-cloud, that heart-sky-shining-greatness, of the Lord, sun-like, touching, feet darkness-conquering.

tal-a02-v50 — This anuttara-abode-discerning, means-shed, made: the ahnika.
