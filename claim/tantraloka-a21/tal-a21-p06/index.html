tal-a21-v51 — Fire emits ciṭi-ciṭā sound with smoke, bears blue-cloud-color, repeatedly blazes, subsides.

tal-a21-v52 — Detail, terrible form and, runs on earth and downwards; or crow-etc unheard sound: then him the guru should mark.

tal-a21-v53 — By brahmin-killing and the rest, by sins and by association with them minor sins: then of him not to be done, initiation in this unperformed rite.

tal-a21-v54 — Nine-souled, standing inside the phaṭ cavity, again endowed with five phaṭs: of so-and-so thus, sins, I burn according to eight phaṭs.

tal-a21-v55 — Thus thousand-fold fire-offering to be done with sesame and rice; at the end full and to be given; then to him by initiation the guru.

tal-a21-v56 — Up to the supreme goal should do purification of principles; even in direct presence of the paśu situated, of the sinner, Bhagavan-filled.

tal-a21-v57 — Power of the one who obtained: the eldest, exactly thus rite should perform; if or the teacher properly not blazing of him before that.

tal-a21-v58 — By expiations so, by gifts, by breath-controls and, purification having done, this rite and also initiation should perform without doubt.

tal-a21-v59 — In every way being even, the tattva-knower liberates beasts by will alone; Śiva directly — therefore him should worship always.

tal-a21-v60 — Deceit there not to be done; and having done that downwards should go the disciple. Not again should proclaim of him sin; the proclaimer should go.
