tal-a21-v11 — The guru should perform the initiation redeeming the dead, bestowing Śiva; that is said by the Supreme Lord in Śrī-Mṛtyuñjaya-Siddha and the rest.

tal-a21-v12 — For the uninitiated, king and the rest, the lazy, the fallen, the dead, child, sick, woman, old — and one should arrange the redemption of the dead.

tal-a21-v13 — The rite all previously told: that is desired to be concise, devoid of guru-and-other worship, in the external for enjoyment, because it.

tal-a21-v14 — Preliminary consecration, caru, field, bed, maṇḍala-preparation are not useful here; (they are) for the disciple's purification and dream-seeing.

tal-a21-v15 — But the maṇḍala is for making mantras present and satisfied; and on the following day the worship of gods is not directly helpful for this.

tal-a21-v16 — Through rite, instrument, place, maṇḍala, form, mantra; through meditation, union, sole devotion, knowledge, identification — (these are the causes of presence).

tal-a21-v17 — Of one who entered into that, of disciples and of the guru also: these eleven are told as causes for presence.

tal-a21-v18 — Higher and higher superior, also from freedom from mixture: the rite abundantly, flowers and the rest, superior, endowed with marks.

tal-a21-v19 — Single liṅga and the rest as place, where the self is pleased; the maṇḍala three-trident-lotus-wheel, which in mantra-maṇḍala.

tal-a21-v20 — Even when not invoked, seen — the accomplishment of samaya-status of the good; that is said in the Mālinī-tantra: accomplished samaya-maṇḍala.
