## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a21-v01** — Now the initiation-rite of the one situated in absence is described.
**tal-a21-v10** — Or by itself, by the force of compassion arisen towards that person even, (of one) who is the bearer of a portion of śaktipāta come towards him known.
**tal-a21-v02** — For accomplishment of enjoyment and liberation one is led to the true guru: in this Mālinī sentence 'prati' is expressive of facing-towards.
**tal-a21-v03** — Facing-towards of the disciple consists in becoming the seat of his grace; the saying 'worshipping him' is an indicator of the cause of grace.
**tal-a21-v04** — From that connection then someone is liberated instantly: thus is also the meaning of this jewel of the Mālinī sentence.
**tal-a21-v05** — 'In that instant' means there is no further moment of desire to approach; but exactly thus the guru, struck by compassion, should uplift him.
**tal-a21-v06** — Of one who, body wasted by guru-service, died without obtaining initiation — or whose desire arose at the moment of death.
**tal-a21-v07** — Or of one who shared the initiation-rites of lower tantras etc., having obtained samaya-status, (but) not having obtained the supreme initiation.
**tal-a21-v08** — Of one struck by a ḍimba, or devoured by a yogeśī through sorcery, or slain by yantra-tantra etc. by the guru — the dead.
**tal-a21-v09** — Of one who, having obtained initiation, violated his samaya — even so, through the vehement entreaty of relatives, wife, friends, sons.
