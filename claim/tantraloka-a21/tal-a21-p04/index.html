tal-a21-v31 — From being in an eligible body, in humanity — but bodied, not then liberated; from body, at end of body, but should go to Śiva.

tal-a21-v32 — In that body but some of his arises: supreme Śāṅkara grace, from root and from knowledge or from served teacher.

tal-a21-v33 — Situated in that body, even this living soul, by net-force etc. this, pervades the body of darbha and the rest, by own abiding even unconscious.

tal-a21-v34 — By the force of abundant yoga-mantra-rite-knowledge again, even the human body this one then immediately releases.

tal-a21-v35 — Even sleeping-like, even bodiless, who is living soul: he even, drawn from the net, comes to the darbha body, of fruit, and or.

tal-a21-v36 — Jasmine fruit and the rest, whatever by that, or body-fashioning: by suitability both inside and outside, that here superior is said.

tal-a21-v37 — Then the living soul brought by the net method stands as if asleep, from absence of obtaining apparatus of mind-endowed body and the rest.

tal-a21-v38 — It neither vibrates nor knows nor speaks nor certainly desires; for such a one indeed, all purificatory rites as before should prepare.

tal-a21-v39 — By seedless initiation yoga, all having done the previously-told rite producing union, with full oblation together, should throw and him.

tal-a21-v40 — In the body of darbha and the rest, offered into the fire of mantra together with full, freed from bonds goes to Śiva, free from return again.
