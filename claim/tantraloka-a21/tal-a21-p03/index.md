## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a21-v21** — By which, as soon as seen — thus, from the two words 'siddha' and 'mātra': the form radiant in form, which, mantra like it very radiant.
**tal-a21-v22** — The rest is clearly therefore not told here in detail again; having made the maṇḍala, having worshipped the god there with kuśa grass — and.
**tal-a21-v23** — With cow-dung he should make the form, disciple-like, should place; then in it one path to be purified, from the contemplation of pervasion.
**tal-a21-v24** — Having deposited up to prakṛti, again he should perform the rite by the great-net-practice, from the middle of all the paths.
**tal-a21-v25** — Having drawn the mind situated there — should do: that rite is said. Having risen from the mūlādhāra, the rod of the path of the vast spread endless nāḍī, having occupied by vigor, reached the nose-sky, throwing, desires to pervade up to the wheel of the path with a net of smoke-beautiful dense tips, covering — bringing the desired life — thus: the practice called the great net.
**tal-a21-v26** — By this to-be-covered goes dependence on another; facing-towards-ness at first; is led to five, if all — then from the middle of the path as desired, in drawing and extracting, or in the matter of the dead person to be drawn — then in the living, yoga called the net, taught in the Śrī-Śaṃbhunātha-āgama, spoken by me.
**tal-a21-v27** — When long broken apart, as two armies joined: again horse, elephant, man run towards their own caste by taste; by the nāḍī-wheels with the instrument-winds just so, joined, each with its own taste unified, by their own net subjugated.
**tal-a21-v28** — Drawn by the great net, the living soul, by the knowing one, releases heaven, ghost, animal, hell — then at that time this one.
**tal-a21-v29** — Obtained by that knowledge-mantra-yoga, this person also, artificial, like a yogī, then from the heart of the sādhya abandons identification.
**tal-a21-v30** — The states from immobile etc., wondrous, nearness to that world — should abandon, thus: not wonder; he thus who, by action even, or.
