## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**ptv-p01-v01** — O Lord, what is the Unsurpassable that grants Kaula fruit by itself — known by a mere touch of knowing, rising to kinship with the Sky-Goer?
**ptv-p01-v02** — Tell me this secret of secrets, Lord — the Power that dwells in the heart, Kaulinī, mistress of the clan.
**ptv-p01-v03** — Tell me of her, Lord of gods, by whom I come to fullness. — Listen, blessed Goddess: even the answer has one beyond it — the Unsurpassable.
**ptv-p01-v04** — I tell it, no doubt: swift Kaula-fruition. This Kaula rite, Goddess, abides in my heart-sky.
**ptv-p01-v05** — First now: all lunar days, vowels ending in the drop; within them, by time's conjoining, moon and sun are declared.
**ptv-p01-v06** — Realities from earth to person, in five groups in order — in the ka-series and the rest, ending with the ma-sound, O fair-vowed one.
**ptv-p01-v07** — Fourfold the stations of wind, fire, water and their lords; above them the famous śa-series, and before, the five Brahmas.
**ptv-p01-v08** — Rootless, in due order, to be known, ending in kṣa — this forthgoing declared; of all mantras and wisdoms indeed, O glorious one.
**ptv-p01-v09** — This is the womb, ever told in all Tantras — joined with the fourteen and the tithi-lord's end; the third brahma, fair-hipped, the heart of Bhairava himself.
