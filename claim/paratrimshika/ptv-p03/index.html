ptv-p03-v19 — Who thus truly knows even the unseen circle shares fruition ever — yogin and initiate alike.

ptv-p03-v20 — By this mere known, all powers know; kinsman of the śākinī clan he may be, even without yoga.

ptv-p03-v21 — Ignorant of rite yet knowing ordinance, he turns to worship — having made time-fire from the first, ending in māyā, gone to the brahma-body.

ptv-p03-v22 — Śiva, whose end is endless all, deemed the supreme power-triad; whatever stands on the pure path abides within that.

ptv-p03-v23 — The atom soon attains pure lordly knowing; its mover is Śiva to be known, all-knowing highest Lord.

ptv-p03-v24 — All-going, stainless, clear, full, self-aboded, bright — like the great power-formed tree standing in the banyan seed.

ptv-p03-v25 — So the moving-unmoving world stands in the heart-seed; who knows thus in truth — his is going-to-freedom.

ptv-p03-v26 — Initiation becomes undoubted, free of sesame-ghee offering — on crown, face, heart, secret, and image, so indeed.

ptv-p03-v27 — Placing done, topknot bound twenty-seven-muttered; with each one the ten quarters' binding he should have made.
