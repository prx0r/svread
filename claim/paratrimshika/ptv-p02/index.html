ptv-p02-v10 — Yoginī-born or Rudra — neither plainly wins this heart of the god of gods, swift giver of yoga's release.

ptv-p02-v11 — At its right utterance, the great host of mantra-seals comes face to face at once — marked by possession of one's own body.

ptv-p02-v12 — Who remembers for a moment, in the kiss, navel-sealed — he binds body then, and the mantra-seal host, the man.

ptv-p02-v13 — Asked, he tells past and future things — even within three hours, uttering the meant god-form.

ptv-p02-v14 — Face to face he sees the undoubted, drawn by Rudra's powers; in two watches' time, sky-dwelling, remembering.

ptv-p02-v15 — By three: all mothers, mighty yoginīs, heroes, hero-lords, adepts, and the strong śākinī throng.

ptv-p02-v16 — Come, pledged, urged by Bhairava — they grant supreme fruition, whatever fruit is wished.

ptv-p02-v17 — By this the perfected will perfect, and make mantra-men perfect.

ptv-p02-v18 — Whatever in Bhairava's Tantra succeeds from this — by mantra-potency's possession-power, with no controller.
