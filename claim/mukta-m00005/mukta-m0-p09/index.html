mukta-m0-p09-v208 — Because there are no manifestors such as kalā and the rest, that soul’s cognition and action are not manifest.

mukta-m0-p09-v209 — Impurity is in a beginningless connection with the beginningless, is common, and is imperishable.

mukta-m0-p09-v210 — For that very reason, impurity must indeed be admitted as eternal.

mukta-m0-p09-v211 — Before the arising of great delusion, this individual self was stainless.

mukta-m0-p09-v212 — The connection of impurity with the self must be admitted to be beginningless.

mukta-m0-p09-v213 — O wise ones, if the connection between the soul and impurity is accepted as having a beginning—

mukta-m0-p09-v216 — And its powers, fixed for each soul, obstruct the soul’s qualities.

mukta-m0-p09-v219 — Thus, according to the revered Svāyambhuva and other scriptures, they are to be known as indeed adventitious.

mukta-m0-p09-v220 — The five modifications of object-directedness, beginning with pride, are not proper.

mukta-m0-p09-v221 — But there is no contradiction, since they have impurity as their material cause.

mukta-m0-p09-v222 — It is not proper to say that they are manifested merely through connection with the means of experience.

mukta-m0-p09-v223 — But according to you, pride and the rest are indeed distinct from the modifications of the guṇas.

mukta-m0-p09-v224 — In due order, two are tāmasa, one is sāttvika, and two are rājasa.

mukta-m0-p09-v225 — The modifications are not distinct from these; therefore, as modifications distinct from the root, they do not exist.
