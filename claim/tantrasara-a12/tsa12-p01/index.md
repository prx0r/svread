## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a12-v01** — Initiation and the rest must be told — it is said. So, to expound the nature of initiation, first the to-be-done bath is taught.
**tsa-a12-v10** — Inward, thus, by holding earth-and-so-on-formed forms — here, there, in earthen-and-rest circles — being-made-of-That.
**tsa-a12-v02** — Bathing is called purity; purity is immersion in the Supreme Lord's own form.
**tsa-a12-v03** — Washing off turbidity is indeed purity; turbidity — even in That whose sole form is That — is the conceit of admixing another form of alien nature.
**tsa-a12-v04** — Here, in the self whose essence is free-bliss-consciousness-alone — or everywhere in the world too — the conceit of admixing another-formed reality is impurity; that is dispelled by immersion in great-Bhairava — for someone suddenly, for someone else awaiting further means.
**tsa-a12-v05** — Therein too, by one-two-threefold division, whole or part-wise, somewhere, for someone, sometime, and so from assurance-attainment: difference is manifold.
**tsa-a12-v06** — And eightfold, in earth-water-wind-fire-sky-moon-sun-self forms, made into Supreme-Lord forms by mantra-placing majesty; by identity, in the Lord-entered body, immersion in the Supreme arises for anyone — though from bathing-clothes-and-so-on gladness it becomes the means to the Supreme. Thus told the chief fruits therein: firmness, nourishment, vigor, impurity-burning, pervasion, creation-capacity, maintenance-capacity, non-difference.
**tsa-a12-v07** — But by heroes-rule the distinction: as battle-dust, hero-water, great wind, hero-ash, cremation-ground-sky — sun-and-moon placed in them, self undiscriminating.
**tsa-a12-v08** — Again too, as outer-inner twoness, by oneness with the outward-worshipped mantra, made-of-That: there, there plunging — thus told.
**tsa-a12-v09** — But bliss-substance, hero-abiding, by looking, having-made-Śiva: right-there mantra-circle-worship; thence by that very one, body-and-breath deity-circle satisfied — thus the chief bath.
