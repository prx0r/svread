tsa-a11-v05 — In the doctrine-of-two-inseparables-of-free-Lord, however, it is sustained: as the Supreme-Lord by self-covering-sport attains cattle-being, individual, atom — no place-time-form-difference-contradiction of him; likewise, by withdrawal-of-self-hiding self-recovery — suddenly or in-order, taking-refuge, the atom is called vessel-of-power-descent. tsa-a11-v07 — And this power-descent ninefold in intense-middle-mild, by-superiority-equality-inferiority; again from-triplicity.
