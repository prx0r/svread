tsa-a11-v03 — Therein some say: from absence of knowledge, saṃsāra rooted in ignorance; from its departure's knowledge-arising, power-descent. To them: knowledge-arising alone is distorted — to be said. If act-produced, [it behaves] like act-fruit; in enjoyment-ness, in the enjoymenter too — the two possibilities of power-descent are excessive consequence. In being-caused-by-the-Lord's-will, however: knowledge-arising's mutual-dependence and futility; in the Lord, passion-and-rest-issue. Of two contradictory acts of equal force, mutual-obstruction: act-equivalence — then power-descent? No: in being-sequential, from impossibility-of-contradiction; even in contradiction, of the uncontradicted act's enjoyment-giving issue — in non-contradicted act's non-occurrence, just-then body-death issue: birth-and-lifespan-giving act is not obstructed; enjoyment-giving is. If asked why: in that-act's-presence, if power might-fall, then she — of the enjoyment-giver — why would she fear?
