tal-a27-v21 — By row-chakra-triśūla-lotus-rite should resort to tūra; its mark I speak, in venerable Picu-śāstra was set forth.

tal-a27-v22 — In tūra yoga always is praised, giver of siddhi in faultless; by nets-torn, by holes-teeth-deficient-excess disease.

tal-a27-v23 — In suitable and in tūra loss would be, in its deficient sacrifice supreme; desired indeed would-be-tūra — thus some adopted.

tal-a27-v24 — Gurus but in rite, in desired, from effort reject faults — thus they explain Picu-spoken; not in obligatory rite — thus this.

tal-a27-v25 — In venerable Siddhātantra was said and tūra-mark-supreme: in one-to-four-fragment, in cow-mouth, in full-moon.

tal-a27-v26 — In lotus-go-rocanā-pearl-salt-crystal-like, in one-to-five-hundred-holes-vidyā-line-possessing, in auspicious.

tal-a27-v27 — Not in rough-crooked-fragment-long-depressed-with-bindu: by smooth, by diamond-needle here, clearly, goddess-group-possessing.

tal-a27-v28 — All should draw worshipful, all-limbs-beautiful; this very should follow in argha-vessel also, mark.

tal-a27-v29 — In venerable Brahmayāmala was said: vessel, cow-mouth-supreme, elephant-tortoise-base, pot-round-śakti-born-form.

tal-a27-v30 — Bead-thread and should make there indeed worship sequence, hero-substance-water-born-pearl-gem-gold-born.
