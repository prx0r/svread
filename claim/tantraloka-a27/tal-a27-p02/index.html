tal-a27-v11 — Guru and here in so-called nirodha: time thus in the Lord he should say; living in this until fruit-end, you should stand until life-end — or.

tal-a27-v15 — Of it also by altar-and-the-rest-enjoined-rite he should purify; mantra-offering likewise should be nirodha as said by reason.

tal-a27-v16 — In fire and tarpaṇa, by abundance especially dakṣiṇā of guru, poor-and-the-rest-satisfaction-abundance-sacrifice: thus superior rite.

tal-a27-v17 — In all unmanifest liṅgas primary should be unconstructed; so and there there-said in mark in Pārameśvara.

tal-a27-v18 — In thread, vessel, banner, cloth, svayambhū-Bāṇa-worshipped, river-spring-risen: and no calling, nor even construction.

tal-a27-v19 — Seat-temple-mantra-portion-time-rule, and not manifest or in painting-book-and-the-rest, god-wood-gold-born.

tal-a27-v20 — Now by initiated good-artisan-made installs guru, or-else mark-endowed-head-shell-supported.
