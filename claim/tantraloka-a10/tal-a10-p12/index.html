tal-a10-v111 — Even those mantras, when measurable, have their lord as mother; but when full awareness arises there, measurability is removed.

tal-a10-v112 — He of arisen full form is mother, the mantra great lord; when knowability is obtained there, supreme self-luminous Shiva appears.

tal-a10-v113 — The knower is Shiva, whose self-sameness illuminates all knowable; he is not measurable because illumination does not depend on another.

tal-a10-v114 — That measurability is not there, for he is the self-luminous lord; when no other is accepted in self-luminosity, measurability is absent.

tal-a10-v115 — A regress from non-luminous to luminous is insurmountable; otherwise the universe would remain asleep.

tal-a10-v116 — Since Shiva is self-luminous, other logical displays are unnecessary.

tal-a10-v117 — The supreme soul was said from the beginning; yet knowability is questioned even in self-luminous Shiva.

tal-a10-v118 — Otherwise instruction and teacherhood convention could not exist; truth is created thus by the supreme lord for knowability.

tal-a10-v119 — Led to the mantra-lord level, he is made to meditate; thus knowable, he is not unlimited consciousness.

tal-a10-v120 — Knowability is fitting for the full one, but in reality shiva-selfhood is not found in knowable nature.
