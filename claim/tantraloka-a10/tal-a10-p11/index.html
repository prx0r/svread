tal-a10-v101 — Therefore even in pradhāna as object, the man with arisen sheath — the perceiver is indeed sakala, established according to the fifteen-aspect doctrine.

tal-a10-v102 — The fifteen-fold sakala, situated within earth to its end, even in sakala has an interior of sakala; even in the object of knowledge the knower is indeed.

tal-a10-v103 — The gross enveloped principles, yellow and so forth, number fourteen in earth and the other elements.

tal-a10-v104 — Inertness become own form, then knowerhood is declared as holding in the breath and body path in the Vijayottara.

tal-a10-v105 — When measurability is assumed for man, the arisen sheath portion is measurable, but that is not his knowerhood.

tal-a10-v106 — Therefore knowerhood does not exist for the all-named one; thirteen-ness remains after excluding the power and powered duality.

tal-a10-v107 — The subordinate sheath, mother, joined there with dissolution kala, and Maya-entered vijnanakala and others remain as before.

tal-a10-v108 — In the Maya principle's knowable form, even the subordinate sheath is measurable, because Maya is very subtle and unified with the sheath.

tal-a10-v109 — Indeed vijnanakala is here; then the mother sheath, even in Maya-entered kala, gives eleven-fold selfhood.

tal-a10-v110 — In pure vijnana, the knowable well established after sheath destruction, motherhood belongs to mantras desiring awakening.
