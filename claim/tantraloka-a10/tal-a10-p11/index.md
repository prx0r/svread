**tal-a10-v109** — Indeed vijnanakala is here; then the mother sheath, even in Maya-entered kala, gives eleven-fold selfhood.
**tal-a10-v110** — In pure vijnana, the knowable well established after sheath destruction, motherhood belongs to mantras desiring awakening.
