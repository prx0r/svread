tal-a10-v41 — Thus the causal connection is primary between the moved and the mover; another too, in the world, acts accordingly, from that intention.

tal-a10-v42 — Here, in the words he makes the arrow go, one who posits the operation named impetus, thinking it the mover — that mover arises by figuration alone.

tal-a10-v43 — Here wind throws mountain, but both are inert and imagined by seers as mover and moved.

tal-a10-v44 — Thus in relation with an inert thing, primary causal sense is not; let it be elsewhere, for I have spread this out in detail.

tal-a10-v45 — Manifestation in meaning is metaphor; let it be; if it shines, then that itself is like a falling mountain.

tal-a10-v46 — In metaphor, wind becomes by some cause; here 'throws mountain' has as cause an action done by that.

tal-a10-v47 — On the mountain, this attains fall from destruction of conjunction; but here knowledge of an object is not action at all.

tal-a10-v48 — How can metaphor indeed be? That too is an unmanifest thing; an unmanifested object is not manifested by metaphor of manifestation.

tal-a10-v49 — Just as a child is not burned by mere approach to fire, so approach alone lacks the seed of burning.

tal-a10-v50 — But where truth is established in consciousness, the approach of light has a fitting and clear seed.
