tal-a10-v187 — This difference, as fifteenfold and so on, Sri Shambhu taught; in all meaning here, in the ungraspable, there are sixteen moment-sparks.

tal-a10-v189 — The second is the form of grasper expansion, manifested; but the last has become self-nature by identity with graspedness.

tal-a10-v190 — Indeed the penultimate grasper moment is not to be separately manifested; but from the third moment, six moments are established.
