tal-a10-v31 — How would unperceptible-natured entities attain perceptibility? This is a very great absurdity — look at the entity itself.

tal-a10-v32 — Not light-natured is that consciousness — unlit, resting on that; unlit are mind, lamp, eye and the rest — and just so is that.

tal-a10-v33 — What is called being its light when the world sleeps on every side? Surely knowledge's object-illumination has lamp-form.

tal-a10-v34 — Nothing unprecedented is known here, and we perform no woman-dance — therefore let it be stated what form knowledge's object-illumination has.

tal-a10-v35 — If the object is light — is the object the form, or knowledge itself? Now, whatever illumination of the object there is, that is called its form.

tal-a10-v36 — If the sixth is stated in the agent sense, the defect is hard to remove; now, this sixth in the object sense — the making-force stands firm at heart.

tal-a10-v37 — And so we show this: what, does light shine? That thing is not without light either — and so nothing at all is.

tal-a10-v38 — Then how is causal sense spoken of in the world when what abides is conscious? Its primary field is among the inert; toward consciousness it is only figurative.

tal-a10-v39 — Thus though Caitra is able to go, taking the going as dependent on another, thinking so he speaks: I am gone, master.

tal-a10-v40 — The master too, knowing the power in that going, seeing dependence plainly and fearing the stop, speaks: I shall make him go.
