tal-a10-v151 — For one counting differences and subdifferences there is no limit anywhere; therefore this varied world-arrangement is established.

tal-a10-v152 — Even in earthliness there is no equality between Rudra and Vishnu worlds; what talk of equality elsewhere, in enjoyment or in own form?

tal-a10-v153 — That detail is not directly speakable, although it cannot be spoken; nevertheless, being told by path only, let it be distinguished.

tal-a10-v154 — When the seven mother-powers divide mutually, the form is forty-nine and more.

tal-a10-v155 — All is all-souled; therefore all powers of dissolution, art, and so on truly originate in the mother.

tal-a10-v156 — Let that unclear portion be; the lord may give it, for among them and their mutual differences there is much difference.

tal-a10-v157 — When principal differences are distinguished by water and similar elements, infinite rule variety is stated in the mode of principal difference.

tal-a10-v158 — The arisen and subordinated powers of the whole clearly extend another difference.

tal-a10-v159 — Thus another difference arises for dissolution powers and the like from impression, skill, or decay.

tal-a10-v160 — When one knows without entering the state of a subordinated power, another knowability indeed exists there.
