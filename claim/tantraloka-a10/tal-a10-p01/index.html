tal-a10-v01 — It is said that the secret of the Trika scripture is the differentiation of the tattvas.

tal-a10-v10 — By conditions beginning with knower and knowing, not produced by stain; but by knowers from sakala to Shiva, its knowability is that which...

tal-a10-v02 — The further difference among those tattvas, following in their own classes, is also declared in the scripture called Sri Trika.

tal-a10-v03 — By the difference of shaktiman and shakti, the range from earth to the root last is divided into fifteen by its own nature along with anara.

tal-a10-v04 — Up to kala, with two differences omitted, pralayakala is rudra-like; similarly maya is ninefold, and jnakala is sevenfold again.

tal-a10-v05 — Mantras are those lords in fivefold, mantra-lords are threefold, but Shiva is not divided, being one light-dense consciousness.

tal-a10-v06 — Shiva, mantra, mahesha, isha, mantra, akala, yuk, and kali are named; there are seven shaktimans and likewise fourteen shaktis.

tal-a10-v07 — Its own nature is the fifteenth; that ground is fifteenth-natured, for the three powers of the Lord were described before.

tal-a10-v08 — Those very powers are established in the threefold form of knower, knowing, and known; the supreme part is knower-form here, and the instrument part is intermediate.

tal-a10-v09 — The known is supreme; the power-holder part and power are its own nature; thus there the nature of the ground is that which stands separately as inert.
