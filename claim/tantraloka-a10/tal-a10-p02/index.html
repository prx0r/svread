tal-a10-v11 — By power-holders with unmanifest powers, seven divisions of those; from sakala to Shiva, in powers with raised self.

tal-a10-v12 — Knowability-born seven divisions; thus fourteen; of sakala, the instrument part which is vidya-kala-natured.

tal-a10-v13 — That generic-natured portion is counted in power, not as distinct from it; layakala's measure-portion is that same generic, most clear.

tal-a10-v14 — But jnanakala's measure is the melting vidyakala-covering, joined with the impression of destroyed impure-knowledge activity.

tal-a10-v15 — Yearning to awake, pure knowledge becomes the saviors' instrument; awakened, pure knowledge is joined with its own refinement.

tal-a10-v16 — The mantra-lords' measure is devoid of that impression; the mantra-great-lords' measure is called power as instrument.

tal-a10-v17 — The divine will-power, existing by mere freedom — that same is Shiva's instrument; by her he knows and he acts.

tal-a10-v18 — The seven measurers from Shiva to sakala are twofold; by diminished or excessive power, their division differentiates the perceptible.

tal-a10-v19 — Thus: perceptibility named is only the entity's own body — 'I know the perceptible through Caitra' — so what appears here?

tal-a10-v20 — Surely here only Caitra-cognition shines; perceptibility so-called does not shine as the entity's property the way blue does.
