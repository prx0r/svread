tal-a10-v21 — And if perceptibility were the entity's property by nature, then everything would be perceptible to all, like pot, blue and the rest.

tal-a10-v22 — Now, though the entity bears perceptibility through perceiver-awareness force, that too is defective — like weaver-made cloth.

tal-a10-v23 — But if that so-called perceptibility-property is itself unperceived, it is like a sky-flower; if it exists as perceived, there too is regress upon perceptibility.

tal-a10-v24 — Then nothing would be perceptible, and the world would be stupefied — but, you say, let the body be declared as fashioned by the cognizer's conditioning.

tal-a10-v25 — As this knowledge, whose nature is the entity's meaning-manifestation, is unreal — under single-knower knowability there is no knowability by another knower.

tal-a10-v26 — Under all-knower knowability there is no single-knower knowability — therefore perceptibility so-called is no entity's property at all.

tal-a10-v27 — The entity's perceptibility is just that uprising of consciousness whose form is object-grasping, where awareness stands in itself.

tal-a10-v28 — The manifest object unites with it — counter to that stands this very perceptibility. Here we say: if perceptibility is no property of objects...

tal-a10-v29 — They would indeed be unknowable even when knowledge is truly described — as with the pot of broad-based form, even though it is real.

tal-a10-v30 — Cloth, not having that nature, does not take on broad-based form — so too, though knowledge exists conjoined with the knower.
