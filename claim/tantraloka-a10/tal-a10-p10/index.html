tal-a10-v100 — The ones named sakala-abode have all sheaths subdued; the knowledge-akalās have that sheath destroyed — thus the conclusion.

tal-a10-v91 — Thus knowability, called a property of being, is established and shines.

tal-a10-v92 — But in union with non-manifestation, own nature is said, clearly dispelling doubt of adjunct union.

tal-a10-v93 — By which form of one's own self the meaning shines, that own body; I know the pot here, endowed with knowability's coloring.

tal-a10-v94 — The pot indeed shone by its own form, it is declared; but there, is self-knowable being dependent on mantra and so on?

tal-a10-v95 — Also it indeed exists; let it be, but it does not appear; the unknowable indeed, of Meru having fire-body, supreme directions.

tal-a10-v96 — "Mine" in consciousness: the supreme pure thing shines; though knowable due to shining, not that, as knowable, from shining.

tal-a10-v97 — Indeed perception itself is imperceptible; in this way we inquire — this fifteen-aspected earth, and water and the like.

tal-a10-v98 — From their unmanifest-end, perceptibility toward sakala is said — endowed from kalā down to earth.

tal-a10-v99 — Sakala is so called by the excess of its six sheaths — the sheaths of arisen impure consciousness, attachment, kalā and the rest, of rasa.
