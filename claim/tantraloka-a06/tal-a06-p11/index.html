tal-a06-v101 — There in the solar disc the moon, dissolved, drips its honey; once heated, the sun-companion — Simhika's son — drinks that honey.

tal-a06-v102 — The sun is the measure, the moon the measurable, both knowledge-and-action-natured; Rahu the maya-observer is skilled at covering them.

tal-a06-v103 — From that clash, the darkness-natured one cannot melt them; from the union of the two, the primary measurer is the melter.

tal-a06-v104 — From the clash of sun, moon and Rahu: measure, knowable and knower as non-dual — therefore this great graha is meritorious.

tal-a06-v105 — Even without new-moon this clash is a great graha — as when the sun is in Aries and Rahu in Asvini on an Asvini day.

tal-a06-v106 — When on new-moon the half is dissolved in the half of pratipada and the pratipadi is fully pure — that is liberation in the distant rite.

tal-a06-v107 — Between eclipse and liberation: bathing, meditation, homa, japa and the rest — worldly and unworldly — their further fruit becomes beyond-worldly.

tal-a06-v108 — Entering the moment of dissolution of the eclipsed and eclipsor disturbance, one performs liberation-share meditation, worship and the rest at the moon-sun eclipse.

tal-a06-v109 — Lunar-day cut brings debt, cough, increase, exhalation, wealth; effortless and effort-born signs differ by exhalation and restraint.

tal-a06-v110 — Thus breath enters the consciousness-sun, which fills the nectar-filled moon one by one with consciousness-rays by measure.
