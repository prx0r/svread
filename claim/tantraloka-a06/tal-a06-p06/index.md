**tal-a06-v51** — There too that effort is not situated as knowable; but life-breath movement is distinguished from knowable effort from the heart.
**tal-a06-v52** — Of lord Shiva, powers Vama, Jyeshtha, and Raudri are those two self-life-breaths causing effort.
**tal-a06-v53** — Lord power is sometimes primary, as in limb wind stirring; self power sometimes, as in kanda contraction spanda.
**tal-a06-v54** — Life power is sometimes in heart movement; clearly three or two may be primary for attentive yogis.
**tal-a06-v55** — Even inversion toward primacy of life and self powers occurs from attention, unseen part, strength, and thus moving.
**tal-a06-v56** — Vama is lord of bound beings and causing lord power; Jyeshtha is for well-awakened, and Raudri for those desiring to know.
**tal-a06-v57** — Vama vomits samsara; Jyeshtha is Shiva-filled; Raudri melts diseases and obstructs all actions.
**tal-a06-v58** — Not knowing the creation principle, one is not liberated nor liberates; in Shri Yogacara liberation is from all illumination.
**tal-a06-v59** — Yogis who do not know creation, preservation, and dissolution are not liberated; they are bound by that ignorance.
**tal-a06-v60** — Creation and related processes are all time-dependent, no doubt; that breath-essence is therefore clearly said as utterance.
