**tal-a06-v71** — Supreme powers, vama-sha, and hero leaders are named, eight and eight, enjoying pervaded-pervading state.
**tal-a06-v72** — Gross and subtle things rise in order; in eight praharas of day and night, cruel and gentle actions occur without doubt.
**tal-a06-v73** — Liberation, whether through cruelty or gentleness of intention, is determined at the destruction of day and night by pervasion, meditation, and yoga.
**tal-a06-v74** — Those were also spoken by the supreme lord in the vira lineage: white and black, long and short, merit and demerit, in day and night.
**tal-a06-v75** — If that initiation destroys day and night through pervasion, meditation, and yoga, then day and night are spoken in breath movement, and a month is called.
**tal-a06-v76** — Day is dark, night is bright; fortnights act as before, and among the sixteen stated lunar days are those in east and west.
**tal-a06-v77** — But in those, rest occurs half and half; the other fifteen lunar days, with a quarter and two fingers, divide day and night by a lunar day.
**tal-a06-v78** — Indeed, those day and night depend on light and rest, so awareness occurs every moment, because the yogini is light and bliss.
**tal-a06-v79** — Those two are fixed: as much as by that, so much day and night, because awareness is clearly risen and set.
**tal-a06-v80** — A moment, eon, or blink is only that much; let it be so, as much as the rising of awareness devoted to grasping one object.
