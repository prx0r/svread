tal-a06-v01 — The external means is clearly distinguished by its named arrangement.

tal-a06-v08 — That same power, outside consciousness, makes ordered and unordered clear as breath movement.

tal-a06-v09 — Pure consciousness, light and supreme meaning, shines distinct like sky when the measured self is abandoned.
