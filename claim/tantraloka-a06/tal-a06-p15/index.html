tal-a06-v141 — Having burned the three worlds, by smoke it puts another three to sleep, before the hells, since the manifestation of time-fire.

tal-a06-v142 — "The Lord is stationed below, O Ishvara"—thus is the glorious Raurava opinion: when Brahma's breath is blown away, in ashes, by sweat water.

tal-a06-v143 — The universe flooded by that remains until the arrival of night; at the end of that night, all beings have subtle bodies.

tal-a06-v144 — Impelled by fire-speed, people in the world become dissolution only; while Kushmanda, Hataka, and others play in the great abode.

tal-a06-v145 — At night's end he makes creation again, begun from darkness; at the end of his own hundred years stands that ruin — the Vaiṣṇava day.

tal-a06-v146 — And the night is of that measure — thus the hundreds named Viṣṇu and Rudra, in order at each one's own hundred-end, perish here with the egg's waning.

tal-a06-v147 — Thus at the ends of the unmanifest tattva from the start, a hundred years in order — the day-night division would so obtain, measured by each one's own hundred-year span.

tal-a06-v148 — By thousands of Brahmā's dissolution-joys with the rasāgni fires — among the Rudras standing in the unmanifest, day and night are each of that measure.

tal-a06-v149 — Then Śrīkaṇṭha alone would be the lord directly doing dissolution; all the Rudras likewise at the root are charged with māyā's womb.

tal-a06-v150 — In the one named avyakta he indeed sits with Śrīkaṇṭha of Brahmā's line, lower work withdrawn — for Brahmā is there in the lower reach of thought.
