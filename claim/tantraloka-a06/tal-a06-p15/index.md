**tal-a06-v148** — By thousands of Brahmā's dissolution-joys with the rasāgni fires — among the Rudras standing in the unmanifest, day and night are each of that measure.
**tal-a06-v149** — Then Śrīkaṇṭha alone would be the lord directly doing dissolution; all the Rudras likewise at the root are charged with māyā's womb.
**tal-a06-v150** — In the one named avyakta he indeed sits with Śrīkaṇṭha of Brahmā's line, lower work withdrawn — for Brahmā is there in the lower reach of thought.
