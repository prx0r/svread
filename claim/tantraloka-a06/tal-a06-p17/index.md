**tal-a06-v161** — At the end of his time, sadashiva occupies the bindu, crescent, and obstructing powers, takes the moving and still universe, and dissolves into sound.

**tal-a06-v163** — Thus far is to be known: shakti dissolves again into the pervading principle at the end of its own time, day and night.
**tal-a06-v164** — The pervading principle dissolves that day and night; it remains unsupported, even when shakti time is struck and unsupported.
**tal-a06-v165** — At that time, day and night occur; even time multiplied by half-counts goes to dissolution in the state named equality, the samanasa state.
**tal-a06-v166** — That time, named equality, would be eternal and auspicious, made of parts; its samanasa form is equality, brahman, all-pervading.
**tal-a06-v167** — Therefore, from samanasa time, from the measure of a blink onward, minute and so forth up to the end of half-counts is produced; this is established here.
**tal-a06-v168** — The series runs: ten hundred thousand ten thousand, laksha, niyuta, koti, with arbuda, vṛnda, kharva, nikharva, shankha, lotus, ocean, middle, end, and then half-count.
**tal-a06-v169** — From one onward, counting tenfold by order, one should declare the state among eighteen.
**tal-a06-v170** — These four are principal dissolutions and creations, their moments, and the four egg-worlds.
