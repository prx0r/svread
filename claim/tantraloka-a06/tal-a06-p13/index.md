**tal-a06-v128** — Whatever division and juncture stands there among prahara, half-day, night, month, season, year and sun — that juncture is sacred for meditation and worship.
**tal-a06-v129** — Thus in the breath-rise this time — single-bodied as power, world-self dwelling within — has its outer form posed.
**tal-a06-v130** — Six breaths make a cup, sixty a tube; there the tithi by thirty makes the month, and twelve of those make the year.
