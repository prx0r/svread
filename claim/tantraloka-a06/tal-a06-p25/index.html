tal-a06-v241 — Some say eight vargas rise by half a watch each day, with decrease and increase at night, while others disagree.

tal-a06-v242 — This varga rise, moving by half a watch day and night, is counted as thirteen hundred fifty breaths.

tal-a06-v243 — Indeed, one and a half transitions occur per varga in day and night, and four hundred twenty-seven rise.

tal-a06-v244 — For the nine vargas, one hundred breaths are assigned, and a one-third transition per varga is stated.

tal-a06-v245 — In the union of day and night, the gross varga rise, counted in hundreds, is called by this ocean-like name.

tal-a06-v246 — Each letter has two hundred sixteen breaths, thus also at night and outside.

tal-a06-v247 — There, one hundred eight raudra and shakta are counted, and rudra and shakti remain indivisible in yamala union.

tal-a06-v248 — In day-night division, sight, fire, water, moon, and recitation with a fifth part nadi are remembered as outside letter rise.

tal-a06-v249 — Thus this fifty-verse discussion of letters is completed; some call it one less, and we declare their opinion.

tal-a06-v250 — Four vedas, fifth part less, and one each of four halves are more in a letter, doubled and indivisible in day and night.
