tal-a06-v100 — There on pratipada, that prior half of the half-tuti being new-moon, by lunar-day-cut one performs and enters the solar eclipse.

tal-a06-v91 — How can the unawakened place be day? Enough with this; this is not my prior opinion.

tal-a06-v92 — But shown to disciples, seers of the true path explain the dark fortnight, the moon gone to breath.

tal-a06-v93 — By nourishing the self one by one, one digit is abandoned each tithi; near the twelve-end, that fifteenth drop remains.

tal-a06-v94 — Here at new moon the waned moon enters the breath-sun; it is said in Shri Kamika, and supreme nature, Maya, proceeds in half-halves in two-part Shiva form.

tal-a06-v95 — By moon-sun self the body is filled and dissolved; nectar is by moon form, twofold and sixteenfold again.

tal-a06-v96 — All gods drink the ten-five supreme digits; new moon remains in the cave and nourishes the world.

tal-a06-v97 — Thus the fifteen digits of the moon are consumed in order; the sixteenth, identical with nourishing immortal form, is not consumed.

tal-a06-v98 — There the fifteen-digit portion is the tuṭi of the waned moon; its upper half is called the fortnight-juncture.

tal-a06-v99 — Therefore from rest in the half-tuti at new moon, the prior half, the supreme pratipada, and the other half — that juncture is posited.
