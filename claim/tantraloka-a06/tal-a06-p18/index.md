**tal-a06-v171** — Time-fire destroys on earth; at maya's end the time-principle king, Shrikantha, root, makes creation and destruction in one place.

**tal-a06-v173** — In its creation, countless creation-destructions of worlds are included; therefore shakti is called the great creation.
**tal-a06-v174** — In dissolution, Brahma, Hari, and Rudras proceed mutually by order to dissolution at maya's end.
**tal-a06-v175** — In maya-principle dissolution these go to the supreme state; above maya, white-path dwellers dissolve in supreme Shiva.
**tal-a06-v176** — There too, by conditional difference in dissolution, knowers know difference; thus in the truth-lord group, dissolved, creation returns to the supreme.
**tal-a06-v177** — Its practitioners, desired by Shiva, dwell in its place; Brahmi, power of the supreme, should be cast there.
**tal-a06-v178** — Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra and others follow order from Vaishnavi etc; leaving another power-holder, power does not go to such.
**tal-a06-v179** — Covered and manifested all; power and Shiva are one; thus creations and dissolutions are established in one place.
**tal-a06-v180** — He too is in consciousness; pure consciousness, devoid of knowable, is only pure consciousness; the goddess is the supreme lady.
