**tal-a06-v228** — In the glorious Mātaṅga, whose self is the dharma-mass, is Śiva, from whom it comes — the parāmarśa wheel's lord, the ruler.
**tal-a06-v229** — The coarse recitation of eighty-one words is manifested through reflection, and from that the reflection is completed as one.
**tal-a06-v230** — So long that word is spoken, not bound by sleep-verb rule, but empowered by the great reflection of eighty-one words.
