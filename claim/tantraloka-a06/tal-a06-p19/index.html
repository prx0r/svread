tal-a06-v181 — The thirty-eighth principle is the heart, supreme and inferior, by which consciousness itself pulsates by nature.

tal-a06-v182 — Thus dissolution and arising are praised in breath through the sixty-year cycle, established by will alone, with discussion of action's variety.

tal-a06-v183 — Time power arises from that; the external body is not fixed, whether in dream, sleep, or thought's range.

tal-a06-v184 — In samadhi, distinguishing universe dissolution and creation, even a measured portion of time shines extensively.

tal-a06-v185 — In difference of knowers, variegated extensiveness appears; thus in breath time is born from the power of action variety.

tal-a06-v186 — So in apana too, spreading from the heart's root seat, contraction and expansion of the great root seat occur.

tal-a06-v187 — For brahma and supported ends, creation and dissolution are gathered; although apana always carries thus, that is different.

tal-a06-v188 — Unperceived effort is worshipped by yogis through effort, reaching the end of twelve heart-bulb bliss contractions and expansions.

tal-a06-v189 — Brahma and unsupported ends are served here by good yogis, and these dwell in the universe by supreme lord power.

tal-a06-v190 — Thus obtaining the body, its causes are stated in Kamika through childhood, youth, old age, death, and rebirth.
