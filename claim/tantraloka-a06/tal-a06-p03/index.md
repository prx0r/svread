**tal-a06-v21** — Enough of digression; now the subject is distinguished: the whole path is indeed established in prana.
**tal-a06-v22** — That path is divided in two ways, by action and form; prana is indeed the crest spoken of in Srimat Trishiras.

**tal-a06-v26** — Midday is liberation-giving: the sun stands in the middle of the sky, its essence has not set, and it awakens the wheel of creatures.
**tal-a06-v27** — Point, breath, day, and sun stand together in one place; the great twilight is the third, very peaceful in nature, and established.
**tal-a06-v28** — Thus where the bound crest assigns fruits, therefore in consciousness all this path stands after resting.
**tal-a06-v29** — The appearance of form and action from formless, all-pervading, actionless consciousness is indeed the path of the great lord.
**tal-a06-v30** — The path is to be traversed by stages; for dualists it is a cause of arrival, while for the awakened it is aimed from the enjoyable state.
