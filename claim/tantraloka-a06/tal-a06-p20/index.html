tal-a06-v191 — In liberation and body, brahma and others act in six seats; at the end the supreme goddess is where the joined one is not born.

tal-a06-v192 — By this alone, when known, one becomes the doer of initiation and grace, because all causes unfold in the well-known state.

tal-a06-v193 — One who finds causeless Shiva, the cause of the universe, sees this downward-facing form that utterly destroys the stain of duality.

tal-a06-v195 — As long as one performs with tuṭi and so on according to finger divisions, the rule is the same even in the heart nadis.

tal-a06-v196 — Moving in all directions, it is conceived tenfold; ten principal great nadis fill those gone there.

tal-a06-v197 — A nadi residing in another nadi has all positions in the body; moving in eight directions, this moves according to the direction-lord's order.

tal-a06-v198 — Imitating actions, fierce and gentle, he shines; indeed in three nadis, left, right, and middle.

tal-a06-v199 — Moving and staying day and night in the principal moon, sun, and fire channels, two and a half nalikas, nine hundred pranas are established.

tal-a06-v200 — Flowing so much day and night, he moves in twenty-four ways; on the equinox day, in the morning, with fractions, a nali, he is middle-going.
