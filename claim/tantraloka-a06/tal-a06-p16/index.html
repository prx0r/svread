tal-a06-v151 — The knower in office is not the mere experiencer; having turned only to Śiva he would become Śiva — this intermediate laya at its end is called creation.

tal-a06-v152 — At that day's face Śrīkaṇṭha creates again toward those perfected in Sāṃkhya, Vedas, and the rest — yet complete liberation is not of this kind.

tal-a06-v153 — In pradhāna the day-night born of it — a hundred years of the Lord — is Śrīkaṇṭha's lifespan, the day of those dwelling in the kañcukas.

tal-a06-v154 — From that sequence Niyati, Kāla, Rāga, Vidyā, and Kalā enter one another's dissolution; their lifespan is the dense day.

tal-a06-v155 — At that day's wasting the universe dissolves in māyā; in the night of that measure Gahaneśa should create again.

tal-a06-v156 — Thus the unmanifest time is struck through by ten parārdhas; māyā's night of that measure would be the dissolution — that is the one so named.

tal-a06-v157 — The maya-time, multiplied by half-counts and by a hundred, becomes the lordly day; sound and breath create the world.

tal-a06-v158 — During that lordly night, breath subsides, and the universe, contained in the breath-womb, moves by the sushumna path.

tal-a06-v159 — When breath is pacified in the brahma cavity, consciousness also remains; dissolution of this proceeds part by part, from subtle to subtler.

tal-a06-v160 — Multiplying the lordly time by half-counts and by a hundred gives the sadashiva day; the night is the great dissolution.
