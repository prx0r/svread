tal-a04-v111 — Without another facing it, conception arises by freedom in the self-luminous pure self.

tal-a04-v117 — Immersion of the body in white ash is called bathing; thus bathing satisfies infinite deities.

tal-a04-v119 — That impurity is declared supreme; purity is crushing that intellect, so one's body becomes a vessel of awareness with dissolved difference.

tal-a04-v120 — Seeing mere awareness, the lord stands free; whatever is mind-pleasing arises wherever there is sense-station.
