tal-a04-v31 — Having attained it, for a long time, consumed by that enjoyment; at the end of dissolution of that principle, then one goes to a higher creation.

tal-a04-v32 — It becomes mantra by awakening from Ananteśa's creation; we will explain this ahead; let that be for now here.

tal-a04-v33 — He who is mocked by doctrines fabricated by ignorant people, rooted in false guru, is colored by the Māyā bond.

tal-a04-v34 — He too, by union of right reasoning, is led toward the true guru; right reasoning is pure knowledge, and that is the will of the supreme lord.

tal-a04-v35 — In the Śrīpūrva scripture it is said by him: desiring, by Śiva's will, for accomplishment of enjoyment and liberation, one is led toward the true guru.

tal-a04-v36 — But grace there proceeds gradually, for one who, abiding in the true guru or in another scripture, has taken the right path.

tal-a04-v37 — Here, in the being guided by guru and scripture, the variety of descent of power is distinguished beforehand.

tal-a04-v38 — As said in the Svacchanda scripture, māyā deludes all teachers, even in liberation, through desire for liberation.

tal-a04-v39 — But one rooted there, skilled in reflection, is purified by descent of power through the glory of pure knowledge.

tal-a04-v40 — He ascends the true path free from obstacles, and his reasoning proceeds from himself.
