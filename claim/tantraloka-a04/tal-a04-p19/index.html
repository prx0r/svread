tal-a04-v181 — There worship is already whole, because the resonance that arises of itself is nothing but self-reflection.

tal-a04-v182 — That ever-arising self-reflection is called the supreme, great heart; seated in the heart, it has melted the whole universe.

tal-a04-v183 — Scripture gives it the common name spanda: the current running from the first grasp of a state to its end, rising in the self.

tal-a04-v184 — This slight stirring, this shimmer of nothing else, is the wave on the ocean of awakening, never present without consciousness.

tal-a04-v185 — Ocean-ness is a movement where waves are never two; the essence of all this is consciousness, while the inert is only the world.

tal-a04-v186 — Because everything rests on it, that essence is the great heart, the root of brahman named the egg of māyā.

tal-a04-v187 — Will, knowledge and action are never truly named apart from their ascent, and that ascent is the threefold power rising in the consciousness-self of Bhairavī.

tal-a04-v188 — What is released from that, and set outside, is released once more; thus their true form is the self of the threefold power.

tal-a04-v189 — Drawing the emission back by supreme knowledge, it goes on; that alone, wearing an outer form, is burnt away in the fire of prior knowing.

tal-a04-v190 — The inner-sounding reflection is left as residue, and that is already enough: void-selfhood itself is reached from beyond the range of the three powers.
