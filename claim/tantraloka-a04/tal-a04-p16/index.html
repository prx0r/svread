tal-a04-v152 — Having withdrawn doubt except its doubtable meaning, in the being-circle she indeed considers withdrawal, from dissolving in her own self-fire.

tal-a04-v153 — And that dissolving-natured being-withdrawal is indeed touched in the self, by which this may shine as swallowed by me.

tal-a04-v154 — The withdrawable condition of this is indeed the own nature of consciousness; in purified, conditionless consciousness-form it is established.

tal-a04-v155 — Even when the being-flood is dissolved, she then makes some being sticky; doubt indeed remains in impression-form.

tal-a04-v156 — By auspicious and inauspiciousness, this will be born with fruit-abundance, before or after enjoyment; this doubt stands apart.

tal-a04-v157 — Even what is congealed elsewhere is dissolved by consciousness itself, like rites dissolve grave acts.

tal-a04-v158 — Consciousness discerns form through constriction and dissolution, dissolving and then congealing again.

tal-a04-v159 — When something enjoyable is enjoyed, consciousness withdraws its instruments and discerns twelve in the self.

tal-a04-v160 — The group of action, intellect, and senses is twelvefold and expands in distinct objects because of its illuminating sun-nature.
