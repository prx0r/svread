**tal-a04-v223** — Thus here earth would be purified from water and water from earth, a mutual dependence that is futile and leads to infinite regress.
**tal-a04-v224** — This mutual dependence, even in impurity, makes the order futile: impure water would purify earth and vice versa.
**tal-a04-v225** — From air water, from fire air, or from elsewhere; many-formed mantras are purifiers, and purity arises in them.
**tal-a04-v226** — If mantras are pure by nature, why then Shiva-nature? If purity is in them, why is that needed there too?
**tal-a04-v227** — Non-recognition of Shiva-nature is not in mantras as in earth; if they are pure by that, then cognition of purity is purity itself.
**tal-a04-v228** — For the yogi that purity exists in things; indeed, by injunction the determination of pure, impure, and so forth is made.
**tal-a04-v229** — Let it be so; even so, what injunction spoken by Shiva would be true if this is elsewhere?
**tal-a04-v230** — If this is overruled by the Vedic, would the opposite not become true, if you think correctly, from specific objectness?
