tal-a04-v21 — A fool may be attached in Vaishnava teachings, colored by attachment, because that is his divine power called Vama.

tal-a04-v22 — Some foolish ones are seen blaming royal enjoyment, and this expands from Pañcaratra, Brahma, and Buddhist traditions.

tal-a04-v23 — Not from contentment, but from desire proceeding in their own enjoyments, minds become intent on blaming Bhairava possession.

tal-a04-v24 — They become fallen by very terrible powers; therefore, even knowers in other teachings should know the greatness of the Shaiva teaching.

tal-a04-v25 — Having been reassured, one should not cross from the great ocean of difference; this is clearly stated in the Śrīkāmikā and in the section on bonds.

tal-a04-v26 — Those wise seers who know Veda, Sāṃkhya, and Purāṇas, devoted to Pāñcarātra, and devoted to other scriptures.

tal-a04-v27 — All Buddhists, Arhats, and others are colored by attachment to knowledge; because they are bound by the Māyā bond, they do not obtain Śiva initiation.

tal-a04-v28 — By the word rāga is stated the rāga principle as regulator in Māyā; and that regulates him in that scripture.

tal-a04-v29 — Even the liberation of Vaiṣṇavas and others is imagined by their own resolve: union with higher nature, or bliss-form.

tal-a04-v30 — Or only purified mind, or lamp-like cessation of continuum; that is composed of dissolution of knowable and unknowable self into kalā.
