tal-a04-v01 — Now we declare the circle of shakti means for supreme self-knowledge.

tal-a04-v10 — But the supreme lord, pure through difficult doing and free will, is skilled in the play of concealing himself.

tal-a04-v02 — In that supreme lordly nature, one desiring entry into distinction should refine it directly.

tal-a04-v03 — Refined distinction produces a self-refined distinction equal to itself, and that produces another similar one.

tal-a04-v04 — In four distinctions, refinement gradually moves from unclear to becoming clear and fully clear-natured.

tal-a04-v05 — Then it becomes clearer until the clearest; even from the unclear beginning, difference remains between stages.

tal-a04-v06 — Then consciousness, filled with the clearest nobility of form, approaches pure non-distinct nature.

tal-a04-v07 — Therefore, for those reflecting again and again, the clear selfhood of that consciousness-natured radiance arises.

tal-a04-v08 — But how can consciousness, supreme knower made of reflection, reflect by itself in such creation without becoming inert?

tal-a04-v09 — It is said that self-consciousness is full by nature alone; nothing is to be removed or added, as said before.
