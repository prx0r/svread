tal-a04-v261 — Fullness by universal form is described to know; conventional practice existence is taught as to be protected.

tal-a04-v262 — By difference as life, abandoning each purifies principle; convention prohibition is said in doctrinal scriptures.

tal-a04-v263 — Let boundless complete self-awakening be understood; this form is another's, but my own is to be meditated.

tal-a04-v264 — Flame linga belongs to another, but skull etc is my own; etc includes asceticism and hospitality.

tal-a04-v265 — Named with Sakti-Siva as extremes — this is mine, not otherwise; and lineage, guru-continuum, called mathika-kula.

tal-a04-v266 — Srisamtati named Tryambaka, called half-destruction — thus here half-four mathikas, in the Sankara order.

tal-a04-v267 — By yuga-sequence the siddha-continuum from Kurma to Fish — and by the word beginning: house, village, pithas and sub-pithas.

tal-a04-v268 — Mudra is the kiss, and their self-other-situated procedure — for identity-realization indeed resort to your own continuum.

tal-a04-v269 — Enjoy, worship the wheel — but not indeed through another's tormentor; and this is forbidden in mata-treatises, since it is refutation.

tal-a04-v270 — Even in the undivided supreme reality, by this difference it arises — thus field-entry and the rest, from the continuity-rule's end.
