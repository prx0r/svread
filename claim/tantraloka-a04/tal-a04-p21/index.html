tal-a04-v201 — Without regard for inner fuel-provision, constantly — blazing, its fierce flame spread over floods of eyes, the fire.

tal-a04-v202 — In such knowledge-fire, beings entering — raising his true glory — attain the causality of sacrifice-acts.

tal-a04-v203 — Whosoever, purified by the supreme Lord's power-descent, manifested before — let him stand himself; as said, he is initiated.

tal-a04-v204 — From japa through homa, though in each single rite supreme custom arises — nevertheless thus it is determined.

tal-a04-v205 — Just as a horse driven in a painted land with equal lows and highs goes along a path imagined by mere wish.

tal-a04-v206 — So, through the varied patterns of consciousness, peaceful, fierce, and the rest, duality is abandoned and becomes Bhairava.

tal-a04-v207 — Just as, perceiving one's own face in a mirror placed before one, one again knows that same face as one's own self.

tal-a04-v208 — So, in the mind-mirror of meditation, worship, and ritual, seeing oneself as Bhairava, one soon becomes identified with him.

tal-a04-v209 — Becoming identified is the attainment in the supreme self; it is the supreme limit of fullness, and here there is no other fruit.

tal-a04-v210 — All fruit is imagined in incompleteness, there and there; but in unimagined fullness, what other fruit could be spoken?
