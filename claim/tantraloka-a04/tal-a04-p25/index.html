tal-a04-v241 — All attains consciousness-identity — pure thence, established; and in the glorious Vira-row, in pure-impure discernment, it is said.

tal-a04-v242 — The living is the carrier of all — nothing is non-living; whatever is lifeless is impure — know that.

tal-a04-v243 — Therefore what is not too far from consciousness would bring purity — by a non-conceptual state even sages thus became.

tal-a04-v244 — But for world-protection that principle was concealed by them — though outer things exist, purity and impurity are not like blue.

tal-a04-v245 — This indeed is cognizer-dharma, from experiencing consciousness as one and not-one — or if even object-nature, bound to measure-dependence.

tal-a04-v246 — In the Sautramani liquor is pure for the hotr — for another's pure things, reversal; by this, injunctions too are contradicted by his own words.

tal-a04-v247 — As sometimes shown, like brahmahatya injunction-prohibition — food-rules too, relying on this reasoning, are practiced.

tal-a04-v248 — And at the supreme-knowledge beginning the Great Lord speaks indeed — what sages gods Brahma Visnu Rudra and the rest declared.

tal-a04-v249 — By increasing superiority overcoming prior upon prior — the Shaiva is never to be refuted by Vaishnava words.

tal-a04-v250 — The Vishnu-related should be discussed through Brahma-born and the rest — who obstructs from perversity is deluded and becomes sin's sharer.
