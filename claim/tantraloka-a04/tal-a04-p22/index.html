tal-a04-v211 — This sacrifice rite exists in someone's heart, and it pleases the wheel of consciousness quickly after birth.

tal-a04-v212 — In this sacrifice one attains isolation, though seen by people while standing in the body-bond rite.

tal-a04-v213 — Here the lord stated conduct in the eighteenth chapter: there is neither purity nor impurity, nor consideration of food and the like.

tal-a04-v214 — Neither duality nor non-duality; neither linga worship and the like, nor abandonment of that, nor non-possession or possession.

tal-a04-v216 — Entry into fields and the like, observance of vows and the like, other forms, lingas, names, clans, and the like are not essential here.

tal-a04-v217 — Here nothing is prescribed and nothing is prohibited; even what is prescribed is also prohibited.

tal-a04-v218 — But what is prescribed here, O goddess? The mind must be steadied in truth by a very serene yogin.

tal-a04-v219 — He should act accordingly as it is for him; with mind steady in truth, even while enjoying sense objects.

tal-a04-v220 — He is not touched by faults, like a lotus leaf by water, even while eating, protected by poison-removing mantra and the like.
