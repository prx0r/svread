tal-a04-v131 — As yoni and linga union flows immortal, so from immortal fire union they flow; no doubt.

tal-a04-v132 — From that wheel pressure at night light shines sun-moon going; seeing that supreme moonlight, time-knowledge proceeds.

tal-a04-v133 — The thousand-petaled wheel stands above those two, and from that wheel the cosmic egg arises, which is called that.

tal-a04-v134 — Standing there, the moon, kindled by fire, releases a stream and thus creates the entire endless world within itself.

tal-a04-v135 — Thus, in order, supreme nectar is everywhere in the wheels, from sixteen and twelve spokes to eight.

tal-a04-v136 — The moon flows as long as the path of five wheels; that is again drunk with delight by the shining swan, swan.

tal-a04-v137 — Once, by hearing, one is not stained by merit or sin, having become modified in the five-spoked wheel from the moon-flowing nectar.

tal-a04-v138 — The all-pervading one runs through the three-essence-spoked secret wheels, from which the world is born and dissolved by its own play.

tal-a04-v139 — There is bliss, and the celibate devotee devoted to that; there perfection and liberation are both attained equally.

tal-a04-v140 — Therefore one goes upward until the brahma-natured state, where fire and moon, equal, are created by self in self.
