tal-a04-v101 — Neither his knowledge nor word itself can the guru implant in intellect — therefore the self-awakening sequence is certain.

tal-a04-v106 — And in the former scripture it is said — neither duality nor non-duality — all liṅga-worship and the rest, beginning thus, by Shambhu.

tal-a04-v109 — The mind should be made steady in truth, and thus the supreme lord destroys dualistic reflection.

tal-a04-v110 — One situated in constant reflection on pure own-nature uses sense-objects as means.
