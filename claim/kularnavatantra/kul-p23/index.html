kul-p23-v564 — Know that, O beast; you are Śiva, indeed you are Śiva.

kul-p23-v565 — The supreme self is in that bliss; therefore serve this, beloved.

kul-p23-v566 — Taking and worshiping, O Devi; otherwise it becomes fruitless.

kul-p23-v567 — One should not perform worship with substance without fish and meat.

kul-p23-v568 — By once offering satisfaction alone, one obtains the fruit of a crore sacrifices.

kul-p23-v570 — One devoted to kula worship indeed becomes most beloved to me.

kul-p23-v571 — When male, female, and neuter are worshiped, desired fruit is given.

kul-p23-v574 — All accomplishments are obtained; here no deliberation is to be done.

kul-p23-v575 — He who cannot give should perform worship and seeing.
