## Passage (excerpt as hashed)

```
sā ca na syādvinācāryamityācāryaparamparāantareṇopadeṣṭāraṃ mantrāḥ syunīṣphalā yataḥguruṃ mantrāgamābhijñaṃ samayācārapālakamkiñcitkālaṃ vidhāyetthaṃ svaśiṣyāya samarpayetdehānte śāśvatī muktiriti śaṅkarabhāṣitamsampradāyamavicchinnaṃ sadā kuryāt guruḥ priyepaścādupadiśenmantramanyathā niṣphalaṃ bhavetdadatā gṛhṇatā devi kulaśāpo bhaviṣyatiupadeśaṃ dadad gṛhṇan prāpnuyātāṃ piśācatāmbhuñjāte tāvubhau ghorānnarakānekaviṃśatimvinaśyati ca tanmantraṃ saikate śālibījavattasmāt parīkṣya karttavyamanyathā niṣphalaṃ bhavetsannidhāyātmanaḥ śiṣyaṃ vadenmantraṃ na cānyathātajjñānaṃ tattuśāstrārthaṃ tadvidadhyādakhaṇḍitamtat prayātyapavitratvaṃ gokṣīraṃ śvaghṛtādivadevatāśāpamāpnoti kṛtañca niṣphalaṃ bhavetsaṃvatsaraṃ tadarddhaṃ vā tadarddhaṃ vā prayatnataḥprāṇadravyapraṇāmādyairādeśaiśca svayaṃ samaiḥpakṣapātairudāsīnairanekaiśca muhurmuhuḥguruḥ kṛpāṃ karotīti mudā sañcintayet sadāvandane paricaryāyāmāhvāne preṣaṇe priyeyeṣāṃ syuste'tra yogyāśca dīkṣāsaṃskārakarmaṇiānandādyairjapastotradhyānahomārcanādiṣuvedhakatvaṃ parijñāya śiṣyo bhūyānna cānyathāadhamā madhyamāḥ śreṣṭhāḥ śiṣyā devi prakīttītāḥ
```
