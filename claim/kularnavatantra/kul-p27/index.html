kul-p27-v651 — Therefore, having drawn the mandala properly, one should worship there.

kul-p27-v652 — The mandala by which the three worlds are adorned is always auspicious.

kul-p27-v654 — Common Śrīguru enjoyment, offering vessels, are fivefold.

kul-p27-v655 — Place them from right to left, worship with liquor, but.

kul-p27-v656 — There, a māṣa measure of fish and meat should be placed.

kul-p27-v657 — By such reasons, heated offering in another vessel becomes fruitless.

kul-p27-v658 — With delightful means, O Maheśāni, the offering becomes fruitful.

kul-p27-v659 — Life, glory, fame, prosperity, wealth, and grain destroying.

kul-p27-v660 — Otherwise one goes to hell; for giver and eater there is no doubt.

kul-p27-v661 — Foolish men who remember suffer at every step.

kul-p27-v662 — Because of mutual opposition, how is worship performed?

kul-p27-v663 — The substance should be suitable for offering and pleasing to the deity.

kul-p27-v664 — Wine with twenty-four mantras is supreme nectar.

kul-p27-v665 — They are called moonlight, moonbeam, beauty, moonlight, glory, delight, and limb-giving.

kul-p27-v666 — The sixteen gentle, desire-giving kalas are born from vowels.

kul-p27-v667 — The twelve kalas beginning with ka-bha and ending in saurāṣṭha give wealth.

kul-p27-v668 — The ten fiery kalas beginning with ya give dharma.

kul-p27-v669 — The kalas born from a and from Brahma are for creation.

kul-p27-v670 — The kalas born from u and from Viṣṇu are for preservation.

kul-p27-v671 — The kalas born from m and from Rudra are for destruction.

kul-p27-v672 — The kalas born from Īśvara and from bindu are for concealment.

kul-p27-v673 — Indhika, Dīpikā, Recikā, Mocikā, and the supreme one are named.

kul-p27-v674 — The grace kalas born from Sadāśiva and from nāda arise in order.

kul-p27-v675 — Tryambaka should be the third; the fourth is tatpada and so on.
