kul-p05-v101 — O goddess, one with a thousand-year lifespan does not indeed reach the end of the scriptures.

kul-p05-v102 — Therefore one should discern the essence, just as a swan separates milk from water.

kul-p05-v103 — Like a person seeking grain discards the chaff, one should set aside all the scriptures.

kul-p05-v104 — O Goddess, for one who knows the truth, there is no further need of scripture.

kul-p05-v105 — O Goddess venerated by heroes, liberation comes only from knowledge, and in no other way.

kul-p05-v106 — In the same way, all the scriptures have knowledge alone as their cause.

kul-p05-v107 — From this toil of carrying a load of wood, there is one supreme, life-giving thing.

kul-p05-v108 — It is obtained through the mouth of the guru, and not otherwise, not even by millions of scriptures.

kul-p05-v109 — Sound-brahman consists of the scriptures, but the supreme brahman is born of discrimination.

kul-p05-v110 — They know my truth as free from both duality and non-duality.

kul-p05-v111 — A creature is bound by the thought 'this is mine' and released by the thought 'this is not mine'.

kul-p05-v112 — Other actions, other kinds of knowledge, and skill in crafts lead only to toil.

kul-p05-v113 — As long as there is restlessness of the senses, so long, whence is discourse on truth?

kul-p05-v114 — As long as the mind lacks steadiness, truth-discourse is impossible.

kul-p05-v115 — As long as guru compassion is absent, truth-discourse is absent.

kul-p05-v116 — As long as truth is not found, veda-scripture-agama discourse remains empty.

kul-p05-v117 — One may become devoted to truth, goddess, if one desires liberation of the self.

kul-p05-v118 — Burnt by the three afflictions, one should seek the shade of the liberation tree.

kul-p05-v119 — Without kula-dharma there is no release; this is truth, no doubt.

kul-p05-v120 — Easily, goddess, one is released from terrible samsara bondage.

kul-p05-v121 — In brief, kuleshani, what more do you desire to hear?

kul-p05-v122 — Establishment-discourse is named the first illumination.

kul-p05-v123 — Kula-dharma, god, has been hinted by you, not revealed.

kul-p05-v124 — Speak to me, supreme lord, if your compassion is in me.

kul-p05-v125 — By merely hearing it, one may become dear to the yoginis.
