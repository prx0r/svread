```
prāśayeddevatāprītyai svābhilāṣavivajītaḥyāgakālaṃ binānyatra dūṣaṇaṃ kathitaṃ priyemadyapānaṃ tathā kāryaṃ samaye bhogamokṣadampañcamudrā niṣeveta cānyathā patito bhavedvīro'pyatra vṛthāpānī devatāśāpamāpnuyātpaśupānavidhau pītvā vīro'pi narakaṃ vrajetyo'smin śāstre pravartteta taṃ tvaṃ pīḍayasi dhruvamsa mahāpātakī jñeyaḥ sarvadharmabahiṣkṛtaḥna siddhayaḥ kulabhraṃśastatsaṃsargaṃ na kārayetsa siddhimiha nāpnoti paratra na parāṃ gatimna tasya sadgatiḥ kvāpi tapastīrthavratādibhiḥsvapriyeṇa hataṃ māṃsaṃ rauravaṃ narakaṃ vrajetkeśasaṃkhyā smṛtā yāvattāvattiṣṭhanti rauravetadaṅgaromasaṃkhyātaṃ bhūtayoniṣu jāyatena dhyānaṃ na tapo nārcā na dharmo na ca satkriyākevalaṃ viṣayāsaktaḥ patatyeva na saṃśayaḥkaulopadeśahīno yaḥ so'kṣayaṃ narakaṃ vrajetkuleśi brahyaniṣṭho'pi nindyatāmadhigacchatipīṭhasthānāni cāgatya mahāpadmavanaṃ vrajetciccandrakuṇḍalīśaktisāmarasya sukhodayaḥsudhāpānamidaṃ proktamitare madyapāyinaḥpare layaṃ nayeccitaṃ palāśī sa nigadyatemattsyāśī sa bhaveddevi śeṣāḥ syuḥ prāṇihiṃsakāḥśaktiṃ tāṃ sevayet yastu sa bhavet śaktisevakaḥya āste maithunaṃ tat syādapare strīniṣevakāḥ
```
