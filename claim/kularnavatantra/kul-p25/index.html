kul-p25-v613 — As long as the remembered hair count, so long they stay in Raurava.

kul-p25-v614 — According to that body-hair count, one is born in the element-wombs.

kul-p25-v615 — There is no meditation, no austerity, no worship, no dharma, and no good rite.

kul-p25-v616 — One attached only to sense objects falls indeed; there is no doubt.

kul-p25-v617 — He who is without Kaula instruction would go to imperishable hell.

kul-p25-v618 — O Kuleśī, even one devoted to Brahma obtains reproach.

kul-p25-v619 — Having come to the seat-places, one would go to Mahāpadmavana.

kul-p25-v620 — The harmony of consciousness, moon, kuṇḍalinī, and śakti is the arising of bliss.

kul-p25-v621 — This nectar-drinking is what is said; the others are wine-drinkers.

kul-p25-v622 — The others should lead mind to dissolution; he is called Palāśī.

kul-p25-v623 — He would be the fish-eater, O goddess; the rest would be living-being-harmers.

kul-p25-v624 — Whoever should serve that śakti, he would become a śakti-servant.

kul-p25-v625 — He who performs sexual union—that would be [counted among] the others, the women-servants.
