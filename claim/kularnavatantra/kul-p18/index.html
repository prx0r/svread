kul-p18-v431 — O beloved, having uttered the rest as before, one should place it on the forehead.

kul-p18-v432 — And having uttered the rest as before, one should place it in the brahmarandhra.

kul-p18-v433 — And one should place the pervading element for the goddess nādhipā, śrīparā.

kul-p18-v434 — Keśava, Nārāyaṇa, Mādhava, Govinda, and Viṣṇu.

kul-p18-v435 — Vāsudeva, Saṅkarṣaṇa, Pradyumna, and Aniruddha.

kul-p18-v436 — And Ṛddhi, Rūpiṇī, Luptā, and she who is the sole leader in cutting away faults.

kul-p18-v437 — And the bearer of a bone garland—thus are declared the deities of the vowels.

kul-p18-v438 — Aghora, Sadyojāta, and Vāmadeva are thus declared.

kul-p18-v439 — Then, beyond, comes the deity who consists of meters, abides in the world, and shines like a blazing star.

kul-p18-v440 — These are the deities of the letters beginning with ka and bha and ending with ṭha and ḍa.

kul-p18-v441 — The ten deities, called Hiraṇyagarbha and others, are Brahmā and the rest in sequence.

kul-p18-v442 — Lalitā and Kṣamā are declared to be the deities of the letters beginning with ya.

kul-p18-v443 — One should place them, joined with the dative ending and 'namas', on the head and on the face.

kul-p18-v444 — O supreme goddess, one should place them from the right side to the left.

kul-p18-v445 — One should place Brahmā and the others, together with their powers, in the ten supports.

kul-p18-v446 — Having thus performed the placement of forms, one should carry out the placement of mantras.

kul-p18-v447 — And there is a distinction among all mantras whose nature is the praṇava and other single syllables.

kul-p18-v448 — One should say: 'Salutation to the one-peaked sovereign mother goddess.'

kul-p18-v449 — The supreme three-peaked formula, beginning with ṛṃ, ṛṃ, ḷṃ and with fire and so on, is as before.

kul-p18-v450 — Oṃ, auṃ, aṃ, aḥ: five hundred thousand, sun and the rest, as before, beyond.
