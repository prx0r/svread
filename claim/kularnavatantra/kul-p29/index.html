kul-p29-v712 — All worship and the like done becomes useless, O Shambhavi.

kul-p29-v713 — When she is worshipped in the yantra, O goddess, she becomes pleased immediately.

kul-p29-v714 — In this, which they call a yantra, the god, when worshipped, pleases.

kul-p29-v715 — So a yantra is installed for all the gods.

kul-p29-v716 — Having learned all from the guru's mouth, O beloved, one worships by rule.

kul-p29-v717 — Abandoning limb-limbhood, one obtains the deity's curse.

kul-p29-v718 — One worships the deities with covering by that procedure.

kul-p29-v719 — A mantra-holder with a restless mind obtains sin by both.

kul-p29-v720 — Worshipping by rule, the deity becomes very pleased.

kul-p29-v721 — One worships by the root mantra with sandal, flower, rice and so on.

kul-p29-v722 — One worships with om and so on, ending in namah, by each name.

kul-p29-v723 — O Kulanayika, the flow from one's own vessel's vibration is offered according to rule.

kul-p29-v724 — Having raised the inner power, one satisfies the body deities.

kul-p29-v725 — With the union of ring finger and thumb, one should satisfy the Kula lineage.
