kul-p43-v1063 — That place too becomes purified; how much more those standing there.

kul-p43-v1064 — What then of merit enjoyed by many; it is not counted at all.

kul-p43-v1065 — One devoted to kula dharma should become and worship the kula knower.

kul-p43-v1066 — So long, varna and ashrama conduct is to be done for liberation from action.

kul-p43-v1067 — Liberation would be by shiva through that; therefore perform action.

kul-p43-v1068 — The action-freed desiring happiness, the action-devoted goes to happiness.

kul-p43-v1069 — Who renounces the fruit of action, that one is called a renouncer.

kul-p43-v1070 — Having removed ego sense, having removed it, one who acts is not smeared.

kul-p43-v1071 — Water does not touch the truth knower, as it does not touch a lotus leaf.

kul-p43-v1072 — They go; performed actions are not smeared again.

kul-p43-v1073 — The knower who has renounced all resolves renounces action.

kul-p43-v1074 — Heretics, those who think themselves learned, go to hell, dear.

kul-p43-v1075 — Having obtained truth, the yogi thus renounces attachment to action.
