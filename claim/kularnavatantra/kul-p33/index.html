kul-p33-v807 — Contemplate the body from head to feet as pure.

kul-p33-v808 — The śiva-principle is the supreme end; the world has three principles.

kul-p33-v809 — He is liberated while living, as Śaṅkara says.

kul-p33-v810 — Taking what the guru gives, drink the second nectar.

kul-p33-v811 — Not sitting, drink wine; thus is the scripture's decision.

kul-p33-v812 — He who acts without knowing becomes a refuge of dangers.

kul-p33-v813 — Therefore, O kula-nāyikā, it is to be done by the mantra rite.

kul-p33-v814 — The cause of cutting the paśu bond is spoken by Bhairava.

kul-p33-v815 — By that oneness, the emotions are dissolved in essence.

kul-p33-v816 — Therefore I, being full, drink this wine-goddess.

kul-p33-v817 — With unagitated mind, one should drink nectar slowly.

kul-p33-v818 — In the kuṇḍalī-form cit, offer the substance with mantra.

kul-p33-v819 — The mark of accepting the oblation is in the fire of supreme I-ness.

kul-p33-v820 — In teaching, drink honey until the limit of joy.

kul-p33-v821 — In kula, attainment of the supreme state from drinking is thus declared as three.

kul-p33-v822 — Know as nectar that food which is with wine.

kul-p33-v823 — Drinking without chewing is only poison-increasing.

kul-p33-v824 — Divine drinking before the goddess is done by the hero in the mudrā seat.

kul-p33-v825 — Drunk by one's own will like a paśu, it is called paśu-drinking, thus declared.
