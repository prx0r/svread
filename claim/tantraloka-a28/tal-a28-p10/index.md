**tal-a28-v100** — A wise person should not give a ritual fee smaller than half a rūpaka; for the initiates it should increase in order, doubled step by step, up to the guru.

**tal-a28-v95** — One should place the little finger of the right hand upon the left, join the forefinger and thumb, and place the fingers of the left hand below the right.
**tal-a28-v96** — When the two hands are bound without any gap, that is called the rolled shell; but if, at the time of drinking, drops fall to the ground, the gesture has not been properly formed.
**tal-a28-v97** — By that stream the rays please Vetala, Guhyaka, and the other deities, and Bhairava too is pleased; therefore drinking from the hand is the supreme practice.
**tal-a28-v98** — No one should be given entry to this rite in advance; but if someone enters by carelessness, one should not conduct any inquiry about that person.
**tal-a28-v99** — Having performed the rite in this way, the wise person should, at its conclusion and in due order, distribute the ritual fee together with respectful touch, betel, cloth, and other such gifts.
