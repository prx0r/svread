tal-a28-v231 — It grasps in a womb-born or other body; in an action-painted body, that body awakens then like one risen from sleep.

tal-a28-v232 — Even for that, enjoyment, destruction, and immortalities occur; creation, maintenance, and destruction arise from action strength.

tal-a28-v233 — Therefore grace, for one following destiny, time, and variety, is that which blossoms in its own nature.

tal-a28-v234 — As knowledge-self, how does it await action-destiny and the like? Because of contracted life, action, time, and destiny await.

tal-a28-v235 — In the form of contraction-loss, how is the cause in grace? Grace is divided into gradual and intense.

tal-a28-v236 — This was spoken in detail before; what need repetitions? By that, initiation and Shiva-knowledge burn the contraction bond.

tal-a28-v237 — At body-end, Shiva indeed; for him there is no staying in another body, even for those descended in truth and followers of Shankara's command.

tal-a28-v238 — Dead in houses of self-born, sage, god, sage, man and other worlds, having reached that city, are initiated by city-lords gradually.

tal-a28-v239 — Having descended into mortal or not, they go to Shiva without rebirth; there self-born are in two ways, some devoted to grace.

tal-a28-v240 — Some serve only the place of their own-duty-come part; among those commanded for grace, the man who dies.
