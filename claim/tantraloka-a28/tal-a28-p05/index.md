## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a28-v41** — When the after-sacrifice is carried out completely, following the sequence of hidden and open rules and the conduct of the initial sacrifice, it is indeed foremost in every ritual application.
**tal-a28-v42** — When the proper time for the after-sacrifice is available, one should make every effort to use it. But in the month of Ashvayuj there is no such special lunar-mansion and planetary time, so that month is excluded.
**tal-a28-v43** — The reckoning of time, lunar mansion, and planet has been explained for eleven months. In Phalguna, the twelfth day of the bright fortnight is declared as a special fulfilment time.
**tal-a28-v44** — Here the foremost special factor is the conjunction involving the piercing of the prior lunar day. Day and night are divided into three parts, giving a first, middle, and last division.
**tal-a28-v45** — Within that threefold division, the best time for worship is when the foremost time-slot coincides with the right lunar mansion and planet, and the ritual circle aligns with them.
**tal-a28-v46** — On fulfilment times, the initial sacrifice and desire-driven rites can succeed through worship alone, provided the day, time-slot, lunar mansion, and planet are properly arranged and their gentle or fierce nature is taken into account.
**tal-a28-v47** — Having understood the nature of the timing, the foremost practitioner should perform the appropriate task. But what if the lunar mansion, planet, and lunar day fall outside the declared time for worship?
**tal-a28-v48** — Some say that if there is a conflict, one should ignore the special astrological factor and follow the conventional worship time. But our teachers disagree: here the lunar day is the decisive special factor, not the mere time-slot.
**tal-a28-v49** — The lunar day becomes fully clear through the moon portion and the clusters of sun-rays, measured by planets and stars.
**tal-a28-v50** — Therefore the lunar day is primary here, qualified by union of planets and stars, while time is not principal; thus speaks the supreme lord.
