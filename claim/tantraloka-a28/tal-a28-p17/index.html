tal-a28-v161 — Four in other scripture, five is said in disciple; for linga-bearers only, ten knots should be arranged in the arch.

tal-a28-v162 — Eight knots should be in the gates; having done thus and worshipped the thread, the mantra net is placed there, then.

tal-a28-v163 — Having prepared the threads, one should do the confluence rite; then for a year one should meditate Bhairava, witness of holes.

tal-a28-v164 — Having given the full offering, O goddess, one should bow to mantra Bhairava; om, toward the lord who fills all rite faults, the vow.

tal-a28-v165 — Whatever unmade or corrupt made, O son of Mother, all that is mine, O lord of gods, may perish by your grace.

tal-a28-v166 — In every way, O lord of the ray wheel, salutation to you; be gracious to me; by this the invitation thread should be given to the god.

tal-a28-v167 — The teacher should then give offering to yogini field mothers: five-cow-products, oblation, and tooth-stick, by disciples all around.

tal-a28-v168 — The teacher should sleep; rising early, do the daily rite; then, having worshipped the rite, gather the threads.

tal-a28-v169 — Join tooth-stick, clay, nurse, prosperous nurse, water, and ash in the five places.

tal-a28-v170 — Place the five purifiers in order in the front, right, west, up, and left mouths within Shiva's range.
