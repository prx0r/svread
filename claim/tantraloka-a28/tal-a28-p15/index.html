tal-a28-v141 — The period is four months, seven days, or three days from non-attainment; at its end, appease the god and dismiss mandala and so forth.

tal-a28-v142 — Fire and afterward circle sacrifice are to be done as previously stated: in month, in month, in four months, in year, or even thread offering.

tal-a28-v143 — In every way it is to be performed according to means; in absence of wealth, again to be done even with kusha bound.

tal-a28-v144 — When wealth is present, again deceit leads to disease and hell; in daily worships, fullness comes from festival worship completion.

tal-a28-v145 — Even there, fullness comes from thread worship; but if the thread is lost, the wise one should chant expiation.

tal-a28-v146 — Being well purified, one should act again according to the supreme lord's command; now the clear rite for the three-headed form is declared and written.

tal-a28-v147 — Here is said the Shaiva three-measure system, five-five-natured, with ten and eighteen distinctions, of six streams.

tal-a28-v148 — Those men who have fallen from vow, corrupt teacher and scriptures, and lack obligatory and occasional ritual joints.

tal-a28-v149 — Whether from unwillingness or willingness, those committing subtle sins have the thread made for their pacification in Shiva.

tal-a28-v150 — From the start of Shravana to the end of Kartika, in the bright fortnight that gives auspiciousness, not in the dark fortnight that gives sorrow, among doers, realms, kings and others.
