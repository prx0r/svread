tal-a28-v01 — Thus the obligatory rite has been declared; now the occasional rite is stated.

tal-a28-v10 — There we speak of the occasion rite: it is twofold, kula and akula; the first was declared in the glorious yoga course.

tal-a28-v02 — When the fixed future obligatory rite is established, primacy and identification follow; therefore all is occasional.

tal-a28-v08 — Scripture explanation, city residence, endings, ordered emergence, deity vision, dream command, and vow expiation are listed.

tal-a28-v09 — Thus occasional rites are determined in the glorious tantra essence in twenty-three divisions as the foundation of special worship.
