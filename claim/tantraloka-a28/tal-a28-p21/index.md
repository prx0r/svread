## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a28-v201** — The suitable means for this is the lineage of knowledge, operating through a stream of such and similar knowledge that gradually becomes clear.
**tal-a28-v202** — As what is of a different kind fades away, the goal is quickly attained; in this way, even a knowledge lineage long accustomed to beginningless worldly life is transformed.
**tal-a28-v203** — When that worldly lineage is destroyed, transcendent knowledge arises and takes on the character of another, noble lineage not accustomed to worldly life.
**tal-a28-v204** — The primary and original cause is the teacher's knowledge entering the self, clearly bestowing its own distinctive qualities.
**tal-a28-v205** — That knowledge is indeed a fitting material cause; for even when bodies differ, the difference and non-difference that apply to the bodily lineage do not apply in the same way to the lineage of knowledge.
**tal-a28-v206** — It is not the same as a yogi animating a corpse-body by will; when a yogi brings life to another body and so on, that does not create the same kind of continuity, since the yogi does not abandon his own body.
**tal-a28-v207** — Even if a yogi, without abandoning the body, becomes the basis for various knowledges, that does not change the fact that the lineage of knowledge is primary over the bodily lineage.
**tal-a28-v208** — Thus, when there is a mutual lineage of teachers established in the knowledge of Shiva, a threefold structure is recognized: a primary cause and an assisting cause.
**tal-a28-v209** — Thus the teacher's group is understood in terms of cause and effect: the teacher is the cause, and his wife is the assisting cause.
**tal-a28-v210** — Because one without power would not be eligible for ritual, the outer counterpart of the inner noble power of awareness is ordinarily required.
