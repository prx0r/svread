## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a28-v81** — The circle-women and other fitting partners in power-union will be explained later; worship joined with them becomes the circle-joined rite, which grants all fruits.

**tal-a28-v83** — Outside there are three enclosures in due order, extending up to the pledged initiates; or all may be arranged in rows, but in every case the teacher is always in the middle.
**tal-a28-v84** — Then, following the order of the circle, one should worship with his scent, incense, garland, touch, and garment, recognizing, 'This is that very circle.'
**tal-a28-v85** — In a one-spoked circle one should contemplate the single-hero rite; in a two-spoked circle the paired rite; and in others, such as a six-spoked circle, the triad and so forth.
**tal-a28-v86** — One should place six yoginīs in a sixfold form, a group of seven in a seven-spoked circle, octads in an eightfold form, or any other such group in a circle of corresponding shape.
**tal-a28-v87** — Then the wise one should first worship the circle in a vessel filled with liquid, placed on a support; satiation should never be done without a support.
**tal-a28-v88** — Without a support there is a lapse, the radiant powers are not pleased, and the vessel becomes like a ghost, even if it holds śākta nectar and liquor.
**tal-a28-v89** — There the power is the enjoyer, and that same reality is Śambhu, the supreme lord; thus one should meditate on the united triad of the individual, power, and Śiva.
**tal-a28-v90** — Then the satiation should be performed for each enclosure in order; by the method of reabsorption, one should then lead it back within.
