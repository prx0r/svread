**tal-a28-v341** — The body is remembered before because it was previously established and shining; therefore final remembering occurs in all-knowing mothers.
**tal-a28-v342** — Determination of another body is never accessible; clear memory of relatives, drinks, and so forth does not prove it.
**tal-a28-v343** — Memory is neither attached to another body nor final, because a latent impression will awaken at the end of the body.
**tal-a28-v344** — In bodily existence, clear experience arises from fitness, as the Purana tells through the thirst of a deer calf.
**tal-a28-v345** — Some sage dwelling in deer nature has that experience, but it is not a cause for next birth.
**tal-a28-v346** — His latent impression is the cause, like crow and palm; he remembers that very thing, as is always said.
**tal-a28-v347** — Shaped by that state, he remembers that very thing; thus I am, I will be, and that state is called becoming.
**tal-a28-v348** — Becoming of the future is imagined, never of what exists; imagination is declared as gradual making clear.
**tal-a28-v349** — Experience of the clear is not imagination; this is clear, as with a newborn child, animal, insect, or tree.
**tal-a28-v350** — Even in delusion, prior imagination then became clear, through awakening of impressions at the end of the deluded body.
