tal-a28-v241 — He goes to clear grace without mortal descent, and he who does his own task does not partially abandon that place.

tal-a28-v242 — As gauri practicing austerity in kashmir goes to a cave, or as narahari the lord is in meditation lift.

tal-a28-v243 — Leading vitasta and frightening proud demons, risen in saligrama, as vishnu or shiva of self-enjoying.

tal-a28-v244 — Practicing austerity in badari, nara and narayana, and such gods are established in their own task portions.

tal-a28-v245 — Worshiped gods quickly grant fitting fruit, and those established in their own task part who go to the end of the abode.

tal-a28-v246 — There, having enjoyed enjoyments, they descend among mortals; those descended become part-bearers and that again.

tal-a28-v247 — By initiation, knowledge, practice and sequence they go to shiva selfhood; immobile and mobile animals, beasts in this duality, are dead.

tal-a28-v248 — By purification of own action piercing that world, men and mere beasts taking variegation attain same world without discernment.

tal-a28-v249 — Non-discernment, special unfolding from dullness, and thus immobile beings take increasing state, or.

tal-a28-v250 — They do not attain direct rudra state; that gradually again, in swan and duck filled various tree thicket.
