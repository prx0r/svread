**tal-a28-v71** — In that consumer, O goddess, even hundreds of families of the giver are quickly released from the ocean of hell-torture.

**tal-a28-v75** — There the god is present with the goddess and attendants; therefore, one should first honor the teacher who is skilled in knowledge.
**tal-a28-v76** — One should perform the worship of his form; according to the Yogīśvarīmata, this is done during the thread offering, at śrāddha, and fully on festival days.
**tal-a28-v77** — It should also be done at solar and lunar eclipses, at worldly festivals and celebrations, at marriages and the like, and during the sacrificial rites of brahmins.
**tal-a28-v78** — The form-worship should be performed at initiation, consecration, purification of observances, and for the sake of a desired aim; it is of five kinds.
**tal-a28-v79** — The five types are the single, the paired, the mixed, the circle-joined, and the hero-mingled. The single is done by teachers alone, and the mixed is done by teachers together with others.
**tal-a28-v80** — The paired worship is done with practitioners and others together with their wives; it is of two kinds, either through union with one's wife or through union with a courtesan who has been brought by payment.
