## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a28-v271** — Even those initiated into a higher level, if they lack sufficient discernment, may reach only a lower end of it. Still, they partake of the fruit of that initiation.
**tal-a28-v272** — Such people do not abandon their faith in that initiation, even if it rests on scriptural authority. Lacking discernment, they hold to it on the strength of scripture and common repute.
**tal-a28-v273** — The Supreme Lord taught that one who is merely a bound soul attains the same world, the initiated attains nearness, and one who is devoted to that path attains union.
**tal-a28-v274** — If someone who follows the higher teaching abandons faith in it through doubt, then even when going to its abode he obtains no fruit at all.
**tal-a28-v275** — On this point the God of gods said that for such a person initiation, knowledge, and even sacred places are in vain, and so on, as explained in detail elsewhere.
**tal-a28-v276** — Even if someone is unfit at first, if he remains steadfast in the abode of Shiva, then through that enduring bond of faith he will later obtain that very fruit.
**tal-a28-v277** — Merit gained from places like rivers, mountains, and pools is not the highest thing at death. Those who die with that merit enjoy the pleasures of heaven.
**tal-a28-v278** — Those who had gained knowledge and discernment, but near death take refuge in lower abodes, become veiled there.
**tal-a28-v279** — This is described as a defilement of their knowledge and may indeed count as sin. The established view is that it is removed through the proper sequence of initiation into the lords of those respective realms.
**tal-a28-v280** — But those who, in mind and conduct, defile initiation, sacred abodes, and knowledge all go to hell.
