tal-a28-v261 — Of beasts this account; those initiated in that principle go to nearness to that lord by own fitness yoga.

tal-a28-v262 — Born from fitness dependence, whose tendency where only, he there only should be appointed, from city lord upward purity sharer.

tal-a28-v263 — Thus previously said auspicious; in auspicious svayambhuva also, who where desires enjoyments, he there only appointed.

tal-a28-v264 — Siddhi bearer, mantra power, etc., described elsewhere; those in principle knowledge, mantra, practice, etc.

tal-a28-v265 — When those knowers die, they attain union with that Rudra. Yet even after reaching that union, the form it takes varies according to their own latent impressions.

tal-a28-v266 — In this way, descents that come from a portion of that Rudra can be highly varied. This is widely reported in the Siddhanta scriptures, in the Puranas, and elsewhere.

tal-a28-v267 — Even when all are equally descents of Rudra, their actions and experiences differ. This is because the form of any being is made up of many different powers.

tal-a28-v268 — A being is not something apart from its powers; it exists only as their collection. Therefore, what descends as a portion from Rudra and the rest is a particular configuration of powers.

tal-a28-v269 — A portion descends so that the task suited to it can clearly be accomplished. The verse then turns to those who, after receiving initiation at a lower level, abandon their faith in it for something higher.

tal-a28-v270 — Those who die fixed in that lower level still attain its highest reach. But those who let go of that elevation attain what lies above it.
