tal-a28-v11 — Ocean moon and sage are thus for maheśi; first and fifteenth belong to brahma lineage, and two to kaumari.

tal-a28-v12 — Ocean protector moon belongs to vaiṣṇavi; thirteenth to aindri; two to vārāhi; and a pair to caṇḍi.

tal-a28-v13 — Two lunar days belong to all; the tenth belongs to yogeśi, and the eighth also belongs to her, so it is proclaimed as two days.

tal-a28-v14 — Other akula occasions are marked in reverse; we speak of them as kula occasion as stated in bhairava kula.

tal-a28-v15 — In haiḍara, in trika existence and trika kali kula etc., this breath-supported time was first declared in detail.

tal-a28-v16 — That produces some own consciousness in wheel-difference movement; the time of gaining fullness of own consciousness is called occasion.

tal-a28-v17 — Occasion means filling; or the word occasion has etymology from the root meaning filling, from that filling.

tal-a28-v18 — Here in haiḍara the word is heard twofold; those skilled in that wheel movement have full consciousness.

tal-a28-v19 — Those joined in that assembly are devoted to that worship always; even one not identified worships at that time through own ordered worship.

tal-a28-v20 — From that yogini and siddha assembly one becomes identified, as in a spectacle seer consciousness becomes nondifferent.
