## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a03-v14** — When the spread of unsurpassable-bliss runs in will and the rest, the letter-pair is e and oṃ.
**tsa-a03-v15** — Further still, from the union of unsurpassable-bliss, the pair ai and au.
**tsa-a03-v16** — This is action-power — and that very quad is e ai o au.
**tsa-a03-v17** — Then at the end of action-power everything stands as effect; as far as it will enter the unsurpassable, just so long the prior stands as the essence of consciousness, as mere light, as the point: am.
**tsa-a03-v18** — Then from that very place the visarga of the unsurpassable is born: aḥ.
**tsa-a03-v19** — Thus the sixteenfold seed-form of the contemplations is declared.
**tsa-a03-v20** — Born from them, expression-natured, the womb-form.
**tsa-a03-v21** — There from the unsurpassable comes the ka-class — of faith; the ca-class — of object-bearing will; the pair ṭa- and ta-classes — from awakening; the pa-class — from union with the five powers; hence fivefoldness.
**tsa-a03-v22** — Of threefold will, ya ra lā come from awakening, and the va-letter; of threefold will, śa ṣa sā come from the visarga, and the ha-letter; the kṣa-letter is born of the womb-union.
**tsa-a03-v23** — Thus this Lord is the unsurpassable himself, in the form of the clan-Lord.
**tsa-a03-v24** — His is one kaulic power of visarga, by which, from bliss-form onward to the end of outward creation, throbbing, the class-contemplations and the rest outward have attained the form of tattvas.
**tsa-a03-v25** — That visarga is threefold: āṇava, the form of mind's rest; śākta, marked by mind's awakening; śāṃbhava, the form of mind's dissolution.
**tsa-a03-v26** — Thus the visarga itself is the Lord's power in the birth of the world.
**tsa-a03-v27** — When contemplation is by undividedness alone, then the Lord is one as the seed-womb — part-wise in contemplation, the power-endowed and the power.
**tsa-a03-v28** — Separate in the eightfold contemplation, in union with the wheel-Lord, nine classes; in the prevalence of each single contemplation, fiftyfold.
**tsa-a03-v29** — Even there, in contemplation of a possibly differing part, eightyfold.
**tsa-a03-v30** — In reality there are only six contemplations; by spreading and returning, twelve — they nourish the fullness of the Supreme Lord's world-power.
**tsa-a03-v31** — Those very powers, being contemplation-formed, are the Lord's — Śrīkālikā they are named.
**tsa-a03-v32** — These too, pure contemplations that are power-formed, in pure knowledge by the para-apara form, from the mere contraction of māyā's awakening, share the nature of the knower and the known.
**tsa-a03-v33** — In māyā again, the crystal-clear divisions take māyic letter-nature — those who, attaining mundanity in paśyantī, madhyamā and vaikharī, stand up to the nature of external tattvas. When they are seen as body-like and revived by those pure contemplations — the life-replacing ones — they become vigorous, granting enjoyment and liberation. Thus, seeing oneself as merely the rest of all contemplations, as the reflection of all tattva-being-world divisions, by non-conceptuality, by the immersion of śāṃbhava — liberated while living.
**tsa-a03-v34** — Even here, as before, there is no obstruction of mantra and the like.
