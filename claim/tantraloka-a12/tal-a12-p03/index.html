tal-a12-v21 — Inside the world-prison it forges gross-post shackles; mantras are letter-natured, substance five-elemented.

tal-a12-v22 — The breathing kind which is consciousness-selfed — where is mixture there, how? With mixture absent, what is this doubt — even hers clearly baseless.

tal-a12-v23 — One should not doubt; thus doubt dissolves through disregard — in the Sarvācāra, Vīrālī, Niśācara, Krama and the rest.

tal-a12-v24 — And this is spread in the śāstras — there it is said: for from doubt arises weariness, from doubt being a vessel of obstacles.

tal-a12-v25 — And said Utpaladeva, the venerable guru of my guru: the all-doubt thunderbolt path — we bow to the Māheśvara path.

tal-a12-v26 — For attaining the anuttara state, this āṇava with means shown at great length — indeed, make it grow, O wise ones!
