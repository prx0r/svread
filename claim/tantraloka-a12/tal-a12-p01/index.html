tal-a12-v01 — Now the use of this path, our present subject, is set forth.

tal-a12-v10 — Unshaking contemplation of fullness brings firmness; and through inner-mutter practice, reflecting, one becomes a mantra-vessel.

tal-a12-v02 — Thus this whole path, as it rests in consciousness, through that rests also in void, intellect, breath, channel-wheels, and bodies.

tal-a12-v03 — And outside too, everywhere in liṅga, image, fire, altar and the rest; so it stands, whole and part-wise, in sequence and all at once.

tal-a12-v04 — Laid out from consciousness-principle to the outer, this path: at each level one should make one's own form shine, by own freedom, in fitting form.

tal-a12-v05 — And everything's form everywhere — even his does not not shine; for we cannot bear exclusion anywhere, even in dream.

tal-a12-v06 — Thus one should behold the body as filled with the universe-path, painted by time's activity — the abode of pulsation made of space and time.

tal-a12-v07 — So beheld, he is made of the deities within the universe; to be meditated, worshipped, libated — entered then, one is freed.

tal-a12-v08 — Thus pot, cloth, liṅga, altar, book, water — or whatever one may see anywhere: there one should reach identity-with-that.

tal-a12-v09 — Therein offering of things is held worship through non-difference; and contemplation of full-formedness is called meditation.
