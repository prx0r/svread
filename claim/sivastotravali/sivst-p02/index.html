[Stotra 5]

labdhabhavadbhāvaḥ svātmollāsamayaṃ jagat & paśyan bhaktirasābhogair bhaveyam aviyojitaḥ ākāmkṣaṇīyam aparaṃ yena nātha na vidyate & tava tenādvitīyasya yuktaṃ yat paripūrṇatā hasyate nṛtyate yatra tathā śarīraceṣṭāsu karaṇaracitāsu & sarvatra sarvadā me puraḥsaro bhavatu bhaktirasaḥ śivaśivaśiveti nāmani tava niravadhi nātha japyamāne 'smin & āsvādayan bhaveyaṃ kam api mahārasam apunaruktam
