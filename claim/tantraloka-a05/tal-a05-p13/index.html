tal-a05-v121 — The beautiful lingam, heart of the yogini, gives birth to some consciousness through the union of seed and womb.

tal-a05-v122 — Here, without effort, all deities dwell in the bliss-filled abode, being of eternally arisen consciousness.

tal-a05-v123 — Here shines the difficult power of Bhairava, with contraction and expansion, and without contraction and expansion.

tal-a05-v124 — Sprinkled by the bliss stream of this lingam's union and emission, the very true universe always newly arises.

tal-a05-v125 — Here the supreme means is described by sameness alone; even when lamps blaze, why would the sun not shine?

tal-a05-v126 — In objects, in their enjoyment, and in pain or pleasure arising from them, even without entering them, minds absorbed know the inner joy of activity ceasing.

tal-a05-v127 — In the true self, in consciousness greatness, in mind, and in such service, the yogi stands clearly, and that reality full of rays is to be perceived.

tal-a05-v128 — Thus the recitation method is declared; the instrument is examined, and that was spoken by the supreme lord in the three-headed scripture.

tal-a05-v129 — By grasped, grasper, consciousness, pervasion, abandonment, impulsion, and placement, the instrument is called sevenfold practice preceded by awareness.

tal-a05-v130 — In impulsion preceded by its pervasion, the instrument's self-establishment is to be understood from the guru's mouth, although the instrument is clear.
