tal-a05-v71 — In shakti agitation, clan entry, pervasion at tips of all channels, and all-self contraction, the wise one enters the heart.

tal-a05-v72 — From mutual friction of moon and sun phase nets, in the fire-moon abode, emission bliss opens.

tal-a05-v73 — Enough of secret talk; this is hidden by its own nature. Resting there in the yogini-heart, the wise one becomes accomplished.

tal-a05-v74 — Grown familiar in the conduct of rejecting, accepting, and disregarding, one sees the universe from non-dual conduct, out of consciousness-relish.

tal-a05-v75 — Abandoning prayer born of neediness in outer and inner self, reaching cessation-bliss in sky-form, one resorts to the blossomed sound-stage.

tal-a05-v76 — With the mouth within, rightly bloom consciousness forth; the lofty imperishable wheel of consciousness, non-different from the knowable, becomes thence.

tal-a05-v77 — That knowable is dissolved away by the consciousness-named fire; dissolved, it dissolves in the power-fire of the triangle here.

tal-a05-v78 — There, well at peace in noble perception-drop-being, rested in the withdrawal seed, the yogi becomes supreme-measured.

tal-a05-v79 — In inner and outer both, beautiful beyond common and uncommon, the self of consciousness-throb and three powers holds contraction and expansion.

tal-a05-v80 — Even uncontracted expansion, from its shining forth thus, aimed within with outward gaze, reaches the supreme state.
