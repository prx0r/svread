tal-a05-v61 — Here rest in the supreme, through all-filling union, is established as world-entry by that full activity alone.

tal-a05-v62 — In supreme reflection free from prior activity, let the self-rest of light be seen as I-am.

tal-a05-v63 — The supreme I-maker of consciousness reflection first shines; then, by effort, he becomes the twelve-part self.

tal-a05-v64 — Let the sun illuminate being, then fill and contemplate; then the sixteen-part moon slowly devours emission.

tal-a05-v65 — The reviving nectar is emitted into consciousness fire, throbbing through the subtle channel of will, knowledge, and action powers.

tal-a05-v66 — That divine nectar, satisfying among consciousness goddesses, is emission nectar thus far in the fire of offering called consciousness.

tal-a05-v67 — If all becomes emitted and offered, the sixfold path circle arises; because of the supreme lord, emission and clan leader agitation expand from ka to ha as reality path.

tal-a05-v68 — Thus aṃ, accompanied by the clan mistress, indeed makes clan lordship supreme; the world is called that which is filled by emission separation.

tal-a05-v69 — Thus worship, offer, and meditate upon this reviving phase filled with consciousness, breath, quality, body, and outer substance.

tal-a05-v70 — Situated with two bliss channels pulsing attentively, he approaches this ground of emission flow and palace.
