tal-a05-v21 — The principle-knower sees within the heart a banana-sheath-shaped form with outer, inner, and middle, like a flower.

tal-a05-v22 — There one should meditate with unwavering mind on the conjunction of moon, sun, and fire; that meditation agitation makes great Bhairava consume offerings.

tal-a05-v23 — Blazing in the great heart-basin, fullness would arise for that power-possessor with expanded power and Bhairava radiance.

tal-a05-v24 — One should contemplate knower, knowing, and known by abode non-difference; the fire, sun, and moon powers become that same triad.

tal-a05-v25 — The supreme, supreme-and-lower, and lower goddesses are always arisen; through creation, preservation, and dissolution they are individually threefold.

tal-a05-v26 — The fourth unlimited form of them is unimagined; thus twelve goddesses are situated like sun discs.

tal-a05-v27 — Of each of them, fire, sun, moon, and their peace-radiance is this supreme wheel, from heart and eyes and so on.

tal-a05-v28 — It goes indeed through voids in each object domain; by that wheel radiance there in meaning, by creation, preservation, and dissolution order.

tal-a05-v29 — The moon-sun-fire radiance-nature form stands together; thus in sound and other objects, by the ear and other void paths.

tal-a05-v30 — By this falling wheel one should contemplate identity; by this ordered union, wherever that falls.
