tal-a05-v51 — Unstruck awareness, strengthened by supreme nectar, is where meditations have no primary connection.

tal-a05-v52 — That world-bliss Shambhu gives us; there rest is to be taken by heart-utterance.

tal-a05-v53 — That right rest there is the supreme abiding, even in heart and other one-natured places, in one's own abode.

tal-a05-v54 — The sixfold breath-born form is then called pervasion by use of the breath staff, equalizing prior and later.

tal-a05-v55 — Dwell in the four-petaled lotus hanging palace; having crossed the trident ground, reach the three-channel-joined state.

tal-a05-v56 — When will, knowledge, action, and power are equal, the wise one enters one expanding, more-contracted, radiant state.

tal-a05-v57 — Dwell on the brow-point sound-end power staircase; there on the upward coiled ground a beautiful throb arises.

tal-a05-v58 — Emission rests there in the fish-belly state, as a she-donkey or mare rests in its own abode, a temple of bliss.

tal-a05-v59 — Entering the expansion-contraction in the heart, one rejoices in the momentary full crowd of absorbed and emitted states.

tal-a05-v60 — Dwell in the expansion-contraction-rooted Bhairava couple, the unified great root trident emission, in the heart.
