tal-a05-v101 — For one desiring distinction, bliss arises before touching fullness; even from that, lightning-strike-like, body-abandoned.

tal-a05-v102 — In the abode, for a moment, from immersion arises clear flooding, like water and dust, from practiced consciousness-body unity loss.

tal-a05-v103 — From own force entry and body looseness one obtains trembling; when dissolved, in certainty of body identity within enjoyment.

tal-a05-v104 — One sleeps before rooted in consciousness-self; then rooted in true state, consciousness as world-self.

tal-a05-v105 — Knowing, one reels; reeling is great pervasion, remembered; in self nonself conception, indeed true in nonself.

tal-a05-v106 — Self-conceit in body etc is bondage; liberation however is its dissolution; in beginning nonself selfhood, dissolved, obtained in own self.

tal-a05-v107 — In self nonself destruction great pervasion proceeds; bliss, arising, trembling, sleep, and reeling are fivefold.

tal-a05-v108 — Thus said teaching indeed in holy Malinivijayottara, in this shown bliss etc fivefold, when.

tal-a05-v109 — The yogi sometimes forcefully enters the lordship of each wheel, as the body, though pervaded by consciousness, is governed by the all-lord.

tal-a05-v110 — Just as the eye can know form, so entry into a wheel somewhere can make someone or something arise.
