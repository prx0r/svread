tal-a05-v31 — The all-natured wheel, like each all-earthly king, thus dissolves the world path layer effortlessly.

tal-a05-v32 — In the Bhairava great wheel surrounded by consciousness, then by impression only, even the world is destroyed.

tal-a05-v33 — By own self rising, one should meditate the great revolving wheel; then by that burnable dissolution and that impression destruction.

tal-a05-v34 — One should contemplate the calming wheel; then peaceful, then peace; by this meditation union the world dissolves in the wheel.

tal-a05-v35 — In that consciousness, then consciousness-dissolved meaning alone shines; by consciousness nature, then again creation, which is conscious great lady.

tal-a05-v36 — Thus every moment, dissolving and emitting the world in own consciousness, then again one should go to perpetual Bhairava state.

tal-a05-v37 — Thus, from trishula onward, by the four-five-ara sequence up to fifty ara, the yogi should visualize the wheel.

tal-a05-v38 — Or the sixty-four-hundred-ara wheel, or the thousand-ara wheel, or the countless-thousand-ara wheel, the one of undivided mind should meditate.

tal-a05-v39 — Of the great lord of consciousness, of the god of arising consciousness, there is no calculation at all of the great lord of world-power.

tal-a05-v40 — His powers are the whole world; the power-holder is the great lord. Thus, in the auspicious scripture, Sri Srikantha explained.
