## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tsa-a17-v01** — For those standing in scriptures from the Vaiṣṇavas to the southern tantras at the end, or vow-takers thereof — and even highest-scripture-standers serving unentitled lower-scripture gurus: when they are turned hither by the Lord's descent of power, then this is their rule. Therein, let the fast-observed one on the next day — worshipped with the common mantra, his doings told — be led in before the Lord; there, having taken his vow, let him cast it in water; then let him bathe; then, having sprinkled, having purified with gruel and tooth-stick, having led him blindfolded in, by the common mantra let him have the Lord's worship performed.
**tsa-a17-v02** — Then, in fire made Śiva by the common mantra, let him purify the vow; having casketed his name in his mantra, 'I purify the expiation,' ending in svāhā, let him offer a hundred times.
**tsa-a17-v03** — Then too the full oblation, ending in vauṣaṭ.
**tsa-a17-v04** — Then, having invoked the Vow-lord, having worshipped, by Śiva's order — 'be thou his no-harm-doer' — having announced it, having satisfied him, having dismissed him, let him dismiss the fire. Thus the liṅga-lifting.
**tsa-a17-v05** — Then his consecration-dwelling and the rest, as before.
