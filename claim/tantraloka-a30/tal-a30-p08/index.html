tal-a30-v71 — Composed of the hamsa great mantra, eternal you; expecting auspicious and inauspicious; situated in the middle of the mandala; cause of the great bridge of shakti, great meaning.

tal-a30-v72 — Situated in both lotuses, come to awakening, divine body; this noble statement along with rudra-count words.

tal-a30-v73 — From ignorance you are bound; awaken and rise, O god.

tal-a30-v74 — This fifth half-verse has seven words; go to the palate-named end, the bronze-struck great gate.

tal-a30-v75 — Having reached, proceed, ho ho, or to Vamadeva station; this sixth sentence has fourteen words.

tal-a30-v76 — O lord of knots, supreme self, peaceful one, having reached the great palate hole transcend; O lord of body, quickly go to stainless Shiva station.

tal-a30-v77 — The seventh sentence may have fourteen words; the reading in the exhalation teaching says you are wind.

tal-a30-v78 — Having traversed the middle path, gathered prana and apana, and abandoned merit and demerit, Narayana, go to the peaceful end.

tal-a30-v79 — This eighth sentence has nine words: O Brahman, O Vishnu, O Rudra, you are Shiva, you are Vasudeva.

tal-a30-v80 — Destroy the eternal clay lump of Agni-Soma, O great space; this sentence is proclaimed counted by worlds.
