tal-a30-v11 — The rati crest mantra of this has a face limb with short and long vowels: fire, prana, fire-destruction, time, indra, water, wind.

tal-a30-v12 — With sixth vowel, bindu, moon, and so forth, the nine self's pervasion through bindu, nada, and so on is held in the triadic head doctrine.

tal-a30-v13 — The procedure of throwing over, overcoming, awakening and establishing consciousness is thus named: that consciousness, that attainment.

tal-a30-v14 — Such is the great pervasion, proclaimed as embodied here; transformation and its dissolution are called salutation.

tal-a30-v15 — The three-syllable mantra, joined below with six long vowels, yields the six limbs beginning with the Heart; construct its faces likewise.

tal-a30-v16 — With destruction-sound seeds blazing and bindu-adorned, with jha as the breath of withdrawal, and sa with sixth vowel and drop.

tal-a30-v17 — This is the abode of Bhairava adorned with half-moon and the rest; the matrka and malini mantras were proclaimed before.

tal-a30-v18 — Then om, the name in fourth-case ending, obeisance thus in order; among Ganesha and the rest the seed is mantra where none is declared.

tal-a30-v19 — The name's first letter illumined with a, bindu and moon is the seed of all seeds, joined with fourteen and sixty.

tal-a30-v20 — Of the triad beginning with the invoked Ghora, seeds arise in order: visarga-bearing maya, hum, and ha with visarga.
