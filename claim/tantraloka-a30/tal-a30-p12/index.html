tal-a30-v111 — At the three letters' end the four semivowels ending in ta; first-of-class and horse with triangle-bindu joined, from three letters after pa.

tal-a30-v112 — The great-golden-sound seven-syllable mantra of the goddess, addressed forgive-thus in three syllables, ending sin.

tal-a30-v113 — The six-syllabled deluding word beginning with the sinful word; then kill, shake off, in twice-twice-ten syllables, such is the word.

tal-a30-v114 — By Rudra-Sakti power the six-letter form ending in fifth case; then the one syllable declared, the visarga-brahman.

tal-a30-v115 — In union with that act recite: hole-ocean letters, this vidya called initiation-knowledge.

tal-a30-v116 — Maya-syllable in supreme brahman with four vidyas, three feet; eight syllables then five, named yoga-bearer.

tal-a30-v117 — A ten-syllabled form ending in e, situated in knowledge and made of three words, is proclaimed as the form of self, inner self, and supreme self.

tal-a30-v118 — May it be, O beloved of Rudra, that this veda-syllable Rudra-shakti burns my sin; this formula is twelve-syllabled and four-footed.

tal-a30-v119 — O gentle Sadashiva, this convention-removing formula contains a pair and six, brings bindu and arrow, and has four half-syllables.

tal-a30-v120 — This knowledge, counted as half-syllable, kha, and arrow, is the supreme lady; glorious Bhutiraja described this triad of knowledges.
