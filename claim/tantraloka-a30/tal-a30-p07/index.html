tal-a30-v61 — Contract the fingers, then stimulate; crossing from knee and other chakras, contract from chakra to chakra.

tal-a30-v62 — The secret teaching giving immediate liberation is supreme; now brahma-knowledge is stated, giving immediate conviction.

tal-a30-v63 — Shiva, Sri Bhutiraja, taught us when death of all beings is imminent.

tal-a30-v65 — It immediately removes death-pain born from prana severance; hearing it, even one overcome by great delusion gradually attains.

tal-a30-v66 — Awakening, the speaker's presence approaches swiftly by itself; from the supreme state you have come here; eternal you, abandon the body-end.

tal-a30-v67 — From toe etc, lord, binding; indeed fierce bond; this noble statement first becomes by world-named words.

tal-a30-v68 — At ankle, knee-placed, collarbone-placed binding; likewise at groin abandon the foremost middle city; from heart-lotus you arise.

tal-a30-v69 — By these many words this noble statement is second; Hamsa, Hayagriva, lord, you are supreme Sadashiva, called Jiva.

tal-a30-v70 — Sun-moon-fire-group-drop-body, hahaha, transcend; third noble statement, by previous counts plus one words.
