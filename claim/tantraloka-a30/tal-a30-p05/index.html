tal-a30-v41 — This radiant eye, common to all, is remembered; tara, shlim, beast, hum, and phat form that weapon of rasa color.

tal-a30-v42 — With long lara taksha vaya joined with bindu, indra and others have those weapons; short forms belong to vishnu and prajapati.

tal-a30-v43 — In memory, by fourth and second short forms in the lotus wheel, the classes nama, svaha, vaushat, hum, vashat, and phat arise.

tal-a30-v44 — In the six limbs in order, those classes are of that nature in rites, in recitation, offering, nourishing, agitating, and pacifying.

tal-a30-v45 — In hostile rites, for mantras, the classes from namaskara onward are second, going to eye and six sage groups with bindu.

tal-a30-v46 — By yoni syllable, the seats of mothers, with time drawing, are approved, whether omitted at beginning or end.

tal-a30-v47 — Life, standing inside the prana fold, with radiance equal to time fire, is very radiant and adorned with the left foot on the head with bindu.

tal-a30-v48 — This form following the right knee, worshipped by all mother groups, grants desired fruit to all living beings.

tal-a30-v49 — This supreme reality belongs to the mothers and to Bhairava, so the mantra practitioner who desires supreme accomplishment should recite it.

tal-a30-v50 — Rudra power entry is always established here, because this supreme power is declared as distinct from another.
