tal-a30-v01 — Now the fitting cluster of mantras is explained in order, for the accomplishment of those not yet risen to reflection.

tal-a30-v10 — Taking from support power to trident peak, one should worship with fire, air, earth, water, sixth vowel, and bindu.

tal-a30-v02 — Indeed those awakened mantras, whose nature is reflection alone, are endowed with agency from the freedom of the free consciousness-light.

tal-a30-v03 — The mantras that enter the teacher, from established identity, make him free, and the instruments too attain agency.

tal-a30-v04 — In the support power, earth and so forth, they say the ordered four-letter set is hrim, kshlam, kshvim, vam, ksham.

tal-a30-v05 — Ham in the channel, yam, ram, lam, vam in the duty fourfold, and rim, rrim, lim, llim in the fourfold, arise in reverse order.

tal-a30-v06 — Om, aum, this threefold in knowledge, maya, and kala; anusvara and visarga belong to knowledge, lord, and supreme principle.

tal-a30-v07 — The letters from ka to bha are in the filaments; prana with eight vowels and bindu is in the eight petals; one's own name is illumined.

tal-a30-v08 — For the nine powers, sha-sha-sa with bindu are in three circles; kshmam is in preta, and jram is assigned to trident peaks.

tal-a30-v09 — In separate seat worship these mantras are remembered in order; in brief worship, the first to last seed syllable is used.
