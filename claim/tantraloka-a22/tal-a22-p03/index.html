tal-a22-v21 — Of them, from the middle, one mantra to him should bestow: he too, day and night, indeed it, should mutter, little-eating, fasting.

tal-a22-v22 — Mantra to him, having bestowed, then, in the rite-purified fire, libated that mantra, vow-purity he should perform.

tal-a22-v23 — With the worshipped alone, mantra, having made name — his, enclosure: expiation, 'I cleanse' — through phaṭ-svāhā-ūha practice.

tal-a22-v24 — A hundred, a thousand, or, having offered, again the full oblation, likewise, through use vauṣaḍ-ending, and having cast, having invoked Vrateśvara.

tal-a22-v25 — 'Tāra, to Vrateśvara' — quoted; 'homage!' — and him should worship: and should proclaim, by you, not his to be done, anything by Śiva's command.

tal-a22-v26 — Then Vrateśvara to be libated, with svāhā-ending: and then he, having asked pardon, to be dismissed, should be; then of fire too, dismissal.

tal-a22-v27 — That proclamation, and, to the god: 'forgive!' — quoted, is dismissal; then on the third day, as before, all the rite is remembered.

tal-a22-v28 — Eve-rite-and-the-rest, ending in own-desired-initiation-rite: of the former mark-bearers, liberation-initiation, devoid of qualification.

tal-a22-v29 — On the adept-teacher path, not fit are they, the reborn ones; even of the reborn, kindled by knowledge, they become the abode of guruhood.

tal-a22-v30 — For liberation alone, not for enjoyment; for enjoyment too, through means: thus said in his own Paddhati — Īśānaśiva the teacher.
