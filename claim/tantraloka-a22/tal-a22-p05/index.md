## Reading (machine draft, verse-mapped)

**tal-a22-v41** — In the Bhairavīya sixty-four: those beasts should be initiated into Trika; in the Siddhavīrāvalī-essence, in the Bhairavīya, in the Kula too.
**tal-a22-v42** — Taken up through the five-initiation-sequence, initiation called highest, hence everyone low-placed, even, by liṅga-lifting is graced.
**tal-a22-v43** — Whoever, from heart-dwelling-Maheśāna impulsion, vast scripture-knowledge should seek, he too, should go to many gurus.
**tal-a22-v44** — Their initiations also, should take, with-consecration-not-last: knowledge-strengthened, they, for by those knowing ones performed.
**tal-a22-v45** — Told, and, in the venerable scripture, there, there, and, muchly, delight-seeking, as the bee roams from flower to flower.
**tal-a22-v46** — Knowledge-seeking, so the disciple, from the guru: to another guru, quoted: among many gurus, for whence knowledge is highest.
**tal-a22-v47** — Attained, he, his guru; initiation, not here, is primary, for in consciousness, the all-knowledge-treasure: but having reached the guru, well-established.
**tal-a22-v48** — Him alone may the wise worship, eager with that inquiry into that: thus the initiation-rule, told, liṅga-lifting-crowned.
