tal-a22-v31 — Of the venerable Devī, rightly proclaiming the truth of the Yāmalī saying, of the guru, even of one sunk in downward gaze: this rite — perform.

tal-a22-v32 — Having performed, the secret one should tell, not otherwise, in the Kāmika, indeed: though initiated in another tantra, the secret not disclose.

tal-a22-v33 — Standing free, yet having approached an ignorant guru, being unassured there after: there too, the rite he should perform.

tal-a22-v34 — Come through an ignorant teacher's mouth, powerless — mantra, this: which muttered, he as guru — even here: not from authority-told defect.

tal-a22-v35 — Then his purity, first having made, then initiation he should perform: by one established in a lower view, formerly initiated.

tal-a22-v36 — Through intense śakti, afterwards, when he should approach the true guru: then too, his, of the child indeed, purity having made, the true guru.

tal-a22-v37 — Initiation-and-the-rest rite, entire, should do as toldly enjoined: attained even, the true guru, fitness-state of his, not knows, if.

tal-a22-v38 — In knowledge-giving, that, the disciple, fitness, should show his own: in every way, but non-speaking, this one; speaking, or contrary.

tal-a22-v39 — Ignorant, in reality, just: having abandoned that, so the rite he should perform; not concealment-doubt here is to be made, by the intelligent.

tal-a22-v40 — Lower-touching: concealment, not higher-means-discernment, in the Siddhānta, initiated in the tantra of ten-and-eight-division.
