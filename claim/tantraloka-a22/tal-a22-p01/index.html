tal-a22-v01 — Now the so-called liṅga-lifting we speak: solely enjoined in the Śiva-teaching.

tal-a22-v10 — And the sequence: śakti-descent, impurity-loss, departure-desire, initiation, awakening, abandonable-loss, acceptable-dissolution-nature.

tal-a22-v02 — Told in the venerable Mālinītantra, indeed: the earth-upholding — having declared, who is yoked where, he from it does not turn back.

tal-a22-v03 — Arisen from fitness, whose just where the teaching: he just there is to be appointed; at initiation-time, then, but this one.

tal-a22-v04 — Fruit, all, having attained, in Śiva: though yoked, released; though unyoked, upper purification, having attained from Bhuvaneśa.

tal-a22-v05 — Purified, he comes to Śiva-hood, burnt-saṃsāra-bonded; having declared man-upholding — and told, this Vedāntic, by me.

tal-a22-v06 — To Kapila formerly told, in the first chapter, so: by this, through sequential yoga, attained — the highest state.

tal-a22-v07 — Not again to beast-hood goes: in the pure own self he stands; hence, for, is hinted the meaning — this, even for those below the Śiva-principle.

tal-a22-v08 — In the principles, of the yoked there is re-extractability: of the giver of all-śāstra-taught thing-distinctions.

tal-a22-v09 — Of the Śiva-āgama is distinction than all — even — āgamas; and through Śiva-knowledge, without, again even beast-arising.
