tsa-a09-v17 — In that much — the complete's knower-nature from the raised passion-and-rest curtain — the complete's fifteenfoldness too thus, from its that-much knowability.

tsa-a09-v18 — Spread, this is settled in the Tantrāloka.

tsa-a09-v19 — From the male up to the segment-tattva: thirteenfold.

tsa-a09-v20 — Complete's there, by union with knower-nature, from suppression of its two differences power/power-bearing-self: thus complete's own-nature merely; the dissolution-portion's in own-nature, five's in knower-nature: eleven differences.

tsa-a09-v21 — The knowledge-portion's in own-nature, four's in knower-nature: nine differences.

tsa-a09-v22 — Mantra's in own-nature, three's in knower-nature: seven.

tsa-a09-v23 — The mantra-Lord's in own-nature, two's in knower-nature: five.

tsa-a09-v24 — The mantra-great-Lord's in own-nature, the Lord's one alone in knower-nature, from power-power-bearing-difference: three.

tsa-a09-v25 — Śiva's, however — dependent on light-one-consciousness-autonomy: no difference at all, from fullness.

tsa-a09-v26 — Thus tattva-difference itself is established in the Supreme-Lord's unsurpassable-policy-one-name, and makes the variegation of world-difference: of hell, heaven and rudra-worlds, earthness being equal too, from the farther nature-difference being said.

tsa-a09-v27 — And here, by mutual construction of difference, curious for inner-difference-knowledge: let him determine the Tantrāloka alone.

tsa-a09-v28 — Thus by following each pot and the rest too, the difference of earth-and-rest tattvas is established.

tsa-a09-v29 — Now the whole earth-tattva, targeted as knower-known-form, is established: what by earth-tattva-non-difference is light — that is Śiva.

tsa-a09-v30 — As the Vedic text: in earth this very brahman.

tsa-a09-v31 — It impels the giving of earth-tattva-attainment: the earth-mantra-great-Lord; impelled, the earth-mantra-Lord; his expressive mantra made of imagined-image-nature, by the order of Sāṃkhya-and-rest bonded-knowledge-crossed-Śiva-knowledge, the practiced earth-yoga not reaching the stable stage: the earth-knowledge-portion.

tsa-a09-v32 — By bonded-knowledge-order the practiced earth-yoga: at cosmic-end or in death, earth-dissolution-alone.

tsa-a09-v33 — In deep sleep, from tattva-immersion alone, varied dream would arise; the earth-identity-grasped, however: earth-complete.

tsa-a09-v34 — Even here by power-raising-absence-nature: fourteenfold — thus the differences of the earth-tattva having attained knower-nature; own-nature, however, pure knowable — thus elsewhere-here-in-even.

tsa-a09-v35 — Now in one knower, by being established in breath, the difference-determination: here the blue-grasping one, breath, sixteen-segment-self, rises up to knowable-immersion. There the first segment indivisible-one-form; the second knower-exultation-form; the last knowable-identical pervaded; the penultimate manifested-knower-form; in the middle, the twelve-segment — from among them the first six-group non-conceptual-natured, covering-concept sixness: as its own nature one segment; in the covering-concept five-form-nature, dawning-willingness, already-dawned — she two-segment-bodied from pulsation's absence-of-one-instant-form, own-work-agent-nature — thus from the covering-concept's fivefoldness, and from own-nature six non-conceptual instants; then from non-conceptual's dissolution-likeness: dissolution of concept, dawning-willingness, two-segment-bodied already-dawned: six segments.
