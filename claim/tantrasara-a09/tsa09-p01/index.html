tsa-a09-v01 — That, sevenfold, the six-and-half-fold scripture is taught by the Supreme Lord alone.

tsa-a09-v10 — She alone, desiring the will-power-form, autonomy-natured, of the mantra-great-Lord.

tsa-a09-v11 — Will-natured, distinct-autonomy-natured, of Śiva — thus the power-differences: seven primary.

tsa-a09-v12 — And the difference in the power-bearing knower-objects is made by its tainting: instrument-difference ends in agent-difference; and from power alone — non-separate — being able to act; not of another, from the fallacy of infinite regress and the rest.

tsa-a09-v13 — In reality, however, one alone — consciousness-autonomy-bliss-resting knower; there earth, resting in own-nature-merely, when known, its own nature alone shines: seen by the mind's eye, known by the mind, I know — there the other own-nature made by complete-power, made by power-bearing-form, shines too; thus up to Śiva also is to be said: world established in Śiva-power, resting in Śiva-nature, I know — from the conviction being of a different kind.

tsa-a09-v14 — But if the state's knowability is its own body, then knowability toward all, knowability of the knowable too — infinite regress; by that, the world's blindness well-luminous; by that, the union of opposite qualities in knowable-unknowable: fault — so it is said here.

tsa-a09-v15 — Not that own body: from own-nature being separately said; but what? In knower-power and in the knower the rest-receptacle, the form — that self-luminous itself shines, not toward anything at all: thus omniscience; regress and opposite-quality-union — far removed.

tsa-a09-v16 — Known by infinite knower-objects too: one alone, its form in those, from the one-shining-form nature: no obstruction by another knower's knowing-inference; and that its form is real, from doing work for meaning; like seeing a beloved seen by another becoming jealous: seeing Śiva-nature, the rest-pot, one enters completely; seeing reality resting in all infinite knower-objects one becomes full, like watching a dancer; her blue's form resting in the knower — from the arising of self-luminous contemplation: thus the fifteenfold nature of earth up to the principal tattva.

tsa-a09-v02 — There: Śivas, mantra-great-Lords, mantra-Lords, mantras, knowledge-portions, dissolution-portions, complete — seven power-bearing.

tsa-a09-v03 — Of these seven powers, by their difference up to the earth-and-rest principal tattva: each its own form by fourteen differences — fifteenfold.

tsa-a09-v04 — There: own form fit for knowability, self-established, by the grace of Aparābhaṭṭārikā — in the power-raised knower-objects the rest-receptacle: that is the śākta form itself, by the grace of Śrīmatparāparā; and that too is sevenfold, from the powers being as many.

tsa-a09-v05 — In the power-bearing-form-principal, however, what rests in the knower-class: that power-bearing Siva-form, by the grace of Śrīmatparābhaṭṭārikā — sevenfold too.

tsa-a09-v06 — From Śiva to the complete-limit — from those many being said.

tsa-a09-v07 — There the difference of knower-objects is from power-difference alone; and for clarification it is said by the order of complete and the rest: in complete, knowledge-portion, power — from being its specific form (the mind-, action- and instrument-powers); of the dissolution-portion, those same from objectlessness, indistinct.

tsa-a09-v08 — Of the knowledge-portion, those same, in the dissolving-portion, helper of its impression, awakening — the pure knowledge of mantra.

tsa-a09-v09 — Devoid of that impression: she alone awakened, of the mantra-Lord.
