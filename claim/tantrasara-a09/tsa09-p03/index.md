**tsa-a09-v36** — Own-work-agent-nature is knower-form-nature — said; she is not counted further — thus the wise-wealthed, practising the teacher's instruction, distinguish fifteenfoldness everywhere by division.
**tsa-a09-v37** — In concept-lack: segment-lack, in bliss-and-rest-knowing — like, as far as non-conceptualness alone.
**tsa-a09-v38** — The worlds too, by concept-rest, consider that ego-made, the spread-of-this-being-concept-covered, non-conceptual contemplation-abode unlit — like a suffering-state resting in bliss; but by concept-decrease she shines indeed — thus this attentiveness in the relation of knowable and knower: the Abhinavagupta-teachers.
**tsa-a09-v39** — Thus in fifteenfold standing, as far as the lack of the distinct this-ness-self's difference, so much pair-pair diminishes; as far as two-segment Śiva-immersion: there the first segment everywhere complete; the second pervaded-by-all-knowledge-instruments being-practised, fit for omniscience-omnipotence — not the first.
**tsa-a09-v40** — As Śrīkallaṭa says: segment-fall — here the fall-word: she alone the Goddess Śrīmatkālī, mother-being-nature, Bhairava, intuition — enough, by secret-and-unsecret.
**tsa-a09-v41** — Thus from the mantra-great-Lord-segment onward, from the practice of that-and-that, attainment of that-and-that.
**tsa-a09-v42** — Now here the waking-and-rest states are established: the variegation of the knowable and its object and consciousness — mutually-relative being — that is a state: not of the knowable-merely, nor of consciousness-merely, nor two separate.
**tsa-a09-v43** — There when, by object-rulership, by external-form-nature, shining: waking-state, in-knowable, in-mother, in-measure.
**tsa-a09-v44** — When, however, right there by substratum-form-nature shining — will: dream-state.
**tsa-a09-v45** — When, however, right there by ruler-form-nature shining, by seed-self-nature alone: deep-sleep-state.
**tsa-a09-v46** — These three known-instrument-knower-states, each by the waking-and-rest difference: fourfold said.
**tsa-a09-v47** — When, however, in the very knower-rest-gone, from the knower's fullness-turning, by its door fullness-turning shining: fourth-state; she form I-see — of this kind the triad-of-portions crossed, I-see-thus: unmediated knower-nature, autonomy-essence, not by sameness-middleness-farness gives the anointing of knower-instrument-known-ness; from serving the three states: threefold.

**tsa-a09-v49** — And the corresponding-sense is shown here in the Tantrāloka and the Ślokavārttika.
