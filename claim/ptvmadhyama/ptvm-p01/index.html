ptvm-p01-v01 — O Lord, what is the Unsurpassable that grants Kaula fruit at once — known by a mere touch of knowing, rising to kinship with the Sky-Goer?

ptvm-p01-v02 — Tell me this secret of secrets, Lord — the Power that dwells in the heart, Kaulikī, mistress of the clan.

ptvm-p01-v03 — Tell me of her, Lord of gods, by whom I come to fullness. — Hear, blessed Goddess: even the answer has one beyond it — the Unsurpassable.

ptvm-p01-v04 — This Kaula rite, O Goddess, standing in my heart-sky — I tell it, O lady of gods: it gives swift Kaula fruit.

ptvm-p01-v05 — First now: all lunar days, vowels ending in the drop; within them, by time's conjoining, moon and sun are named.

ptvm-p01-v06 — Realities from earth to person, in five groups in order — in ka-series, in sounds, ending with the ma-sound, O fair-vowed one.

ptvm-p01-v07 — Fourfold the holds of wind, fire, water and their lords; above them, śa beginning the famed row, and before, the five Brahmas.

ptvm-p01-v08 — Rootless, rightly ranged, to be known, ending in kṣa — this forthgoing declared; of all mantras indeed, and of wisdoms, O glorious one.

ptvm-p01-v09 — This is the womb, told in all Tantras in every way — joined with the fourteen, O lucky one, joined with the tithi-lord's end; the third godhead, O fair-hipped, the heart of Bhairava himself.
