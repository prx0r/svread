ptvm-p03-v19 — By this mere knowing, all powers know; a kinsman of the śākinī clan he may be, even without yoga.

ptvm-p03-v20 — Ignorant of rite, knowing ordinance, he turns to worship — having made time-fire from the first, ending in māyā, gone to the brahma-body.

ptvm-p03-v21 — Śiva, whose end is endless all, deemed the supreme power-triad; whatever stands on the pure path abides inside that.

ptvm-p03-v22 — The atom soon attains pure lordly knowing; its mover is Śiva to be known, all-knowing highest Lord.

ptvm-p03-v23 — All-going, stainless, clear, full, self-aboded, bright — like the great power-formed tree standing in the banyan seed.

ptvm-p03-v24 — So stands this moving-still world in the heart-seed; who knows it truly — his is the freedom-going.

ptvm-p03-v25 — Initiation becomes undoubted, free of sesame-ghee offering — on crown, face, and heart, on the hidden, on the form, and so indeed.

ptvm-p03-v26 — Placing done, topknot bound, twenty-seven-muttered; with each one let the ten quarters' binding be yoked.

ptvm-p03-v27 — Three claps first, sounded, to clear obstacles; with topknot-count thrice-told, then sprinkle water.
