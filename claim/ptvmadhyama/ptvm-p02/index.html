ptvm-p02-v12 — Who remembers for a moment — in the kiss, navel-sealed — he binds then all, body and mantra-seal host, the man.

ptvm-p02-v16 — They come, pledge got, by Bhairava urged — they grant supreme fruition, whatever fruit is wished.

ptvm-p02-v17 — By this the perfected will perfect, and make mantra-men perfect; whatever is in Bhairava's Tantra, all succeeds from this.

ptvm-p02-v18 — Who thus truly knows even the unseen circle shares fruition ever — yogin and initiate alike.
