## 2 · The three adoptions (concrete)

### A. ARGUMENT-FABRIC as review-as-compiler v2
Map our Scholar pipeline onto their five layers with their six outcomes:
- `essay-claim/1` types gain a **formal tier**: every SUPPORTS-link to a Lean theorem carries theorem id + content hash + scope; PUSHBACK gains formal issues (claim exceeds theorem scope; content smuggled across `contentEq`; R-tier cited as T).
- Adopt the **six outcomes** for PUSHBACK verdicts per claim: accepted / accepted-with-penalty / needs-review / hollow / outside-formal / refuted. `hollow` and `outside-formal` are currently collapsed into "honest remainder" — splitting them is the upgrade.
- Adopt **tradition scoping**: same term in different traditions = different node until bridged (their pramāṇa example maps directly onto our kañcuka/mala terms across Trika vs Abhidhamma readings).
- Adopt **falsifier-required**: no claim moves from `needs-review` without either a supporting span or a recorded falsifier (their gate-before-posterior, minus the Bayesian part).
- Adopt **negative controls**: each formal rung keeps its Broken twin (already our practice — now required by the shared spec, not just habit).
- Do NOT adopt: Bayesian posteriors, branch probabilities, GNN anything, auto-bridging.

### B. The flaws-doc practice
Rewrite BUILD-PLAN §3 in their format: each flaw gets severity (CRITICAL/HIGH/MODERATE/LOW), the exact mechanism of failure, the mitigation, and the **gate that would catch it**. Add the ones we're missing: (a) EO-equivalent gap — our Treeka gap report exists but the assembler is one unbuilt tool (same shape as their EO-critical admission); (b) opportunity-cost rule with the 2-week content test; (c) autonomy paradox (API keys/quotas/model drift — we share it); (d) Sanskrit/translation overlap rule (TO-vs-RO equivalent: our L-layers vs argmaps — state the boundary the way they did: layers own text-fidelity, argmaps own argument reconstruction).

### C. Video + translation strictness upgrades
- Packs gain per-scene review + decision log (their HITL loop §17), on top of our existing approval SHAs.
- L200/C1 gain TO-shape fields (alternatives list, per-unit confidence, pass log) as *metadata*, never as substitutes for verbatim binding or human sign-off.

## 3 · The UNO engine placement (from the geometricengine audit)

The UNO engine is the teacher's missing move-selector: `state → (function, mechanism, register) → next-state` with learned weights. Fit to our architecture:
- Functions/mechanisms/registers become the teacher's move vocabulary (mapped in the teacher spec's four moves: relay / scoped reading / question / gated practice unit).
- **Retrain, don't reuse**: their weights are self-consistent fiction (predicted impact vs authored next turn, same hand). Our transitions — tension arcs open→loosening→resolved with what preceded the loosening — are the first real supervision this architecture has ever had. Retraining UNO-shape on our sessions IS the N1 miner + trajectory spec implemented.
- Phase arc maps cleanly: UNMAKING (deconstruct) → REMAKING (rebuild) → SELF-MAKING (direct seeing) → META (process discipline) mirrors our recognition-arc template (affliction → inquiry → recognition).
- Keep the evidence boundary: engine outputs are proposals (heuristic shelf), gated like everything else. The 18 functions' own risk notes transfer with them.

## 4 · What stays separate, and why

- **Bayesian posteriors ≠ audit receipts.** Their legitimacy is a number that moves; ours is a receipt that pins. These are incompatible theories of trust. We cite their per-question record shape, not their update rule.
- **Reputation ≠ signature.** Community tiers vs owner signature. No merge.
- **Organism paths differ.** Their convergence builds organisms; our N3 ledger is owner-gated and personal-first. Same horizon, no shared machinery yet.
- **No code imports from the generated dumps** (final please / satan / tcis2): untrusted scaffolds with mocks and contamination. Ideas (UIM/PDM/TVM/SRM/EFGM shapes, belief-graph + safety-governor pattern) may inform specs; code does not transfer.
