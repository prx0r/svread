# svatantrya

the inherent condition of sovereign freedom (my own interpretation)

## This site aims to achieve these 4 goals

- free and reuseable translations of important sanskrit texts
- free AI pipelines for creating and publishing your own: translations, essays, books, video explainers, podcasts, courses, interactive practise modules
- formalisation of tantric traditions and practices
- the realisation of freedom for everyone, by themselves, independent of institutions
