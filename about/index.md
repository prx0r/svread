# About Svatantrya Reader

Canonical: https://svread.pages.dev/about/

We publish checkable translations of Trika Shaivism texts: every claim names its source bytes, its gate status, its formal status, and its witness status — and lets anyone recompute the hashes.

Authoritative checkability, not authoritative translations. Machine-proposed until a scholar signs. Witnesses UNPINNED until manuscript eyes verify one. Lean models check finite counts; they never prove a reading is true.

Rights: Sanskrit sources edition-labelled (largely GRETIL, non-commercial). Our English layers quoted only from pinned bundles. Scholar essays self-attested unless independently reviewed.
