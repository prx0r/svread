  3. formalisation of tantric traditions and practices
  4. the realisation of freedom for everyone, by themselves, independent of institutions

What this site is, technically

A checkable Trika Shaivism library and workshop: Sanskrit sources with layered translations, machine derivations with visible disagreement, formal models with must-fail twins, and one URL per claim so anyone can re-verify it. Reading and practice are free. Nothing is auto-published, auto-signed, or auto-promoted.
