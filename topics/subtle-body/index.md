# The subtle body, compared — Trika's two eights and Vedānta's 17/19 (puryaṣṭaka · sūkṣmaśarīra · liṅgaśarīra)

Two systems, four inventories, and one warning that the number eight is not a shared unit. In Trika, Abhinavagupta gives the puryaṣṭaka ("city of eight") in two different ways — a prāṇa-based formula (five vital breaths + the two sense-organ groups + the inner organ = 8; expanded 18) and a tanmātra-based formula (five tanmātras + manas + buddhi + ahaṃkāra = 8) — and says the difference is in the formulation, not in the thing. In Advaita Vedānta the subtle body has 17 parts (5 prāṇas + 5 jñānendriyas + 5 karmendriyas + manas + buddhi) or 19 (adding ahaṃkāra and citta). The formal model below machine-checks the arithmetic and the non-identity of the partitions; it does not decide which inventory is metaphysically correct.
