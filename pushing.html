SCHOLARLY PERSPECTIVES

Use published scholarship

Choose an available work above, then look for published Scholar essays. Authors decide separately whether an exact essay version can be used in answers or drafts.

Permission covers the selected use in a model request; it does not prove a claim or grant other publication rights.

05 · PUSH DEEPER

Ask Qwen

Send only the selected branch and the chosen passage and its available layers. Qwen's response is a proposed reading, not a verified claim.

Requires sign-in and the separately configured Scholar Worker and Qwen provider. You will be asked to confirm before anything is sent.

06 · MAKE SOMETHING OF IT

From enquiry to essay

Turn the tree into a working brief. Review each claim and source before it becomes an essay.

Only the style edits you select are sent to Qwen, with your confirmation.

Saving creates a new private Scholar essay draft for the selected work. Review, sign-off and publication are separate actions.
