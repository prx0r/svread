hṛdi vande — In the heart I bow

Abhinavagupta, Bhairavastava v.1; translation-work bhairavastava · Bhairavastava bh-p01

content l03-hrdi-vande

1 · Original witness

… भैरवनाथम् अनाथशरण्यं त्वन्मयचित्ततया हृदि वन्दे
… bhairavanātham anāthaśaraṇyaṃ tvanmayacittatayā hṛdi vande

Bhairava, Lord, refuge of the helpless — with a mind become Yours, in my heart, I bow.

  • author: Abhinavagupta
  • work: Bhairavastava
  • verse: 1
  • edition: translation-work/bhairavastava (gold)
  • variants: Extract closes the verse; opening is an acc. object chain.

2 · Recitation

tvad-ma-ya-cit-ta-ta-yā | hṛ-di | van-de
  • vowel lengths: ṛ vocalic in hṛdi; e in vande
  • articulation: vande = 1sg middle -e; hṛdi locative -i
  • metre: Bhairavastava opening cadence

Learn it — practical entry

Decode it — rigorous language instruction

Rule box 3 — where vs by-what
Locative answers “where?” (hṛdi). Instrumental answers “by/with what?” (cittatayā).

7 · Syntax

  • case roles: hṛdi loc place; vande acc object chain; tvanmayacittatayā instr means
  • agreement: acc.m -am; instr.f -ayā
  • finite verbs: vande — 1sg present middle
  • implied: subject “I” in the ending

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: by-mind-become-Yours(INSTR) · in-heart(LOC) · I-venerate

Literal: With mind become Yours, in the heart, I venerate [Bhairava-Lord, refuge of the helpless].

Readable: Bhairava, Lord, refuge of the helpless — with a mind become Yours, in my heart, I bow.

Alternatives: Locative hṛdi unambiguous in L0.

Recognise it — Trika exegesis

Text / Abhinavagupta: Interior veneration: instrument is a mind already “become Yours,” not a separate ego.

Bridge: Locative says where; Trika reads heart as site where recognition already operates.

  • hṛdi (loc) — heart as locus of practice
  • tvanmayacittatā (instr) — mind made of You — non-dual instrument
  • vande (1sg mid) — bowing as self-involved act

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary here.

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing only.

10 · Practice and mastery

  • recite: Hand on heart once.
  • write: hṛdi under “where?”; vande under “what verb?”
  • memorise: Memorise 1sg middle -e from vande.
  • when: Morning or before study.

  • Reverse hṛdi.
