Lesson 5 / 10 · stage-3-instructions

mā kiṃcit tyaja — Abandon nothing

Abhinavagupta, Anuttarāṣṭikā v.2 (imperative extract); translation-work anuttarastika · Anuttarāṣṭikā anu-p02

content l05-ma-kimcit

1 · Original witness

मा किंचित् त्यज मा गृहाण विहर स्वस्थो यथावस्थितः
mā kiṃcit tyaja mā gṛhāṇa vihara svastho yathāvasthitaḥ

Abandon nothing; grasp nothing; live at ease, just as you are.

  • author: Abhinavagupta
  • work: Anuttarāṣṭikā
  • verse: 2
  • edition: translation-work/anuttarastika (gold; AgAna_2)
  • variants: Extract from rope-snake verse closing. Full verse: mithyāmohakṛt …

2 · Recitation

  • metre: instructional cadence

Learn it — practical entry

Prohibitions and a command to abide.

Rule box 5 — mā + imperative = don’t
Prohibitive mā with imperatives: mā tyaja, mā gṛhāṇa. na negates statements.

7 · Syntax

  • case roles: 2sg imperatives; kiṃcit acc object; svasthaḥ/yathāvasthitaḥ nom describing you
  • agreement: m.nom -aḥ
  • finite verbs: tyaja, gṛhāṇa, vihara — 2sg imperatives
  • implied: subject “you” in endings

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: do-not · anything · abandon · do-not · grasp · live · healthy · as-standing

Literal: Do not abandon anything; do not grasp anything; live at ease, as you stand.

Readable: Abandon nothing; grasp nothing; live healthy, staying as you are.

Alternatives: L0 morph on long compound noisy — teach imperative core first.

Recognise it — Trika exegesis

Text / Abhinavagupta: After rope-snake diagnosis: if bondage is not real tattvatas, neither grasping nor rejecting is the fix.

Bridge: mā+imp is grammar; “live as you are” is the verse’s own practice.

  • mā + 2sg imp — non-grasping as imperative
  • yathāvasthita — what is already the case
  • mithyāmohakṛt — saṃsāra as error-maker

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary here.

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing; L3 from translation-work.
