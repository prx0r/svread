Mahopadeśaviṃśatikā 8 — the complete verse

Abhinavagupta, Mahopadeśaviṃśatikā v.8 full; translation-work mahopadeshavimshatika · Mahopadeśa mpa-p01-v08

1 · Original witness

अलं भेदानुकथया त्वद्भक्तिरसचर्वणात् । सर्वमेकमिदं शान्तमिति वक्तुं च लज्जते
alaṃ bhedānukathayā tvadbhaktirasacarvaṇāt | sarvamekamidaṃ śāntamiti vaktuṃ ca lajjate

No more of difference-talk. From chewing devotion's sap for you — all this one, at peace, though saying so is shy.

  • case roles: alaṃ+instr; abl cause; nom.n chain; inf purpose of lajjate
  • agreement: sarvam/idam/śāntam neuter chain
  • finite verbs: lajjate only — 3sg middle
  • implied: copula in “all this [is] one”

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: enough · difference-talk(INSTR) · devotion-sap-chewing(ABL) · all-one-this · peace-thus · to-say · even · is-shy

Literal: Enough with difference-talk; from chewing the sap of devotion to you, all this is one, at peace — though even to say so is shy.

Readable: No more of difference-talk. From chewing devotion's sap for you — all this one, at peace, though even to say so is shy.

Alternatives: L3 song human-review — faithful placement, no silent retranslation.

Recognise it — Trika exegesis

Text / Abhinavagupta: Difference-discourse stops because bhakti-rasa saturated thought; non-dual claim arrives shyly (C1 essence 7).

Bridge: Shyness to say “all is one” is language’s limit — after every case is proven.

  • bheda-nukathā — discourse of separation
  • sarvam ekam idam — recognition as neuter predicate
  • lajjate — ineffability as affective sign

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary for this verse.
