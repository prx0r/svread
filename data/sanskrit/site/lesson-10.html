1 · Original witness

इह ज्ञानं मोक्षकारणं बन्धनिमित्तस्य अज्ञानस्य विरोधकत्वात्
iha jñānaṃ mokṣakāraṇaṃ bandhanimittasya ajñānasya virodhakatvāt

Here knowledge is the cause of liberation because it opposes ignorance, the cause of bondage.

Rule box 8 — -tva + -āt = because of the fact that
Abstract -tva/-tā then ablative -āt builds causal “because”: virodhakatvāt.

7 · Syntax

  • case roles: jñānaṃ nom; mokṣakāraṇam nom pred; gen chain; virodhakatvāt abl of cause
  • agreement: neuters in causal spine
  • finite verbs: none in focus sentence — nominal predication
  • implied: “is” implied; because-logic in -āt

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: here · knowledge(NOM) · liberation-cause(NOM) · bondage-cause(GEN) · ignorance(GEN) · opposing-ness(ABL)

Literal: Here knowledge is the cause of liberation, [because] of being opposed to ignorance, the cause of bondage.

Readable: Here knowledge is the cause of liberation because it opposes ignorance, the cause of bondage.

Alternatives: Machine-proposed gold; focus sentence from syllabus; no invented completion.

Recognise it — Trika exegesis

Text / Abhinavagupta: Opening causal argument: knowledge opposes ignorance — recognition theme in philosophical prose.

Bridge: Ablative of cause builds “because”; Trika content is the jñāna/ajñāna opposition the grammar encodes.

  • jñāna (nom) — knowledge as liberating cause
  • ajñāna (gen) — ignorance as bondage-cause
  • virodhakatva (abl) — opposition as causal hinge
