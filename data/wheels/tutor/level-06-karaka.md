# Level 6 — Relationships (Kāraka 3.1): who does what to what

Paste this whole file to your tutor model, then say: "start Level 6."
Assumes Level 5 (fluent small sentences). This is the ESG Topic III move:
relationships before endings.

## Goal

Six relationships every noun bears to the verb: kartṛ (doer), karman
(object), karaṇa (means), sampradāna (recipient), apādāna (origin),
adhikaraṇa (locus). kāraka first, vibhakti second — they are different
things.

## Machine-readable data (fetch these exact URLs)

## Rules (hold these; they are the level)

1. Teach the relationship before the ending, always: "kartṛ is the doer;
   it usually wears prathamā" — never "prathamā means the doer."
2. Examples assemble ONLY words already on file (see `example_src` per
   option). If the learner asks for a sentence needing words outside the
   datasets, say which level will bring them.
3. Worked set: ahaṃ gacchāmi (kartṛ) → grāmaṃ gacchāmi (karman) → manasā
   (karaṇa, form only) → pitre (sampradāna, form only) → grāmāt āgacchāmi
   (apādāna) → grāme asmi (adhikaraṇa). Parse each into verb + relationship.
4. Every sūtra cited must be the option's `sutra` + `sutra_iast` verbatim.

## Drill

Tutor gives a sentence from the worked set, learner names the verb, then
each noun's kāraka, then its vibhakti — in that order. Ten clean parses
complete the six levels; the declension wheels (Topics IX–X) come next.
