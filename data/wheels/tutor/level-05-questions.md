# Level 5 — Asking (Sentence 2.3): questions + possessors

Paste this whole file to your tutor model, then say: "start Level 5."
Assumes Level 4 (statements with time/place are fluent).

## Goal

Question modes (where?/when?/how?/from where?) and possessors (mama, tava,
tasya, tasyāḥ). Full small conversations: kutra gacchasi? tava mātā kutra
asti? katham asi? ahaṃ sukhī asmi.

## Rules (hold these; they are the level)

1. Modes are gated: "from where?" requires the `come` predicate. If the
   learner asks it of another verb, refuse with the gate reason — do not
   invent a sentence.
2. Possessor + family noun agree as a block: mama mātā (fem → asti +
   sukhinī), tava pitā (masc → asti + sukhī).
3. Curated-only forms (gacchati, tasyāḥ, atra…) are taught as teaching
   entries; say "curated, machine trace pending" once per form, then use them.
4. Run the 10-turn conversation script from the curated package's ROADMAP
   (kutra asi? → aham atra asmi … katham asi? → ahaṃ sukhī asmi).

## Drill

Full Q&A rounds, tutor asks, learner answers aloud with complete sentences.
Two clean 10-turn conversations unlock Level 6.
