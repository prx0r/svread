## Key corrections to avoid

- √श्रु: first singular imperfect **अशृणवम्**, third singular imperfect **अशृणोत्**. Do not apply an invented regular stem. See [AVG Sanskrit](https://avg-sanskrit.org/2011/06/21/%E0%A4%85%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%B6%E0%A5%83%E0%A4%A3%E0%A5%81%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D-3as-%E0%A4%B5%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%A7%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B2%E0%A4%81%E0%A4%99%E0%A5%8D/) and [Sanskrit Kalp](https://sanskritkalp.com/samskrit-dhaturoop/shru).
- √कृ: करोमि → अकरवम् → करिष्यामि; 3sg करोति → अकरोत् → करिष्यति. See [Sanskrit Study Companion](https://sanskrithelp.org/study-guides/).
- √दा: ददामि → अददाम् → दास्यामि; 3sg ददाति → अददात् → दास्यति; not regular *अददम्. See [Dhātu Rūpāvalī](https://www.cdsharma.in/dhatu-rupavali?id=1252).
- √पठ् present, imperfect, and future paradigms: [NCERT / Philoid](https://philoid.com/index.php/ncert/chapter/ihsh116).
- गुरुः is **u-stem**, not the -a-stem pattern of शिष्यः. [CBSE guru declension](https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/publication/archive/Kanika-VII.pdf).
- Modern coffee काफी (f.) and tea चायम् (n.) are pragmatic spoken Sanskrit choices; avoid projecting them backwards into ancient literature. [Kosha](https://kosha.app/word/en/coffee); [modern literary tea usage](https://www.wisdomlib.org/history/compilation/discovery-of-sanskrit-treasures/ocr/1527669/67).
- पात्रम् is a general receptacle/vessel; in this wheel it can refer to a cup or bowl, **not an exact lexical equivalent of thali**. If a precise cup is desired, add चषकः after the core pack, not silently in preliminaries.
- 'His own cup' in the target sentence uses स्वपात्रात् (sva- owned by the agent, ablative of पात्र), but English 'his' could also refer to another male. Ask if ambiguous; in the benchmark explicitly say “his own”.
- Location आश्रमे (in the ashram) and destination आश्रमम् (to the ashram) differ. The same distinction holds for उद्याने vs उद्यानम् and आपणे vs आपणम्.
- Time words: अद्य today, ह्यः yesterday, श्वः tomorrow. A future action may occur today; do not infer tense from time adverb in unrestricted language, though **our diagnostic exercises** intentionally align simple time/tense pairs.
- Lङ् here is the imperfect, not all possible Sanskrit past tense. Not all real-world uses of लङ् map cleanly onto 'yesterday' independent of grammatical tradition/context.
- Modern pedagogical models often separate words to show morphological boundaries. When scoring, accept equivalent external sandhi and compare underlying grammatical forms. Do not reject मातृका spelling variants without evidence. The morphology inventory uses unsandhied words for teaching, and does not purport to generate all sandhi variants.
- Kāraka is a semantic relation, not a numbered case; genitive ownership is not itself one of the canonical six kārakas. 'Recipient' normally uses dative, 'source' ablative, 'location' locative, but meanings and case selection depend on construction.
- Ātmanepada is **not synonymous with passive**. Voice/dual/plural are outside wheel v1 and require their own reviewed expansion.
