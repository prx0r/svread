tāmevālokayec chaktiṃ manāk kumbhakavṛttinā /
etal lakṣaṇam uddiṣṭam utthāpanam ataḥ paramjuṣadrecakavṛttyā tu mantraṃ caiva samuccaret /
prabuddhāṃ cintayecchaktiṃ daṇḍavat parameśvarīmādhāramadhyādāyātā suṣumnāmārgam āśritā /
utthāpanaṃ samākhyātaṃ bodhanaṃ paratastathākandastho vedhayen nābhiṃ tato hṛtsthaṃ pitāmaham /
kaṇṭhasthamacyutaṃ sākṣādrudraṃ tālutale sthitambhruvormadhyagataṃ tv īśaṃ brahmadvāre sadāśivam /
bodhayitvā vrajed āśu padaṃ cānāśritaṃ śivametadbodhanam uddiṣṭaṃ cakraviśrāmaṇaṃ tataḥ /
svābhāvikaṃ dalaṃ dīptaṃ dravaṃ sthiranabhopamamamṛtaṃ śekharaṃ caiva śaktirbrahmā tathaiva ca /
bindunādaṃ tathā proktaṃ cakradvādaśakaṃ kilavedhayantī kramāc chaktiścakre cakre pratikṣaṇam /
viśramet sā mahādevī cakraviśrāma uttamaḥhṛdayaṃ kampate pūrvaṃ tālukadvārameva ca /
śiraśca bhramate tasya dṛṣṭisaṃkrāntilakṣaṇamekaikaṃ bhramayaty aṅgam aṅgapratyaṅgasaṃdhiṣu /
ghūrṇate hṛdayaṃ cāsya samyagvidyāprabhāvataḥ
