## tal-a02-p02 (2.11-2.20)

## tal-a02-p03 (2.21-2.30)
[and]-aprakāśe@tha tasminvā vastutā kathamucyate / na prakāśaviśeṣatvamata evopapadyate ata ekaprakāśo@yamiti vāde@tra susthite / dūrādāvāritāḥ satyaṃ vibhinnajñānavādinaḥ prakāśamātramuditamaprakāśaniṣedhanāt / ekaśabdasya na tvarthaḥ saṃkhyā cidvyaktibhedabhāk naiṣa śaktirmahādevī na paratrāśrito yataḥ / na caiṣa śaktimāndevo na kasyāpyāśrayo yataḥ naiṣa dhyeyo dhyātrabhāvānna dhyātā dhyeyavarjanāt / na pūjyaḥ pūjakābhāvātpūjyābhāvānna pūjakaḥ na mantro na ca mantryo@sau na ca mantrayitā prabhuḥ / na dīkṣā dīkṣako vāpi na dīkṣāvānmaheśvaraḥ sthānāsananirodhārghasaṃghānāvāhanādikam / visarjanāntaṃ nāstyatra kartṛkarmakriyojjhite na sanna cāsatsadasanna ca tannobhayojjhitam / durvijñeyā hi sāvasthā kimapyetadanuttaram ayamityavabhāso hi yo bhāvo@vacchidātmakaḥ / sa eva ghaṭavalloke saṃstathā naiṣa bhairavaḥ asattvaṃ cāprakāśatvaṃ na kutrāpyupayogitā / viśvasya jīvitaṃ satyaṃ prakāśaikātmakaśca saḥ

## tal-a02-p04 (2.31-2.40)
[and]-ābhyāmeva tu hetubhyāṃ na dvyātmā na dvayojjhitaḥ / sarvātmanā hi bhātyeṣa kena rūpeṇa mantryatām śrīmattriśirasi proktaṃ parajñānasvarūpakam / śaktyā garbhāntarvartinyā śaktigarbha paraṃ padam na bhāvo nāpyabhāvo na dvayaṃ vācāmagocarāt / akathyapadavīrūḍhaṃ śaktisthaṃ śaktivarjitam iti ye rūḍhasaṃvittiparamārthapavitritāḥ / anuttarapathe rūḍhāste@bhyupāyāniyantritāḥ teṣāmidaṃ samābhāti sarvato bhāvamaṇḍalam / puraḥsthameva saṃvittibhairavāgnivilāpitam eteṣāṃ sukhaduḥkhāṃśaśaṃkātaṃkavikalpanāḥ / nirvikalpaparāveśamātraśeṣatvamāgatāḥ eṣāṃ na mantro na dhyānaṃ na pūjā nāpi kalpanā / na samayyādikācāryaparyantaḥ ko@pi viśramaḥ samastayantraṇātantratroṭanāṭaṃkadharmiṇaḥ / nānugrahātparaṃ kiṃciccheṣavṛttau prayojanam svaṃ kartavyaṃ kimapi kalayaṃlloka eṣa prayatnānno pārārthyaṃ prati ghaṭayate kāṃcana svapravṛttim / yastu dhvastākhilabhavamalo bhairavībhāvapūrṇaḥ kṛtyaṃ tasya sphuṭamidamiyallokakartavyamātram taṃ ye paśyanti tādrūpyakrameṇāmalasaṃvidaḥ / te@pi tadrūpiṇastāvatyevāsyānugrahātmatā

## tal-a02-p05 (2.41-2.50)
