valmīkatāḍanāddevi mṛtaḥ kinnu mahoragaḥ bhramanti jñānivalloke bhrāmayanti janānapi karmabrahmobhayabhraṣṭaṃ taṃ tyajedantyajaṃ yathā caranti gardabhādyāśca yoginaste bhavanti kim mṛdbhasmavāsino grāmyāḥ kiṃ te muktā bhavanti hi hiraṇādimṛgā devi yoginaste bhavanti kim maṇḍūkamatsyapramukhā vratinaste bhavanti kim janānāṃ purato devi vibudhāḥ kiṃ bhavanti hi na pibanti mahītoyaṃ yoginaste bhavanti kim tiṣṭhanti śūkarādyāśca yoginaste bhavanti kim mokṣasya kāraṇaṃ sākṣāttattvajñānaṃ kuleśvari paramārthaṃ na jānanti paśupāśāniyantritāḥ kālomīgrāhagrastāśca tiṣṭhanti hi kutākīkāḥ viḍambakasya tasyāpi tat sarvaṃ kākabhāṣitam paṭhantyahanīśaṃ devi paratattvaparāṅmukhaḥ cintayā duḥkhitā mūḍhāstiṣṭhanti vyākulendriyāḥ anyathā śāstrasadbhāvo vyākhyāṃ kurvanti cānyathā ahaṅkārahatāḥ kecidupadeśavivajītāḥ na jānanti paraṃ tattvaṃ darvī pākarasaṃ yathā paṭhanti vedaśāstrāṇi durlabho bhāvavedakaḥ gopaḥ kakṣagataṃ chāgaṃ kūpe paśyati durmatiḥ na nivartteta timiraṃ kadāciddīpavārttayā devi prajñāvataḥ śāstraṃ tattvajñānasya kāraṇam sadvidyādānaśūrādyairguṇaivīkhyātamānavāḥ evaṃ ye śāstrasammūḍhāste dūrasthā na saṃśayaḥ
